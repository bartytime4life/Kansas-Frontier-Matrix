# Settlements and infrastructure supplement

Evidence snapshot: `main@ebcc4988a08a3b96d10ee65ae9b4b09a37af5ebe`. Draft reference; formal lane acceptance remains pending.

## Research scope

Use settlements and infrastructure records to investigate places, facilities, footprints and documented relationships. A geometry or address can locate a record; it does not establish current condition, ownership, legal boundary, operating status or service availability.

## Investigation packet

Record the question and audience, selected area and dates, source product/version, native object identifiers, geometry support, exclusions and unresolved status fields. Keep a source's published inventory date separate from the date a facility began or ended operation. Compare revisions with explicit identity matching and retain changed/omitted records for review.

## Validation and handoff

Use the owning domain's actual contracts, schemas and tests to assess the packet. The domain contains mixed maturity; a family of files does not establish complete semantic validation or operational telemetry. A reviewer should be able to identify the exact candidate and the tests that ran, including unsupported fields and sensitive representations.

This supplement supplies navigation and an investigation method. It does not activate a source, admit infrastructure details or publish a facility assessment.
