## Reconciliation and selected increment

Current source contracts answer whether a descriptor is current, whether terms changed, and whether later activation is supported. They do not record the acquisition agent's proposed conduct. Expanding those objects would mix distinct review clocks and increase migration risk.

The selected increment is one additive preflight candidate that composes existing source and review families by opaque reference. It makes terms, robots, rate, identity, proxy, distribution, and review posture explicit without fetching or activating anything.

## Source-to-profile mapping

| Respect rate constraints. | Source-default policy reference or explicit bounded concurrency and delay. | No throttling execution or source-capacity claim. |

## Directory Rules basis

Semantic meaning is placed beside source contracts. Shape, fixtures, validator, tests, workflow, source map, and generated receipt stay in their established responsibility roots. No new root or parallel source registry, rights authority, connector, credential store, scheduler, policy, release, or publication authority is introduced.

## Deferred questions

- Whether the preflight remains a separate record or is later referenced by a compatible `SourceActivationDecision` version remains undecided.
- Live terms and robots snapshot acquisition, authentication, retention, and drift monitoring require separately reviewed source-evidence contracts.
- Route-specific retry, backoff, caching, and incident response remain connector and operations responsibilities.

## Validation and rollback

Focused validation covers closed shape, deterministic identity, UTC timestamps, canonical review references, route/robots coherence, terms evidence, rate declarations, anti-disguise and anti-evasion rules, explicit exceptional-route support, unresolved abstention, malformed Unicode, and unknown-field rejection.

Rollback is a focused revert of the additive packet. No source deactivation, credential revocation, job cancellation, data cleanup, release withdrawal, cache invalidation, or public-artifact cleanup is required because the profile has no consumer and performs no acquisition.
