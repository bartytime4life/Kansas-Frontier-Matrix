## Highest-confidence conclusions

1. Most copy-ready code in the source has been overtaken by more governed repository surfaces and must not be pasted into parallel watcher, policy, receipt, Atlas, tile, or digest lanes.
2. Biodiversity and NDVI ideas are useful corroboration for existing source-retrieval and fixture-only assessment work, not authority for live network sweeps or county presence claims.
3. Missing-person and consumer-DNA proposals touch living people, purpose limitation, consent, contracts, correction, and reidentification. Public visibility or user export does not establish KFM admission or reuse rights.
4. Fixed privacy or performance numbers (`k=5`, an epsilon range, zoom limits, latency, decode, memory, feature-count, or frame-rate thresholds) are proposals requiring a named mechanism, owner, dataset, threat model, device class, and validation evidence.
5. ETags and `Last-Modified` values can support retrieval and change detection, but they do not replace content digests, source authority, EvidenceBundle closure, rights, policy, review, or release state.
6. A TEE attestation can support an execution claim only. It cannot prove admissibility, ethical purpose, statistical disclosure safety, evidence sufficiency, or public-release fitness.
7. Automated issue creation, message posting, catalog mutation, commits, pushes, or publication are external effects requiring explicit control-plane authorization, separation of duties, idempotency, audit, and rollback.

## Unsafe direct transfers

| Packet pattern | Why direct transfer is unsafe | Required correction |
|---|---|---|
| Treat a successful HTTP probe as source readiness | Availability does not establish identity, rights, content quality, admissibility, or claim support. | Preserve probe facts as bounded observations and route through source admission. |
| Use ETag as the EvidenceRef identity or artifact digest | ETag semantics are server-specific and may be weak, multipart, mutable, or absent. | Bind source-native identity, retrieval receipt, content digest where permitted, and catalog lineage separately. |

| Commit generated digest artifacts from scheduled CI | Self-mutating workflows blur review, release, and branch protections. | Generate read-only candidates; publish or commit only through a separately authorized reviewed transition. |
| Publish missing-person index, heatmap, or point layers from public portals | Living-person risk, purpose limitation, removal/correction, source terms, small-cell disclosure, and harm remain unresolved. | Hold source-specific work until authoritative review; default public behavior to no record exposure. |
