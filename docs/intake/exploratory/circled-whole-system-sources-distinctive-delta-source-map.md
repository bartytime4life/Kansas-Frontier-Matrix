## 3. Retained distinctive deltas

### 3.1 Two-axis assessment: authority is not maturity

A dated assessment should state two independent answers whenever it makes a material repository-capability claim.

| Axis | Example vocabulary | Question answered | Must not imply |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authority / epistemic posture | `CONFIRMED`, `PROPOSED`, `UNKNOWN`, `NEEDS VERIFICATION`, `HOLD`, `DENY`, `ABSTAIN` | How strongly may the stated claim or decision be relied on for its exact scope? | Implementation, deployment, source activation, release, or publication |
| Capability maturity | `IMPLEMENTED`, `PARTIAL`, `DRAFT`, `DEPRECATED`, `ABSENT`, `NOT INSPECTED` | What implementation evidence exists for the capability at the pinned snapshot? | Acceptance, evidence sufficiency, rights clearance, policy approval, review, or public fitness |

Examples:

- an accepted placement decision may have partial implementation;
- an implemented validator may enforce only a proposed inactive profile;
- a confirmed source fact may remain held because rights or sensitivity are unresolved; and
- a complete synthetic fixture packet may still lack target-runtime or public-release proof.

**Bounded rule:** do not infer either axis from the other. If one axis was not inspected, record that explicitly. This is a reporting discipline, not a new machine enum.

### 3.2 Five-session contributor rhythm

Use the following sequence only to organize bounded work and handoffs:

| Session | Focus | Expected handoff to existing owners |
|---|---|---|
| `FRAME` | Current truth, bounded claim or question, place/time scope, user value, owner, non-goals, and stop conditions | Intake record, issue, or current task/PR contract |
| `TRACE` | Source roles, rights, sensitivity, identity, temporal model, and evidence target | Existing source, evidence, temporal, rights, and sensitivity surfaces |
| `PROVE` | Contract, schema, fixture, validator, policy dependency, positive cases, and negative cases | Responsibility-owning contracts, schemas, policy, tests, and receipts |
| `SHOW` | Governed API/map/drawer/focus interaction with visible `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, `HOLD`, stale, conflict, and correction states | Existing carrier and public-safe interaction surfaces |
| `REHEARSE` | Human review, release dry run, correction, rollback, unresolved items, and next decision | Existing review, release, correction, rollback, and handoff records |

The sequence must not rename the KFM lifecycle, gate statuses, policy outcomes, promotion decisions, ADR states, or release states. Completing all five sessions does not prove the claim or authorize merge, release, deployment, publication, or public use.

### 3.3 Five briefing lenses

Recurring briefing stories may be grouped through five stable lenses without turning those labels into domain types or authority:

| Lens | Includes | Required separation |
|---|---|---|
| Repository governance and control | Branch/PR state, CI ambiguity, review coverage, ruleset drift, campaign cursors, compatibility migration | Repository observations are not geospatial lifecycle or public-truth events. |
| Volatile advisories and public-safety events | Alerts, advisories, closures, rescissions, status checks | Retrieval failure must not become a false clear state; native issuer and event status remain visible. |
| Conditions, observations, classifications, and forecasts | Station observations, modeled grids, surveys, statewide classes, forecasts | Source role, scale, resolution, time, and uncertainty must not collapse. |
| Governance events and public participation | Meetings, hearings, workshops, rulemaking, comment windows, recommendations, decisions | Announcement, participation, recommendation, decision, and implementation are separate states. |
| Funding, projects, and measured outcomes | Programs, eligibility, applications, reviews, awards, agreements, projects, payments, milestones, completion, observations, evaluation | Funding, work completion, and observed outcomes must not be inferred from one another. |

**Bounded rule:** a briefing item may have one primary lens and secondary tags, but its native object family, source role, real-world state, KFM lifecycle state, evidence, policy, review, and release state continue to control. These lenses add no new schema or routing authority.

## 4. Combined non-authoritative use

The three retained ideas form one compact contributor view:

1. **Classify the story, not its truth.** Select the briefing lens that best identifies the owning responsibility and the anti-collapse risk.
2. **Assess two independent axes.** Record authority/epistemic posture and capability maturity separately at a pinned evidence snapshot.
3. **Organize work without inventing state.** Use `FRAME -> TRACE -> PROVE -> SHOW -> REHEARSE`, mapping every output to an existing KFM owner and vocabulary.
4. **Handoff, do not self-authorize.** Unresolved authority, evidence, rights, sensitivity, policy, review, release, correction, or rollback remains visible in its owning surface.

This combination is useful because each retained idea answers a different question:

| Question | Retained lens |
|---|---|
| What kind of recurring story or responsibility is this? | Five briefing lenses |
| What may be claimed, and what is actually implemented? | Separate authority and maturity axes |
| How can a contributor move a bounded slice toward an honest handoff? | Five-session contributor rhythm |

No row above is a decision, priority score, roadmap commitment, lifecycle stage, or release gate.
