# Statewide research pathways — 2026-11-27

## What changes

Research now leads with eight equally weighted subjects and 24 named entries
into existing public tools. The page starts with a question, explains the tool’s
coverage and limits, and offers connections to other disciplines. The source
catalogue, direct data access, readable reuse credits, three-source comparison,
shareable filters and scientific methods remain available.

The large Konza promotion becomes a closed, optional case study after the main
research experience. Its acquisition handoff, one-tile sampling details,
pending-review status and original ecological references remain intact. The
hero and navigation now describe the broader Kansas scope; Topics and the
Research atlas link back to these pathways.

| Subject | Working entries | Coverage to keep in view |

| Water & watersheds | River Flow, County Drought, Water Chemistry | Three gauges, 105 counties and one chemistry station are different samples. |

| People, counties & places | County Pulse, County Conditions, County Observatory | Annual population components and nonoverlapping ACS periods keep their uncertainty. |

Each subject links to registered map context without claiming that those map
sources supply every tool’s data. Context comparisons preserve exact source IDs,
provider dates, representations, limitations and reuse credits. Native links
reload the same Research route for a different comparison so the initial server
selection cannot be overwritten by state from the previous comparison.

## Delivery and scientific boundaries

The GoDaddy destination remains app `urdng3v1vk`. Chrome attachment currently
requires a desktop network policy, and the available GoDaddy connector does not
expose Node project deployment. Dashboard revision readback and publication have
not been performed by this change. Reverify the connected branch and exact
Preview revision, then the public route, assets and `/api/build` after delivery.

These pathways improve access to shipping tools; they do not establish complete
statewide coverage, compatible samples, causation, source admission or scientific
release. The original dirty checkout and separate private Sites composition,
its audience and storage remain separate. No DNS, settings, relay, credentials,
models or persisted data changes are required. Rollback is a focused application
revert followed by verified redeployment; no data migration applies.
