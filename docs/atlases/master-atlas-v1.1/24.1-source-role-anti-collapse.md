## 1. Purpose and role

§24.1 is the source-role doctrine for the whole project. KFM treats source role as a **first-class identity attribute**: an observed reading is not interchangeable with a modeled estimate, a regulatory determination is not interchangeable with an administrative compilation, an aggregate publication is not interchangeable with candidate evidence, and synthetic content is never the same thing as observed reality. The lifecycle and the governed API both **fail closed** when these roles are conflated.

This file is the **navigation carrier for §24.1**, paired with the receipt-catalog carrier (§24.2) and the pipeline-gate-reference carrier (§24.6). It exists because:

- §24.1 is referenced from every prior session-authored carrier: `receipt-catalog.md` §3 (`SourceDescriptor` is the first receipt class and consumes the seven-role enum); `pipeline-gate-reference.md` §3 (Admission Gate enforces source-role assignment); `domains-v1.1.md` §6 (source-role posture table); `domains-v1.1-ch14.md` §13 (administrative-as-observation anti-collapse for Settlements specifically). Without a §24.1 carrier, those references trace back to the incomplete source-derived Markdown carrier or the source PDFs. A dedicated carrier reduces navigation cost.
- §24.1 is the **highest-leverage** Chapter 24 section without a companion carrier. Receipt-catalog (§24.2) and pipeline-gate-reference (§24.6) cover the operational machinery; §24.1 covers the **identity discipline** that makes the machinery's outputs trustworthy.
- The session-authored anti-collapse content in `domains-v1.1-ch14.md` §13 (administrative-as-observation), `maplibre-master.md` §9 (rendering-related anti-patterns), and the receipt-catalog's `RedactionReceipt` / `AggregationReceipt` discipline all depend on the seven-role enum being canonical somewhere in `docs/atlases/`.

**This file is not authority.** Two non-collapse rules apply:

1. **Atlas v1.1 §24.1 wins on wording.** Where this carrier paraphrases, the section is authoritative.
2. **Chapter 24 registers are navigational aids, not authority.** Per Atlas v1.1's own §24 preamble: *"A Chapter 24 table that disagrees with v1.0 is treated as a drift entry, not as a correction."* That rule applies one level out: where this carrier disagrees with §24.1, drift is filed to `docs/registers/DRIFT_REGISTER.md`.

## 4. §24.1.1 Canonical source-role classes

> **Source:** Atlas v1.1 §24.1.1. All rows `CONFIRMED doctrine`. Reproduced verbatim from the source.

| Role | Definition (`CONFIRMED doctrine`) | Typical example | Allowed downstream role | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Observed** | A direct reading, measurement, or first-hand evidentiary record tied to a place and time. | Stream-gauge stage reading; soil pedon description; air quality monitor sample; ground archaeological observation. | May feed `modeled` or `aggregate` products; never relabeled as `regulatory` or `administrative`. | `[DOM-HYD] [DOM-SOIL] [DOM-AIR] [DOM-ARCH]` |
| **Regulatory** | An authoritative determination by a regulatory or governing body with legal or administrative force. | NFHL flood-zone designation; air-quality non-attainment ruling; designated critical habitat unit; protected-species listing. | Cite as regulatory context; never labeled an `observed` event or a `modeled` estimate. | `[DOM-HYD] [DOM-AIR] [DOM-FAUNA] [DOM-FLORA]` |
| **Modeled** | A derived product from inputs, assumptions, or fitted parameters; uncertainty and provenance of inputs must be preserved. | Hydrograph reconstruction; smoke trajectory model; suitability raster; population estimation surface; AODRaster. | Cite with model identity, run receipt, and bounds; never labeled an observation. | `[DOM-HYD] [DOM-AIR] [DOM-HAB] [DOM-AG]` |
| **Aggregate** | A published summary, total, or average over a unit (county, year, watershed); irreversible loss of individual record fidelity. | USDA crop county totals; Census tract aggregates; decadal climate normal; resource estimate summary. | Cite with aggregation receipt; never treated as a per-place record. | `[DOM-AG] [DOM-PEOPLE] [DOM-GEOL] [DOM-AIR]` |
| **Administrative** | A compiled record produced by an agency for administration, registration, or accounting purposes — not necessarily an observation or a regulation. | Land office tract book; deed index compilation; county incorporation record; transport facility roster. | Cite as administrative context; never collapsed with observation or regulation. | `[DOM-PEOPLE] [DOM-SETTLE] [DOM-ROADS]` |
| **Candidate** | A proposed record awaiting validation, evidence resolution, deduplication, or steward review; not yet authoritative. | Quarantined connector output; unresolved person assertion; duplicate site candidate; unmerged crop observation. | May be cited as candidate evidence in `WORK` / `QUARANTINE`; must not appear in `PUBLISHED` without promotion. | `[ENCY] [DOM-PEOPLE] [DOM-ARCH]` |
| **Synthetic** | Content generated by simulation, reconstruction, AI, or interpolation that has no underlying first-hand observation. Includes synthetic surfaces, generated text, AI-drafted notes. | Synthetic terrain surface; reconstructed historical scene; AI-drafted summary of an EvidenceBundle. | Carries `RealityBoundaryNote` and `RepresentationReceipt`; must never be presented or queried as observed reality. | `[ENCY] [MAP-MASTER] [UIAI] [GAI]` |

<a id="41-reading-note"></a>

### 4.1 Reading note (§24.1.1, end of section)

> **`CONFIRMED doctrine`** *(reproduced verbatim)*: the role of a source is set at admission (`SourceDescriptor`) and is **preserved through every promotion**. Promotion does **not** upgrade an observation to a regulation, or a model to an aggregate, or a candidate to a verified record — those are separate governed transitions with their own evidence and review requirements. `[ENCY] [DIRRULES]`

This reading note is the **central operational rule** of §24.1. Three consequences worth pinning:

1. **Role immutability at admission.** Once `SourceDescriptor.source_role` is set, it cannot be edited in place. Corrections produce a new descriptor + `CorrectionNotice` (per §6 below).
2. **Promotion ≠ relabeling.** The lifecycle invariant (`RAW → WORK / QUARANTINE → PROCESSED → CATALOG / TRIPLET → PUBLISHED`) moves a record between phases; it never moves a record between roles.
3. **Role transitions are separately governed.** Upgrading a `candidate` to a verified `observed` record (for example) requires its own evidence + review process, separate from any phase-level promotion gate.

## 6. §24.1.3 Roles to `SourceDescriptor` fields (PROPOSED)

> **Source:** Atlas v1.1 §24.1.3. Field surface is `PROPOSED`; schema-home convention is `CONFIRMED doctrine`.

### 6.1 Schema-home note (verbatim)

> **`PROPOSED` schema-home note:** `source-role` is a `SourceDescriptor` field; the canonical schema home defaults to **`schemas/contracts/v1/source/source-descriptor.json`** per Directory Rules §7.4 and ADR-0001, unless an accepted ADR relocates it. `NEEDS VERIFICATION`: actual file presence and field names are not asserted here. `[DIRRULES]`

### 6.2 Proposed descriptor surface (illustrative, not authoritative)

| Field | Type / vocabulary | Required? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `source_role` | enum: `observed` \| `regulatory` \| `modeled` \| `aggregate` \| `administrative` \| `candidate` \| `synthetic` | **MUST** | Set at admission. **Never edited in-place**; corrections must produce a new descriptor and a `CorrectionNotice`. |
| `role_authority` | string (issuing body / model identity / steward) | **MUST** when role in `{regulatory, modeled, aggregate}` | Disambiguates the authoring authority for downstream cite text. |
| `role_aggregation_unit` | geometry-scope token (`county`, `HUC`, `tract`, `year`, `decade`, etc.) | **MUST** when `source_role = aggregate` | Prevents geometry-scope drift on join. |
| `role_model_run_ref` | `EvidenceRef` → `ModelRunReceipt` | **MUST** when `source_role = modeled` | Pins the inputs, parameters, and version that produced the value. |
| `role_synthetic_basis` | structured: `{ method, inputs, reality_boundary_note_ref }` | **MUST** when `source_role = synthetic` | Records what is and is not real in the carrier. |
| `role_candidate_disposition` | enum: `pending` \| `merged` \| `rejected` \| `quarantined` | **MUST** when `source_role = candidate` | Tracks promotion state; `PUBLISHED` edge forbidden until merged. |

> `NEEDS VERIFICATION`: implementation of these fields in the mounted `SourceDescriptor` schema.

### 6.3 Why each field exists

The descriptor surface is not arbitrary. Each field operationalizes a specific anti-collapse failure mode from §5:

| Field | Failure mode it operationalizes |
|---|---|
| `source_role` (always required) | All seven failure modes in §5 — every DENY decision starts here. |
| `role_authority` | "Regulatory zone labeled as observed" (banner attribution); "Administrative compilation cited as observation" (issuing body disambiguation); "Modeled product labeled as observed" (model identity). |
| `role_aggregation_unit` | "Aggregate cited as per-place truth" — the geometry-scope token is what the validator checks against on join. |
| `role_model_run_ref` | "Modeled product labeled as observed" — `ModelRunReceipt` pins inputs, parameters, version; without it the model-product is unsafe to publish. |
| `role_synthetic_basis` | "Synthetic content presented as observed reality" — `reality_boundary_note_ref` is what surfaces in the Evidence Drawer. |
| `role_candidate_disposition` | "Candidate record exposed on public surface" — `PUBLISHED` edge is forbidden unless disposition is `merged`. |

## 8. Operational implications

### 8.1 Validator obligations

For an admission to PASS the Admission Gate (`pipeline-gate-reference.md` §3), the following validators must run against the `SourceDescriptor`:

| Validator | Checks |
|---|---|
| **`source_role` enum validator** | `source_role` is one of the seven canonical values; admission DENY otherwise. |
| **`role_authority` required-when validator** | When `source_role ∈ {regulatory, modeled, aggregate}`, `role_authority` is non-empty. |
| **`role_aggregation_unit` required-when validator** | When `source_role = aggregate`, `role_aggregation_unit` carries a valid geometry-scope token. |
| **`role_model_run_ref` required-when validator** | When `source_role = modeled`, `role_model_run_ref` resolves to a `ModelRunReceipt`. |
| **`role_synthetic_basis` required-when validator** | When `source_role = synthetic`, `role_synthetic_basis` includes a `reality_boundary_note_ref`. |
| **`role_candidate_disposition` required-when validator** | When `source_role = candidate`, `role_candidate_disposition` is one of `{pending, merged, rejected, quarantined}`. |
| **Role-immutability validator** | A `SourceDescriptor` PR that mutates `source_role` on an existing descriptor is rejected; corrections must produce a new descriptor + `CorrectionNotice`. |
| **Cross-promotion role-preservation validator** | A promotion that changes `source_role` is rejected; role transitions are separately governed. |

### 8.2 Public-surface obligations

For any `PUBLISHED` artifact (per Atlas §24.6 Release Gate):

| Obligation | Source-role consequence |
|---|---|
| Every public claim resolves to an `EvidenceBundle`. | The bundle's underlying `SourceDescriptor` carries an immutable `source_role`. |
| Public layers must distinguish `regulatory` vs. `observed` content. | Atlas §24.1.2 row 2 requires *"Separate regulatory-layer and observed-event lanes; banner in UI."* |
| Public layers must distinguish `synthetic` vs. `observed` content. | Atlas §24.1.2 row 6 requires `RealityBoundaryNote`, `RepresentationReceipt`, UI badge. |
| Public AI surfaces must cite released evidence. | Atlas §24.1.2 row 7: AI text is `source_role = synthetic`; cannot stand alone as evidence; requires `AIReceipt`. |

### 8.3 Drift-detection signals

Two operational signals indicate source-role drift requiring `DRIFT_REGISTER.md` entry:

1. A `SourceDescriptor` is found with a `source_role` value outside the seven canonical values.
2. A promotion PR is found to mutate an existing descriptor's `source_role` (rather than producing a new descriptor + `CorrectionNotice`).

Both are PR-time validator catches, not runtime catches.
