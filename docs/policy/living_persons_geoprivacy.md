## Directory Rules basis

Accepted [`ADR-0029`](../adr/ADR-0029-adopt-directory-governance-standard-v2.md) adopts the exact [`Directory Rules v2`](../doctrine/directory-rules.md) bytes. Applied here:

1. A path is an authority claim.
2. `docs/` owns human-readable explanation, not executable policy, personal data, consent credentials, evidence, test execution, or release state.
3. `policy/` is the singular normative admissibility source root.
4. `contracts/` owns meaning; `schemas/` owns machine shape; `tests/` owns executable conformance; `fixtures/` owns synthetic examples; `release/` owns state-bearing release, correction, withdrawal, and rollback decisions.
5. A noncanonical compatibility or containment path requires one writable source, explicit limits, verified consumers, exit criteria, and rollback.
6. Unknown placement, rights, sensitivity, consent, or consumers remains `HOLD`.

This file is an existing tracked human document. Updating it in place does not create a new root or parallel authority. It does not authorize growth of `docs/policy/` as a canonical documentation lane.

## Inherited safety posture

The bounded human posture is fail closed when the exact operation depends on unresolved living status, identity, consent, rights, sensitivity, location precision, review, release, correction, or rollback.

| Condition | Human routing posture | This page does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| Living status is unknown, stale, disputed, or inferred | `HOLD` or `ABSTAIN`; require governed evidence and qualified review | That the subject is living or deceased |
| Person identity is unresolved or source aliases conflict | `HOLD` or `ABSTAIN`; preserve separate assertions | A canonical person identity |
| Consent is absent, expired, revoked, out of scope, or belongs to another person | `DENY` or `HOLD` the requested exposure | Legal validity or authority of consent |
| Rights or source terms are unresolved | `DENY`, `HOLD`, or restrict access | Rights clearance |
| Exact or combined location detail could identify, contact, track, target, or harass a person | `DENY` ordinary public exposure; route to public-safe transformation and review | A safe transformation threshold |
| Person–parcel, household, family, DNA, migration, or infrastructure joins increase reconstruction risk | Apply the most restrictive posture and review the produced derivative | That each input's independent classification is sufficient |
| Evidence or source role is insufficient | `ABSTAIN` or `HOLD`; do not fill gaps with generated language | Identity, residence, relationship, ownership, or location truth |
| Review, release, correction, or rollback support is missing | Keep the candidate outside public state | Release readiness |
| Policy evaluation errors or cannot run | Fail closed; never fall back to allow | Operational evaluator behavior |

`HOLD` is a governed work-state, not a public runtime outcome. Current outward KFM policy contracts use finite outcomes such as `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, and `ERROR`; exact normalization belongs to accepted contracts and evaluator bindings, not this crosswalk.

## Protected content and safe authoring

### Never place here

- real living-person names paired with non-public location information;
- home, mailing, workplace, school, care, shelter, or habitual-location detail;
- exact residence, migration, travel, occupancy, or visitation events;
- contact details, account identifiers, source-system credentials, or private source identifiers;
- living-status evidence or private identity-resolution evidence;
- private person–parcel, person–household, person–DNA, or family-location joins;
- DNA kit identifiers, match lists, segment data, genotype data, or genomic material;
- consent tokens, revocation credentials, representative-authority evidence, or signed URLs;
- exact protected geometry, transform secrets, or operational thresholds that enable reconstruction;
- hidden review notes, restricted reason text, legal opinions, or incident details;
- real source payloads, EvidenceBundles, receipts, proofs, decisions, or release records.

### Safe examples

Use synthetic, non-identifying, non-geocodable examples such as `PersonA`, `SyntheticHousehold`, `ToyPlaceRef`, `ToyParcelRef`, and broad outcome descriptions. Do not combine enough synthetic-looking fields to mirror a real person or address.

Public-safe reason text should explain the finite condition without disclosing the protected fact. For example, report that “the requested precision is not available for this audience” rather than revealing the restricted location or the exact rule parameter that produced the denial.

This page is not legal advice and does not state that a particular handling pattern satisfies any jurisdictional privacy, consent, records, title, health, genomic, or civil-rights obligation.

## Living status, identity, consent, location, and release separation

These responsibilities must remain separate:

```text
publicly discoverable record
  != living-status evidence
  != canonical person identity
  != consent for the requested use
  != rights clearance
  != sensitivity clearance
  != public-safe location derivative
  != policy approval
  != release authorization
  != publication
```

| Concern | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| Living status | A governed, time-bounded assertion supported by admissible evidence; never guessed from age, silence, an obituary search, a tree, or stale source data |
| Person identity | `PersonAssertion`, identity candidate, and reviewed canonical projection remain distinct |
| Relationship | Relationship assertion or hypothesis is not automatically a confirmed kinship, household, guardianship, or association |
| Consent | Purpose-, audience-, subject-, field-, relation-, precision-, operation-, and time-scoped; revocable; insufficient without independent gates |
| Public availability | A directory, news story, social post, court record, assessor record, family tree, or government record does not imply consent for KFM republication or recombination |
| Location | A source location, administrative record, parcel, map feature, and public-safe derivative are different objects with different evidence and exposure burdens |
| Parcel and title | A parcel or assessor/tax record does not prove residence, occupancy, ownership, title, or legal boundary |
| DNA and genomic evidence | Raw or segment-level material remains outside public surfaces; derived claims remain evidence- and consent-bound |
| Policy | Evaluates a supplied context; does not create identity, evidence, rights, consent, or release state |
| Review | Assesses a fixed subject and scope; does not replace policy or release |
| Release | Separate state-bearing decision with correction and rollback; not a file move, workflow pass, PR, merge, map layer, or AI answer |

One person's consent does not automatically authorize another person's data. An asserted representative, family member, guardian, executor, researcher, source contributor, or record custodian must not be treated as authorized without independently governed authority evidence and scope.

## Minimum human review packet

A request to expose or transform living-person location-related information should identify, without placing protected payloads in public review text:

1. exact subject reference, version, digest, and immutable locator;
2. requested operation, audience, purpose, fields, precision, time window, volume, and surface;
3. living-status evidence and its observation/effective interval;
4. person-identity posture, unresolved aliases, and collateral persons;
5. source identity, source role, rights, terms, retention, and citation posture;
6. consent applicability, holder/subject/representative binding, scope, expiry, revocation, and disputes;
7. sensitivity and geoprivacy references without exposing protected details;
8. proposed public-safe derivative and non-reconstructability assessment;
9. transform implementation/version and receipt reference;

11. policy bundle/evaluator version and finite decision;
12. required reviewer identity, assignment, independence, conflicts, and protected-context access;

A prior review remains historical evidence. It must not silently authorize a changed subject or use.
