# Lyon County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Lyon County planning lane, make its
> floodplain, agriculture, soil, geology, habitat, archaeology, property,
> transportation, emergency, and currentness boundaries visible, and route
> future work through KFM evidence, policy, qualified review, correction,
> rollback, and release controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation or release evidence.**
> This directory contains this README and the Lyon County build plan. Their
> presence establishes planning lineage only; it does not admit a source,
> resolve evidence, activate policy, validate a runtime payload, deploy a
> product, or publish county knowledge.

> [!CAUTION]
> **The existing path remains a compatibility lane.** Accepted ADR-0029 adopts
> Directory Rules v2. This change keeps the tracked README in place and does
> not create a second county tree, move a file, or infer a structural migration.

> [!WARNING]
> **Map context is not legal, safety, or property truth.** Floodplain overlays,
> parcel maps, roads, habitat layers, geologic maps, agricultural summaries,
> and historic-route context can support public orientation, but they do not
> establish title, insurance, engineering, emergency, species-occurrence,
> archaeological-site, or current operational conclusions by themselves.

## 4. Proposed county proof slice

The linked plan proposes a multi-lane county evidence cockpit centered on
Lyon County and Emporia, with transportation, floodplain, agriculture, soils,
geology, habitat, and historical context. That composition is valuable only if
those lanes remain source-role-aware and policy-bounded.

| Proposed element | Intended value | Required boundary | Current status |
|---|---|---|---:|
| County and municipal orientation | Public geographic context. | Boundary geometry is not parcel, title, service, or jurisdictional advice. | `PROPOSED` |
| Transportation context | I-35, U.S. 50, major roads, rail/trail history. | No real-time closure, access, engineering, or vulnerability conclusion. | `PROPOSED` |
| Floodplain context | General floodplain-management and hazard orientation. | Not insurance, engineering, evacuation, parcel-specific, or emergency advice. | `PROPOSED` |
| Agriculture | County-scale statistics and land-cover summaries. | No individual farm/operator inference; derived maps remain derivatives. | `PROPOSED` |
| Soil | Public-safe soil map-unit and interpretive context. | No site-specific suitability or engineering conclusion without fit-for-use evidence. | `PROPOSED` |
| Geology | County-scale geology and groundwater context. | Scale, method, source role, and uncertainty must remain visible. | `PROPOSED` |
| Habitat | Generalized tallgrass/Flint Hills context. | Exact sensitive species or habitat locations require denial/generalization and review. | `PROPOSED` |
| Heritage | Santa Fe Trail, rail, settlement, and local-history context. | Public history does not authorize exact archaeology or culturally sensitive locations. | `PROPOSED` |

### Composition rule

A future county Focus Mode may connect these themes only through governed,
released identifiers. It must not reconstruct denied detail by combining public
layers. Examples include deriving a sensitive archaeological location from a
historic-route layer plus parcels, or identifying a specific farm/operator from
aggregate agriculture plus imagery and property layers.

<a id="5-source-role-and-temporal-fitness"></a>

## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The build plan names several candidate source families. They remain **source
seeds**, not admitted KFM sources, until current identity, terms, rights,
coverage, cadence, sensitivity, and allowed-use posture are verified.

| Candidate source role | What it may support after admission | What it must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| County/city GIS and civic sources | Public orientation and source-native administrative context. | Title truth, emergency status, unrestricted redistribution, or universal county truth. |
| KDOT or public transportation sources | Road/project and transportation context at the source's stated date/scale. | Real-time closure, safety guarantee, or vulnerability map. |
| FEMA/KDA floodplain sources | General flood-map or floodplain-management context. | Insurance, engineering, evacuation, or parcel-specific legal determination. |
| KDA/USDA NASS/CDL | Aggregate agriculture statistics and reviewed land-cover derivatives. | Individual farm/operator truth or economic forecast. |
| NRCS soil sources | Soil survey/map-unit and interpretation context at stated scale. | Site-specific engineering, legal, or health determination. |
| KGS | Geology and groundwater scientific context. | Property, mineral-title, private-well, or legal water-right decision. |
| Habitat/ecology sources | Generalized ecological context. | Exact protected occurrence or unrestricted sensitive-location disclosure. |
| Historical institutions and public collections | Historical interpretation, route, place, and documentary context. | Exact restricted archaeology, access authorization, or unqualified present-day claim. |

A source page, map, downloadable file, or official publisher name is not enough
by itself. Future use should preserve observation/publication/retrieval/release
and correction time separately where material.

<a id="6-flood-property-habitat-heritage-and-current-safety-boundaries"></a>

## 6. Flood, property, habitat, heritage, and current-safety boundaries

### Floodplain and emergency questions

KFM may eventually provide released, dated floodplain reference context. It
must not act as an emergency-warning, evacuation, insurance, engineering, or
parcel-specific legal authority. When current official status is required, the
system should redirect to the appropriate authoritative channel or abstain.

### Property and parcel questions

County GIS or assessor geometry does not automatically establish title,
ownership, easement, lawful access, boundary location, valuation, or legal
rights. Those questions require source-role and legal fitness appropriate to the
claim. Otherwise the result is `ABSTAIN` or `DENY`.

### Habitat and species sensitivity

Generalized habitat or tallgrass-prairie context may be suitable for a public
county view after review. Exact rare-species occurrences, nesting/roosting sites,
protected habitat, or other harmful-precision details remain fail closed.

### Archaeology, heritage, and historic routes

Public history and trail context can support orientation. Exact archaeology,
burial, sacred, culturally sensitive, private-land, or access-restricted details
require qualified review and may need suppression or generalization. Historical
interpretation must remain distinguishable from direct observation or legal
site status.

### Roads and current operational state

A planning document, map layer, project page, or cached dataset does not prove
a road is currently open, safe, restricted, under construction, or passable.
Current operational questions require current authoritative evidence.

<a id="7-finite-outcomes"></a>

## 7. Finite outcomes

Future Lyon County runtime behavior should preserve KFM's four terminal
outcomes:

| Outcome | Lyon County use |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Released, in-scope evidence supports the bounded county claim and required citations/policy checks pass. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence is missing, stale, conflicted, too coarse, too precise, or unfit for the requested legal/temporal/spatial claim. |
| `DENY` | Rights, privacy, exact sensitive location, property, infrastructure, archaeology, habitat, or other policy constraints block exposure. |
| `ERROR` | A required resolver, validator, policy evaluator, release lookup, adapter, or runtime component failed. |

Restriction metadata may qualify one of these outcomes. It must not silently
create a fifth terminal outcome or turn a denial into plausible prose.

<a id="9-smallest-safe-follow-up"></a>

## 9. Smallest safe follow-up

After review, the smallest useful implementation candidate is a **no-network,
synthetic, non-release Lyon County proof packet** rather than a live connector.

It should exercise a small set of fictionalized county layers and negative cases:

- one county frame and municipal orientation fixture;
- one floodplain-reference fixture with non-emergency limitations;
- one aggregate-agriculture fixture;
- one soil/geology context fixture;
- one generalized habitat or heritage fixture;
- invalid cases for exact sensitive archaeology, rare-species location,
  parcel/title inference, stale operational road status, and unsupported
  flood/insurance advice;
- deterministic validators asserting `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR`;
- documentation that states precisely what the synthetic proof establishes and
  what remains unproven.

Stop if implementation would require live credentials, private records,
unreviewed exact sensitive locations, source activation, public release,
deployment, promotion, publication, or a new parallel authority path.
