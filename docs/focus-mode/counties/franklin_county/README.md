# Franklin County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Franklin County planning lane, make its
> Ottawa cultural-authority, sensitive-history, flood-currentness, property, and
> evidence boundaries visible, and route future work through KFM source,
> evidence, policy, review, release, correction, and rollback controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation, consent, approval, or
> release evidence.** This directory contains this README and one Franklin
> County build plan. Their presence prevents accidental duplicate planning; it
> does not establish source admission, Nation authorization, semantic
> correctness, validator success, runtime behavior, release, deployment, or
> publication.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains a compatibility question.** This same-path update repairs
> an existing README under `docs/focus-mode/counties/franklin_county/`.
> Accepted Directory Rules v2 governs placement; proposed ADR-0027 describes a
> plural, kebab-case control plane but remains unaccepted. No file is moved,
> renamed, copied into a parallel tree, or declared canonical here.

> [!WARNING]
> **Sovereign cultural authority comes before county storytelling.** The linked
> build plan identifies Ottawa Tribe of Oklahoma public materials as the leading
> candidate authority source for substantive Ottawa cultural context. County,
> city, state, federal, historic-inventory, tourism, or generated sources cannot
> replace Nation-authored evidence or appropriate review for cultural meaning,
> identity, consent, endorsement, or sensitive-place decisions.

> [!WARNING]
> **Sensitive and unsafe inference must fail closed.** Exact or reconstructive
> burial, cemetery, sacred, archaeological, culturally restricted,
> living-person, parcel, title, tax, flood-safety, emergency, access, or
> infrastructure-vulnerability detail must not reach an ordinary public client.
> Missing authority, evidence, rights, safe geometry, freshness, policy, or
> review closure requires `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR`, not a fluent inference.

## 4. Proposed Franklin County proof slice

The build plan proposes a public-safe learning slice organized around three
related but non-interchangeable concerns:

1. **Ottawa civic and county orientation** — public routing and geographic
   context without parcel, title, tax, ownership, or living-person exposure.
2. **Ottawa Nation-authored cultural authority** — an authority card that
   points users toward Nation-authored public material and makes KFM's limits
   visible.
3. **Marais des Cygnes flood and time-basis restraint** — an explainer that
   distinguishes regulatory flood products, provisional observations, local
   notices, and present safety or property judgments.

### Proposed first-slice surfaces

| Proposed surface | Intended value | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Franklin County orientation frame | Locate the county and official county/city source routes | No parcel, property-owner, tax, title, residence, or private-access inference |
| Ottawa Nation Authority Card | Make Nation-authored source precedence visible before any cultural narrative | No generated substitute for Nation authority, consent, endorsement, or cultural meaning |
| Marais des Cygnes flood-source and time-basis card | Explain source role, effective state, supersession, provisional status, and official redirects | No present safety, insurance, buildability, evacuation, road, or individual property determination |
| Historic evidence withheld-detail card | Show that a public historical record can still require review and suppression | No burial, cemetery, sacred, archaeological, or culturally sensitive precision |
| Official operational redirect card | Route current questions to responsible county, city, federal, or observation sources | Link-out and checked-at state only; no cached KFM alert or operational verdict |
| Evidence Drawer and boundary panels | Expose evidence state, source role, time basis, limitations, review, and reason codes | Unresolved evidence, policy, or release state cannot be polished into an answer |

These are **PROPOSED planning surfaces**. No card, layer, payload, source
descriptor, evidence bundle, policy decision, route, or released artifact is
confirmed by this README.

### Deferred planning surfaces

- authoritative river geometry, watershed joins, or stream-gage overlays;
- effective flood-hazard polygon display;
- trails, recreation, or current public-access layers;
- structured historic-site geometry;
- agriculture, soils, or other cross-domain extensions;
- live operational notices or current-condition integrations.

Each deferred item needs source identity, rights, version/effective state,
geometry fitness, sensitivity review, public-safe transformation, evidence
closure, policy, and release support before display.

### Excluded or denied by default

- burial, cemetery, sacred, archaeological, or culturally sensitive precision;
- generated Ottawa cultural authority or unreviewed cultural interpretation;
- names, addresses, parcel identifiers, tax/payment information, ownership,
  title, residence, or living-person inference;
- property-specific flood safety, insurance, buildability, access, or legal
  conclusions;
- cached emergency, warning, closure, evacuation, or road-safety advice;
- direct public access to `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, restricted source
  material, or model output.

## 5. Sovereign authority and source-role boundary

### Authority before synthesis

The build plan identifies Ottawa Tribe of Oklahoma public materials as the
leading candidate source role for substantive Ottawa cultural context. That
planning choice is consistent with KFM's evidence-first posture, but it is not
proof of permission, consent, endorsement, a review relationship, or an
authorized public scope.

Before any substantive cultural card can be released, the implementation must
establish at minimum:

- the correct Nation-authored source identity and authority role;
- the exact public statements or assets proposed for citation or display;
- rights, attribution, excerpting, caching, and derivative-use obligations;
- the appropriate review relationship and accountable reviewer;
- protected, withheld, generalized, or link-only categories;
- correction, withdrawal, supersession, and contact behavior.

### Source-role anti-collapse

| Candidate source role named by the plan | May support after admission and review | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| Ottawa Tribe public material — `cultural_authority` candidate | Attributed Nation-authored public context within the reviewed scope | KFM-originated cultural authority, consent, endorsement, sensitive-place disclosure, or unrestricted derivative product |
| Franklin County — `administrative` candidate | County identity, service routing, and official map entry points | Cultural authority, title truth, individual safety verdict, or permission to expose property records |
| City of Ottawa — `municipal_operational` candidate | Civic orientation and current official redirect destinations | Cached live alert, closure, emergency, or road-safety answer |
| FEMA flood product — `regulatory_flood_product` candidate | Effective-product sourcing, metadata, and supersession limitations | Individual flood safety, insurance, buildability, evacuation, or property-risk advice |
| USGS water surface — `provisional_observation` candidate | A future admitted observation or official redirect with provisional status | Definitive safety, legal, or emergency outcome |
| KSHS/SHPO/KHRI material — `historic_inventory` candidate | Reviewed public historical context and evidence of a source record | Nation authority, present property/access truth, or burial/sensitive-site precision |
| Generated KFM narrative — `generated_narrative` | A bounded summary of resolved, released, policy-safe evidence | Evidence, authority, consent, review, policy, or release state |

### Cultural and historical precision

Public availability does not equal public-display fitness. A historical
inventory or document may be readable yet contain relationships, descriptions,
or location details that should not be reproduced in a map, fixture, screenshot,
search index, AI answer, log, or export.

The public product should communicate only the category and reason for
withholding. It must not reveal the suppressed detail through coordinates,
map centering, labels, identifiers, prose hints, metadata, tiles, accessibility
text, caches, or repeated-query reconstruction.

### Generated-language boundary

AI may summarize only an already resolved, policy-allowed, review-supported,
released public-safe evidence bundle. It cannot:

- infer a Nation's meaning, identity, consent, endorsement, or current position;
- decide cultural or burial sensitivity;
- convert an historic record into current property, access, or legal truth;
- decide source rights, policy, review, promotion, or release;
- use hidden reasoning or model confidence as evidence.

## 6. Public safety and governed outcomes

### Finite outcomes

| Outcome | Franklin County use | Required visible state |
|---|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | A bounded public-safe orientation, authority, source-fitness, or historical-context response is supported by released evidence and policy | Evidence references, source roles, time basis, limitations, review/release state, and correction route |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, authority, effective state, freshness, geometry, or scope is insufficient; current flood, emergency, property, or access judgment is requested | Stable reason code, evidence gap, official redirect where safe, and no answer-like fallback |
| `DENY` | The request seeks cultural/burial precision, unsupported cultural authority, property/living-person detail, restricted source content, or trust-membrane bypass | Stable reason code and safe alternative without leaking protected detail |
| `ERROR` | A required schema, evidence resolver, policy evaluator, citation check, release record, or governed service failed | Stable error code; no permissive answer or raw-data fallback |

### Candidate reason-code families from the build plan

| Boundary | Candidate reason codes | Expected posture |
|---|---|---|
| Nation authority | `NATION_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED`, `GENERATED_CULTURAL_AUTHORITY_PROHIBITED` | `DENY` unsupported cultural representation |
| Cultural and burial sensitivity | `CULTURAL_BURIAL_PRECISION_WITHHELD`, `CULTURAL_SITE_PRECISION_WITHHELD`, `SENSITIVITY_SCREEN_REQUIRED` | `DENY` or hold public display |
| Flood and currentness | `FLOOD_PROPERTY_DETERMINATION_OUT_OF_SCOPE`, `LIVE_OPERATIONAL_GUIDANCE_REDIRECT`, `SOURCE_EFFECTIVE_STATE_UNRESOLVED` | `ABSTAIN` and route to current official authority |
| Property and privacy | `PROPERTY_LIVING_PERSON_OR_TITLE_EXCLUDED`, `PRIVATE_ACCESS_NOT_DETERMINED` | `DENY` or exclude before runtime |
| Evidence integrity | `EVIDENCE_UNRESOLVED`, `SOURCE_ROLE_UNRESOLVED`, `CITATION_VALIDATION_FAILED` | `ABSTAIN` or `ERROR` |
| Trust membrane | `TRUST_MEMBRANE_VIOLATION`, `DIRECT_MODEL_OUTPUT_NOT_EVIDENCE` | `DENY` |
| Release integrity | `RELEASE_CLOSURE_MISSING`, `CORRECTION_PATH_MISSING`, `ROLLBACK_TARGET_MISSING` | Reject release or return `ERROR` |

These codes are plan-derived candidates. They are not confirmed as the
repository's canonical reason-code registry and must be reconciled before
machine use.

### Required negative paths

A credible Franklin proof must include deterministic, no-network negative
fixtures showing at least:

1. generated cultural-authority claims fail closed;
2. burial or sensitive cultural precision cannot enter a public card or layer;
3. an unscreened historic extract cannot become public content;
4. a flood product cannot become a present property-safety verdict;
5. cached operational notices cannot become current guidance;
6. property-owner, tax, title, or living-person material is excluded;
7. unresolved evidence cannot produce `ANSWER`;
8. public clients cannot read non-published lifecycle lanes;
9. a public-release claim without review, manifest, correction, and rollback
   closure is rejected.

### Map, API, and AI boundary

A public map or Focus answer must consume a governed, public-safe projection.
It must never use the county planning directory as a data source and must never
treat a selected feature, map extent, badge, schema pass, model response, or
Markdown claim as evidence truth.

The build plan's proposed Authority & Sensitivity and Time-Basis panels are
useful trust surfaces only when they project real evidence, policy, review, and
release objects. UI polish without those dependencies must remain explicitly
demonstrative.
