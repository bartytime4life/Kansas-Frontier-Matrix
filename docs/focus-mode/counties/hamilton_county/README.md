# Hamilton County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Hamilton County planning lane, make its
> water-authority, flood-currentness, environmental-health, private-operation,
> sensitive-location, and evidence boundaries visible, and route future work
> through KFM source, evidence, policy, review, release, correction, and
> rollback controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation, adjudication, approval,
> or release evidence.** This directory contains this README and one Hamilton
> County build plan. Their presence prevents accidental duplicate planning; it
> does not establish source admission, water-right status, semantic correctness,
> validator success, runtime behavior, release, deployment, or publication.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains a compatibility question.** This same-path update repairs
> an existing README under `docs/focus-mode/counties/hamilton_county/`.
> Accepted Directory Rules v2 governs placement; proposed ADR-0027 describes a
> plural, kebab-case control plane but remains unaccepted. No file is moved,
> renamed, copied into a parallel tree, or declared canonical here.

> [!WARNING]
> **Mapped water context is not a water-right, supply, health, or safety
> determination.** The linked plan proposes bounded public context for Syracuse,
> the Upper Arkansas corridor, aquifers, floodplain mapping, conservation
> programs, and county agriculture. KFM must not infer legal priority,
> entitlement, pumping permission, compliance, well viability, present water
> availability, flood safety, or drinking-water safety for any person, parcel,
> well, farm, or operation.

> [!WARNING]
> **Sensitive and current-condition inference must fail closed.** Individual
> water records, private program participation, parcel or farm attribution,
> exact ecological or archaeological locations, live river or flood guidance,
> and infrastructure-vulnerability detail must not reach an ordinary public
> client. Missing authority, evidence, rights, safe geometry, freshness, policy,
> or review closure requires `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR`, not a fluent
> inference.

## 4. Proposed Hamilton County proof slice

The build plan proposes a public-safe learning slice organized around three
related but non-interchangeable concerns:

1. **Hamilton County and Syracuse orientation** — public geographic and
   administrative context without parcel, title, private-farm, or living-person
   inference.
2. **Upper Arkansas and High Plains aquifer context** — time- and source-labeled
   scientific and planning context without a well, water-right, health, safety,
   or private-operation conclusion.
3. **Water-authority and currentness restraint** — an always-visible boundary
   separating education and official routing from adjudication, live guidance,
   environmental-health judgment, and individual program status.

### Proposed first-slice surfaces

| Proposed surface | Intended value | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Hamilton County and Syracuse orientation frame | Locate the county and route users toward official public sources | No parcel, property-owner, title, residence, private access, or individual-operation inference |
| Water Authority & Legal Limits panel | Explain the responsible authority role and KFM's non-adjudication boundary before water-related answers | No right, priority, allocation, permit, compliance, entitlement, pumping-permission, or legal conclusion |
| Upper Arkansas corridor context card | Explain the plan's proposed river and alluvial-aquifer setting at a bounded scale | No live flow, flood, water-quality, parcel, well, or safety judgment |
| High Plains aquifer context card | Provide regional scientific context with scale, uncertainty, and source-role labels | No current farm, well, supply, health, economic, or legal prediction |
| Floodplain-mapping status and source-routing card | Make source role, checked-at date, effective-state uncertainty, and official routing visible | No present parcel hazard, insurance, permit, evacuation, road, or individual safety determination |
| Conservation-program context card | Explain the plan's proposed corridor-program context | No participant, parcel, eligibility, enrollment, compliance, or retired-right inference |
| County agriculture aggregate card | Present a dated county-scale aggregate only after evidence and rights closure | No farm, operator, pumping, causation, blame, or future-outcome attribution |
| Official observation-source routing card | Identify a future official observation route while leaving live values deferred | No raw feed, cached alert, trend, current availability, or safety interpretation |
| Generalized public-land/ecology candidate | Offer broad public context only after currentness, rights, and sensitivity review | No exact wildlife, habitat, spring, management, or hunting-sensitive detail |
| Evidence Drawer and boundary panels | Expose evidence state, source role, time basis, limitations, review, and reason codes | Unresolved evidence, policy, review, or release state cannot be polished into an answer |

These are **PROPOSED planning surfaces**. No card, layer, payload, source
descriptor, evidence bundle, policy decision, route, or released artifact is
confirmed by this README.

### Deferred planning surfaces

- current stream observations, trends, alerts, or operational hydrology;
- water-right, well-level, irrigation, program-participant, or parcel records;
- effective flood-hazard geometry or parcel interaction;
- conservation enrollment, eligibility, or retired-right geometry;
- water-quality, salinity, nitrate, drinking-water, or health interpretation;
- exact ecology, wildlife, habitat, spring, or recreation detail;
- Santa Fe Trail, Fort Aubrey, or other historical/cultural spatial layers;
- live notices, closures, travel, emergency, or current-condition integrations.

Each deferred item needs source identity, authority, rights, version or
effective state, geometry fitness, sensitivity review, public-safe
transformation, evidence closure, policy, and release support before display.

### Excluded or denied by default

- individual water rights, priority, allocation, entitlement, permits,
  compliance, pumping permission, or legal advice;
- names, addresses, parcel identifiers, title, residence, program participation,
  private farm operations, or living-person inference;
- site-specific well viability, future supply, irrigation economics, or crop
  outcome predictions;
- present river, flood, drought, water-quality, drinking-water, travel, or
  emergency advice;
- individual causation, blame, contamination, or resource-stress attribution;
- exact ecological, wildlife, hunting-sensitive, archaeological, burial,
  sacred, or culturally sensitive locations;
- direct public access to `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, restricted source
  material, ungoverned observations, or model output.

## 5. Water authority and source-role boundary

### Authority before synthesis

The build plan names Kansas water-administration, geological-science,
floodplain-mapping, conservation-program, agricultural-statistics, observation,
ecology, and transportation sources as candidates with distinct roles. That
planning inventory is not proof of admission, currentness, rights, complete
authority scope, safe transformation, review, or release.

Before any consequential Hamilton card can be released, implementation must
establish at minimum:

- exact source identity, publisher, role, version, retrieval or effective time,
  and authority limitation;
- allowed public claim scope and prohibited legal, health, safety, property,
  private-operation, and sensitive-location inferences;
- rights, attribution, excerpting, caching, screenshot, export, and derivative-
  display obligations;
- safe geometry, scale, generalization, withholding, and reconstruction tests;
- appropriate water-governance, floodplain/currentness, environmental-health,
  privacy, sensitivity, accessibility, security, and release review;
- correction, withdrawal, supersession, cache-invalidation, and rollback
  behavior.

### Source-role anti-collapse

| Candidate source role named by the plan | May support after admission and review | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| Water-administration authority candidate | Authority routing and public explanation of institutional responsibility | A KFM water-right, priority, entitlement, allocation, permit, compliance, or pumping decision |
| Geological and hydrogeological science candidate | Scale-bounded river, aquifer, and corridor context | Current well yield, farm supply, drinking-water, health, legal, or property conclusion |
| Floodplain project or regulatory-product candidate | Effective-state metadata, project status, limitations, and official routing | Present parcel hazard, insurance, permitting, evacuation, or individual safety advice |
| Conservation-program candidate | Public program purpose and reviewed aggregate context | Participant, parcel, eligibility, enrollment, compliance, or retired-right inference |
| County agricultural-statistics candidate | Dated county-scale aggregate | Private-farm behavior, pumping, causation, blame, compliance, or future outcome |
| Observation-source candidate | Future admitted time-labeled observation or official source route | Warning, alert, current safety, legal availability, raw-feed bypass, or unqualified trend claim |
| Public-land or ecology candidate | Reviewed generalized public context | Exact occurrence, habitat, water, hunting, management, or sensitive-location disclosure |
| Historical or cultural candidate | Separately reviewed, attributed public history at a safe scale | Archaeological, burial, sacred, culturally sensitive, or reconstructive location output |
| Generated KFM narrative | A bounded summary of resolved, released, policy-safe evidence | Evidence, authority, legal judgment, health or safety advice, review, policy, or release state |

### Water-law, health, and operational boundary

Public administrative or scientific material may be informative while still
being unfit for an individual decision. A water record, aquifer study, flood
map, observation, conservation description, or county statistic must not be
combined into a legal right, compliance finding, well or farm forecast,
environmental-health conclusion, present safety instruction, or attribution of
responsibility.

When a user asks for a current or individual determination, the public product
must return a finite reason code and a safe official route when one is available.
It must not fill an evidence gap with a model inference, map proximity, county
average, stale observation, or cross-source correlation.

### Generated-language boundary

AI may summarize only an already resolved, policy-allowed, review-supported,
released public-safe evidence bundle. It cannot:

- adjudicate a water right, priority, entitlement, permit, compliance, or legal
  question;
- predict current or future well, farm, river, flood, water-quality, health, or
  safety conditions;
- infer individual participation, property status, causation, blame, or
  responsibility from county, program, map, or observation context;
- decide source rights, effective state, sensitivity, policy, review,
  promotion, or release;
- use hidden reasoning or model confidence as evidence.

## 6. Public safety and governed outcomes

### Finite outcomes

| Outcome | Hamilton County use | Required visible state |
|---|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | A bounded public-safe orientation, source-role, scientific-context, program-context, or aggregate response is supported by released evidence and policy | Evidence references, source roles, time basis, limitations, review/release state, and correction route |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, authority, effective state, freshness, geometry, rights, or scope is insufficient; a current or unsupported contextual conclusion is requested | Stable reason code, evidence gap, official redirect where safe, and no answer-like fallback |
| `DENY` | The request seeks water-right adjudication, individual or private-operation detail, health/safety advice, sensitive precision, restricted content, or trust-membrane bypass | Stable reason code and safe alternative without leaking protected detail |
| `ERROR` | A required schema, evidence resolver, policy evaluator, citation check, release record, or governed service failed | Stable error code; no permissive answer, raw-data fallback, or direct model path |

### Candidate reason-code families from the build plan

| Boundary | Candidate reason codes | Expected posture |
|---|---|---|
| Individual water right or entitlement | `INDIVIDUAL_WATER_RIGHT_OR_ENTITLEMENT_EXPOSURE` | `DENY`; official authority routing only |
| Program, private, or legal inference | `PROGRAM_PARTICIPATION_OR_WATER_RIGHT_INFERENCE` | `DENY`; no parcel or person status |
| Well, farm, or economic prediction | `SITE_SPECIFIC_WELL_OR_ECONOMIC_PREDICTION_OUT_OF_SCOPE` | `DENY` |
| Current water or safety advice | `LIVE_WATER_SAFETY_OR_OPERATIONAL_ADVICE_OUT_OF_SCOPE` | `DENY`; route to current official sources |
| Raw observation or public bypass | `RAW_OR_UNGOVERNED_OBSERVATION_TO_PUBLIC_UI` | Validation failure or `DENY` |
| Flood, property, or permit conclusion | `PROPERTY_FLOOD_OR_PERMIT_CONCLUSION_OUT_OF_SCOPE` | `DENY` |
| Environmental-health overclaim | `ENVIRONMENTAL_HEALTH_CONCLUSION_UNSUPPORTED` | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` |
| Individual causation or compliance | `INDIVIDUAL_COMPLIANCE_OR_CAUSATION_INFERENCE` | `DENY` |
| Sensitive precision | `SENSITIVE_ECOLOGY_LOCATION`, `SENSITIVE_CULTURAL_ARCHAEOLOGY_LOCATION` | `DENY` or reviewed generalization only |
| Source, evidence, or currentness gap | `SOURCE_RIGHTS_UNVERIFIED`, `SOURCE_CURRENTNESS_UNVERIFIED`, `SOURCE_ROLE_COLLAPSE_REQUESTED`, `EVIDENCE_REF_UNRESOLVED` | `ABSTAIN`, quarantine, or validation failure |
| Trust membrane | `PUBLIC_INTERNAL_LIFECYCLE_ACCESS`, `MODEL_OUTPUT_NOT_EVIDENCE` | `DENY`, `ABSTAIN`, or validation failure |
| Review and release closure | `REQUIRED_REVIEW_NOT_RECORDED`, `PUBLICATION_GATE_INCOMPLETE` | Block display, promotion, or publication |

These codes are plan-derived candidates. They are not confirmed as the
repository's canonical reason-code registry and must be reconciled before
machine use.

### Required negative paths

A credible Hamilton proof must include deterministic, no-network negative
fixtures showing at least:

1. an individual water-right or entitlement conclusion fails closed;
2. conservation-program participation, eligibility, or retired-right inference
   is denied;
3. a public study cannot become a site-specific well or farm forecast;
4. an observation cannot become current river, flood, or safety guidance;
5. an ungoverned raw feed cannot reach a public client;
6. flood context cannot become a parcel, insurance, permit, or property verdict;
7. hydrogeology cannot become a drinking-water or health conclusion;
8. county aggregates cannot support private-farm causation, blame, or compliance;
9. exact ecology, archaeology, burial, or cultural precision cannot enter a
   public card or layer;
10. unresolved evidence, rights, currentness, review, correction, or rollback
    cannot produce `ANSWER` or a public-release claim;
11. public clients cannot read non-published lifecycle lanes.

### Map, API, and AI boundary

A public map or Focus answer must consume a governed, public-safe projection.
It must never use the county planning directory as a data source and must never
treat a selected feature, map extent, badge, schema pass, model response,
observation, or Markdown claim as evidence truth.

The build plan's proposed Water Authority & Legal Limits, Evidence Drawer, and
time-basis surfaces are useful only when they project real evidence, policy,
review, and release objects. UI polish without those dependencies must remain
explicitly demonstrative.

## 9. Open questions

### Water authority, science, and claim scope

- Which exact administrative source statements may support an authority-routing
  card without turning KFM into a water-right or legal decision surface?
- Which river, aquifer, floodplain, and county geometries are authoritative and
  fit for the proposed public scale?
- Which scientific claims remain supportable at county or regional scale, and
  which require narrower time, geography, uncertainty, or study-context labels?
- Which combinations of otherwise public sources could enable an unsafe right,
  well, farm, health, property, compliance, causation, or blame inference?
- Should the first proof remain entirely static and fixture-based until every
  currentness and authority boundary has deterministic negative coverage?

### Source rights, currentness, and operational routing

- Which plan-named sources remain current, authoritative for the intended role,
  and licensed or permitted for citation, excerpt, screenshot, cache, export,
  transformation, and public map display?
- Which exact flood product, effective date, supersession state, community
  identifier, and limitations would govern a future status card or layer?
- What freshness, stale-state, revision, outage, and withdrawal rules would
  govern a future observation card?
- What is the safe redirect contract for current water, flood, warning, road,
  health, or emergency questions without caching an operational verdict?
- How are correction and withdrawal propagated when an official product,
  observation, program statement, or scientific interpretation changes?

### Privacy, ecology, history, and harmful precision

- Which conservation-program and agriculture details are safe only as county-
  level aggregates, and which must be excluded entirely?
- What policy and query controls prevent joins that reveal participant, parcel,
  water-right, farm, operator, pumping, compliance, or blame information?
- Which public-land or ecology context can be generalized without revealing
  sensitive occurrence, habitat, water, hunting, or management detail?
- Which historical claims have an appropriate authoritative source and review,
  and which archaeology, burial, sacred, or cultural details must remain
  withheld or link-only?
- Who approves public-safe transformations and verifies that coordinates,
  labels, metadata, tiles, logs, accessibility text, exports, and repeated
  queries do not reconstruct protected detail?

### Contract, schema, policy, and validation

- Which surface owns Focus request, response, payload, runtime-envelope, and
  citation-report machine shape after the current Focus/UI overlap is resolved?
- Does Hamilton require any county-specific contract or policy extension, or
  should it be entirely expressed through shared profiles and fixtures?
- What is the canonical reason-code registry and how are water-authority,
  source-role, rights, time, privacy, health/safety, sensitivity, and release
  obligations encoded?
- Which validator covers the current singular two-file lane without weakening
  the proposed control-plane checks?
- What no-network fixture set proves the highest-risk denial and abstention
  paths before runtime integration?

### Ownership, release, correction, and public parity

- Who owns the county composition and who independently reviews water
  governance, hydrology, floodplain/currentness, environmental health,
  rights/privacy, ecology/sensitivity, history/culture, security,
  accessibility, and release?
- What review separation is required before a public-safe card can be promoted?
- Which release-manifest, correction-notice, withdrawal, and rollback object
  families are canonical in the current repository?
- How are cache invalidation, search removal, map-state recovery, citation
  updates, and AI-answer correction tested?
- What evidence would demonstrate parity between the released manifest,
  governed API, MapLibre surface, Evidence Drawer, exports, and public endpoint?
