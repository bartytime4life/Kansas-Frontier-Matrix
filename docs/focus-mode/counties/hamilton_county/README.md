# Hamilton County Focus Mode Documentation Boundary

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers and reviewers to the repository-present
> Hamilton County planning lane, its proposed Upper Arkansas water-governance
> proof slice, and the evidence and governance work still required before any
> runtime or public claim can exist.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This directory contains planning documentation, not a released county
> product.** The sibling plan proposes public-safe county orientation, water-
> authority explanation, regional aquifer and river context, floodplain/source
> routing, conservation-program context, aggregate agriculture, generalized
> public-land/ecology context, and future historical interpretation. Those ideas
> remain proposals until admitted evidence, policy, review, release, correction,
> and rollback controls support them.

> [!WARNING]
> **Mapped water context is not a water-right, supply, health, property, or
> safety determination.** Nothing here establishes legal priority, entitlement,
> allocation, pumping permission, compliance, well viability, current water
> availability, flood safety, drinking-water safety, parcel status, private-farm
> behavior, or current emergency conditions.

> **Current and sensitive questions fail closed.** Individual water records,
> program participation, parcel or farm attribution, exact ecological or

## 4. Proposed Hamilton County proof slice

**PROPOSED thesis:** Hamilton County can demonstrate how KFM combines western
Kansas county orientation, river/aquifer science, water-governance routing,
flood/currentness restraint, conservation-program context, aggregate
agriculture, public-land context, and future historical interpretation without
becoming a legal, operational, health, property, private-operation, or
sensitive-location decision system.

The first slice should remain deliberately narrow:

- orient users to Hamilton County and Syracuse at public-safe scale;
- explain that water administration, hydrologic science, observations,
  floodplain products, conservation programs, and agriculture statistics have
  different source roles and clocks;
- present regional river and aquifer context with scale, uncertainty, and
  source-role labels;
- expose an unavoidable Water Authority & Legal Limits panel;
- show dated county-scale agriculture only after evidence and rights closure;
- route current water, flood, warning, road, health, and emergency questions to
  responsible official authorities instead of caching a KFM verdict;
- generalize or defer ecology and cultural-resource detail;
- expose source identity, date basis, evidence state, limitations, review,
  release, correction, and rollback state;
- demonstrate a visible `EvidenceRef` to `EvidenceBundle` path for any
  consequential future claim;
- keep public clients on governed APIs and released public-safe artifacts.

### Non-goals

This slice is not:

- a water-right, permit, priority, allocation, entitlement, impairment, or
  compliance service;
- a well-yield, pumping-permission, farm-supply, irrigation-economics, crop-
  outcome, drought, flood, water-quality, drinking-water, health, travel, or
  emergency advisor;
- a parcel, title, access, owner, residence, program-participant, or
  living-person profiling service;
- a live river or flood dashboard;
- an exact ecology, wildlife, archaeological, burial, sacred, or culturally
  sensitive location finder;
- a vulnerability-analysis surface;
- an unrestricted AI question-answering interface.

## 5. Source roles and temporal fitness

The sibling plan names candidate source families. Their public roles must remain
separate after admission; one source family cannot silently inherit another's
authority.

| Candidate source-role class | Safe use in a future governed slice | Unsafe collapse |
|---|---|---|
| County/city administrative source | Public civic and service orientation at a stated date | Legal advice, property truth, emergency status, or unrestricted redistribution |
| Water-administration authority | Authority routing and bounded explanation of institutional responsibility | KFM decision on priority, entitlement, allocation, permit, compliance, pumping, or impairment |
| Geological/hydrogeological science | Scale-bounded river, aquifer, and corridor context | Current well yield, farm supply, drinking-water, health, legal, or property conclusion |
| Official observation source | Time-labeled released observations or an official source redirect | Warning, alert, current safety, raw-feed bypass, or unqualified trend/availability claim |
| Floodplain/project/regulatory product | Effective-state metadata, project status, limitations, and official routing | Present parcel hazard, insurance, permit, evacuation, or individual safety verdict |
| Conservation-program source | Public program purpose and reviewed aggregate context | Participant, parcel, eligibility, enrollment, compliance, or retired-right inference |
| KDA/USDA-style aggregate statistics | Dated county-scale agriculture context | Individual farm, operator, household, pumping, causation, blame, or future outcome |
| Public-land/recreation/ecology source | Generalized reviewed public context and official-current routing | Exact occurrence, habitat, water, hunting, management, access, or sensitive-location disclosure |
| Historical/cultural source | Separately reviewed public history at safe scale | Archaeological, burial, sacred, culturally sensitive, contested, or private-access detail |
| Map, tile, or index | Downstream carrier of a released public-safe projection | Source authority or evidence by itself |
| AI-generated explanation | Bounded synthesis over released evidence | Evidence, legal/health/safety judgment, source admission, policy decision, or release authority |

### Time semantics

Every future visible Hamilton claim should distinguish, where material:

- observation or valid time;
- source publication, revision, or effective time;
- retrieval time;
- KFM processing time;
- review and release time;
- correction, withdrawal, or supersession time.

A study, map, program page, county statistic, or observation must not become an
evergreen current-condition claim merely because it remains online.

## 6. Water, property, health, and sensitivity boundaries

> [!WARNING]
> **Water-law boundary:** KFM may identify responsible authority and explain
> released regional context. It must not decide or imply an individual's water
> right, priority, entitlement, allocation, permit, compliance, pumping
> permission, impairment, or legal position.

> [!WARNING]
> **Property and private-operation boundary:** parcel, tax, program, well,
> agriculture, or map context must not become title, owner, residence, lawful
> access, participant, private-farm, operator, pumping, debt, compliance, or
> living-person profiles.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Health and current-safety boundary:** aquifer science, flood mapping,
> observations, county averages, or cached pages cannot answer whether a
> particular well, tap, field, road, river reach, or property is safe now.

> [!WARNING]
> **Sensitive-location boundary:** exact ecology, wildlife, hunting-sensitive,
> archaeological, burial, sacred, culturally sensitive, fragile, or newly
> inferred locations must not become public discovery surfaces.

> [!WARNING]
> **Infrastructure boundary:** public information about water systems, wells,
> diversions, levees, dams, monitoring, roads, utilities, or emergency response
> must not be assembled into vulnerability or tactical-response analysis.

Public availability does not itself establish KFM source admission,
redistribution rights, geometry authority, transform permission, public-safe
precision, review, or release.

  correction, withdrawal, rollback, deployment, or public-parity evidence.

### If this README is wrong

1. Correct it in a focused follow-up branch.
2. Preserve the evidence snapshot and state which prior claim changed.
3. Update the sibling plan separately when its planning lineage also requires
   correction; do not silently rewrite it through this README.

The release layer must support a correction notice, withdrawal or supersession
state, affected evidence and artifact identifiers, corrected release reference,
cache/search/map/AI propagation, and rollback target. Those capabilities are
**not proven by this README**.
