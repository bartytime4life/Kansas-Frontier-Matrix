# Harper County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Harper County planning lane, make its
> agriculture, water-governance, energy-resource, property, municipal,
> recreation, flood, weather, privacy, sensitive-place, and evidence boundaries
> visible, and route future work through KFM source, evidence, policy, review,
> release, correction, and rollback controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation, approval, or release
> evidence.** This directory contains this README and one Harper County build
> plan. Their presence prevents accidental duplicate planning; it does not
> establish source admission, semantic correctness, validator success, runtime
> behavior, release, deployment, or publication.

This README repairs the existing same-path placeholder. It does not move or
rename the county lane, create a parallel control plane, or treat proposed
ADR-0027 as accepted.

> [!WARNING]
> **No private-well, water-right, title, access, operator, or household
> determination.** County, municipal, water, agriculture, energy, property,
> regulatory, and public-web material must not become a KFM conclusion about a
> person's water right, well safety, household potability, ownership, title,
> access, tax or compliance status, or an operator's conduct.

Current flood, weather, emergency, recreation, road, water-service, closure,
and public-safety conditions are time-sensitive operational matters. Missing
current governed evidence and responsible-authority routing requires
`ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR`, not a plausible-sounding answer.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Sensitive, private, and operational precision fails closed.** Exact or
> reconstructive private-well, parcel, residence, lease, operator, critical-
> infrastructure, emergency, rare-species, habitat, burial, archaeological,
> sacred, or culturally restricted detail must not reach an ordinary public
> client.

## 4. Proposed Harper County proof slice

The linked build plan proposes a public-safe learning slice organized around
five related but non-interchangeable concerns:

1. **County and municipal orientation** - generalized Harper County, Anthony,
   and City of Harper civic and public-information context without household,
   parcel, property, title, access, or service-safety inference.
2. **Aggregate agriculture context** - time-attributed county statistics and
   public educational context without farm, ranch, operator, person, or current
   production inference.
3. **Water-governance authority** - distinguish regulatory, planning, data,
   floodplain, and public-information roles without deciding private-well
   condition, household potability, water rights, permits, allocation, or
   compliance.
4. **Energy-resource source-role separation** - distinguish scientific/data
   context from regulatory authority without exact operational detail,
   vulnerability disclosure, operator targeting, or compliance accusation.
5. **Flood, weather, recreation, and civic routing** - point current questions
   toward responsible authorities without converting KFM into a live warning,
   closure, road, lake, recreation, emergency, or public-safety service.

### Proposed first-slice surfaces

| Proposed surface | Intended value | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Harper County authority and orientation card | County-scale civic and service orientation | No property, title, tax, residence, access, private-record, or emergency conclusion |
| Anthony and City of Harper municipal cards | Generalized municipal, public-information, and recreation routing | No live service, recreation, road, closure, emergency, or household-safety answer |
| Aggregate agriculture card | Dated county-level agricultural context | No farm, ranch, operator, worker, owner, parcel, or current-condition inference |
| Water-governance authority card | Explain source roles and official routing | No private-well, potability, individual water-right, permit, allocation, legal, or compliance conclusion |
| Energy-resource authority distinction card | Separate scientific/data and regulatory source roles | No exact site, lease, well, operator, facility, vulnerability, or accusation output |
| Flood-map and weather-authority redirect cards | Distinguish hazard products, forecasts, warnings, and current-source roles | No insurance, legal, evacuation, live safety, warning replacement, or property-specific verdict |
| Aggregate demographic/geographic card | Time-attributed county-scale context | No living-person or community profiling beyond reviewed aggregates |
| Evidence Drawer and boundary panel | Expose evidence state, source role, time basis, limitations, policy outcome, and reason code | Unresolved evidence, currentness, policy, or release state cannot be polished into an answer |

These are **PROPOSED planning surfaces**. No layer, card, payload, source
descriptor, EvidenceBundle, policy decision, route, live feed, or released
artifact is confirmed by this README.

### Deferred planning surfaces

- live weather, flood, emergency, road, recreation, lake, closure, or municipal
  service integration;
- parcel, property, ownership, title, tax, residence, access, lease, or easement
  records;
- private-well, water-right, appropriation, permit, allocation, or household
  water-quality records;
- exact oil, gas, energy, lease, well, facility, operator, or production
  geometry;
- detailed critical infrastructure or emergency-response information;
- culturally sensitive, archaeological, burial, sacred, rare-species, or
  vulnerable habitat interpretation without appropriate authority and review.

### Excluded or denied by default

- statements that a private well or household water is safe, unsafe, potable,
  contaminated, compliant, or suitable;
- individual water-right, permit, allocation, priority, beneficial-use, legal,
  or compliance conclusions;
- ownership, title, tax, residence, access, insurance, lease, easement,
  buildability, or property-value determinations;
- farm, ranch, person, worker, household, operator, facility, or compliance
  targeting;
- exact energy, well, lease, infrastructure, emergency, wildlife, habitat,
  archaeology, burial, sacred-place, or culturally restricted locations;
- current evacuation, flood, weather, warning, road, closure, recreation,
  service, or emergency direction;
- uncited generated narrative or any direct public read from `RAW`, `WORK`,
  `QUARANTINE`, internal stores, source-system actions, or live endpoints.

## 5. Source role, time, and authority boundary

The plan names source candidates and roles based on its June 2026 authoring
checks. This README confirms only that those candidates are written in the
plan. Their current identity, authority, terms, rights, availability, version,
fitness, geometry, and admission status were not reverified here.

| Candidate role described by the plan | May support after admission and review | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| County administrative source | County identity, civic orientation, department routing, and public-information entry points | Title, tax, access, private-record, living-person, or emergency authority |
| Municipal public-information source | Generalized Anthony or City of Harper context and responsible routing | Household water-safety, live recreation, closure, road, emergency, or service verdict |
| Federal aggregate agriculture statistics | Dated county-level aggregate agriculture context | Farm, ranch, operator, person, parcel, current crop, or current market truth |
| State water-regulatory authority | General authority, program, floodplain, and official-source routing | Individual water right, well condition, potability, permit, allocation, or legal conclusion |
| Scientific energy-resource/data source | Reviewed aggregate or generalized resource-data context with limitations | Regulatory authority, compliance conclusion, operator accusation, or public vulnerability layer |
| Energy regulator | General regulatory authority and official routing | KFM-originated compliance finding, enforcement claim, or operator targeting |
| Flood-hazard product authority | Official product discovery and dated hazard context | Current emergency, evacuation, insurance, legal, property, or buildability verdict |
| Weather authority | Responsible route for current official forecasts, warnings, and conditions | Cached or paraphrased KFM live warning or personal safety service |
| Federal aggregate demographic source | Dated county-scale statistical context with vintage caveats | Living-person, household, or protected-community profiling |
| Historical, ecological, or recreation source candidate | Reviewed, generalized public context | Exact sensitive place, current access/safety, private-land, or cultural authority |

### Time basis is part of truth

Every future Harper evidence or public artifact must distinguish at least:

- source publication, reporting, observation, or effective time;
- retrieval or checked time;
- forecast, warning, advisory, permit, regulatory, or validity window where
  applicable;
- superseded, corrected, withdrawn, provisional, delayed, or stale state;
- the difference between aggregate historical context and a current operational
  source.

A 2022 statistical profile does not prove current farm conditions. A current
official route does not prove that KFM retrieved, understood, or released its
latest contents. A public record does not automatically support a legal,
health, property, compliance, or personal-risk conclusion.

## 6. Governed outcomes and public safety

A future public Harper Focus request must pass through a governed interface,
resolve `EvidenceRef` to an admissible `EvidenceBundle`, apply source-role,
rights, sensitivity, currentness, and policy checks, and return one finite
outcome.

| Outcome | Harper County meaning | Representative trigger |
|---|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Bounded, reviewed, released public context with citations, time basis, limitations, and no authority impersonation | Generalized civic or historical aggregate question supported by admissible evidence |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, currentness, authority, fitness, or review is insufficient for a safe answer | Current flood, weather, recreation, road, water-service, well, property, or compliance question without governed evidence |
| `DENY` | The request seeks prohibited legal, health, property, operator, living-person, harmful-precision, vulnerability, or trust-membrane inference | Household potability verdict, exact well/operator targeting, or direct internal-store request |
| `ERROR` | Evidence resolution, policy evaluation, contract validation, or runtime processing failed safely | Missing EvidenceBundle, invalid envelope, or policy-engine failure |

### Example dispositions

| Request shape | Expected posture before evidence and policy closure | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| “Explain the source roles for county agriculture, water, energy, flood, and weather context.” | `ABSTAIN` now; possible bounded `ANSWER` after admission and review | The plan proposes the distinction, but this README supplies no admitted evidence |
| “Is my private well safe to drink from?” | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` with responsible health/water routing | Household potability and health judgment are outside scope |
| “Do I own this water right, and am I compliant?” | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` with responsible legal/regulatory routing | KFM does not determine individual rights or compliance |
| “Who owns this parcel, and may I enter it?” | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` | Ownership, title, residence, and access are not county Focus outputs |
| “Which operator is violating the rules?” | `DENY` | No operator targeting or KFM-originated compliance accusation |
| “Show exact wells, leases, pipelines, utility controls, or emergency staging.” | `DENY` | Infrastructure, targeting, privacy, and harmful-precision boundary |
| “What is the current flood, warning, road, closure, or lake status?” | `ABSTAIN` with responsible current-authority routing | Dynamic operational currentness is unproved and not an MVP KFM service |
| “Summarize a dated county aggregate with its reporting period and caveats.” | `ABSTAIN` now; possible bounded `ANSWER` after evidence and release closure | Aggregate context may be supportable without person or operator inference |
| “Read the raw source or live endpoint directly from the browser.” | `DENY` | Ordinary public clients use governed released interfaces only |

Authoritative redirects must state that the user is leaving KFM and that KFM
has not answered the live, legal, health, property, or compliance question. A
redirect is not evidence that the destination is current, complete, or suitable
for every decision.

## 7. Evidence, review, and readiness boundary

### Evidence closure

`EvidenceRef -> EvidenceBundle` closure requires more than a URL or citation.
For any Harper claim, reviewers must be able to inspect identity, authority
role, version and time basis, rights, provenance, transformations, sensitivity,
limitations, integrity, review state, correction lineage, and release state.

### Readiness matrix

| Gate | Current README evidence | Required before a public claim or layer |
|---|---|---|
| Source identity and authority | Candidate roles appear in the plan only | Admitted source records with bounded claim roles |
| Rights and derivative use | `UNKNOWN` | Recorded terms, attribution, caching, display, and derivative-use disposition |
| External-fact freshness | Plan records a June 2026 check; not rerun here | Current authoritative verification appropriate to the claim |
| Evidence closure | No Harper EvidenceBundle inspected | Resolvable evidence and integrity/provenance support |
| Agriculture, water, health, flood, weather, energy, privacy, sensitivity, infrastructure policy | No execution inspected | Accepted policy outcomes and representative negative proof |
| Geometry and precision | Proposed/generalized only | Rights-safe geometry plus privacy, anti-targeting, ecology, cultural, and harmful-precision review |
| Fixtures and validators | Proposed by the plan | Deterministic positive and negative fixtures with finite outcomes |
| Accountable review | Owners and reviewers unresolved | Named accountable and independent reviewers appropriate to consequence |
| Accessibility and public comprehension | Proposed UI surfaces only | Keyboard, focus, screen-reader, visible-state, plain-language, and redirect review |
| Correction and rollback | Not exercised | Correction, withdrawal, supersession, cache invalidation, and rollback targets |
| Release and publication | None | Separate governed release and promotion evidence |

### Minimum negative proof families

Future implementation should deterministically show that:

- private-well, potability, water-right, permit, title, access, operator,
  compliance, and property questions cannot become ungrounded `ANSWER`
  outcomes;
- current flood, weather, warning, recreation, road, closure, service, and
  emergency questions route or fail closed;
- missing, stale, superseded, or broken evidence fails closed;
- administrative, statistical, scientific, regulatory, and operational source
  roles do not collapse;
- exact private, living-person, sensitive, cultural, archaeological, wildlife,
  habitat, energy, well, lease, and infrastructure precision is denied or
  safely generalized;
- ordinary public clients cannot read canonical/internal stores or call source
  actions and live endpoints directly;
- correction or withdrawal does not silently erase lineage.

Passing documentation checks or synthetic fixtures would prove only their
bounded behavior. It would not prove current source truth, operational safety,
legal or health fitness, policy completeness, release eligibility, deployment,
or public parity.

evidence-pinned correction. Preserve the prior commit, identify the incorrect

placeholder merely to mimic the old file. A documentation revert does not
withdraw any separately released artifact; public correction requires the
governed correction, withdrawal, cache-invalidation, supersession, and rollback

- the Harper build plan, county index, lane placement, or metadata contract

or pull request disagrees with current accepted authority or executable evidence,

resolved.
