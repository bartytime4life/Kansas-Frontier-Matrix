## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The build plan names official county, Census, USDA/NASS, and NWS source
families plus county planning, wind-project, maps, road, utility-district, and
emergency-service material. They remain **source seeds**, not admitted KFM
sources, until current identity, terms, rights, coverage, cadence, sensitivity,
and allowed-use posture are verified.

| Candidate source role | What it may support after admission | What it must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| County planning/commission/resolution sources | Public planning and governance chronology. | Proof that a proposal is adopted, implemented, current, or legally dispositive unless source state says so. |
| Wind-project document collections | Dated document-role and public-process context. | Current project-status feed, project advocacy, legal advice, owner profile, or security clearance. |
| County maps and service districts | Public administrative/geographic orientation after review. | Tactical emergency, infrastructure weakness, title/access, or unrestricted recombination authority. |
| USDA NASS / agriculture sources | County aggregate statistics with source quality/suppression flags. | Individual farm, operator, parcel, financial, or production truth. |
| Census | Aggregate demographic/geographic context for supported vintages. | Person-level identity or current county operational state. |
| NWS / emergency authority | Current authoritative redirect when consulted in scope. | Cached plan text as a current warning, forecast, or tactical response guide. |
| Property/tax/parcel systems | Source-native administrative context when lawful and necessary. | Title, ownership, debt, fraud, access, or living-person profiling truth. |

For volatile material, future answers should distinguish document/event time,
publication/revision time, retrieval time, validity time, KFM release time, and
correction/withdrawal time where material. A dated agreement or map cannot be
silently promoted into current operational status.

<a id="6-agriculture-property-wind-energy-infrastructure-and-current-safety-boundaries"></a>

## 6. Agriculture, property, wind-energy, infrastructure, and current-safety boundaries

### Agriculture and reidentification

County-level agricultural statistics may support public aggregate explanation
when source role, release period, suppression, uncertainty, and fitness remain
visible. KFM must fail closed on individual farm/operator identification,
field-level behavior inferred through layer joins, financial inference, and
small-cell or sparse-category reconstruction.

### Property and living-person data

Parcel, tax, appraisal, meeting, project, or map records must not be recombined
into living-person or landowner profiles. Public availability of a record does
not automatically make every derived join, profile, or inference public-safe.
Title, ownership, easement, access, valuation, tax, debt, and legal questions
require claim-appropriate source authority; otherwise return `ABSTAIN` or
`DENY`.

### Wind-energy governance

Wind-project materials may eventually support a neutral, dated explanation of
public planning and governance. KFM must not infer current construction or
operation status from older documents, advocate for or against a project,
provide legal conclusions, identify or profile affected landowners, or turn
site/transmission/haul-route material into vulnerability analysis.

### Infrastructure and emergency detail

Electric, water, road, transmission, 911, EMS, fire, haul-route, and related
service-area data require security review. Exact capacity, weakness,
dependencies, disruption opportunities, tactical routing, response gaps, or
other vulnerability-relevant detail remains deny-by-default unless qualified
policy and release authority support a bounded public transformation.

### Current hazards and operations

KFM is not an emergency alert or operational-status authority. Current weather,
road, utility, hazard, emergency, construction, or response questions require
current official evidence or an authoritative redirect. Dated planning text and
cached layers must never substitute for live guidance.

### Cultural and ecological sensitivity

Exact archaeology, burials, sacred places, rare species, nests, roosts, dens,
and other harmful-precision locations remain fail closed. Public use requires
reviewed redaction/generalization and a transform record appropriate to the
risk.

## 7. Finite outcomes and candidate reason codes

Future Nemaha County runtime behavior should preserve KFM's four terminal
outcomes:

| Outcome | Nemaha County use |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Released, in-scope evidence supports a bounded claim and citation/policy/review requirements pass. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence is missing, stale, conflicted, legally unfit, too coarse, too precise, or current status is unresolved. |
| `DENY` | Privacy, living-person profiling, individual-farm inference, infrastructure security, sensitive location, or prohibited transformation blocks exposure. |
| `ERROR` | Resolver, schema, citation, manifest, policy, integrity, or governed dependency fails. |

The build plan also proposes Nemaha-specific reason codes. They remain
**PROPOSED planning vocabulary**, not registered policy/runtime codes:

| Candidate code | Intended bounded use |
|---|---|

| `NM-EVIDENCE-STALE` | Evidence outside currentness window → `ABSTAIN` |
| `NM-PROPOSAL-NOT-ADOPTION` | Proposal does not establish adoption → `ABSTAIN` |
| `NM-WIND-PROJECT-STATUS` | Current wind-project status unverified → `ABSTAIN` |
| `NM-RIGHTS-UNCLEAR` | Rights or derivative-use posture unresolved → `ABSTAIN` |
| `NM-PROPERTY-LEGAL` | Title/access/tax/legal question lacks proper authority → `ABSTAIN` |
| `NM-OWNER-PROFILE` | Landowner/living-person profiling request → `DENY` |
