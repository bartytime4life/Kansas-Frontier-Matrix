## County boundary

The Ness County plan proposes a county experience organized around **Ness City and smaller communities, county-scale agriculture, the historic Ness County Bank, municipal/public-administration context, geology, and dated oil-and-gas production evidence**. Its central trust problem is that useful public records can become misleading or unsafe when flattened across time, source role, ownership, property, health, regulation, or operational scale.

The first governing rule is therefore:

> **County context is not private energy, property, health, or operational truth.**

Any future layer, evidence object, card, export, or AI answer must preserve the distinction between public county-scale interpretation and exact wells, leases, operators, parcels, households, facilities, infrastructure dependencies, ownership, mineral rights, royalties, compliance, or current operational state.

## Proposed first proof slice

The sibling plan proposes a bounded Ness County proof slice that would demonstrate KFM can:

- present county-scale agriculture while preserving statistical suppression and avoiding individual-farm inference;
- summarize oil-and-gas production only at an approved aggregate scale with explicit reporting periods and partial-year labels;
- keep KGS scientific/data authority distinct from Kansas Corporation Commission regulatory authority;
- explain the historic Ness County Bank from historical evidence without treating nomination-era ownership, condition, access, or use as current truth;
- route users toward current municipal, county, weather, water, and regulatory authorities instead of copying volatile status into durable prose;
- preserve visible negative states when currentness, rights, privacy, source authority, safe scale, review, or release closure is missing;
- avoid precise fossil, archaeological, burial, collecting, private-well, household-water, infrastructure-vulnerability, or emergency-operational exposure.

These are **PROPOSED** behaviors until implementation, validation, evidence closure, policy review, and release state are verified.

## Public-safe rules

| Rule | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Evidence before interpretation | Consequential claims resolve through governed evidence; generated language, map pixels, database labels, and historical forms never substitute for evidence. |
| Cite or abstain | Missing or stale support must narrow the answer or return a finite negative state. |
| Time basis is explicit | Partial-year, historical, nomination-era, and current operational records must not be collapsed into a timeless county claim. |
| Energy detail is minimized | Exact well, lease, field, injection, plugging, pipeline, facility, operator, worker, or emergency-operational detail stays out of the first public-safe product. |
| Regulatory authority stays distinct | KGS scientific/data records do not substitute for KCC regulatory determinations. |
| Agriculture remains aggregate | County statistics must not become individual-farm, owner, worker, or parcel profiles; suppression markers remain meaningful. |
| Historic significance is not current access | National Register or historical evidence does not establish present ownership, occupancy, safety, accessibility, or permission to enter. |
| Property and personal conclusions are out of scope | Appraisal, deeds, taxes, utility, health, cemetery, genealogy, mineral-right, royalty, title, and household records must not be fused into personal or legal profiles. |
| Water and environmental-health conclusions are bounded | Municipal-water links, private-well material, environmental records, or contamination context must not become household potability, exposure, source-attribution, or compliance conclusions. |
| Sensitive scientific/cultural locations fail closed | Precise fossil, archaeological, burial, collecting, or culturally sensitive locations require separate governed treatment. |
| Public clients stay downstream of trust | UI, map, search, and AI surfaces consume governed/released evidence rather than canonical or working stores. |
| Correction and rollback remain visible | Future corrections must be able to supersede or withdraw affected public products without rewriting history. |

## Finite outcomes

For this county lane, the expected public-facing outcome vocabulary is:

- `ANSWER` — released evidence is in scope, time-bounded, policy-safe, appropriately generalized, and citation-valid;
- `ABSTAIN` — evidence, currentness, source role, identity, safe scale, privacy, rights, or release closure is insufficient;
- `DENY` — the request would cross a property, ownership, personal, environmental-health, sensitive-location, infrastructure, operational, regulatory, or other protected boundary;
- `ERROR` — a resolver, validator, policy engine, source adapter, or runtime dependency failed.

These outcomes are a **design contract**, not proof that the current repository implements them for Ness County.

## Source-role posture

The build plan discusses several source families, each with a different role. Their presence in planning material does **not** establish admission or public-release authority.

| Source family | Intended role | Must not be treated as |
|---|---|---|
| USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service | County-scale agricultural aggregates and suppression-aware statistics | Individual-farm, parcel, worker, owner, or causation truth |
| Kansas Geological Survey | Oil-and-gas production, well-statistics, geology, and scientific/data context | Final regulatory authority, mineral-right/royalty truth, or permission for unrestricted precise operational mapping |
| Kansas Corporation Commission | Regulatory context when admitted and current | A substitute for evidence closure, legal advice, ownership, or generalized KFM interpretation |
| National Park Service / preservation sources | Historic significance and nomination-era documentation | Current ownership, occupancy, structural safety, public access, or permission to enter |
| Ness County / Ness City | Public administration, municipal services, records routing, and current-information links | Personal, household, legal, title, tax, utility, health, emergency, or cemetery-profile authority for the public product |
| National Weather Service | Current operational weather authority | Durable KFM prose that can outlive the official current state |
| Hydrology / environmental sources | Bounded water, flood, geology, or environmental context where admitted | Household potability, contamination-source, liability, compliance, or emergency decisions |

## Validation expectations for future implementation

A future Ness County implementation should not be considered complete merely because Markdown exists. At minimum, the changed area should eventually prove:

1. aggregate-versus-precise energy-data handling with explicit safe-scale rules;
2. complete-year versus partial-year production labeling and stale/current behavior;
3. suppression-aware agricultural handling with no individual-farm inference;
4. source-role separation between KGS, KCC, NASS, preservation, county/city, weather, hydrology, and environmental authorities;
5. positive and negative fixtures for `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, and `ERROR` behavior;
6. no-network deterministic tests for the county trust boundary;
7. EvidenceRef-to-EvidenceBundle closure for consequential claims;
8. privacy, property, rights, sensitivity, infrastructure, and public-safe policy checks;
9. governed API/UI behavior with visible negative states;
10. correction, supersession, withdrawal, and rollback behavior;
11. no direct path from raw source, operational record, or model output to public truth;
12. release evidence appropriate to the significance of any energy, property, heritage, environmental-health, or public-safety claim.
