# Cherokee County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Cherokee County planning lane, make its mining, remediation, Route 66, Spring River, borderlands, ecology, property, public-health, infrastructure, and historical-context boundaries visible, and route future work through KFM evidence, policy, review, release, correction, and rollback controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation or release evidence.** This directory contains this README and one Cherokee County build plan. Their presence prevents accidental duplicate planning; it does not establish source admission, factual accuracy, semantic correctness, validator success, runtime behavior, qualified review, release, deployment, or publication.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains unresolved.** This same-path update modernizes an existing README under `docs/focus-mode/counties/cherokee_county/`. Accepted Directory Rules v2 treats Focus Mode as a composition scope; proposed ADR-0027 describes a different plural, kebab-case control plane but remains unaccepted. No file is moved, renamed, copied into a parallel tree, or declared the final canonical county write target here.

> [!WARNING]
> **Mining, remediation, water, property, health, and sensitive-location records require fail-closed handling.** Exact or reconstructive abandoned-mine openings, unsafe exploration locations, private-well or household details, parcel-linked conclusions, sensitive ecological or cultural locations, and infrastructure-vulnerability information must not reach an ordinary public client. Regulatory, remediation, environmental, public-history, and tourism records must retain their source roles and must not become health diagnoses, legal or liability conclusions, title determinations, current safety advice, or evidence merely because they appear on a map. Missing evidence, rights, safe resolution, policy, qualified review, or release closure must produce a bounded `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR` rather than a fluent inference.

## 5. Source-role and cross-domain boundaries

The build plan names source families that answer materially different questions. They must not be merged into one undifferentiated Cherokee County truth layer.

| Planned source or object class | Legitimate bounded role | Must not be treated as |
|---|---|---|
| County or municipal site, GIS, or civic record | Source discovery, administrative routing, and candidate geography or service context after rights review | Automatic reuse permission, title authority, comprehensive history, evidence closure, or public release |
| Geological, mining, or archival source | Source-scoped geology, industrial history, production-era context, or documented historical claim | Present hazard, access permission, current environmental condition, health conclusion, or legal finding |
| Environmental regulator or remediation program record | Record-specific administrative, investigation, status, remedy, or program context within its published scope and date | Diagnosis, exposure proof, compliance finding beyond the record, liability, property-value judgment, or guarantee of present safety |
| Water observation, water-quality, hydrography, or flood source | Identified station, reach, basin, observation, regulatory map, or model context with visible time and role | Private-well condition, drinking-water advice, live flood authority, causation, or unqualified present state |
| Route 66, historic-register, marker, museum, or public-history source | Source-specific documented property, route, event, artifact, or interpretation | Comprehensive history, automatic corroboration, legal status, or evidence merely because it is public-facing |
| Tourism or promotional source | Discovery, visitor routing, public interpretation, or candidate lead requiring corroboration | Sovereign historical, environmental, safety, property, or legal evidence |
| Parcel, assessor, ownership, or local-property source | Purpose-limited administrative context after privacy, rights, and field review | Title truth, access entitlement, named-person profiling, liability, contamination, or health inference |
| Ecology, land-cover, habitat, or reclamation source | Generalized environmental or restoration context within its source role and approved precision | Exact sensitive occurrence, cave, roost, survey, nest, or protected-site disclosure |
| Cross-state Tri-State district source | Source-specific Kansas, Missouri, Oklahoma, federal, or regional evidence with explicit jurisdiction and identifiers | A silently harmonized district-wide truth layer or authority outside the source's jurisdiction |
| Generated narrative, map styling, score, or derived indicator | Plain-language explanation or visualization of already admissible evidence | Evidence, source authority, policy decision, expert review, release state, or causal proof |

### Cross-source combination rule

Juxtaposition is not proof. The system must not infer contamination, exposure, causation, liability, title, health effect, safe access, cultural meaning, ecological occurrence, or current condition merely because mining, remediation, water, parcel, tourism, history, or ecology cards appear together. Every combined claim needs an explicit relation, evidence support, source roles, spatial and temporal scope, policy decision, review state, and release posture appropriate to its significance.

## 6. Public safety and governed outcomes

### Fail-closed matrix

| Request or condition | Required public outcome | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Exact or reconstructive abandoned-mine opening, shaft, collapse, unsafe exploration, or trespass-enabling location | `DENY` | Do not confirm existence or expose precision through map, text, metadata, search, accessibility output, cache, telemetry, export, or generated explanation |
| Private-well, household, named-person, parcel-linked environmental, or health detail | `DENY` | Ordinary public Focus Mode is not a household exposure, diagnosis, ownership, or private-property intelligence surface |
| Contaminated-site or remediation record used to answer exposure, diagnosis, compliance, liability, title, property-value, or present-safety question | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` | The record's existence and program role do not support the requested personal, legal, or current-condition conclusion |
| Tourism, museum, marker, or promotional text used as sole historical proof | `ABSTAIN` | Require admitted evidence and explicit source-role treatment; visibility or popularity is not authority |
| Mining-history record used to infer current hazard, safe access, active status, or environmental condition | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` | Historical and present operational or environmental roles remain separate |
| Water observation, model, flood map, or regulatory record used as private-well, drinking-water, live flood, or legal advice | `ABSTAIN` | Require the appropriate current authority and exact supported operation |
| Exact sensitive cave, bat, species, habitat, burial, cemetery, sacred, or archaeological location | `DENY` unless an accepted public-safe transform and qualified review explicitly permit the operation | Public-source availability is not blanket permission for derived precision |
| Infrastructure or remediation-system vulnerability request | `DENY` | Ordinary public Focus Mode is not a vulnerability-analysis surface |
| Cross-state sources conflict or lack a stable identity, jurisdiction, time, or relation | `ABSTAIN` | Do not silently harmonize or choose the more convenient source |
| Material claim lacks a resolving `EvidenceRef` to `EvidenceBundle` chain | `ABSTAIN` | Cite-or-abstain |
| Policy, evidence resolver, validator, or governed runtime fails | `ERROR` | Never fallback to an answer or a less restrictive display |
| Evidence, source role, policy, qualified review, and release closure support the exact bounded public operation | `ANSWER` | Include citations, time basis, spatial scope, source roles, limitations, sensitivity posture, and correction/release state |

### Public client boundary

A future Cherokee County client may consume only governed finite envelopes and released public-safe artifacts. It must not read:

- RAW, WORK, QUARANTINE, or internal candidate stores;
- unrestricted source payloads or direct source-system responses;
- protected mine, cultural, ecological, property, person, well, health, or infrastructure records;
- direct model output;
- policy internals or sensitive denial reasons;
- unreleased receipts, proofs, review notes, or release candidates.

A refusal is part of the trust surface. Public explanations should state that information is unavailable or withheld under the applicable public-safety boundary without revealing whether a protected record exists, how precise it is, or how a protection transform works.

## 8. Smallest safe follow-up

The next dependency-closed implementation should remain narrower than a live county feature.

1. **Recheck path and control-plane authority.** Keep the current lane stable unless ADR-0027 or a successor is accepted with migration and consumer evidence.
2. **Recheck source identity, terms, rights, and currentness.** Build a dated assessment packet for the plan's candidate county, geological, regulatory, water, historic, tourism, ecology, property, and cross-state source families; do not fetch, ingest, or publish payloads.
3. **Define source roles and decision roles.** Record which source can support which claim, which operation it cannot support, and which mining, remediation, water, ecology, history, cultural, privacy, legal, security, and release reviews are required without inventing people or approval.
4. **Define one no-network synthetic assessment profile.** Use non-sensitive synthetic references to prove source-role separation, exact-mine-location denial, private-well and household denial, unsupported health/legal/property abstention, tourism-as-proof rejection, cross-state conflict abstention, and fixed non-effects.
5. **Keep public runtime work on HOLD.** No Cherokee County route, map layer, live connector, governed AI answer, release, deployment, or publication should follow until contracts, machine shape, policy, evidence resolution, qualified review, correction, and rollback close.

A useful first executable slice would prove that an exact abandoned-mine request is denied, private-well and household requests are denied, a remediation record cannot produce a health or liability conclusion, tourism text cannot stand alone as proof, a cross-state identity conflict abstains, and every mutation, source-admission, release, deployment, publication, and repository-setting effect flag remains false. It would not retrieve or admit a live source.
