# Grant County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Grant County planning lane, make its
> dated-source, energy-operation, infrastructure, environmental-health,
> property/mineral/access, groundwater, water-right, lawful-use, emergency, and
> future-supply boundaries visible, and route future work through KFM evidence,
> policy, qualified review, correction, rollback, and release controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation or release evidence.**
> This directory contains this README and one Grant County build plan. Their
> presence establishes planning lineage only; it does not admit a source,
> resolve evidence, activate policy, validate a payload, deploy a product, or
> publish county knowledge.

> [!CAUTION]
> **The existing path remains a compatibility lane.** Accepted ADR-0029 adopts
> Directory Rules v2, while ADR-0027 still proposes a plural, kebab-case county
> control plane. This update modifies the tracked README in place and keeps any
> move, rename, alias retirement, or parallel-tree creation on **HOLD**.

> [!WARNING]
> **A public energy or water record is not a present operational, environmental,
> property, or legal verdict.** Dated KGS context, public energy-data services,
> DWR administrative geography, WIMAS records, county parcel links, and map
> overlap must remain role- and time-bounded. They cannot establish that a well,
> facility, pipeline, parcel, mineral interest, lease, water right, environment,
> health condition, emergency, or future supply has a particular current state.

<a id="5-source-role-and-temporal-fitness"></a>

## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The plan records seven official-source seeds checked on 2026-05-24. This README
does not recheck them. A future source ledger must preserve publisher, stable
identity, retrieval and content/update dates, source role, allowed claim,
rights, sensitivity, limitations, correction behavior, and public-use
disposition.

| Planned source role | May support after admission | Must not be treated as |
|---|---|---|
| County public-service routing | A verified official route to county services. | Imported parcel truth, title evidence, private-person data, or live emergency guidance. |
| Dated KGS technical context | Bounded historical or project-defined Hugoton context. | Current well, facility, pipeline, production, supply, safety, or regulatory status. |
| KGS public energy-data service | Description of the service, its stated update, and its declared limitations. | Certified completeness, individual operation status, compliance, safety, or public-display permission for every downstream record. |
| DWR administrative groundwater context | GMD membership and expressly scoped dated regional context. | Current individual water-right standing, lawful use, private-well condition, or supply forecast. |
| WIMAS limitation/redirect | Currentness, lawful-use non-determination, and official redirect posture. | A KFM-issued water-right or legal conclusion. |

### Minimum source-admission questions

1. What stable source identity, publisher, version, content date, retrieval date,
   and digest bind the candidate?
2. Is the source authoritative for the narrow claim, or only for context,
   service routing, dataset access, or a limitation statement?
3. What rights or terms govern retrieval, storage, transformation, display,
   citation, redistribution, and derived artifacts?
4. Does public use reveal or help reconstruct operational energy detail,
   infrastructure, property, well, water-right, or living-person information?
5. What spatial and temporal resolution is the minimum necessary safe form?
6. What official-current authority is needed for operational, regulatory,
   environmental, health, water-right, or emergency questions?
7. What correction, supersession, withdrawal, and rollback duties apply?

### Anti-collapse rule

Map overlap is not causation, ownership, liability, contamination, access,
lawful use, operational status, or future supply. Dated technical context,
public data services, water administration, county routing, regulatory findings,
environmental evidence, property records, and generated explanation remain
separate source roles.

<a id="6-energy-operations-environment-and-public-safety"></a>

## 6. Energy operations, environment, and public safety

### Minimum necessary display

A future public projection should prefer county- or regional-scale context and
visible limitation cards. Exact or readily reconstructable well, facility,
pipeline, injection, plugging, production, or vulnerability detail is not part
of the first slice. A public map must never make precise operations easier to
target merely because an underlying public service exposes records.

| Request or inference | Default outcome | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| “Which wells are producing now?” | `DENY` | Current operation is not supported by contextual planning evidence. |
| “Show operational weak points or targets.” | `DENY` | Vulnerability-ready infrastructure detail is unsafe. |
| “Is this facility safe or compliant?” | `DENY` | Safety and regulatory status require competent current authority. |
| “Is this property contaminated because it is above the gas area?” | `DENY` | Resource context cannot establish exposure, environmental, or health condition. |
| “How does the dated KGS material describe the regional context?” | `ANSWER` only after evidence resolution | The answer must expose the source role and date and avoid present-status language. |
| “Is there an emergency near a facility now?” | `ABSTAIN` | KFM is not a live alerting or incident-command surface. |

### Non-leaking denial

A denial may name the blocked category, cite the controlling boundary, and route
to a verified competent authority. It must not reveal whether a specific target
exists, confirm coordinates or attributes, provide reconstruction hints, or
echo a sensitive payload.

> [!WARNING]
> “Publicly obtainable” and “safe to aggregate, join, render, summarize, or
> target through KFM” are different questions.

## 7. Property, mineral, access, and water-right boundary

Energy, parcel, and water sources create an especially dangerous join surface.
The first Grant slice must not combine them to infer ownership, wealth,
liability, mineral interest, lease value, access permission, irrigation rights,
lawful use, private supply, or investment advice.

| Prohibited join or conclusion | Why evidence is insufficient | Required posture |
|---|---|---:|
| Energy well or resource context + parcel link → mineral ownership or benefit | Surface parcel routing and subsurface context do not establish title or mineral estates. | `DENY` |
| Resource context + environmental layer → contamination or health condition | Co-location alone is not environmental or exposure evidence. | `DENY` |
| WIMAS record + parcel → current water-right standing | The plan itself records WIMAS currentness limitations and DWR authority. | `DENY`; verified official redirect |
| Water-use record → lawful use | Administrative records cannot establish legal compliance by themselves. | `DENY` |
| Aquifer/GMD context → private well condition or future supply | Regional context does not determine a particular well or forecast. | `ABSTAIN` |
| County parcel or emergency link → authorization to ingest or cache | A link is routing evidence, not redistribution or currentness authority. | `ABSTAIN` / exclude |

Any future property- or right-related surface requires purpose limitation,
minimum necessary fields, current competent authority, rights and privacy
review, public-safe transformation, prohibited-join testing, and a correction
path. Ordinary browsing convenience is not enough.

## 8. Finite outcomes and reason codes

| Outcome | Meaning for Grant County | Required behavior |
|---|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | A narrow contextual claim resolves to public-safe evidence and policy permission. | Show source role, time basis, limitations, and EvidenceBundle-backed citations. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, currentness, authority, rights, or fit is insufficient. | State what is missing and provide only a verified safe redirect. |
| `DENY` | The request crosses an operational, infrastructure, environmental-health, property/mineral/access, water-right, privacy, or precision boundary. | Return a non-leaking reason and no sensitive detail. |
| `ERROR` | Required contract, evidence, policy, or runtime handling failed. | Fail closed; do not convert failure into an answer. |

creating two writable authorities.
