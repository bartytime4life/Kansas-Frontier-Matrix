# Doniphan County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Doniphan County planning lane, make its
> sovereign-authority, evidence, and sensitive-place boundaries visible, and
> route future work through KFM source, evidence, policy, review, release,
> correction, and rollback controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation, consent, approval, or
> release evidence.** This directory contains this README and one Doniphan
> County build plan. Their presence prevents accidental duplicate planning; it
> does not establish source admission, Nation authorization, semantic
> correctness, validator success, runtime behavior, release, deployment, or
> publication.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains a compatibility question.** This same-path update repairs
> an existing README under `docs/focus-mode/counties/doniphan_county/`.
> Accepted Directory Rules v2 governs placement; proposed ADR-0027 describes a
> plural, kebab-case control plane but remains unaccepted. No file is moved,
> renamed, copied into a parallel tree, or declared canonical here.

> [!WARNING]
> **Sovereign cultural authority comes before county storytelling.** The build
> plan identifies the Iowa Tribe of Kansas and Nebraska and the Sac and Fox
> Nation of Missouri in Kansas and Nebraska as sovereign authorities for their
> own identities, histories, cultural significance, and review needs. County,
> state, federal, museum, trail, tourism, or generated sources cannot replace
> Nation-authored evidence or appropriate review for substantive cultural
> claims.

> [!WARNING]
> **Sensitive and unsafe inference must fail closed.** Exact or reconstructive
> sacred, burial, archaeological, culturally restricted, ecological-occurrence,
> living-person, parcel/title, levee, emergency, or infrastructure-vulnerability
> detail must not reach an ordinary public client. Missing authority, evidence,
> rights, safe geometry, policy, or review closure requires `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`,
> or `ERROR`, not a fluent inference.

## 5. Sovereign authority and source-role boundary

Source availability does not make source roles interchangeable. The build plan
requires the following distinctions to remain visible:

| Source role | May support when admitted and reviewed | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| Nation-authored public source | The Nation's own public identity, framing, statements, and specifically approved public scope | Evidence that KFM may infer unstated beliefs, sacred meaning, land or legal conclusions, consent, endorsement, or unrestricted reuse |
| County administrative or GIS source | Bounded administrative context and source routing within its actual authority and terms | Cultural authority, title truth, Tribal-rights adjudication, safe-location clearance, or public-use approval for every field |
| Federal or state flood, river, transport, or ecology source | Its specific regulatory, study, monitoring, program, project, or scientific role with time and limitations | Live emergency authority, property guarantee, cultural authority, engineering conclusion, or permission to expose sensitive detail |
| Public-history, trail, museum, or tourism source | Clearly labeled public interpretation within its documented scope | Sovereign cultural authority, archaeological certainty, land-rights proof, or evidence of Nation approval |
| Agricultural aggregate | Defined county-scale statistics with source year and limitations | Farm, household, operator, parcel, production, eligibility, or property inference |
| Generated language | Bounded interpretation of released, resolving, public-safe evidence | Source authority, review, consent, policy, release, or sovereign truth |

When roles conflict or authority is insufficient, preserve the conflict and use
`ABSTAIN` or `DENY`. Do not blend sources into a smoother county narrative.

## 6. Public safety and governed outcomes

| Request or output class | Bounded posture | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Released, broad, time-labeled county context with resolving citations and required review | `ANSWER` only when evidence, policy, review, and release conditions pass | Keeps the result within admitted public-safe evidence |
| Missing, stale, conflicting, overly coarse, wrong-authority, or unresolvable evidence | `ABSTAIN` | Fluency cannot substitute for evidence, sovereign authority, source role, or currentness |
| Unreviewed claim about what a sovereign Nation believes, owns, ceded, permits, approves, or regards as sacred | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` | KFM cannot infer sovereign or cultural authority from third-party sources |
| Exact or reconstructive sacred, burial, archaeological, artifact, ceremonial, culturally restricted, or sensitive community location | `DENY` | Cultural safety, sovereignty, and site protection require fail-closed handling |
| Tribal-member, living-person, household, community-health, or other personal information | `DENY` | Prevents privacy harm and inappropriate exposure |
| Parcel-owner, title, access, zoning, Tribal-rights, individual legal, eligibility, liability, or property-specific inference | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN`, as accepted policy requires | Administrative and historical records do not adjudicate legal rights or safe reliance |
| Live flood safety, evacuation, river condition, levee vulnerability, property protection, insurance, or engineering direction | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` and route to the responsible live authority | Focus Mode is not the emergency, legal, insurance, or engineering authority |
| Exact sensitive fish or wildlife occurrence, spawning, habitat, or management-operational detail | `DENY` or reviewed generalization | Prevents ecological harm and reconstruction through map, search, export, cache, or AI surfaces |
| Tactical or restricted flood-protection, address, transport, utility, emergency-response, or other infrastructure detail | `DENY` or `QUARANTINE` | Public context does not authorize harmful operational disclosure |
| Tourism, trail, museum, or generated narrative presented as cultural, archaeological, legal, scientific, or sovereign proof | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` | Interpretation cannot replace the correct authority and EvidenceBundle |
| Resolver, policy, carrier, citation, or runtime failure | `ERROR` with no unsupported fallback claim | Failure must remain visible and fail closed |

Client-side hiding is not a sensitivity control. Protected detail must not be
delivered to an ordinary public client, included in public logs or analytics,
or reconstructed from tiles, filters, search, exports, caches, identifiers, or
AI responses.

## 7. Evidence and readiness boundary

A Doniphan County public result must remain downstream of the governed KFM
path:

1. identify a source once in the source-authority surface;
2. review authority, rights, access, time, geography, sensitivity, source role,
   Nation-associated representation scope, and correction behavior;
3. admit candidate material to `RAW`, or hold it in `WORK / QUARANTINE` when
   requirements are unresolved;
4. derive and validate `PROCESSED` material with transform and provenance
   lineage;
5. resolve every consequential `EvidenceRef` to an `EvidenceBundle` and apply
   policy;
6. complete the appropriate independent and sovereign-cultural-authority review
   for the exact public scope;
7. make a separate promotion and release decision with correction and rollback
   targets;
8. serve only released public-safe artifacts through governed interfaces;
9. keep map rendering and AI interpretation subordinate to that evidence,
   authority, review, and decision state.

## 8. Smallest safe follow-up

The smallest legitimate follow-up is a **sovereign-authority and planning-
evidence reconciliation**, not live ingestion:

1. review the build plan against current repository paths, current county
   inventory, accepted Directory Rules, and current Focus contract/schema/policy
   boundaries, then remove only claims proven stale;
2. establish an appropriate reviewer and authority-routing process before
   representing Nation-associated identities, histories, places, names, or
   cultural meaning;
3. classify the first Nation-authored, county, river, floodplain, agriculture,
   transportation, ecology, and public-history candidates by authority, rights,
   cadence, sensitivity, source role, correction behavior, and allowed public
   use before any activation;
4. choose one no-network synthetic county-context fixture using current semantic
   and schema authorities rather than creating parallel object homes;
5. define exact negative outcomes for wrong-authority cultural claims, sacred
   and burial places, archaeology, living-person and Tribal-member information,
   parcel/title and land-rights conclusions, live flood and levee guidance,
   ecological occurrences, infrastructure detail, and uncited generated
   language;
6. run repository-native focused and aggregate checks, then record introduced
   versus inherited failures;
7. keep live source activation, public geometry, release, deployment, and
   publication outside that packet.

This README does not authorize that follow-up; it states the dependency-closed
boundary that a later implementation request must verify.
