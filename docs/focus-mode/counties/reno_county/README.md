agriculture, and public-event proof slice centered on Reno County and
Hutchinson. Its value is not a restricted mine, live hazard, private-well,

source roles can support a bounded public story without being collapsed into
one truth layer.

| Strataca public-history context | Public museum/visitor interpretation | Tourism or operator prose is not independent geology or safety authority | `PROPOSED` |

## 5. Source role, rights, and temporal fitness

The plan's source lists and external claims remain candidate planning evidence
until source admission verifies identity, authority role, rights, allowed
transformation, currentness, spatial and temporal fitness, sensitivity, and
correction obligations.

| Source role | What it may support after admission | What it must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| Geologic or scientific source | Geology and subsurface interpretation within its stated scope | Mining-operation status, access permission, public safety, or engineering certification |
| Mine, operator, museum, or visitor source | Public history, visitor context, and approved interpretation | Independent geology authority, infrastructure-disclosure authority, or unrestricted reuse right |
| Hydrologic observation source | Time-stamped river or groundwater observations | Flood warning, water-quality conclusion, legal water-right decision, or recreation-safety verdict |
| Floodplain or regulatory source | A named administrative or regulatory layer | Forecast, live warning, or property-specific legal advice |
| Ecology source | Reviewed generalized wetland, habitat, or species context | Permission to expose sensitive occurrence geometry |
| Agriculture or statistical source | Aggregate or modeled agricultural context | Household, operator, title, or private-well inference |
| Event or visitor source | Public history and current operator information when fresh | Emergency/security operations or guaranteed future access |
| Historical or educational source | Bounded public-history context | Unreviewed artifact provenance, rights, authenticity, or scientific authority |
| Generated narrative | Interpretation over released evidence | Evidence, policy, review, release, or correction authority |

### Temporal fitness

Every consequential claim should distinguish, where material:

- event or observation time;
- source publication or revision time;
- retrieval time;
- validity period;
- review time;
- release time;
- correction or supersession time.

A dated source may remain useful for history while being unfit for current
operating status. That distinction is especially important for hazards, event
conditions, water, wetlands, public access, and infrastructure.

## 7. Water, wetlands, agriculture, and ecology boundaries

Reno's river-lowlands and wetland themes connect hydrology, agriculture,
ecology, and hazards. Their roles must remain separate.

### Hydrology and groundwater

- River observations are measurements, not flood warnings.
- Floodplain and regulatory layers are not forecasts.
- Groundwater records do not automatically establish water-right status, well
  safety, drinking-water quality, public-health status, or sustainability
  conclusions.
- Models and derived indicators retain method, uncertainty, source role, and
  date.
- Current access, flood, road, recreation, and emergency questions require
  current responsible authority.

### Wetlands and ecology

- Generalized wetland and migration context may be suitable after review.
- Exact sensitive species occurrences, nests, roosts, refuge-use patterns, or
  other harmful precision fail closed unless an approved public-safe
  transformation exists.
- Regional relationships to named wetland systems remain plan themes until
  source support, spatial scope, rights, and sensitivity are reviewed.
- Styling or client-side hiding is not a safe substitute for removing restricted
  geometry and attributes from public payloads.

### Agriculture and private information

- Aggregate crop, livestock, irrigation, land-cover, and soil context may be
  shown when rights, scale, year, and methodology are clear.
- No household-level profiling, private-well exposure, private-farm inference,
  owner identification, or parcel-as-title truth follows from county Focus
  composition.
- Cross-lane joins between agriculture, wells, parcels, hazards, and households
  require explicit policy and privacy review.

## 8. Space history, public events, and interpretive limits

- real-time safety judgments;
- access guarantees;
- law-enforcement or emergency-response operations.

Current event questions route to responsible official channels or return
`ABSTAIN` when KFM lacks released, time-fit evidence.

## 14. Maintenance, migration, correction, and rollback

### Re-review triggers

Re-review this README when:

- the sibling Reno plan changes materially;
- an accepted Focus Mode decision changes the current compatibility lane;
- source admission establishes, changes, or withdraws a Reno source;
- mining, infrastructure, hydrology, ecology, agriculture, privacy,
  public-health, or event policy changes;
- a Reno contract, fixture, API route, renderer, release, correction, or rollback
  object becomes repository-present;
- current repository evidence contradicts a statement here.

### Migration rule

Do not create a second writable Reno lane. A future move or rename requires a
reviewed migration that preserves history, repairs consumers and anchors,
records aliases and exit criteria, and maintains one authority owner.

### Correction rule

Correct stale repository facts through a reviewable documentation change. When
an external claim is challenged, fix the governing source or evidence object and
all affected released derivatives through their correction path; do not silently
rewrite this README as a substitute.

### Rollback

Before merge, close or abandon the draft pull request and branch. After merge,
use a transparent revert or forward-fix pull request against the actual merged
commit, reverting the README and generated provenance record together. Never
rewrite shared history or treat Git rollback as sufficient correction for an
already released public artifact.
