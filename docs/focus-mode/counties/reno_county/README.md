> [!IMPORTANT]

> sibling build plan and this README do not admit a source, create an

> singular, snake_case path. The current task is a same-path documentation update;

## 5. Source-role, rights, and currentness boundary

The sibling plan's source lists and external claims remain candidate planning evidence until
source admission verifies identity, authority role, rights, permitted transformation,
currentness, spatial fitness, temporal fitness, sensitivity, and correction obligations.

| Source role | What it may support after admission | What it must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| Geologic/scientific source | Geology, stratigraphy, subsurface interpretation within stated scope | Mining-operation status, public safety, access permission, or engineering certification |
| Mine/operator/museum source | Public history, visitor context, approved public interpretation | Independent geology authority, infrastructure disclosure authority, or unrestricted reuse right |
| Hydrologic observation source | Time-stamped river/groundwater observations | Flood warning, water-quality conclusion, legal water-right determination, or recreation-safety verdict |
| Flood/regulatory source | Named regulatory/administrative layer | Real-time warning or property-specific legal advice |
| Ecology source | Generalized wetland/habitat/species context | Permission to expose sensitive occurrence geometry |
| Agriculture/statistical source | Aggregate or modeled agricultural context | Household/farm-person inference, title truth, or private-well disclosure |
| Event/visitor source | Public history and current operator information when fresh | Emergency/security operations or guaranteed future conditions |
| Historical/educational source | Bounded public-history context | Unreviewed artifact provenance, rights, or definitive scientific authority |

### Temporal fitness

Every consequential claim should distinguish, where material:

- event/observation time;
- source publication or revision time;
- retrieval time;
- validity period;
- review time;
- release time;
- correction/supersession time.

A dated source may remain useful for history while being unfit for current operating status.
That distinction is especially important for hazards, event conditions, water, wetlands,
public access, and infrastructure.

## 7. Water, wetlands, agriculture, and ecology boundary

Reno's river-lowlands and wetland themes connect hydrology, agriculture, ecology, and
hazards. Those roles must remain separate.

### Hydrology

- River observations are measurements, not flood warnings.
- Floodplain/regulatory layers are not forecasts.
- Groundwater records do not automatically establish water-right status, well safety,
  drinking-water quality, or sustainability conclusions.
- Model outputs and derived indicators must retain method, uncertainty, date, and source role.

### Wetlands and ecology

- Generalized wetland and migration context may be suitable for public use after review.
- Exact sensitive species occurrences, nests, roosts, refuge-use patterns, or other harmful
  precision must fail closed unless an approved public-safe transform exists.
- Regional relationships to named wetland systems are planning themes until source support
  and spatial scope are reviewed.

### Agriculture

- Aggregate crop, livestock, irrigation, land-cover, and soil context may be shown when
  rights, scale, year, and methodology are clear.
- No household-level profiling, private-well exposure, private farm inference, or parcel-as-
  title truth should be created from county Focus Mode composition.

Current event questions should route to current responsible authorities or `ABSTAIN` when
KFM lacks released, time-fit evidence.
