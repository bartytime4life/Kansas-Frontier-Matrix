## 5. Source role, rights, and temporal fitness

The build plan names multiple source families because Logan County crosses scientific, conservation, public-access, agricultural, groundwater, transport, and hydrologic concerns. Those roles must remain distinct.

| Source role | What it may support after admission | What it must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| Land-manager / visitor-information source | Released public visitor context, restrictions, and official routing for a managed site. | Geological truth for unrelated claims, private-land permission, or a cached live-safety service |
| Geological / scientific source | Broad stratigraphy, geomorphology, fossil context, and scientific interpretation. | Access authority, collecting permission, property status, or real-time condition |
| Conservation / ecological source | Broad habitat and stewardship context after sensitivity review. | Public occurrence locator, nest finder, rare/endemic-plant concentration map, or access permission |
| Agricultural statistical source | County-scale aggregates with date and definition. | Person-, operator-, parcel-, liability-, or causal environmental inference |
| Groundwater / water-administration source | Administrative geography, aggregate resource context, or appropriately generalized monitoring. | Individual water-right standing, lawful use, private-well condition, compliance, or future-supply certainty |
| Transport / road source | Network or official public routing context appropriate to its declared date. | Current passability, closure, weather, emergency, or safe-travel verdict without current authority |
| Hydrologic observation source | Dated observation for an identified station or reach after admission. | Flood warning, emergency instruction, private-property implication, or unsupported county-wide condition |

### Temporal rule

Every visible claim should expose the time basis material to interpretation: source date, observation period, retrieval date, validity period, and correction/supersession state where applicable. A historic or scientific source can support historical or scientific context without supporting a current operational claim.

### Rights and redistribution

Public web availability is not equivalent to permission to ingest, cache, transform, redistribute, derive public geometry from, or package a source into KFM artifacts. The sibling plan's source seeds remain `NEEDS VERIFICATION` until source identity, terms, citation duty, derivative-use posture, storage, sensitivity, and correction obligations are recorded.

## 6. Fragile geoheritage, paleontology, and ecological boundary

### 6.1 Fragile landscape

A map can unintentionally become an instruction surface. Fine geometry, terrain derivatives, screenshots, exports, deep links, or camera presets can make a fragile landform easier to access or damage even when prose contains a warning. Public-safe review therefore applies to the **representation**, not only to the text.

Required posture for the first slice:

- use only approved public/generalized geometry;
- avoid hidden canyon, off-trail, erosion-vulnerable, or collecting-supporting detail;
- treat high-resolution terrain, LiDAR, imagery, 3D, and export paths as separate sensitivity decisions;
- preserve source role and visible limitation text;
- deny or generalize requests whose usefulness depends on harmful precision.

### 6.2 Fossils and geological resources

The plan's fossil-bearing context creates a clear anti-collapse rule: **scientific visibility is not collecting authority**. A future Focus Mode may explain that fossils are scientifically important and may cite released evidence, but it should not answer where to collect, where an exposed specimen is located, which private site is promising, or how to reach an unreleased location.

### 6.3 Ecology

Sensitive wildlife and plant information requires its own review. Broad habitat education may be public-safe; exact occurrences, nests, roosts, breeding areas, rare/endemic plant concentrations, or derivative clues may not be. If the system cannot prove the public-safe scale and transform, it should `ABSTAIN` or `DENY`.

### 6.4 3D and remote-sensing derivatives

A terrain mesh, hillshade, 3D scene, orthophoto, point cloud, or derived landform model is a carrier, not sovereign truth. It can also reveal more than a generalized vector layer. Future 3D or remote-sensing work therefore needs explicit representation review, source rights, sensitivity assessment, and export policy before release.

## 7. Groundwater, property, access, roads, and current safety

### 7.1 Groundwater and private wells

Administrative district membership or generalized groundwater context is not an individual water-right, pumping, potability, contamination, compliance, lawful-use, or future-supply determination. Private-well and property-level outputs are especially sensitive and require a separate competent authority and policy path.

### 7.2 Property, mineral, and access non-determination

KFM must not infer ownership, title, mineral interest, lease validity, royalty interest, easement, public access, permission to enter, liability, valuation, or tax consequence from map overlap, public imagery, geological context, or planning prose.

### 7.3 Road, trail, and visitor conditions

Static or periodically refreshed repository artifacts are not a live safety service. Requests such as "is the road open now?", "is the trail safe today?", "can I drive there after rain?", or "is a guided area accessible today?" require current official channels. Absent such evidence, the outcome is `ABSTAIN` with a safe redirect rather than an inferred answer.

### 7.4 Emergency and environmental-health claims

The first Logan slice should not become an incident feed, emergency alert system, contamination verdict, exposure assessment, or health advisory. Those functions require their own authoritative sources, temporal guarantees, policy, and review.

## 8. Finite outcomes and reason-code guidance

| Outcome | Logan County meaning | Required behavior |
|---|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | A narrow contextual claim resolves to released public-safe evidence and policy permission. | Show source role, time basis, limitations, and evidence-backed citation. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, currentness, rights, safe scale, or authority is insufficient. | State what is missing and provide only a verified safe redirect. |
| `DENY` | The request seeks harmful precision, protected-resource detail, off-trail/collecting support, private/legal inference, or disallowed sensitive content. | Return a non-leaking reason without revealing withheld detail. |
| `ERROR` | Required evidence, policy, resolver, contract, or runtime handling failed. | Fail closed; never turn operational failure into an answer. |
