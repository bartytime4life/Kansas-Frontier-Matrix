# Trego County Focus Mode Documentation Boundary

> **Purpose.** Explain the repository-present Trego County planning lane and the
> evidence, source-role, paleontology/geosite, cultural-resource, water-operations,
> property/access, recreation, agriculture, review, correction, and rollback
> conditions that must remain visible before any Trego County Focus Mode can become
> more than a proposal.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This directory contains planning documentation, not a released county product.**
> The sibling build plan proposes a Cedar Bluff Reservoir / Smoky Hill River / Smoky
> Hill Chalk proof slice. Neither that plan nor this README admits a source, resolves
> an `EvidenceBundle`, activates Focus policy, implements a governed API route,
> approves a map or AI answer, or creates promotion, release, deployment, or
> publication state.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Fossil-bearing and cultural landscapes require fail-closed precision.** Generalized
> geology and public-history context may be useful, but exact or inference-enabling
> fossil locality, collecting-site, fragile-geosite, historic-inscription,
> archaeological/cultural-resource, or private-access detail must not become a public
> discovery surface without qualified evidence, rights, sensitivity, and review.

> [!WARNING]
> **Reservoir and river context is not operational guidance.** Static planning material
> and dated source observations cannot establish current releases, lake levels,
> downstream safety, flood conditions, recreation rules, municipal-water conditions,
> or legal water-right status. Current questions must route to appropriate official
> authorities or fail closed.

## 4. Proposed Trego County proof slice

**PROPOSED thesis:** Trego County is a useful proof slice for demonstrating how geology/paleontology, water infrastructure and observations, recreation/public history, and county-scale agriculture can coexist without collapsing source authority or exposing harmful precision.

A first future governed slice should be intentionally narrow:

- orient users to Trego County and Cedar Bluff at generalized public-safe scale;
- explain Cedar Bluff Reservoir and Smoky Hill River as civic, hydrologic, and historical context without making operational claims;
- explain Smoky Hill Chalk and related geologic context while withholding collecting-enabling fossil locality precision;
- represent public-history context without exposing precise sensitive cultural-resource features;
- keep recreation context bounded by currentness and official-authority redirects;
- show agriculture only through suitable county-scale aggregates with explicit vintage and source role;
- expose source identity, time basis, limitations, evidence references, review state, and release state for every consequential claim;
- make `ABSTAIN` and `DENY` visible rather than replacing missing authority with fluent explanation;
- support correction and rollback for any future released artifact.

### Non-goals

This slice is not a fossil-collecting locator, geosite access guide, archaeological-site finder, historic-inscription locator, parcel/title/access service, dam-operations dashboard, flood or emergency service, current recreation-rule service, water-right advisor, private-well advisor, individual farm/operator profile, or unrestricted AI question-answering surface.

## 5. Paleontology, cultural-resource, and access boundary

> [!WARNING]
> **Exact paleontology/geosite precision fails closed.** Generalized scientific descriptions can be useful; coordinates, collecting-enabling locality descriptions, fragile exposed-bed detail, or inferred access paths require explicit sensitivity and release approval.

> [!WARNING]
> **Public-history context is not permission to disclose precise cultural resources.** Historic inscriptions, carvings, archaeological context, sacred or burial context, or similarly sensitive features must not become exact-location discovery surfaces merely because a public source mentions them.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Public access is not universal access.** Public recreation context does not establish ownership, title, legal access, collecting permission, private-road use, or authorization to enter adjacent land.

A future governed product should distinguish at least four dimensions:

| Dimension | Public-safe question | Fail-closed question |
|---|---|---|
| Geological context | What formations and broad landscape history characterize the area? | Where exactly can I collect fossils from exposed beds? |
| Paleontological context | What kinds of fossil-bearing formations are associated with the county? | Give me coordinates or directions to productive fossil localities. |
| Public history | What broad historical themes are associated with Cedar Bluff/nearby landscapes? | Show exact coordinates of inscriptions, artifacts, or vulnerable features. |
| Access | What official public-use resources exist? | Does this map prove I can enter, collect, cross, or use this property? |

## 6. Water operations, recreation, and public-safety boundary

Water sources and operational clocks must remain distinct.

| Source-role class | Safe future use | Unsafe collapse |
|---|---|---|
| USBR project/administrative context | General project purpose, role, and historical context at stated date | Current release, lake condition, flood, safety, or legal conclusion |
| Kansas Water Office operational notice | Dated official operational context when ingested through a governed current-authority flow | Evergreen release status or generalized downstream-safety conclusion |
| USGS monitoring source | Observation identity, method, time-series evidence where admitted and current | Alert, warning, dam-operation command, legal water-right interpretation |
| KDWP/local recreation source | General public-use context plus official-current redirect | Current rule, closure, access, hunting/fishing, boating, or facility-safety guarantee from stale material |
| Weather/emergency authority | Current-authority redirect | Cached KFM warning, evacuation, or safety verdict |

A future runtime should preserve observation/effective time, source publication time, retrieval time, KFM release time, and correction/supersession time where material.

## 7. Agriculture and source-role boundary

The build plan includes county-scale agriculture as contextual evidence. That role should remain aggregate-only.

- County totals may support a dated, source-attributed agricultural context card after source admission and release review.
- Aggregates must not be used to infer individual operators, parcel ownership, farm boundaries, private irrigation behavior, water-right standing, debt, income, or household characteristics.
- Agricultural statistics and reservoir/water context must remain separate source roles unless an explicit governed analysis establishes a lawful, evidence-supported relationship.
- A map layer, dashboard, or generated explanation cannot transform aggregate county data into parcel-level truth.

## 8. Finite outcomes and evidence closure

A future governed Trego runtime should converge on bounded outcomes rather than free-form confidence:

| Outcome | Trego County example |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Released evidence supports a generalized county, geology, reservoir-role, historic-context, or aggregate-agriculture statement and policy permits exposure. |
| `ABSTAIN` | User asks for current reservoir release, lake status, flood/safety state, recreation rule, water-right condition, or another current/legal determination without suitable released authority. |
| `DENY` | Request would reveal collecting-enabling fossil/geosite precision, sensitive cultural-resource locations, private/property detail, infrastructure vulnerability, or prohibited internal paths. |
| `ERROR` | Evidence resolution, policy evaluation, citation validation, or another required trust component fails. |

No `ANSWER` should be authoritative unless its consequential claims resolve through `EvidenceRef` to an admissible `EvidenceBundle` and the required policy/release state permits use.

Candidate Trego-specific reason codes from the sibling plan remain **PROPOSED vocabulary** until accepted contract/policy/validator surfaces register them.
