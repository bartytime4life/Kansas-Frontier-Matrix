## Proposed proof slice

**PROPOSED thesis:** Rice County can become a strong county proof slice by showing how agriculture, water, roads, settlements, and cultural-history context can be combined without collapsing distinct source roles or exposing unsafe precision.

The first slice should be deliberately narrow:

- orient users to Rice County's Arkansas River / Cow Creek corridor and major settlement/transport anchors;
- show county-scale agriculture context with explicit vintage and aggregate scope;
- present floodplain and groundwater context as dated, source-bounded interpretation rather than operational or legal determination;
- distinguish observed statistics from interpreted hydrogeologic or historical context;
- generalize or withhold cultural-resource locations that could be sensitive;
- avoid parcel-owner, title, private-well, infrastructure-vulnerability, or household inference;
- expose source identity, source role, date basis, limitations, and release state;
- demonstrate a visible Evidence Drawer path for consequential claims;
- expose correction and rollback state when any future public artifact is released.

### Non-goals

This slice is not a flood-warning service, groundwater-safety tool, legal floodplain determination, parcel/title interface, emergency-routing product, private-well advisor, archaeological-site finder, unrestricted cultural-location map, or unrestricted AI question-answering surface.

## Source roles and temporal fitness

Source role must remain explicit. Different source families support different claim classes and clocks.

| Source-role class | Safe use in a future governed slice | Unsafe collapse |
|---|---|---|
| Agriculture aggregate | County-scale contextual metric after admission | Producer, parcel, owner, water-use, or property inference |
| Hydrology/floodplain authority | Generalized, dated orientation and source routing | Current emergency status or individualized legal determination without the proper authority path |
| Groundwater/geology publication | Scientific/historical context with publication date and limitations | Current private-well safety, potability, yield, or property-specific conclusion |
| Transportation/county project source | Dated corridor/project context | Current route-safety or emergency access claim without released operational evidence |
| Cultural-history/public interpretation source | Generalized public narrative and period context | Exact archaeological, sacred, burial, restricted, or culturally sensitive location disclosure |
| Parcel/appraisal source | Potential internal reference under strict policy | Title truth, owner profile, property-access permission, or household inference |
| Map or tile | Downstream carrier of released public-safe geometry | Source authority or evidence by itself |
| AI-generated explanation | Bounded interpretation over released evidence | Root truth, source admission, policy decision, or release authority |

Time semantics matter. Every future visible claim should distinguish observation/effective time, source publication or update time, retrieval time, and KFM release time where material.

## Public safety, rights, and sensitivity

The linked plan surfaces several high-consequence boundaries that must remain fail closed.

> [!WARNING]
> **Cultural-resource precision:** exact archaeological, sacred, burial, or restricted cultural locations must not become a public discovery tool merely because public narratives discuss the broader history.

> [!WARNING]
> **Flood and emergency boundary:** floodplain maps and hydrology sources do not authorize current emergency, evacuation, road-safety, or individualized legal floodplain conclusions in Focus Mode.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Groundwater and private-property boundary:** generalized groundwater context must not be converted into well-specific safety, ownership, title, access, or property-value conclusions.

> [!WARNING]
> **Infrastructure boundary:** public road, bridge, utility, or facility context must not become vulnerability analysis or operational-security detail.

Public availability does not itself establish KFM redistribution rights, geometry authority, transform permission, public-safe precision, or policy approval.
