# Edwards County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Restore standard README discovery for the Edwards County lane,
> orient maintainers to its planning artifact, make its evidence and
> sensitive-context boundaries visible, and route future work through KFM
> source, evidence, policy, review, release, correction, and rollback controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation or release evidence.**
> After this filename repair, the directory contains this README and one Edwards
> County build plan. Their presence prevents accidental duplicate planning; it
> does not establish source admission, semantic correctness, validator success,
> runtime behavior, release, deployment, or publication.

> [!CAUTION]
> **This is a bounded filename repair, not a Focus tree migration.** The prior
> one-byte file was literally named `README,md`, so standard README discovery
> failed. This change replaces it with `README.md` in the existing
> `docs/focus-mode/counties/edwards_county/` compatibility lane. Accepted
> Directory Rules v2 governs placement; proposed ADR-0027 does not authorize a
> plural, kebab-case migration or a parallel writable lane.

> [!WARNING]
> **The plan combines public administrative, statistical, water, floodplain,
> transportation, agricultural, and historical context with high-consequence
> boundaries.** Water-right, private-well, potability, property/title/access,
> live flood or road-safety, exact archaeology or burial, sacred-site, sensitive
> ecology, living-person, and infrastructure-vulnerability detail must not reach
> an ordinary public client. Missing evidence, rights, safe resolution, policy,
> or review closure must produce `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR`, not a fluent
> inference.

## 4. Proposed Edwards County proof slice

The build plan proposes a public-safe evidence cockpit centered on Edwards
County and Kinsley orientation, Arkansas River Valley place context,
county-scale agriculture and irrigation aggregates, dated transportation
context, and public history. It treats the difficult boundary as a feature of
the proof: useful water, land, floodplain, transport, and historical context
must not become legal, property-specific, live-safety, or harmful-precision
advice.

That is a **planning thesis**, not a verified current product.

| Proposed element | Evidence needed before reliance | Non-effect of this README |
|---|---|---|
| County, community, and map orientation | Registered authoritative geography source, vintage, geometry identity, rights, and released public-safe carrier | Does not declare a current boundary artifact or public layer |
| Kinsley and public identity context | Admitted municipal or institutional source, time basis, rights, citation closure, and correction route | Does not validate every civic, historical, service, or tourism claim |
| Arkansas River Valley context | Separate geographic, hydrologic, regulatory, observation, historical, and derived source roles; safe scale; valid time; limitations; policy | Does not establish a water right, groundwater availability, well condition, potability, flood determination, or emergency state |
| Agriculture and irrigation at county scale | Admitted aggregate source, definitions, reporting period, transform lineage, privacy review, and citations | Does not authorize farm, field, operator, household, private-well, parcel, or present-condition inference |
| Transportation context | Dated authoritative records, temporal semantics, supersession behavior, limitations, rights, and public-safe geometry | Does not report current traffic, closure, detour, bridge, airport, facility, or road safety |
| Public history and trail context | Authoritative institutional or historical sources, cultural review, rights, scope, citations, safe geometry, and correction path | Does not expose exact archaeology, trail traces, burials, sacred places, access routes, or private-property detail |
| Flood-map redirect | Current responsible-authority link, time basis, role label, and explicit non-reliance copy | Does not create a KFM floodplain determination, insurance, permitting, evacuation, or safety service |
| Map and AI interpretation | Released public-safe carriers, resolvable `EvidenceBundle`s, finite policy outcomes, governed runtime, correction, and rollback support | Does not create a layer, route, model capability, or public answer |

Do not copy source payloads, exact sensitive locations, or proposed machine
objects into this directory to simulate completion. The county lane should
reference governed objects in their owning roots only after those objects exist
and are reviewable.

## 5. Public safety and governed outcomes

| Request or output class | Bounded posture | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Released, broad, time-labeled county context with resolving citations | `ANSWER` only when evidence, policy, review, and release conditions pass | Keeps the result within admitted public-safe evidence |
| Missing, stale, conflicting, overly coarse, rights-unclear, or unresolvable evidence | `ABSTAIN` | Fluency cannot substitute for evidence, source role, currentness, or permission |
| Water-right ownership, priority, impairment, allocation, availability, or legal interpretation | `DENY` and route to the responsible official or legal authority | Geographic and irrigation context cannot establish a legal water conclusion |
| Private-well condition, potability, contamination, yield, safety, or property-level groundwater conclusion | `DENY` and route to the responsible testing or health authority | A county map and public records are not individualized health or engineering evidence |
| Parcel owner, individual tax, title, easement, access, property value, eligibility, liability, or other person/property-specific inference | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN`, as accepted policy requires | Public availability does not authorize aggregation, inference, or unsafe reliance |
| Exact or reconstructive trail trace, archaeology, cemetery, grave, burial, sacred, or protected cultural location | `DENY` | Cultural sensitivity, sovereignty, land access, and site protection require fail-closed handling |
| Exact ecological occurrence, nest, roost, den, sensitive habitat, or other harmful-precision detail | `DENY` or reviewed generalization | Prevents harm and reconstruction through maps, search, exports, caches, identifiers, or AI |
| Current flood, weather, fire, emergency, evacuation, road, bridge, airport, utility, or facility status | `ABSTAIN` and route to the responsible live authority | Focus Mode is not an operational or emergency authority |
| NG911, response, address, water, transport, utility, or other infrastructure-vulnerability detail | `DENY` | Public context does not authorize harmful operational inference |
| Cross-source inference of causation, regulatory status, engineering fitness, ownership, or legal responsibility | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` | Separate contextual sources do not jointly prove the inferred claim |
| Resolver, policy, carrier, citation, or runtime failure | `ERROR` with no unsupported fallback claim | Failure must remain visible and fail closed |

Client-side hiding is not a sensitivity control. Protected detail must not be
delivered to an ordinary public client, included in public logs or analytics,
or reconstructed from tiles, filters, search, exports, caches, identifiers, or
AI responses.

## 6. Source-role separation

The build plan names candidate source families. A named or publicly visible
source is not thereby registered, admitted, current, rights-cleared, or fit for
every claim.

| Candidate family recorded by the plan | Narrow candidate role | Must not be collapsed into |
|---|---|---|
| Edwards County or City of Kinsley public pages | Administrative navigation and self-description, after current verification | Broad historical proof, service availability, property status, emergency truth, or derivative-display permission |
| U.S. Census products | Time-labeled statistical aggregates with dataset cautions | Living-person profiles, exact current conditions, or county administrative authority |
| USDA NASS county products | Reporting-period agricultural aggregates | Farm, field, operator, private-well, present-crop, or current-facility truth |
| Kansas Historical Society or other reviewed institutional history | Scoped historical reference and public interpretation | Access permission, title, exact archaeology, burial or sacred-site location, or complete historical authority |
| Kansas water or floodplain authorities and FEMA | Regulatory-source identification or official redirect, depending on admitted object and vintage | KFM water-law opinion, well advice, property-specific flood determination, or live emergency guidance |
| KDOT or another admitted transportation authority | Dated non-operational project or network context | Current closure, detour, bridge, airport, route safety, or infrastructure-vulnerability advice |
| KGS, USGS, NRCS, ecology, preservation, parcel, or local-history candidates | Distinct scientific, statistical, cultural, property, or interpretive roles only after review | Mutual substitution, person/property inference, unsafe precision, or unsupported causal synthesis |
| Generated summaries or AI responses | Explanation subordinate to released evidence and policy | Evidence, source authority, policy, approval, or release proof |

Every consequential public statement needs a source role, valid-time basis,
allowed claim scope, resolvable evidence, policy outcome, citation, correction
route, and released public-safe carrier. Conflicts remain visible; they are not
smoothed into one synthetic answer.

## 7. Evidence and readiness boundary

An Edwards County public result must remain downstream of the governed KFM
path:

1. identify a source once in the source-authority surface;
2. review authority, rights, access, time, geography, sensitivity, source role,
   allowed claims, disallowed claims, and correction behavior;
3. admit candidate material to `RAW`, or hold it in `WORK / QUARANTINE` when
   requirements are unresolved;
4. derive and validate `PROCESSED` material with transform and provenance
   lineage;
5. resolve every consequential `EvidenceRef` to an `EvidenceBundle` and apply
   policy;
6. make separate promotion and release decisions with correction and rollback
   targets;
7. serve only released public-safe artifacts through governed interfaces;
8. keep map rendering and AI interpretation subordinate to evidence and
   decision state.

The smallest legitimate follow-up is a **planning-evidence reconciliation**, not
live ingestion:

1. review the build plan against current repository paths, current county
   inventory, and accepted Directory Rules, then remove only claims proven
   stale;

   water/floodplain, transportation, and public-history candidates by authority,
   rights, cadence, sensitivity, source role, correction behavior, and allowed
   public use before any activation;

   and schema authorities rather than creating parallel object homes;
4. define exact negative outcomes for water rights, private wells and potability,
   parcel ownership, title and access, exact trail/archaeology/burial/sacred
   locations, ecological occurrences, live flood and travel claims, NG911 and
   infrastructure detail, and uncited generated language;
5. run repository-native focused and aggregate checks, then record introduced
   versus inherited failures;
6. keep live source activation, public geometry, release, deployment, and
   publication outside that packet.

This README does not authorize that follow-up; it states the dependency-closed
