# Haskell County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Haskell County planning lane, make its
> water-right, lawful-use, groundwater-currentness, private-operation,
> operational-precision, and evidence boundaries visible, and route future work
> through KFM source, evidence, policy, review, release, correction, and rollback
> controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation, adjudication, approval,
> or release evidence.** This directory contains this README and one Haskell
> County build plan. Their presence prevents accidental duplicate planning; it
> does not establish source admission, current water-right standing, lawful use,
> semantic correctness, validator success, runtime behavior, release,
> deployment, or publication.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains a compatibility question.** This same-path update repairs
> an existing README under `docs/focus-mode/counties/haskell_county/`.
> Accepted Directory Rules v2 governs placement; proposed ADR-0027 describes a
> plural, kebab-case control plane but remains unaccepted. No file is moved,
> renamed, copied into a parallel tree, or declared canonical here.

> [!CAUTION]
> **This is an intentional stacked documentation change.** Draft PR #3255 owns
> the currently open Hamilton edit to `COUNTY_INDEX.md`; this Haskell slice is
> based on that exact head so it preserves Hamilton's row and adds only Haskell's
> row. Merge or retarget the stack in dependency order.

> [!WARNING]
> **Administrative water data is not a current water-right or lawful-use
> determination.** The linked plan proposes bounded, time-labeled High Plains
> aquifer, groundwater-management, and aggregate irrigation context. KFM must
> not infer whether any right is current or valid, whether it was lawfully used,
> whether a private well or farm is secure, or whether water is available now
> or in the future.

> [!WARNING]
> **Private and operational inference must fail closed.** Individual water
> records, diversion points, private wells or farms, parcel or operation detail,
> current drought or supply advice, and vulnerability-oriented precision must
> not reach an ordinary public client. Missing authority, evidence, rights,
> safe geometry, temporal fitness, policy, or review closure requires `ABSTAIN`,
> `DENY`, or `ERROR`, not a fluent inference.

## 4. Proposed Haskell County proof slice

The build plan proposes a public-safe learning slice organized around three
related but non-interchangeable concerns:

1. **Haskell County orientation** — public geographic context without parcel,
   private-well, farm, household, right-holder, or operation inference.
2. **High Plains aquifer and groundwater-management context** — regional and
   district-scale explanation without a current or future local supply,
   private-well, farm, or parcel conclusion.
3. **Administrative-data fitness and non-determination** — a prominent boundary
   separating dated public records and official routing from current water-
   right standing, lawful use, legal advice, and operational guidance.

### Proposed first-slice surfaces

| Proposed surface | Intended value | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `WaterDataFitnessNotice` | Explain the plan-recorded as-of, currentness, lawful-use, and official-redirect limitations before any administrative-water answer | No current-standing, lawful-use, entitlement, compliance, or legal conclusion |
| Haskell County orientation frame | Establish the county scope for a future public-safe map | No parcel, well, farm, household, operation, right-holder, or individual inference |
| Southwest Kansas groundwater-management context card | Explain the plan's proposed administrative geography and source role | District membership and dated context only; no right, private-operation, or current-supply inference |
| High Plains aquifer context card | Provide regional explanatory context with scale, uncertainty, and time labels | No local well condition, current supply, future availability, drought action, or property conclusion |
| District-level aggregate irrigation/use card | Teach aggregation, geography, and time scope | No silent downscaling to Haskell, a farm, a right, a well, or an operation |
| Agricultural statistics candidate | Add county-level aggregate context only after a fit-for-purpose query, suppression, rights, and evidence review | No producer, parcel, farm, household, water-use, or causal profile |
| Soil context candidate | Add versioned soil interpretation only after area, rights, and scientific-limit review | No land value, compliance, yield, suitability, or water-availability verdict |
| Official-current redirect card | Route present drought, emergency, supply, or right-standing questions to the responsible authority | Link-out and checked-at state only; no cached or generated operational verdict |
| Evidence Drawer and boundary panels | Expose source role, time basis, limitations, evidence, review, and reason codes | Unresolved evidence, policy, review, or release state cannot be polished into an answer |

These are **PROPOSED planning surfaces**. No card, layer, payload, source
descriptor, evidence bundle, policy decision, route, or released artifact is
confirmed by this README.

### Deferred planning surfaces

- county agricultural statistics until a specific query and suppression review
  are admitted;
- soil survey extraction until authoritative area, version, rights, and
  interpretation limits are recorded;
- groundwater observations, trends, models, forecasts, or future-availability
  products;
- effective drought, emergency, restriction, or current-supply information;
- detailed groundwater-management or administrative map assets;
- any source containing point, operation, right-holder, well, farm, parcel,
  household, or vulnerability-relevant detail.

Each deferred item needs source identity, authority, rights, version or
effective state, geometry fitness, sensitivity review, public-safe
transformation, evidence closure, policy, and release support before display.

### Excluded or denied by default

- current water-right standing, validity, lawful use, priority, entitlement,
  allocation, permit, compliance, or legal advice;
- names, addresses, right-holder identity, diversion points, well locations,
  parcel identifiers, private farms, households, or operation detail;
- site-specific well condition, depletion, security, productivity, acquisition,
  suitability, or economic conclusions;
- current drought, supply, emergency, restriction, or action guidance;
- definite future groundwater availability or depletion dates;
- aggregate downscaling into individual, farm, right, well, or operation claims;
- vulnerability-oriented or otherwise harmful operational precision;
- direct public access to `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, restricted source
  material, ungoverned observations, or model output.

## 5. Administrative water and source-role boundary

### Authority before synthesis

The build plan names state water-administration, joint administrative-data-
limitation, regional aquifer, agricultural-statistics, and soil-science sources
as candidates with distinct roles. That planning inventory is not proof of
admission, currentness, rights, complete authority scope, safe transformation,
review, or release.

Before any consequential Haskell card can be released, implementation must
establish at minimum:

- exact source identity, publisher, role, version, as-of or effective time, and
  authority limitation;
- allowed public claim scope and prohibited legal, current-supply, future-
  availability, private-operation, and operational-precision inferences;
- rights, attribution, excerpting, caching, screenshot, export, and derivative-
  display obligations;
- safe geography, scale, aggregation, generalization, withholding, and
  reconstruction tests;
- appropriate water-administration, legal-boundary, currentness, privacy,
  sensitivity, accessibility, security, and release review;
- correction, withdrawal, supersession, cache-invalidation, and rollback
  behavior.

### Source-role anti-collapse

| Candidate source role named by the plan | May support after admission and review | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| Water-administration and groundwater-management authority candidate | Institutional responsibility, public administrative geography, and official routing | A KFM water-right, lawful-use, entitlement, allocation, permit, compliance, or legal determination |
| Administrative-data limitation candidate | Data-fitness notice, temporal limitation, non-determination, and redirect behavior | A replacement water-right record, current-standing decision, or evidence that a right was lawfully used |
| Regional aquifer context candidate | Scale-bounded High Plains aquifer explanation | A local well condition, farm outcome, present supply, future availability, or drought instruction |
| District-level aggregate candidate | Dated, explicitly scoped regional or district context | A Haskell-specific value or farm, right, well, parcel, household, or operation claim |
| Agricultural-statistics candidate | Reviewed county-scale aggregate after admission | Producer, farm, parcel, household, pumping, causation, blame, or compliance profile |
| Soil-science candidate | Versioned, area-bounded scientific context after admission | Land value, yield, compliance, suitability, groundwater, or private-operation verdict |
| Official-current redirect candidate | Safe route to a responsible current authority | Cached alert, restriction, emergency, drought, supply, or legal answer |
| Generated KFM narrative | A bounded summary of resolved, released, policy-safe evidence | Evidence, authority, current standing, legal judgment, review, policy, or release state |

### Legal, currentness, and private-operation boundary

Public administrative or scientific material may be informative while still
being unfit for an individual or current decision. A record, management-district
page, aquifer explanation, aggregate, soil interpretation, map feature, or
observation must not be combined into current standing, lawful use, private-
well condition, farm security, property suitability, present supply, future
availability, compliance, causation, or responsibility.

When a user asks for a current or individual determination, the public product
must return a finite reason code and a safe official route when one is available.
It must not fill an evidence gap with a model inference, map proximity, regional
average, stale record, or cross-source correlation.

### Generated-language boundary

AI may summarize only an already resolved, policy-allowed, review-supported,
released public-safe evidence bundle. It cannot:

- determine current water-right standing, lawful use, entitlement, allocation,
  permit status, compliance, or another legal question;
- predict current or future well, farm, groundwater, drought, supply, property,
  or economic conditions;
- infer an individual, right-holder, well, farm, parcel, household, or operation
  from a district aggregate, map, record, or contextual source;
- decide source rights, temporal fitness, precision, policy, review, promotion,
  or release;
- use hidden reasoning or model confidence as evidence.

## 6. Public safety and governed outcomes

### Finite outcomes

| Outcome | Haskell County use | Required visible state |
|---|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | A bounded public-safe orientation, source-role, data-fitness, regional aquifer, district-context, or aggregate response is supported by released evidence and policy | Evidence references, source roles, time basis, limitations, review/release state, and correction route |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, authority, as-of state, freshness, geography, rights, or scope is insufficient; a current, future, or unsupported contextual conclusion is requested | Stable reason code, evidence gap, official redirect where safe, and no answer-like fallback |
| `DENY` | The request seeks right-standing or lawful-use determination, private or operational detail, legal or current guidance, restricted content, or trust-membrane bypass | Stable reason code and safe alternative without leaking protected detail |
| `ERROR` | A required schema, evidence resolver, policy evaluator, citation check, release record, or governed service failed | Stable error code; no permissive answer, raw-data fallback, or direct model path |

| Current drought, supply, or operational advice | `OFFICIAL_CURRENT_GUIDANCE_REQUIRED` | `ABSTAIN`; route to a fit official current source |
| Operational precision | `OPERATIONAL_PRECISION_NOT_ADMITTED` | `DENY`, exclude, or reviewed generalization only |
| Aggregate downscaling | `AGGREGATE_TO_PRIVATE_INFERENCE` | `DENY` |
| Source, evidence, or temporal gap | `SOURCE_CURRENTNESS_UNVERIFIED`, `SOURCE_ROLE_COLLAPSE_REQUESTED`, `EVIDENCE_REF_UNRESOLVED` | `ABSTAIN`, quarantine, or validation failure |
| Trust membrane | `PUBLIC_INTERNAL_LIFECYCLE_ACCESS`, `AI_NOT_EVIDENCE` | `DENY`, `ABSTAIN`, or validation failure |
| Review and release closure | `REQUIRED_REVIEW_NOT_RECORDED`, `PUBLICATION_GATE_INCOMPLETE` | Block display, promotion, or publication |

These codes are plan-derived candidates. They are not confirmed as the
repository's canonical reason-code registry and must be reconciled before
machine use.

### Required negative paths

A credible Haskell proof must include deterministic, no-network negative
fixtures showing at least:

1. a current water-right standing conclusion fails closed;
2. an administrative record cannot prove lawful use;
3. a dated record without its as-of and limitation state cannot appear current;
4. private-well, farm, parcel, household, or operation detail is denied;
5. a district or regional aggregate cannot be downscaled to Haskell or an
   individual operation without separately admitted evidence;
6. regional aquifer context cannot become a local well or future-supply forecast;
7. dated context cannot become current drought, emergency, restriction, or
   supply guidance;
8. unreviewed operational precision cannot enter a public card or layer;
9. generated narrative cannot serve as evidence;
10. unresolved evidence, rights, time, review, correction, or rollback cannot
    produce `ANSWER` or a public-release claim;
11. public clients cannot read non-published lifecycle lanes.

### Map, API, and AI boundary

A public map or Focus answer must consume a governed, public-safe projection.
It must never use the county planning directory as a data source and must never
treat a selected feature, map extent, badge, schema pass, model response,
administrative record, or Markdown claim as evidence truth.

The build plan's proposed `WaterDataFitnessNotice`, Evidence Drawer, and time-
basis surfaces are useful only when they project real evidence, policy, review,
and release objects. UI polish without those dependencies must remain
explicitly demonstrative.

### Evidence chain required for a public claim

```text
admitted source identity, role, time, and rights
  -> public-safe aggregate, generalized extract, or limitation notice
  -> EvidenceRef
  -> resolved EvidenceBundle
  -> water-administration / currentness / privacy / precision review
  -> PolicyDecision
  -> CitationValidationReport
  -> accountable ReviewRecord
  -> PromotionDecision and ReleaseManifest

  -> public-safe map / Evidence Drawer / Focus response
```

Any missing material link narrows the output to `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, `ERROR`, or
