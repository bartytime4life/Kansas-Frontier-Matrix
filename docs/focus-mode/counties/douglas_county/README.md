## Trust and public-safety boundary

The working trust path remains:

```text
SourceDescriptor
  -> SourceIntakeRecord
  -> EvidenceRef
  -> EvidenceBundle
  -> governed claim or layer candidate
  -> policy and sensitivity decision
  -> review and release decision
  -> public-safe carrier
```

An `EvidenceRef` must resolve to an `EvidenceBundle` before a consequential claim is presented as authoritative. A map marker, Atlas Card, timeline event, generated answer, screenshot, test, pull request, or release-shaped draft does not replace that requirement.

| Request or condition | Public default | Evidence needed to change the default |
|---|---|---|
| Unresolved, missing, conflicted, stale, or out-of-scope evidence | `ABSTAIN` or `ERROR` | Resolved evidence bundle with identity, scope, time, provenance, review, and correction lineage |
| Private student, household, family, or living-person records | `DENY` | No ordinary public override; use a separately governed restricted workflow if lawful and necessary |
| Exact burial, sacred, archaeological, or culturally sensitive locations | `DENY`, generalize, or quarantine | Documented authority, sensitivity review, purpose limitation, precision control, and release decision |
| Haskell or Indigenous-education claims lacking culturally appropriate review | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` | Source-specific evidence plus documented cultural and sensitivity review |
| Traumatic-history claim without source-role or public-memory framing | `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, limitations, perspective, review, and correction path |
| Private addresses, household profiling, or school/university security details | `DENY` | No public disclosure without a separately authorized, lawful, minimum-necessary route |
| Assessor or parcel data presented as title truth | `ABSTAIN` | Authoritative title evidence and appropriate legal/contextual review; this README supplies neither |
| River or floodplain output presented as current regulatory truth | `ABSTAIN` | Current authoritative source, effective date, jurisdiction, layer role, and limitations |
| Current emergency, law-enforcement, or critical-infrastructure detail | `DENY` or generalize | Explicit operational authority, safety review, and public-release decision |
| AI-generated text offered as evidence | `ABSTAIN` | Underlying evidence bundle; generated language remains a downstream carrier |
| Public client attempts to read `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, canonical/internal stores, or direct model output | `DENY` | None for the ordinary public path; use governed APIs or released public-safe artifacts |

The finite public runtime outcomes proposed by current Focus documentation are `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, and `ERROR`. They are response states, not evidence, review, release, or publication states.

## Source roles must remain distinct

Douglas County material can combine sources that answer different questions. Do not collapse them into one authority.

| Source role | May support | Must not be treated as |
|---|---|---|
| Official county, city, university, or agency record | Its scoped administrative or institutional claim at a stated time | Complete historical truth, cultural authority, title proof, or permission to expose restricted records |
| Primary archive or contemporaneous record | Evidence about a bounded event, actor, or period | Self-interpreting, complete, unbiased, or automatically public-safe evidence |
| Museum, heritage-area, marker, or public-history interpretation | Curated context and source routing | A substitute for primary evidence or the only valid community narrative |
| Haskell or Indigenous institutional/community source | Public-safe institutional or community-framed context within its authority | Permission to generalize cultural knowledge, private records, or sensitive locations |
| University or library catalog | Discovery, collection description, and citation routing | Permission to reproduce content or expose student/living-person data |
| USGS or other observed hydrologic source | Measurement or observed feature within stated method and time | Regulatory flood determination, prediction, or timeless condition |
| FEMA or other regulatory flood source | Defined regulatory layer for a stated version and jurisdiction | Real-time flood condition, legal advice, or all-purpose environmental truth |
| Derived map, model, index, or AI summary | Reproducible interpretation with inputs and limitations | Sovereign evidence, official record, policy decision, or release approval |
