# Morris County Focus Mode Documentation Boundary

> **Purpose.** Explain the repository-present Morris County planning lane and
> the evidence, cultural-sovereignty, sensitive-place, hydrology, ecology,
> property, review, correction, and rollback conditions that must remain visible
> before any Morris County Focus Mode can become more than a proposal.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This directory contains documentation, not a released county product.**
> The sibling build plan and this README do not admit a source, create an
> `EvidenceBundle`, establish cultural authority, activate county policy,
> implement a governed API route, approve a map or AI answer, or create release
> or publication state.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains a compatibility HOLD.** Accepted ADR-0029 adopted the
> current Directory Rules v2 bytes and made `docs/doctrine/directory-rules.md`
> the writable human placement authority. This README stays at the existing
> singular, snake_case path. ADR-0027 remains proposed and cannot authorize a
> second plural/kebab county lane or a migration through inference.

> [!WARNING]
> **Cultural sovereignty and sensitive-place protection outrank interpretive
> convenience.** The sibling plan proposes Council Grove, the Neosho River,
> Council Grove Lake, the Flint Hills, Santa Fe Trail context, Kaw Mission, and
> Kaw/Kanza and Osage history. KFM must not turn public-history interpretation,
> generalized maps, archaeological or cultural references, or generated language
> into Nation-authoritative cultural truth, precise sacred/burial/archaeological
> location disclosure, living-person or property profiling, hunting or wildlife
> optimization, or live water-operations guidance.

## 4. Proposed Morris County proof slice

The sibling plan proposes a public-safe county view centered on **Council Grove,
the Neosho River and Council Grove Lake, Flint Hills working lands, Santa Fe
Trail context, and Kaw/Kanza cultural history**. Its central governance test is
whether KFM can be useful without converting living sovereignty, sensitive
geography, public-history interpretation, or operational data into unrestricted
map truth.

### Plan-defined public-safe themes

| Theme in the sibling plan | Bounded future use | Required limit |
|---|---|---|
| Council Grove civic and historic context | County/city-scale orientation and reviewed public-history context | No assumption that public-history interpretation is complete cultural authority |
| Neosho River and Council Grove Lake | General hydrographic/reservoir relationship and time-bounded observations | No emergency alert, navigation, flood-safety, water-rights, or reservoir-operations advice |
| Kaw Mission / Kaw/Kanza history | Reviewed public interpretation with explicit authority labels | No generated Nation voice; no sensitive-place precision; expanded representation requires appropriate Nation-authoritative evidence/review |
| Santa Fe Trail / historic district | Public historical-geography context | No inference of unrestricted access, artifact location, or culturally complete interpretation |
| Council Grove Wildlife Area | Generalized ecological and public-recreation context | No precise sensitive occurrence, nesting, collection, closure, or vulnerability detail |
| Flint Hills / geology | Educational landform/geology context with source scale and interpretation limits | No site-access, engineering, extraction, or hazard assurance |
| Agriculture / working lands | County-level aggregate context | No farm, producer, family, owner, parcel, or causation inference |
| County parcel data | Boundary reference only for first slice | No title, valuation, owner, living-person, or legal conclusion |

### Product and authority boundary

A future Morris Focus Mode may become map-rich and evidence-visible only after the
relevant objects are admitted, validated, policy-reviewed, and released. Until
then, every product concept in the plan remains a proposal.

The minimum trust order is:

```text
scope
  -> resolve source and identity
  -> resolve EvidenceRef to EvidenceBundle
  -> evaluate rights, sovereignty, sensitivity, time, and public-safe scale
  -> apply policy and accountable review
  -> emit ANSWER / ABSTAIN / DENY / ERROR
  -> release only through governed state transition
```

AI may summarize the resulting released evidence. It cannot decide cultural
meaning, source authority, sensitive-place status, legal access, property truth,
water safety, wildlife vulnerability, or release state.

## 5. Public safety, sensitivity, and finite outcomes

### Cultural-sovereignty and sensitive-place rules

| Request or condition | Default outcome before proof closure | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| General public history supported by reviewed released evidence | `ANSWER` when scope and authority labels are clear | Bounded interpretation may be public-safe |
| “What does this place mean to the Kaw Nation?” without Nation-authoritative support | `ABSTAIN` | KFM must not manufacture Nation voice |
| Exact sacred, burial, archaeological, artifact, reinterment, or culturally restricted location | `DENY` | Sovereignty, cultural sensitivity, rights, and harmful precision |
| Request to infer a sensitive site from public layers or cross-layer clues | `DENY` | Derived precision can be as harmful as direct disclosure |
| Public-history source conflicts with Nation-authoritative evidence | `ABSTAIN` or narrower answer pending review | Authority conflict must remain visible |
| Rights or consultation posture is unclear | `ABSTAIN` / `HOLD` | Unknown rights and authority fail closed |

### Hydrology, ecology, property, and operational limits

| Request or condition | Default outcome before proof closure | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Static released river/lake context | `ANSWER` | Bounded educational context |
| Current flood, navigation, reservoir-release, or emergency guidance | `DENY` / route to official authority | KFM is not an operational alert or reservoir-control system |
| Exact sensitive wildlife occurrence or nesting/roosting location | `DENY` or generalized answer | Geoprivacy and ecological vulnerability |
| Hunting/collection optimization using sensitive occurrence data | `DENY` | Harmful downstream use |
| Parcel owner, title, valuation, person, family, or living-person profile | `DENY` for first slice | Property/privacy/legal boundary |
| County-level agriculture aggregate with released evidence | `ANSWER` | Aggregate statistical context |
| Farm-level causation or owner-specific inference | `ABSTAIN` / `DENY` | Unsupported and potentially disclosive |
| Resolver, policy engine, evidence service, or runtime dependency failure | `ERROR` | Never fall back to unsafe allow |

### Finite outcome vocabulary

- `ANSWER` — released evidence is in scope, authority-resolved, policy-safe,
  citation-valid, and appropriately generalized;
- `ABSTAIN` — evidence, authority, rights, identity, time, review, or release
  closure is insufficient;
- `DENY` — the request crosses a sovereignty, sensitive-place, harmful-precision,
  ecological, property/privacy, legal, safety, or operational boundary;
- `ERROR` — a resolver, validator, policy engine, adapter, or runtime dependency
  failed.

These are **design outcomes**, not proof that current Morris County runtime
behavior exists.

## 6. Source role, rights, and currentness boundary

The sibling plan records source seeds checked on 2026-05-22. This README does not
refresh those sources and does not convert them into admitted KFM authority.

| Source family named by the plan | Intended planning role | What it cannot prove by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Kaw Nation official material | Preferred authority for Kaw cultural framing and public cultural representation when appropriately reviewed | Complete authority for every Osage or other Nation claim; source admission, redistribution rights, or KFM release |
| National Park Service / Kansas historical interpretation | Public-history and trail/historic-site context | Nation-authoritative cultural meaning; sensitive-place release permission |
| U.S. Army Corps of Engineers | Council Grove Lake project/recreation/public administration context | Cultural authority, health authority, or permission for unnecessary operational exposure |
| U.S. Geological Survey | Time-stamped Neosho River observation/source context | Flood warning, reservoir operation, health, or legal authority |
| Kansas Department of Wildlife and Parks | Wildlife-area and public ecological/recreation context | License to expose sensitive occurrences, closures, management intelligence, or vulnerability detail |
| Kansas Geological Survey | Geology and mapping context | Site access, cultural authority, engineering assurance, or property truth |
| USDA/NASS | County-level agricultural aggregates | Farm/person identity or parcel-level causation |
| Morris County parcel/appraisal surfaces | Administrative/property reference context | Title truth, owner profiling, living-person inference, or first-slice public need |

Before any source becomes operational, verify at minimum:

- canonical source identity and authority role;
- current endpoint/product/version and retrieval method;
- rights, redistribution, attribution, and derivative-display obligations;
- sovereignty and consultation requirements where applicable;
- sensitivity and safe spatial/temporal resolution;
- valid/observed/source/retrieval time where consequential;
- deterministic source and object identity;
- source-to-evidence mapping and citation form;
- correction, supersession, and withdrawal behavior.

If those cannot be established, quarantine, narrow, abstain, deny, or keep the
source at planning-seed status.

## 7. Evidence and readiness boundary

A Morris County Focus Mode is not ready because a README, map mockup, schema,
fixture, validator, branch, or pull request exists. For a consequential public
claim, readiness should be reconstructable through the trust chain.

### Minimum consequential-claim closure

1. The user and spatial/temporal scope are bounded.
2. Source role and deterministic entity/place identity are resolved.
3. `EvidenceRef` resolves to an admissible `EvidenceBundle`.
4. Rights, sovereignty, cultural sensitivity, harmful precision, ecological
   sensitivity, property/privacy, and public-safe scale are evaluated.
5. Policy returns a finite decision with obligations.
6. Required accountable human or Nation-authoritative review is complete where
   consequence warrants it.
7. Citations resolve to the evidence actually supporting the claim.
8. Any public layer or artifact has integrity and provenance appropriate to its
   significance.
9. Release state, correction path, supersession lineage, and rollback target exist.
10. The client exposes negative, stale, restricted, corrected, or withdrawn states
    rather than silently hiding them.

If any required step is unresolved, the correct result is narrower scope,
`ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, `ERROR`, quarantine, or continued planning—not a confident
county narrative.

### What passing tests would not prove

Even a future green test suite would not by itself prove:

- cultural authority or consultation sufficiency;
- source truth or rights clearance;
- that sensitive geometry is safe for public exposure;
- historical completeness;
- current water or wildlife conditions;
- public-release approval;
- deployed parity;
- that an AI interpretation is authoritative.

The smallest useful implementation after this README is **not** a live source
connector or public cultural map. It is a no-network synthetic proof of the highest

contract, schema, policy, fixture, validator, and review surfaces are verified
