> **Purpose.** This directory is the current human planning lane for a proposed Geary County Focus Mode. It connects the sibling build plan to KFM evidence, military public-context, hydrologic and reservoir, sensitive-location, public-interface, validation, release, correction, and rollback boundaries without claiming that the planned product exists.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Documentation is not a release.** Neither this README nor the sibling plan admits a source, resolves an `EvidenceRef`, creates a governed payload, approves public geometry, deploys a route, or publishes a county experience.

> **Geary planning combines civic, military public-context, reservoir, dam, river, recreation, transport, ecological, cultural, and map themes with potentially sensitive detail.** Operational military or infrastructure detail, private personnel data, live safety guidance, and exact protected ecological or cultural locations must fail closed until evidence, rights, time, geometry, policy, and accountable review are complete.

## Proposed Geary proof slice

The sibling plan names a **Geary County Focus Mode Control Plane** as its recommended first milestone. That name and priority remain `PROPOSED`. A bounded, fixture-first proof slice would separate civic orientation, military public-context, water-system roles, time, evidence, and public-safety decisions:

| Candidate element | Proposed role | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Geary County and Junction City orientation | Broad administrative and civic map frame | Registered geography, vintage, rights, geometry identity, and no property or legal inference |
| Fort Riley public context | Community-facing geography and public history | Public-affairs or public-history sources, no operational detail, no personnel inference, safe geometry, and accountable review |
| Milford Lake and dam public context | Reservoir, recreation, and flood-control orientation | Separate operator, observation, forecast, regulatory, recreation, and emergency roles; no vulnerability or live-operation inference |
| Republican and Smoky Hill river context | Hydrologic and confluence orientation | Distinguish descriptive, observed, modeled, regulatory, forecast, emergency, and derived roles |
| Kansas River formation context | Time- and place-aware explanatory layer | Claim-level evidence, uncertainty, scale, citation, and no current safety or causal overreach |
| Recreation context | Public-use orientation | Responsible current-source routing, accessibility, time, closures, and explicit non-alert behavior |
| Interstate, highway, rail, and historical military-road context | Movement and settlement context | Dated evidence, public-safe resolution, no current routing, access, security, or closure authority |
| Flint Hills edge, riparian, and lake ecology | Generalized environmental context | Admitted source, derivation, season, safe scale, limitations, and no harmful-precision disclosure |
| Evidence Drawer, time controls, atlas cards, and governed answers | Downstream interpretive surfaces | Resolving EvidenceBundles, finite outcomes, governed API, public-safe carriers, release, correction, and rollback support |

The plan describes relationships with adjacent county context, including Riley County, but cross-county product priority, duplication rules, and shared ownership remain `NEEDS VERIFICATION`. The first implementation should prove evidence resolution and negative behavior before it attempts a persuasive map experience.

## Geary County public-safety boundary

| Request or theme | Default posture until closure | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Released, broad, time-labeled county context with resolving citations | `ANSWER` only after evidence, policy, review, and release pass | Keeps claims bounded to admitted public-safe evidence. |
| Missing, stale, conflicting, overly coarse, or unresolvable evidence | `ABSTAIN` | Fluency cannot substitute for evidence, source role, or currentness. |
| Restricted Fort Riley operation, training, security, personnel, deployment, facility, access, or infrastructure detail | `DENY` | Community context does not authorize operational or vulnerability-relevant disclosure. |
| Private military, veteran, family, household, health, service, or personnel record or inference | `DENY` | Public context does not authorize aggregation, identification, or inference about people. |
| Dam, reservoir, utility, water-system, emergency-response, or infrastructure vulnerability detail | `DENY` | Descriptive public context must not become an operational threat surface. |
| Live boating, weather, flood, evacuation, incident, reservoir-operation, closure, or emergency instruction | `ABSTAIN` and route to the responsible live authority | Planning and historical layers are not operational authority. |
| Exact ecological occurrence, nest, protected habitat, burial, sacred, or archaeological location | `DENY` or reviewed generalization | Prevents reconstruction through map, search, export, cache, or AI surfaces. |
| Parcel ownership, access, boundary, title, insurance, liability, engineering, or eligibility conclusion | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` | County-scale and public records do not prove a site-specific legal or technical decision. |
| Claim with an unresolved `EvidenceRef` | `ERROR` | Missing resolution must remain visible; no unsupported fallback. |
| Public request reaching `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, restricted, or direct-model content | `DENY` | Public clients use governed APIs or released public-safe artifacts only. |

These are documentation-level boundaries, not active policy. They require agreement in the canonical policy surface, evaluator, fixtures, review, runtime binding, and released carrier before any public behavior can be claimed.

## Source-role anti-collapse

A future Geary slice must keep source roles explicit rather than combining every official or public page into one undifferentiated authority.

| Candidate source family implied by the plan | Permitted planning role | Must not be treated as |
|---|---|---|
| County, municipal, or other administrative geography source | County frame, place identifiers, civic context, and vintage-specific boundary context | Title, ownership, military, emergency, reservoir-operation, ecological, cultural, or release authority |
| Military public-affairs or public-history source | Approved community-facing context within its published remit | Operational security, personnel, deployment, access, facility, vulnerability, or unrestricted precision authority |
| Reservoir or dam operator public information | Role-specific public project, recreation, or historical context | Engineering assessment, vulnerability disclosure, live operating condition, emergency instruction, or universal water authority |
| River observation, model, forecast, floodplain, and emergency sources | Distinct observed, derived, forecast, regulatory, or protective roles | Interchangeable evidence, property determination, causal proof, or cross-role substitution |
| Recreation and wildlife sources | Public-use or ecological context within stated scope and time | Guaranteed access, real-time safety, emergency, harmful-precision, or legal permission authority |
| Transportation or historical records | Dated network, route, rail, settlement, or movement context | Current routing, closure, bridge safety, military access, private access, or emergency-travel authority |
| Cultural, Indigenous, archaeological, ecological, or sensitive-location authority | Review, standing, and safe-scale decisions within its remit | Detail that administrative sources, generated text, or ordinary map layers may disclose by substitution |
| Generated narrative, cards, maps, and AI output | Downstream interpretation over released evidence | Evidence, policy decision, review approval, current condition, or publication authority |

Public availability, official branding, coordinates, or map detail does not by itself authorize KFM to cache, transform, tile, spatially amplify, combine, or release source material.

## Trust path and finite outcomes

A public Geary County claim would need to follow the established KFM trust direction:

```text
source candidate
  -> RAW capture
  -> WORK / QUARANTINE review
  -> PROCESSED role-aware object
  -> EvidenceRef -> EvidenceBundle
  -> CATALOG / TRIPLETS projection
  -> policy + validation + review + release decision
  -> PUBLISHED public-safe artifact
  -> governed API
  -> Explorer presentation
```

The map, UI, county index, catalog, AI response, and this README are downstream or interpretive surfaces; none is sovereign truth.

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Evidence, policy, citation, time, precision, release, and consumer requirements all permit the bounded claim. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, freshness, authority, rights, geometry, cultural standing, currentness, or release support is insufficient. |
| `DENY` | The request conflicts with military, infrastructure, sensitivity, privacy, property, safety, rights, trust-membrane, or release controls. |
| `ERROR` | Required object shape, resolver, validator, policy evaluator, or governed service failed; do not degrade into an unsupported answer. |

`HOLD` remains a governance or workflow state rather than a public answer.
