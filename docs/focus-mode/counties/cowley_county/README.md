# Cowley County Focus Mode Documentation Boundary

> **Purpose.** This directory is the current human planning lane for a proposed Cowley County Focus Mode. It connects the sibling build plan to KFM evidence, floodplain-status, property, cultural-location, infrastructure, validation, release, correction, and rollback boundaries without claiming that the planned product exists.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Documentation is not a release.** Neither this README nor the sibling plan admits a source, resolves an `EvidenceRef`, creates a governed payload, approves public geometry, deploys a route, or publishes a county experience.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Path convergence remains on HOLD.** The tracked singular, snake_case lane is current repository fact. ADR-0027 describes a proposed plural, kebab-case control plane; it is not accepted authority for a move, rename, or parallel tree.

> [!WARNING]
> **Cowley planning includes safety-, property-, and culturally significant themes.** Effective and draft floodplain context, parcel/title questions, current hazard information, infrastructure detail, archaeology or sacred places, and private operations must fail closed until source role, rights, time, precision, policy, evidence, and accountable review are complete.

## Cowley County public-safety boundary

| Request or failure | Default posture until closure | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Treat draft flood-risk-review material as effective or final regulatory floodplain truth | `ABSTAIN` or `ERROR` | Draft and effective authorities, dates, and legal effects must remain distinct |
| Decide whether a parcel is in a regulatory floodplain or what action a person must take | `ABSTAIN`; route to the responsible current authority | KFM context is not a parcel-specific regulatory, insurance, engineering, or legal determination |
| Give current flood, evacuation, road, bridge, weather, or emergency guidance | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY`; route to official current services | A planned county layer is not an alerting or emergency-operations system |
| Present parcel geometry, assessment data, ownership, title, address, or access as authoritative | `DENY` | Mapping context is not title truth and may expose living-person or private-property detail |
| Infer farm, producer, operator, field, or private activity from county aggregates or land-cover data | `DENY` | Aggregate and derived land-cover sources do not support individual or parcel claims |
| Expose exact archaeology, burial, sacred, ceremonial, tribal-sensitive, or undisclosed historic locations | `DENY`, or use a separately reviewed coarse narrative | Sovereignty, cultural sensitivity, and looting or reconstruction risk |
| Expose exact rare-species, habitat, nesting, roosting, or other sensitive ecology | `DENY`, or use an approved coarse generalization | Public detail may create ecological harm or reconstruct protected locations |
| Convert historical geology or groundwater material into a present well, water-safety, engineering, property, or mineral-right claim | `ABSTAIN` or `ERROR` | Historical/scientific context cannot silently become current operational or legal truth |
| Show operationally useful bridge, rail, utility, flood-control, airport, or other infrastructure vulnerability detail | `DENY` or apply a reviewed public-safe generalization | Public orientation does not require vulnerability exposure |
| Answer from an unresolved `EvidenceRef`, missing citation, inactive policy, unreleased artifact, or direct model output | `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR` according to the failed gate | Fluent generation cannot replace evidence, policy, review, or release closure |
| Public client reaches RAW, WORK, QUARANTINE, an unpublished candidate, canonical store, or ungoverned source | `DENY` and correct the trust-boundary failure | Public consumers must use governed interfaces or released public-safe artifacts |

Generalization, aggregation, suppression, redaction, or delay is a governed transform, not a client-side styling trick. A future public carrier must retain the source, transform, reason, policy/review, release, correction, and rollback lineage appropriate to its risk.

## Source-role anti-collapse

A future Cowley slice must keep source roles explicit rather than combining every official page or map into one undifferentiated authority.

| Source family from the plan | Permitted planning role | Must not be treated as |
|---|---|---|
| Cowley County or municipal GIS and mapping disclaimers | Administrative orientation, local routing, and stated map limitations | Automatic reuse permission, title truth, regulatory flood determination, or evidence closure |
| Kansas Floodplain Viewer or FEMA mapping | Effective floodplain reference within verified jurisdiction, edition, and date | Live warning, legal advice, or automatic authority over a differently scoped draft product |
| Kansas Department of Agriculture flood-risk-review material | Draft or project-status context when current status is verified | Effective map, final regulatory determination, or silent replacement of another authority |
| Municipal floodplain information | Local explanation and routing within its stated scope | County-wide or federal authority, parcel determination, or emergency warning |
| State or federal agricultural statistics and land-cover products | Dated county aggregate or classified land-cover context | Farm, producer, owner, parcel, field, or private-operation truth |
| Kansas Geological Survey material | Scientific, geologic, groundwater, or historical interpretation within source limits | Current water safety, well condition, engineering suitability, mineral-right, or property fact |
| Kansas transportation project material | Dated public project context and official routing | Live traffic, work-zone, emergency, vulnerability, or perpetual current-state authority |
| Public historical or museum material | Reviewed public narrative and source discovery | Archaeology authority, cultural clearance, designation proof, or permission to expose protected locations |
| Generated narrative, cards, maps, and AI output | Downstream interpretation over released evidence | Source evidence, policy decision, review approval, or publication authority |

A source’s public availability, official branding, coordinates, or map detail does not by itself authorize KFM to cache, transform, tile, spatially amplify, combine, or release it.

## Trust path and finite outcomes

A public Cowley County claim would need to follow the established KFM trust direction:

```text
source candidate
  -> RAW capture
  -> WORK / QUARANTINE review
  -> PROCESSED role-aware object
  -> EvidenceRef -> EvidenceBundle
  -> CATALOG / TRIPLETS projection
  -> policy + validation + review + release decision
  -> PUBLISHED public-safe artifact
  -> governed API
  -> Explorer presentation
```

The map, UI, county index, catalog, AI response, and this README are downstream or interpretive surfaces; none is sovereign truth.

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Evidence, source role, rights, citation, time, precision, policy, review, release, and consumer requirements all permit the bounded claim. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, currentness, authority, rights, status, geometry, or release support is insufficient. |
| `DENY` | The requested disclosure or use conflicts with property, cultural, ecological, infrastructure, safety, rights, sensitivity, or release controls. |
| `ERROR` | Required object shape, resolver, validator, policy evaluator, or governed service failed; do not degrade into an unsupported answer. |

`HOLD` remains a governance and workflow state rather than a public answer. The plan’s planning statuses do not replace the finite outward outcome set.
