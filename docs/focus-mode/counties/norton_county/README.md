## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The build plan records official-source checks from 2026-05-24. Those sources
remain **dated planning seeds** here, not newly verified or admitted KFM
sources.

| Source role carried by the plan | What it may support after admission | What it must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| KDWP park information | Stable park identity, general public context, and dated visitor information. | Current service/access/safety guarantee. |
| KDWP wildlife-area/refuge material | Management-area context and source-native regulatory information at its stated date. | A present refuge boundary or current hunting-legal conclusion without fresh authority. |
| Bureau of Reclamation reservoir data | Time-stamped reservoir observations at the exact source timestamp. | A real-time or “today” value without freshness validation. |
| KDHE HAB/public-health material | Official public-health advisory context when current and in scope. | Independent KFM health clearance or a substitute for current official advisories. |

### Required temporal fields for future use

Where material, preserve separately:

1. observation time;
2. publication/update time;
3. retrieval time;
4. validity interval;
5. release time;
6. correction/supersession time.

A source title containing “current” is not itself a freshness guarantee.

<a id="6-refuge-reservoir-recreation-and-public-safety-boundaries"></a>

## 6. Refuge, reservoir, recreation, and public-safety boundaries

### Refuge boundaries and hunting regulations

The build plan records that the wildlife-area source describes a refuge line
that changes with water level. A future KFM surface must therefore treat
refuge geometry as dynamic management information. Cached geometry cannot be
presented as a current legal boundary unless the governing source, timestamp,
policy, and release state support that exact claim.

### Reservoir values and boating/access

Reservoir observations must stay attached to their source timestamps. KFM must
not infer boating access, launch usability, park water availability, or present
recreational conditions from a stale or merely recent water-level observation.

### Lake health and harmful algal blooms

Health/advisory questions require current authoritative public-health evidence.
A historical or stale advisory can support historical explanation only. When a
current health or contact-risk question cannot be supported, KFM should
`ABSTAIN` and direct users to current official channels.

### Wildlife and ecological sensitivity

Generalized wildlife-area or habitat context may be suitable for public release
after review. Exact sensitive occurrences, nesting/roosting/den locations, or
other harmful-precision ecological details remain fail closed.

### Water-supply and allocation meaning

A reservoir elevation or storage value is not a municipal-water condition,
allocation decision, water-right determination, drought declaration, or legal
supply conclusion. Those require their own source roles and authority.

## 7. Finite outcomes and candidate reason codes

Future Norton County runtime behavior should preserve KFM's four terminal
outcomes:

| Outcome | Norton County use |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Released, in-scope evidence supports the bounded claim and all freshness/citation/policy checks pass. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence is missing, stale, too coarse, temporally unfit, or current authority is required. |
| `DENY` | Rights, exact sensitive location, public-safety, legal/regulatory, or prohibited operational detail blocks the request. |
| `ERROR` | Evidence resolution, validation, policy, manifest, or runtime machinery fails. |

The build plan also proposes county-specific reason codes. They remain
**PROPOSED planning vocabulary** until current contracts, schemas, policy, and
fixtures register them. Examples include:

- `REFUGE_BOUNDARY_REQUIRES_CURRENT_KDWP_SOURCE`;
- `CURRENT_REGULATION_STATUS_REQUIRES_AUTHORITY`;
- `RESERVOIR_DATA_TEMPORAL_FITNESS_UNRESOLVED`;
- `CURRENT_ACCESS_OR_BOATING_REQUIRES_AUTHORITY`.

Do not emit these as current runtime facts merely because they appear in the
plan.
