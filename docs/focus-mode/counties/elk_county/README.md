## 4. Proposed county proof slice

The build plan proposes a trust proof in which informative scenic and civic context remains useful without acquiring authority it does not possess:

1. **Scenic geology** — generalized Elk Falls, Pennsylvanian sandstone, Elk River, historic bridge, and mill context.
2. **Cross-county scope** — Chautauqua Hills interpretation must preserve its Elk-to-Montgomery County corridor boundary.
3. **Rural civic GIS restraint** — county appraiser and parcel-related material may explain administrative roles without becoming ownership, title, access, valuation, or personal-profile truth.
4. **Operational separation** — Public Works and Emergency Management pages may route users to competent authorities without becoming current-condition, compliance, water-safety, infrastructure, or emergency evidence.

| Proposed question | Bounded planning answer | Required hold |
|---|---|---|
| What could the scenic lane explain? | Generalized, evidence-visible waterfall, sandstone, bridge, mill, river, and Chautauqua Hills context. | No present access, collecting, route, bridge-condition, or safety conclusion. |
| How could a county service card help? | By explaining the public role and routing destination of an official department. | No title, valuation, road/bridge condition, environmental compliance, or emergency-status conclusion. |
| What could appraiser GIS establish? | At most the administrative and tax-purpose role described by an admitted source. | No owner identity, title, legal boundary, access right, individualized value, or 911-address output. |
| What could a public-works surface establish? | Service categories and official routing, with a checked date and explicit limitations. | No current condition, vulnerability, inspection, permit, well/septic compliance, or water-safety answer. |
| What could an emergency card do? | Route a user to a verified official-current channel. | No cached alert, incident status, protective-action advice, or KFM travel decision. |
| What could the map carry? | Released, generalized, public-safe contextual layers only. | No direct internal-store, parcel-linked, operational-infrastructure, sensitive-wildlife, or current-emergency path. |

## 6. Time, currentness, and official authority

Stable interpretation, dated administrative description, and live operational status are different claim classes.

| Claim class | Minimum time fields | Public posture |
|---|---|---|
| Geological interpretation | Source version or publication identity, retrieval time, scope, and correction state. | May become contextual only after admission and citation closure. |
| Historic bridge or mill interpretation | Event or historical period, source publication/version, retrieval time, and uncertainty. | Public-history context only; never present condition or access proof. |
| County department role | Page/version identity, retrieval time, effective or reviewed date when available, and official destination. | Role and routing only; recheck before reliance. |
| Parcel, appraisal, or GIS content | Dataset/as-of time, tax or administrative purpose, privacy and rights state, and correction behavior. | Not admitted by this README; public title, access, valuation, and personal outputs remain denied. |
| Road, bridge, well, septic, environmental, or infrastructure state | Observation/effective/expiry time plus competent current authority. | No KFM conclusion from the planning sources. |
| Emergency, weather, fire, or travel status | Current official issue time, effective interval, expiry, supersession, and jurisdiction. | Redirect or abstain; KFM is not the alert origin. |

### Currentness rules

- The plan's checked date proves only when its author observed the named pages; it does not prove those pages or facts are current now.
- Stable scientific interpretation still requires source identity, rights, scope, and correction review.
- A historic bridge reference never proves that the structure is open, safe, accessible, or suitable for crossing today.
- Department responsibility does not prove current staffing, response, road status, infrastructure condition, permit disposition, compliance, or water safety.
- A redirect must be verified at use time; a cached link or copied alert is not official-current guidance.
- If a claim's time fitness is unresolved, the result is `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR`, not a plausible narrative.

## 7. Public-safe and sensitive boundaries

| Claim or data family | Public-safe candidate | Restricted, denied, or abstained form | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elk Falls and sandstone | Generalized admitted scientific context with visible limitations. | Exact access directions, private-entry implication, collecting permission, or unsupported condition claim. | `ABSTAIN` until admitted; then bounded `ANSWER`. |
| Bridge and mill history | Time-attributed public-history context. | Present structural condition, opening, crossing, ownership, or access statement. | `ABSTAIN` for current condition. |
| Chautauqua Hills corridor | Generalized scenic geology with explicit Elk/Montgomery scope. | Current route safety, passability, or Elk-only scope inflation. | `ABSTAIN` when geometry or currentness is unresolved. |
| Appraiser and parcel GIS | Agency-role and tax-purpose limitation notice. | Owner identity, title, legal boundary, access right, person-linked valuation, contact, household, or 911-address detail. | `DENY`. |
| Roads, bridges, culverts, utilities, and wind energy | Generalized service category or non-operational context after review. | Condition, vulnerability, inspection finding, precise critical-facility topology, or attack-enabling detail. | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN`. |
| Wells, septic, lagoons, and environmental health | Department-routing context with explicit non-determination. | Permit, code compliance, contamination, environmental-health, or individual water-safety judgment. | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN`. |
| Wildlife | Broad, reviewed, generalized context when rights and sensitivity permit. | Occurrence, nesting, denning, hunting target, rare-species, or stewardship-sensitive precision. | `DEFER` or `DENY`. |
| Emergency and road/weather status | Verified official-current redirect category. | KFM alert, incident determination, protective-action advice, or travel-safety decision. | `ABSTAIN`. |

### Public-surface membrane

Any future public surface must consume only governed APIs or released public-safe artifacts. It must not read `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, canonical internal stores, direct county GIS, operational infrastructure data, private records, source screenshots, or unconstrained model output as its normal path.

The minimum safe visible state includes:

- source identity and role;
- evidence-resolution state;
- checked, effective, and expiry times where applicable;
- rights, privacy, sensitivity, and spatial-generalization posture;
- county and cross-county scope;
- access, title, current-condition, compliance, wildlife, and emergency limitations;
- review, release, correction, and rollback state;
- a finite `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR` outcome.
