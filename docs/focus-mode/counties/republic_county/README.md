# Republic County Focus Mode Documentation Boundary

> **Purpose.** Explain the repository-present Republic County planning lane and
> the cultural-sovereignty, sensitive-place, water-governance, evidence, review,
> correction, and rollback conditions that must remain visible before any Republic
> County Focus Mode can become more than a proposal.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This directory contains documentation, not a released county product.**
> The sibling build plan and this README do not admit a source, create an
> `EvidenceBundle`, activate county policy, implement a governed API route,
> approve a map or AI answer, or create release or publication state.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains a compatibility HOLD.** Accepted ADR-0029 adopted the
> current Directory Rules v2 bytes and made `docs/doctrine/directory-rules.md`
> the writable human placement authority. This README stays at the existing
> singular, snake_case path. ADR-0027 remains proposed and cannot authorize a
> second plural/kebab county lane or a migration through inference.

> [!WARNING]
> **Cultural sovereignty and water governance are the defining public-safety
> boundaries.** The sibling plan proposes Pawnee cultural-landscape context,
> Republican River monitoring, flood mapping, irrigation administration,
> agriculture, and geology. KFM must not turn dated planning sources, public map
> layers, or generated language into Nation-authoritative cultural meaning,
> sensitive-place discovery, burial/sacred-place location, archaeological guidance,
> individual water-right conclusions, current flood-safety advice, pumping
> instructions, infrastructure-operation detail, or private farm/property inference.

## 4. Proposed Republic County proof slice

The sibling plan proposes a **cultural-sovereignty + water-governance proof slice**.
Its central question is whether KFM can explain the Republican River corridor,
county-scale agriculture and geology, and carefully reviewed public-history context
while refusing to become a sensitive-place locator, cultural authority, legal water-right
engine, live flood-warning system, irrigation-operations guide, or private-property profile.

### Plan-defined public-safe themes

| Theme in the sibling plan | Bounded future use | Required limit |
|---|---|---|
| Pawnee cultural-landscape context | Reviewed, already-public broad orientation and source-role explanation | No inferred cultural meaning, sacred/burial site mapping, archaeological precision, artifact-finding, or Nation impersonation |
| Republican River observations | Timestamped hydrologic context when separately governed | No water-right, flood-safety, permanent-condition, or legal inference |
| Flood mapping | Effective/status-aware public context only after authority verification | No emergency guidance; no project/review layer represented as final regulatory status |
| Water administration and irrigation | Generalized administrative context | No individual water-right conclusion, pumping instruction, private-land operations, or vulnerability analysis |
| Agriculture | County-level aggregate statistical context | No farm, operator, person, family, parcel, or private-operation inference |
| Geology | Educational source-labeled county context | No groundwater suitability, construction, resource-development, or engineering assurance |

### Product and authority boundary

A future Republic County Focus Mode may become map-rich and evidence-visible only after
relevant sources and transforms are admitted, validated, policy-reviewed, culturally
reviewed where required, released, and connected to correction and rollback. This README
creates none of those states.

## 5. Cultural sovereignty, water, and sensitive-place boundaries

### Cultural and archaeological boundary

Public context may explain that the sibling plan identifies Pawnee cultural-landscape
considerations and calls for appropriate Nation-authoritative evidence and review. It must
not infer or expose:

- sacred, religious, burial, reinterment, archaeological, artifact, village, trail, battlefield, or traditional-use locations beyond an explicitly reviewed public-safe representation;
- hidden or approximate locations reconstructed from multiple public layers;
- culturally restricted meaning from generated text or secondary interpretation;
- site-likelihood rankings, collection guidance, looting-enabling detail, or subsurface inference;
- a claim that KFM, a museum, a trail marker, or a state/federal interpretation source speaks for the Pawnee Nation or another Nation.

### Water-governance boundary

KFM may eventually present released contextual evidence about the Republican River,
monitoring, flood mapping, irrigation districts, and state water administration. It must not:

- determine whether a person or parcel has a water right;
- issue pumping, diversion, irrigation, allocation, compliance, or legal instructions;
- treat a USGS observation as a legal threshold or permanent water condition;
- turn project/review flood mapping into current emergency guidance;
- expose unnecessary infrastructure topology, operations, vulnerabilities, or private-land details;
- replace current KDA, FEMA, USGS, local administrator, emergency-management, or legal authority.

### Cross-layer reconstruction risk

Even generalized layers can become sensitive when combined. A future release review must
consider whether public history, terrain, hydrography, parcel context, trails, imagery,
ecological data, and archaeological hints could reconstruct a restricted cultural or private
location. If reconstruction risk remains material, public precision must be reduced or the
layer withheld.

### Finite outcomes

| Outcome | Republic County use |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Released evidence is in scope, identity/time resolved, culturally and policy reviewed where required, citation-valid, and public-safe. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, source fitness, currentness, cultural review, geometry authority, or release closure is insufficient. |
| `DENY` | The request seeks sensitive cultural/archaeological detail, burial/sacred-place location, legal water-right interpretation, private-operation inference, infrastructure vulnerability, or another prohibited output. |
| `ERROR` | A resolver, validator, policy engine, adapter, or runtime dependency failed; do not fall back to plausible prose. |

## 6. Source-role, rights, and currentness boundary

The build plan names source families with different authority roles. Their presence in the plan
does not establish current admission, terms, derivative-use permission, or public-release fitness.

| Source family | Intended planning role | Must not be treated as |
|---|---|---|
| Pawnee Nation / appropriate Nation-authoritative source | Cultural framing, consultation context, authoritative public cultural material where explicitly available | Permission to infer, generalize, or expose restricted cultural knowledge |
| Kansas Department of Agriculture / Division of Water Resources | Water administration, mapping-project, MDS, irrigation-district context | Individual legal advice, pumping permission, operational command, or emergency authority |
| U.S. Geological Survey | Time-stamped river observations and scientific hydrologic context | Water-right decision, legal threshold, flood warning, or permanent condition |
| FEMA / flood authorities | Effective flood-risk products where applicable | KFM emergency alert or property-specific insurance/legal determination |
| Kansas Geological Survey | Geology and landform interpretation with scale/vintage limits | Cultural authority, water-right authority, engineering assurance, or private-resource recommendation |
| USDA NASS | County aggregate agriculture statistics | Farm/operator/person/parcel profiling or private-operation truth |
| KDOT / county public context | Public transportation and administrative orientation | Live routing, vulnerability intelligence, title/property truth, or permission to expose private details |
| State/federal public-history sources | Public historic interpretation | Nation-authoritative cultural meaning or permission to reveal sensitive sites |

Before public use, verify source identity, authority role, terms, rights, redistribution,
retrieval time, validity time, sensitivity, citation obligations, and correction behavior.
