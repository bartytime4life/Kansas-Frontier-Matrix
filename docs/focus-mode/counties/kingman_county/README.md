# Kingman County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to Kingman County's planning lane and preserve
> the build plan's Cheney Reservoir, State Park, recreation-currentness,
> water-health, wildlife/refuge, critical-infrastructure, county-service,
> property, burn, road, weather, emergency, rights, and evidence boundaries
> without turning planning material into current operational truth.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation or release evidence.**
> This compatibility directory contains this README and one Kingman County build
> plan. Their presence establishes planning lineage only; it does not admit a
> source, resolve evidence, activate policy, validate a payload, deploy a
> product, or publish county knowledge.

> [!WARNING]
> **Do not use this lane for live park, water, access, or safety decisions.** It
> cannot tell a user whether a campsite or facility is available, whether a
> ramp, dock, marina, swimming area, or water body is safe, whether algae or a
> health advisory is active, whether hunting or fishing is lawful, whether a
> road is safe, whether burning is allowed, or whether an emergency is active.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Sensitive and operational precision fails closed.** Exact wildlife/refuge,
> nesting, roosting, dam, intake, treatment, water-supply, vulnerability,
> parcel, owner, title, access, residence, or other reconstructive detail must
> not reach an ordinary public client without accepted policy, bounded evidence,
> accountable review, and a released public-safe transform.

## 4. Proposed county proof slice

The build plan proposes a compact, map-first demonstration organized around:

- generalized Kingman County orientation;
- Cheney Reservoir and State Park context without live facility or safety
  conclusions;
- explicit expiry for dated park operational notices;
- separation among park context, boating/fishing rules, algae advisories,
  fish-consumption advice, drinking-water authority, and water-supply operations;
- generalized waterfowl-refuge and wildlife context;
- withholding of dam, intake, treatment, and water-supply vulnerability detail;
- county civic routing for departments, notifications, tax/payment, and burn
  information without title, access, owner, or current-legality conclusions;
- an official-current weather/hazard redirect rather than a KFM warning;
- visible evidence, source-role, finite-outcome, correction, and rollback
  boundaries.

Every item above is `PROPOSED`. The README confirms only that the plan contains
the proposal—not that source admission, geometry review, policy, fixtures,
runtime, UI, release, or public delivery exists.

### Observable boundary for any future slice

A future implementation must remain one dependency-closed review boundary and
must prove, with synthetic or admitted evidence, that:

1. stable reservoir/park context is independent of live facility status;
2. publication, check, effective, retrieval, and expiry times remain distinct;
3. water-health, recreation-regulation, water-supply, county, weather, and AI
   source roles cannot collapse into one authority;
4. public clients use governed APIs or released public-safe artifacts only;
5. EvidenceRef resolves to EvidenceBundle before consequential claims display;
6. exact wildlife/refuge, property, and infrastructure detail fails closed;
7. live safety, water-health, access, burn, road, weather, and emergency
   questions do not receive stale or inferred answers;
8. correction and rollback remain separate from commits and merges.

### Minimum source-admission questions

Before any plan source supports a public claim, verify:

1. What is the stable source identity and responsible authority?
2. What exact claim scope can the source support?
3. What publication, reporting, effective, retrieval, and expiry times apply?
4. What rights, terms, citation, caching, and derivative-display constraints
   apply?
5. What health, ecological, property, privacy, security, or harmful-precision
   restrictions apply?
6. Which geometry source, generalization, and public-safe transform apply?
7. Which stale, outage, supersession, correction, and rollback behavior applies?
8. Can every EvidenceRef resolve to a reviewed EvidenceBundle?

### Anti-collapse rule

Public visibility is not authority. A county portal is not title or emergency
truth; a park description is not a live facility feed; a park notice is not
water-health authority; a regulation page is not personalized legal advice; an
algae advisory is not a universal drinking-water conclusion; a weather office
is not a KFM-authored warning; and AI output is not evidence.

## 6. Reservoir, park, recreation, and currentness

### Minimum necessary display

Any future public representation should prefer generalized county and
reservoir/park context, office-level redirects, visible source time, and minimum
necessary geometry. It must avoid access-enabling or vulnerability-ready detail.

| Request pattern | Finite outcome | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| “What public context does the plan describe for Cheney State Park?” | `ANSWER` only with admitted evidence | General context; no current availability, rules, closure, water quality, conditions, or safety. |
| “Is a campsite, cabin, restroom, water service, ramp, dock, or marina available now?” | `ABSTAIN` | Requires current official facility authority and an unexpired governed record. |
| “Is it safe to swim, launch, boat, or use this facility today?” | `ABSTAIN` | Requires current qualified park, water, weather, and emergency authority. |
| “Can I hunt or fish this exact place today?” | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` | Never infer season, license, regulation, closure, refuge status, permission, ownership, or access. |
| “Can I burn or drive this route safely right now?” | `ABSTAIN` | Requires current official burn, road, weather, and emergency authority. |
| “Show the best hidden access route or vulnerable operating point.” | `DENY` | Access-enabling and vulnerability-ready detail is prohibited. |

### Currentness contract

A dated notice must carry source identity, publication/check time, effective
window, expiry, retrieval time, correction state, and release state. Missing or
expired currentness data suppresses the notice or produces `ABSTAIN`; it never
falls back to a cached answer.

### Official redirect is not an answer

A redirect communicates where current authority may reside. It does not assert
that the target is available, current, applicable to the user, or already
resolved by KFM.

## 7. Water health, wildlife, property, infrastructure, and emergencies

| Topic | KFM non-determination | Required future authority |
|---|---|---|
| Blue-green algae, fish consumption, recreation water, or drinking water | No present advisory, potability, exposure, diagnosis, or universal safety conclusion. | Current qualified KDHE, utility, health, or other responsible authority with exact scope and expiry. |
| Wildlife/refuge context | No exact refuge-use, nesting, roosting, occurrence, closure, hunting, or access-enabling precision. | Accepted ecological/geoprivacy policy and accountable wildlife/refuge review. |
| Hunting and fishing | No personalized legality, season, license, permit, method, possession, area, refuge, closure, ownership, or permission conclusion. | Current responsible regulatory authority and qualified legal/access review. |
| Dam, intake, treatment, or water-supply infrastructure | No operational status, configuration, weak point, outage, capacity, security posture, or tactical geometry. | Separately governed infrastructure-security review; ordinary public requests normally denied. |
| Property, parcel, title, owner, or access | No owner profile, legal title, easement, permission, liability, valuation, tax, or access conclusion. | Qualified county/legal authority and approved privacy/access policy. |
| Burn, road, weather, and emergency | No present burn authorization, road condition, storm status, fire instruction, closure, evacuation, or emergency response. | Current official county, road, NWS, emergency-management, or 911 authority. |

### Join boundary

Even individually public records may become unsafe when joined. Do not combine
reservoir/park, refuge, hunting, fishing, county-directory, parcel, owner,
water-supply, health, road, burn, weather, or emergency data to infer identity,
access, vulnerability, condition, exposure, legal standing, or a tactical route.

### Non-leaking denial

A denial names only the policy category and a safe alternative. It must not
confirm whether a sensitive species, refuge-use location, vulnerable asset,
private parcel, access route, or operational condition exists at the requested
place.

## 8. Finite outcomes and reason codes

Current Focus semantics use exactly four terminal outcomes:

| Outcome | Use | Display requirement |
|---|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Admitted evidence supports a narrow claim and policy permits it. | Show time basis, source role, EvidenceBundle-backed citations, restrictions, and release/correction state. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, authority, rights, currentness, applicability, or fitness is insufficient. | State what is missing and provide only a verified safe redirect. |
| `DENY` | The request crosses health, ecological, access, privacy, property, security, or harmful-precision policy. | Give a non-leaking reason and no prohibited detail. |
| `ERROR` | Evidence resolution, policy evaluation, validation, or runtime failed. | Return a bounded error; never guess or coerce to `ANSWER`. |

### Candidate reason-code crosswalk

| Candidate code from the plan | Trigger | Expected outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `CURRENT_PARK_FACILITY_STATUS_REQUIRES_AUTHORITY` | Current campsite, cabin, restroom, water-service, ramp, dock, marina, reservation, or closure status requested. | `ABSTAIN` |
| `RECREATION_SAFETY_NOT_DETERMINED` | Swimming, boating, launch, ramp, dock, marina, or facility safety requested. | `ABSTAIN` |
| `WATER_QUALITY_OR_HEALTH_STATUS_REQUIRES_AUTHORITY` | Algae, fish-consumption, recreation-water, drinking-water, or health conclusion requested. | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` |
| `RECREATION_REGULATION_NOT_PERSONALLY_DETERMINED` | Hunting/fishing legality, season, license, method, closure, refuge, or permission requested for a person/time/place. | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` |
| `SENSITIVE_WILDLIFE_OR_REFUGE_DETAIL_WITHHELD` | Exact refuge, occurrence, nesting, roosting, or targeting detail requested. | `DENY` |
| `CRITICAL_WATER_INFRASTRUCTURE_DETAIL_WITHHELD` | Dam, intake, treatment, water-supply, operational, or vulnerability detail requested. | `DENY` |
| `PROPERTY_OR_TITLE_DETERMINATION_DENIED` | Owner profile, parcel, title, easement, liability, permission, or access inference requested. | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` |
| `OFFICIAL_CURRENT_SAFETY_CHANNEL_REQUIRED` | Current burn, road, weather, closure, emergency, or response guidance requested. | `ABSTAIN` |
| `EVIDENCE_BUNDLE_UNRESOLVED` | EvidenceRef cannot resolve to admissible EvidenceBundle. | `ABSTAIN` or `ERROR` |
| `AI_NOT_EVIDENCE` | Generated language is offered as support. | `ABSTAIN` or `ERROR` |
| `PUBLIC_INTERNAL_LIFECYCLE_ACCESS` | A public request attempts RAW, WORK, QUARANTINE, candidate, or direct-model access. | `DENY` or `ERROR` |

## 15. Maintenance, correction, and rollback

### Documentation maintenance

- Preserve the `doc_id`, county identity, current compatibility path, explicit
  anchors, and planning-only authority until an accepted migration says
  otherwise.
- Update evidence-snapshot hashes when governing repository surfaces are
  materially re-inspected.
- Keep confirmed repository evidence separate from proposed plan content and
  dated external-source checks.
- Correct stale source, safety, health, access, rights, sensitivity,
  infrastructure, property, currentness, release, and public-use statements
  visibly.
- Never set released, published, approved, public-safe, or operational merely
  because documentation, tests, commits, pull requests, or merges exist.

### Structural boundary

Any future path convergence is structural work. It requires an accepted
placement decision, exact source and destination paths, Git-recoverable history,
inbound-reference repair, index and generated-output reconciliation, duplicate-
authority prevention, compatibility/deprecation handling, validator coverage,
and rollback. Never create singular and plural county lanes as independent
writable authorities.

### Before merge

Close or abandon the unmerged draft PR and feature branch. Remote branch
deletion is a separate administrative action and is not required for rollback.

### After merge

Use a transparent revert or forward-fix PR against the actual merged commit.
Do not rewrite shared history. Revert or correct this README and its generated
receipt together.

### Public correction boundary

This documentation-only change creates no source activation, release,
deployment, or publication to undo. If a future public artifact relies on
Kingman content, a Git revert alone may be insufficient: the governed
correction path may also require source-state update, release supersession,
withdrawal, cache invalidation, API/UI correction, and retained lineage.
