> planning lane, reconcile its tracked build plan with current repository

### Intended public-safe learning goals

- why federal grassland context does not establish blanket public access;
- how landscape-restoration history differs from current land or fire status;

  precision;

- how aquifer science and administrative water context differ from individual

## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The build plan names multiple source families. Their role separation is more
important than the mere fact that their public pages were available in 2026.

| Source family in the plan | Proposed role | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| USDA Forest Service | Federal land-management, public interpretation, recreation routing, and operational-notice authority within source scope | Blanket access authorization, parcel/title authority, exact heritage/ecology locator, or silently cached current restriction |
| National Park Service | Public Santa Fe Trail interpretation | Archaeological-site inventory, protected-resource disclosure, access permission, or universal geometry license |
| USGS Water Data | Monitoring-location identity and, after admission, time-bounded observations | Flood warning, safe-crossing, recreation-safety, water-right, or complete hydrologic truth |
| Kansas Geological Survey | Scientific and geohydrologic interpretation; provisional reporting where explicitly stated | Current river condition, final local-well fact, legal water decision, or emergency advice |
| Kansas Department of Agriculture / DWR | Aquifer and administrative water-context routing | Individual water-right standing, lawful-use, pumping, permit, or compliance determination |
| USDA NASS | County-scale statistical aggregate | Farm, operator, household, parcel, causation, or compliance profile |
| Morton County official site | Civic and official-current routing | Title, private access, legal, fire, road, or emergency conclusion beyond current source scope |
| KDOT | County identifier or deferred transport reference | Unsafe infrastructure precision or current road-safety conclusion |
| AI-generated narrative | Bounded explanation after evidence, policy, citation, and release checks | Evidence, source authority, policy decision, review approval, or release authority |

### Temporal rules

1. Preserve the source's observation, publication, update, retrieval, validity,
   revision, and release times when material.
2. A dated planning observation does not answer a current-condition question.
3. An operational notice must not survive as current KFM truth after its
   authority changes, expires, or is superseded.
4. Provisional measurement or reporting status must remain visible and cannot
   be silently normalized to final.
5. Missing or unclear freshness for consequential claims produces `ABSTAIN`,
   `DENY`, or `ERROR`, not a guessed current answer.
6. A currently reachable page does not make every statement on that page a
   current operational status.
7. Corrections and supersessions must remain visible when a source or released
   KFM object changes.

## 6. Heritage, ecology, access, fire, and public safety

### Cultural and archaeological boundary

The plan uses Santa Fe Trail and Forest Service heritage material as public
interpretive seeds. That does not authorize exact site, artifact, burial,
sacred-place, protected-resource, or inferred cultural-location disclosure.
Public interpretation should use reviewed minimum-necessary detail and deny or
generalize harmful precision.

### Ecology boundary

Grassland, ponds, migratory-bird, wildlife, and habitat context can support
general public learning only after source, rights, sensitivity, geometry, and
review gates close. Exact occurrences, nests, roosts, leks, rare habitat,
management operations, or targeting assistance remain denied or generalized.

### Public-land and private-access boundary

A national-grassland map cannot be read as proof that every displayed tract,
road, trail, crossing, or adjacent parcel is publicly owned, open, reachable,
safe, or lawful to enter. Interspersed private land makes ownership, title,
route, permission, and trespass questions especially unsuitable for inferred
answers.

### Fire, closure, road, and emergency boundary

Fire restrictions, closures, road conditions, camping rules, weather hazards,
and emergency instructions are time-sensitive operational matters. The first
slice should use a static explanation and approved official-current routing,
not a copied status layer. Current questions must abstain or redirect to the
responsible authority.

### Cross-layer reconstruction

Generalized public layers must be reviewed in composition, not only one at a
time. Multiple individually safe layers must not reconstruct protected heritage,
sensitive habitat, private access, vulnerable infrastructure, or operational
detail through overlay, proximity, labeling, search, download, or export.

## 7. Water, agriculture, property, and infrastructure

### Water and legal non-determination

River monitoring, aquifer science, groundwater reporting, and water
administration are distinct source roles. None alone—or in combination—allows
KFM to determine:

- current water-right standing;
- lawful use, pumping permission, or compliance;
- private-well condition or exact water depth;
- farm-level water security or behavior;
- current river safety or future groundwater availability;
- drought, emergency, permit, or enforcement action.

These requests should return `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` with responsible-authority
routing as appropriate.

### Agriculture

The build plan's agricultural material is county-scale statistical context.
Any future product should preserve aggregation and the source's reporting year,
limitations, and revisions. It must not join public aggregates to parcel,
person, operator, well, access, or enforcement data to infer private behavior.

### Property and people

Do not turn county, public-land, or GIS sources into:

- title or ownership determinations;
- permission-to-enter advice;
- living-person or household profiles;
- private-land conflict narratives;
- assessed-value, operation, compliance, or vulnerability inference.

### Infrastructure and transport

Exact vulnerability-sensitive infrastructure, emergency routes, facilities,
bridges, utilities, or operational assets should not be exposed merely because
they can be spatially joined or appear in another public source. Public benefit,
minimum necessary detail, rights, sensitivity, and current policy govern any
future exposure.

### Source-role anti-collapse

A single map composition may reference management, history, observation,
science, administration, statistics, civic routing, and generated explanation.
Those roles must remain visible. A visually unified layer does not make them one
authority or permit cross-source conclusions the individual sources cannot
support.

## 8. Finite outcomes and candidate reason codes

| Outcome | Morton County use |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Narrow, released, evidence-supported, policy-safe context with citations and limitations. |

| `DENY` | Request seeks protected cultural/ecological precision, private access/property inference, water/legal judgment, unreleased content, or otherwise prohibited use. |
| `ERROR` | Contract, evidence resolution, policy, citation validation, release lookup, or runtime failure prevents a trustworthy response. |

A reason-code name appearing in this README is not acceptance of a contract,

## 15. Maintenance, migration, correction, and rollback

### Maintenance triggers

Revisit this README when:

- the sibling plan changes materially;
- Focus Mode placement or naming authority changes;
- source admission, rights, geometry, currentness, or sensitivity review
  establishes new facts;
- a Morton County contract, schema, fixture, validator, API, UI, or release
  object is implemented and verified;
- public-safe detail, access, cultural, ecology, fire, water, or infrastructure
  policy changes;
- the county index is recomputed and its Morton row changes;
- a correction, withdrawal, or supersession affects a cited county claim.

### Migration posture

The current singular snake_case two-file path is a repository-present
compatibility lane. A future move or rename requires one accepted target, single
write authority, history preservation, inbound-reference repair, index and
validator reconciliation, compatibility analysis, review, and rollback. Do not
create a corrected parallel tree while this path remains writable.

### Correction posture

A documentation correction should preserve the prior claim's lineage where
material and identify the evidence that changed. A public correction is a
separate governed action and may require API, map, cache, export, search, or AI
propagation beyond a Markdown edit.

### Rollback

Before merge, rollback is to close or abandon the draft pull request and feature
branch. After merge, use a transparent revert or forward fix against the actual
merged commit; do not rewrite shared history. If a future released Morton County
artifact is implicated, a Git revert alone may be insufficient: correction,
withdrawal, cache invalidation, supersession, and release rollback must follow
the applicable KFM controls.
