## Proposed proof slice

**PROPOSED thesis:** Thomas County can become a useful proof slice for showing how heritage institutions, collection descriptions, annual municipal-water reporting, civic aviation, county service routing, and current-authority redirects can be combined without collapsing custody into rights, annual reports into individualized health truth, or public infrastructure facts into operational or vulnerability guidance.

The first slice should be deliberately narrow:

- orient users to Thomas County and Colby through generalized public geometry;
- explain the Prairie Museum and historical society's institutional roles without claiming object ownership, complete provenance, cultural authority, donor permission, or unrestricted media reuse;
- describe collection and historic-building context without publishing restricted records or exact sensitive material;
- expose the year, covered public-water system, retrieval date, limitations, and release state for any municipal-water report card;
- explain why an annual system-level report cannot answer current household, premise, service-line, exposure, diagnosis, or private-well questions;
- show the municipal airport as civic and transportation context while minimizing operational detail;
- route current runway, weather, NOTAM, fuel, service, and aviation-safety questions to appropriate official-current authorities;
- treat county tax, fraud-alert, notification, and property routes as administrative navigation rather than title, access, ownership, fraud, or person profiles;
- route burn restrictions, road conditions, warnings, weather, and emergencies to current official channels;
- expose source identity, source role, time basis, rights state, limitations, review state, and release state;
- demonstrate visible `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, and `ERROR` states;
- preserve correction and rollback requirements for any future released artifact.

### Non-goals

This slice is not a museum-rights clearinghouse, provenance adjudicator, cultural-authority substitute, donor database, media-reuse license, drinking-water or medical service, private-well advisor, utility operations console, airport operations or flight-planning service, infrastructure-vulnerability map, parcel/title/access service, fraud detector, road-status service, burn-ban authority, warning system, emergency service, or unrestricted AI question-answering surface.

## Source roles and temporal fitness

Source role must remain explicit. Different source families support different claim classes, rights postures, spatial scopes, and clocks.

| Source-role class | Safe use in a future governed slice | Unsafe collapse |
|---|---|---|
| Thomas County administrative/current-notice source | County departments, service routing, and dated notice context | Evergreen burn/road/emergency truth, title, access, fraud finding, or person profile |
| City of Colby administrative/utility source | Municipal roles, public project context, current-authority routing | Individual health, service guarantee, private-well status, or infrastructure vulnerability |
| Annual consumer-confidence/water report | Dated reporting context for the identified public-water system | Current tap safety, household exposure, diagnosis, private-well safety, or future condition |
| Prairie Museum / historical-society source | Institutional mission, programs, generalized collection and building context | Object title, complete provenance, unrestricted reproduction, donor permission, or sole cultural authority |
| Municipal airport descriptive source | General civic, administrative, and transportation context | Current runway, NOTAM, weather, fuel, service, security, or flight-safety authority |
| FAA, airport operator, and aviation-weather source | Potential official-current redirect after admission and policy review | KFM operational command, guarantee, or independent flight planning |
| NWS / county emergency source | Potential official-current weather, warning, and emergency redirect | Cached KFM warning, evacuation, fire, road, or safety verdict |
| KDHE / groundwater-management / DWR / KGS source | Potential regulatory, groundwater, water-right-routing, and science context after admission | Individual legal entitlement, potability, private-well, parcel, or health conclusion |
| County tax, appraisal, fraud-alert, or property route | Potential restricted administrative navigation after privacy review | Title truth, owner dossier, lawful access, allegation, debt, liability, or living-person inference |
| Map, tile, or generalized geometry | Downstream carrier of released public-safe content | Source authority, evidence, rights, policy, or release proof by itself |
| AI-generated explanation | Bounded interpretation over released evidence | Root truth, source admission, rights decision, policy decision, health advice, operational guidance, or release authority |

### Time model

Every future visible claim should distinguish the clocks that matter:

| Time field | Example use | Required caution |
|---|---|---|
| `source_effective_time` | Period or date the source statement applies to | A dated notice is not automatically current |
| `reporting_period` | Year or interval covered by a water report | Reporting scope is not real-time condition |
| `source_publication_time` | When the source published the record | Publication date can differ from observed/effective time |
| `retrieved_at` | When KFM captured or checked the source | Retrieval does not prove continued currentness |
| `reviewed_at` | When rights, sensitivity, health, or security review occurred | Review can expire or be superseded |
| `released_at` | When a governed KFM release occurred | A plan or merge is not release |
| `corrected_at` / `withdrawn_at` | When public state changed | Prior public state and correction lineage remain visible |

A future current-status request must abstain when the applicable source, effective period, update cadence, supersession state, or official authority is unresolved.

## Heritage rights, provenance, and cultural authority

> [!WARNING]
> **Custody is not title, and public display is not a reuse license.** A museum or historical society can establish its institutional role and describe collections without proving legal ownership, complete provenance, donor permission, cultural authority, or KFM's right to reproduce every object image or record.

A future heritage path must preserve these distinctions:

| Distinction | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Institutional custody vs. object ownership | Record separately; abstain when title is not established |
| Collection description vs. complete provenance | Expose known scope and gaps; do not imply completeness |
| Public webpage vs. redistribution permission | Verify item/media terms before reuse; suppress when unclear |
| Museum interpretation vs. cultural authority | Identify source role; consult source communities or Nations where applicable |
| Donor record vs. public metadata | Minimize or withhold living-person and restricted donor fields |
| Historic-building context vs. current access | Present dated/generalized context; route current hours, access, and safety to the operator |
| Collection correction vs. silent overwrite | Preserve dispute, repatriation, correction, and withdrawal lineage |

Potentially sacred, funerary, Indigenous, culturally sensitive, contested, donor-restricted, or living-person-linked material must remain quarantined, generalized, staged, or denied until qualified review supports a safer posture.

## Water reporting, health, and private-well boundary

> [!WARNING]
> **A dated public-water-system report is not individualized health advice.** The build plan records a City of Colby page that identified a 2025 report for reporting year 2024. This README does not verify that report's currentness, covered system, parameters, sampling locations, compliance period, active advisory state, or fitness for a particular household, tap, premise, service line, exposure, illness, or private well.

A future water card must make at least these fields visible:

- source and public-water-system identity;
- reporting year and compliance/effective period;
- publication and retrieval dates;
- parameter and sampling-scope limitations;
- whether a newer report, advisory, or correction exists;
- distinction between system-level reporting and premise plumbing/service lines;
- explicit exclusion of private-well conclusions;
- appropriate current official redirects;
- evidence, policy, review, release, correction, and rollback state.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Project context is not vulnerability analysis.** A public waterline or utility project may support dated civic context after review. It must not be transformed into service guarantees, weak-point maps, failure scenarios, tactical detail, or harmful infrastructure combinations.

Groundwater-management, water-right, geology, aquifer, and private-well questions require their own admitted, fit-for-purpose authorities and policy decisions. They cannot inherit authority from a municipal consumer-confidence report.

Before merge, rollback is to close or abandon the unmerged pull request and branch. After merge, use a transparent revert or forward-fix pull request against the actual merged commit; do not rewrite shared history. If a future Thomas artifact reaches a released or relied-upon state, a Git revert alone may be insufficient: correction notices, withdrawal, cache invalidation, manifest supersession, and public lineage may also be required.
