> The sibling build plan and this README do not admit a source, create an

## 4. Proposed Marion County proof slice

The sibling plan proposes a **Marion Reservoir Advisory-Currentness Trust Boundary
Proof**. Its central question is whether KFM can explain a multipurpose reservoir
and related county context while refusing to become a live water-health,
recreation-safety, drinking-water, reservoir-operations, flood/property, or
farm-causation authority.

### Plan-defined public-safe themes

| Theme in the sibling plan | Bounded future use | Required limit |
|---|---|---|
| Marion Reservoir purpose and history | Time-bounded, source-labeled landscape and project context | No current release, operation, safety, vulnerability, or engineering inference |
| Marion County Lake identity | Distinct waterbody identity and county recreation context | Never merge with Marion Reservoir through name similarity or ambiguous “Marion Lake” text |
| KDHE HAB/advisory routing | Explain responsible current-source route and freshness requirements | No copied durable safety status; no KFM swim, drink, fish, pet, or exposure verdict |
| Cottonwood River observations | Timestamped hydrologic context when separately governed | No health, forecast, flood-safety, or reservoir-management substitution |
| County mapping | Administrative context where needed | No parcel, valuation, title, permit, insurance, or legal conclusion |
| County agriculture aggregates | Period-labeled statistical context | No farm, person, family, owner, parcel, well, or causation inference |
| County-scale geology | Educational scientific context | No site engineering, well-yield, contamination, drinking-water, or construction assurance |

### Product and authority boundary

A future Marion Focus Mode may become map-rich and evidence-visible only after the
relevant objects are admitted, validated, policy-reviewed, released, and served
through governed interfaces. The renderer, Evidence Drawer, timeline, search, or
generated explanation remains downstream presentation.

The first observable proof should be synthetic and no-network. It should test
identity ambiguity, stale advisory state, health/safety denial, operational
minimization, and correction behavior before any live source or public carrier is
considered.

## 5. Public safety, sensitivity, and finite outcomes

### Fail-closed request matrix

| Request or display class | Expected bounded posture | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Released, time-labeled Marion landscape context with resolved waterbody identity and evidence | `ANSWER` | Evidence, policy, source role, limitations, and release state are visible |
| Missing, stale, conflicting, revoked, rights-unclear, or identity-ambiguous evidence | `ABSTAIN` | The system cannot support a trustworthy claim |
| “Is Marion Lake safe?” without resolved waterbody identity | `ABSTAIN` / require disambiguation | Marion Reservoir and Marion County Lake are distinct bodies |
| Current swimming, drinking, fishing, pet-exposure, HAB, or recreation-safety verdict | `DENY` and route to responsible current authority | KFM is not a live public-health or recreation-safety service |
| Current reservoir release, outlet, dam-operation, closure, or flood-management instruction | `DENY` | Operational and infrastructure authority remains external |
| Parcel flood, permit, insurance, title, valuation, engineering, or safe-property conclusion | `DENY` | Property and regulatory authority remain external |
| Aggregate agriculture joined to a farm, person, owner, parcel, well, or responsibility claim | `DENY` | Privacy, property, statistical, and causation risk |
| Historical or scientific water-quality material presented as current health or drinking-water condition | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` | Source-role and time-basis collapse |
| Direct UI access to `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, candidate data, or direct model output | `ERROR` / `DENY` | Trust-membrane bypass |
| Runtime, schema, evidence, or policy failure | `ERROR` | A bounded fault must remain visible without unsafe fallback |

### Cross-layer reconstruction rule

A field that appears harmless alone may become sensitive or misleading when joined
with parcel data, well records, infrastructure, waterbody names, historical
advisories, agriculture, or current observations. Future validation must test both
individual layers and combined display/query context. Redaction in one carrier is
not sufficient if another carrier reconstructs the denied fact.

### Public-interface requirements

Any later public Marion surface must make these states visible and accessible:

- `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, and `ERROR`;
- evidence missing, stale, conflicting, corrected, superseded, restricted, or
  revoked;
- resolved waterbody identity and source role;
- observation, publication, retrieval, validity, and release time where material;
- generalized or withheld operational/property detail;
- correction and withdrawal state;
- an official-source route where KFM is not the responsible authority.

A fluent response, polished map, passing schema check, or green workflow must not
hide a negative state.

## 6. Source role, rights, and currentness boundary

The build plan names source families checked during its 2026-05-22 authoring run.
This README does not refresh those external checks and does not admit any source.

| Plan-named source family | Proposed role | Must not be collapsed into |
|---|---|---|
| U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Marion Reservoir material | Reservoir purpose, history, and bounded public project context | KFM health authority, current operations, engineering assessment, or vulnerability analysis |
| Kansas Department of Health and Environment | HAB, water-quality, and public-health source routing | Timeless safety statement, drinking-water verdict, or KFM-issued advisory |
| U.S. Geological Survey | Cottonwood River and related timestamped observation context | Public-health, reservoir-operation, release, or emergency authority |
| Kansas Geological Survey | County-scale geology and scientific context | Water-health, reservoir-operation, engineering, or property authority |
| Marion County public surfaces | Administrative and mapping context | Parcel, valuation, title, permit, insurance, legal, or complete cultural authority |
| Kansas agricultural statistics/program sources | County-scale aggregate context | Farm, owner, worker, parcel, well, responsibility, contamination, or causation truth |

### Admission questions for every source

Before a source can support a public claim, record and review:

1. stable source identity, publisher, authority role, and exact version or
   retrieval state;
2. publication, effective, observation, retrieval, and validity times as distinct
   fields where material;
3. rights, attribution, extraction, transformation, redistribution, and
   derivative-display terms;
4. allowed claim scope and explicitly prohibited inference;
5. waterbody/entity identity and crosswalk behavior;
6. sensitivity, minimum necessary detail, safe spatial/temporal resolution, and
   cross-layer reconstruction risk;
7. freshness, outage, correction, revision, supersession, and withdrawal
   behavior;
8. deterministic provenance from candidate material through any public-safe
   derivative;
9. an `EvidenceRef` that resolves to an admissible `EvidenceBundle`;
10. policy, accountable review, release, correction, and rollback references.

### Dated advisory observation boundary

The sibling plan records that, on its 2026-05-22 check, a KDHE current-advisory
table did not list Marion Reservoir or Marion County Lake. This README preserves
that only as a **dated plan observation**. It is not evidence of the current state
on 2026-08-21 and must not be promoted into a present-tense safety statement.

Public access alone is not permission to extract, transform, join, tile,
redistribute, or publish. An official source role also does not make every field
necessary or safe for a KFM public derivative.

## 7. Evidence and readiness boundary

A Marion public result must remain downstream of the governed KFM path:

1. identify a source once in the appropriate source-authority surface;
2. review authority, rights, access, time, geography, sensitivity, correction,
   identity, and minimum-necessary use;
3. admit candidate material to `RAW`, or hold it in `WORK / QUARANTINE`;
4. derive and validate `PROCESSED` material with transform and provenance
   lineage;
5. create catalog or triplet relationships without treating indexes as truth;
6. resolve every consequential `EvidenceRef` to an `EvidenceBundle` and apply
   policy;
7. make a separate promotion and release decision with correction, withdrawal,
   and rollback targets;
8. serve only released public-safe artifacts through governed interfaces;
9. keep map rendering and AI interpretation subordinate to that evidence and
   decision state.
