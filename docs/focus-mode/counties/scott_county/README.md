# Scott County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Scott County planning lane, make its
> cultural-authority, archaeological-precision, geology-fitness, groundwater,
> LEMA, dated water-quality, agriculture, property, access, currentness,
> evidence, and public-safety boundaries visible, and route future work through
> KFM evidence, policy, review, release, correction, and rollback controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation, consent, approval, or
> release evidence.** At the pinned base, this directory contains a one-byte
> blank README and one substantial Scott County build plan. Replacing the
> placeholder gives maintainers a boundary surface; it does not establish
> source admission, Nation authorization, archaeological clearance, semantic
> correctness, validator success, runtime behavior, release, deployment, or
> publication.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains a compatibility question.** This same-path update repairs
> an existing README under `docs/focus-mode/counties/scott_county/`. Accepted
> Directory Rules v2 governs placement; proposed ADR-0027 describes a plural,
> kebab-case control plane but remains unaccepted. No file is moved, renamed,
> copied into a parallel tree, or declared canonical here.

> [!WARNING]
> **Public heritage visibility does not authorize cultural or archaeological
> extraction.** A park, brochure, registry record, historical publication, or
> generated summary cannot speak for Apache, Pueblo, or another sovereign
> Nation. Exact or inference-enabling archaeological, artifact, sacred, burial,
> culturally restricted, or protected-site detail must not reach an ordinary
> public client. Substantive cultural representation requires appropriate
> Nation-authoritative evidence and review.

> [!WARNING]
> **Water administration is not a KFM legal or current-health answer.** Historic
> groundwater science, a LEMA administrative record, a dated TMDL, agricultural
> aggregates, property tools, and park interpretation have distinct source
> roles. They must not become individual water-right or compliance decisions,
> current lake-safety or ecological conclusions, private-operation disclosure,
> parcel access guidance, or a fluent inference. Missing authority, evidence,
> rights, safe precision, freshness, policy, or review closure requires
> `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR`.

## 5. Cultural, archaeological, and source-role boundary

### Sovereign and cultural authority

Public agency interpretation can be an attributed source for a bounded claim;
it is not authority to narrate a Nation's identity, history, culture, language,
governance, sacred meaning, consent, or endorsement. A future Scott slice must:

- identify the appropriate Nation-authoritative evidence and review relationship;
- record the allowed claim and representation scope rather than infer it;
- minimize cultural content to the reviewed public purpose;
- preserve abstention when authority, rights, scope, or review is unresolved;
- support immediate correction, withdrawal, and cache invalidation;
- avoid confirming or denying the existence of undisclosed cultural details
  through response text, counts, timing, geometry, identifiers, or differential
  behavior.

### Archaeological and spatial precision

Public discoverability of a park, registry entry, report, or map does not make
all underlying location detail safe for KFM redistribution. Public derivatives
must use the minimum reviewed resolution and attribute set. Exact or
reconstructive archaeological, artifact, sacred, burial, culturally restricted,
private-operation, and sensitive-ecology detail remains withheld from tiles,
search, exports, filters, screenshots, analytics, caches, identifiers, and AI
responses.

### Candidate source roles recorded by the plan

The build plan's source list is a research queue, not an admitted registry.

| Candidate source family | May support after admission | Must not be collapsed into |
|---|---|---|
| Kansas wildlife and parks material | Bounded park identity, public-use orientation, and attributed state interpretation within its actual scope | Complete cultural authority, current safety, current rules, ecology clearance, access guarantee, or unrestricted derivative rights |
| National Park Service registry metadata | Bounded listing identity, official metadata, and recorded restriction context | Permission to expose precise archaeology, artifacts, cultural features, sacred places, or a complete cultural story |
| Kansas Geological Survey historic publications | Dated geology, geohydrology, Ladder Creek, basin, groundwater, and source-fitness context | Current groundwater condition, detailed county mapping, legal water conclusion, or feature-level precision |
| Kansas agriculture and water-resources administration | Official LEMA process, order, hearing, and administrative context within the source's scope | Individual water-right, allocation, pumping entitlement, compliance, farm, operator, or legal-advice authority |
| Kansas health and environment TMDL material | Dated regulatory-water context and historical assessment role | Current lake health, drinking-water, fish, recreation, ecological condition, or public disclosure of private/regulated operational inventories |
| USDA NASS and state agriculture summaries | Dated county-scale published agriculture aggregates and stated suppression | Current operations, producer, field, parcel, well, worker, facility, financial dossier, or reconstructed protected value |
| County and city appraiser or parcel routing | Office identity and official service routing within its actual scope | Title, ownership, access, permission, sensitive-site discovery, or cultural-resource evidence |
| Generated KFM maps and language | Downstream explanation of released public-safe evidence and policy outcomes | Sovereign truth, source authority, evidence, policy permission, release proof, or publication authority |

Every admitted source needs authority role, exact identity, rights and terms,
geographic and temporal scope, cadence, limitations, sensitivity, correction
behavior, and the claim types it may support. A browsed page, polished citation,
official logo, map, or public PDF is not an `EvidenceBundle`.

## 6. Public safety and governed outcomes

Only four public runtime outcomes are admissible after current contracts,
schemas, policy, and adapters are actually bound:

| Outcome | Scott County use | Must not do |
|---|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Return a bounded, released public-safe explanation after evidence, authority, rights, sensitivity, time, policy, review, citation, correction, and release dependencies resolve | Fill a missing dependency with plausible prose |
| `ABSTAIN` | Explain that evidence, cultural authority, currentness, fitness, or review is insufficient and route to a responsible current authority when safe | Imply an answer through wording, confidence, source ordering, or map context |
| `DENY` | Withhold prohibited archaeological/cultural precision, private/regulated detail, water-law conclusions, parcel/access inference, or unreleased content | Echo, confirm, count, locate, or help reconstruct the protected detail |
| `ERROR` | Report an evidence, policy, contract, adapter, release, or trust-membrane failure without leaking internal material | Fall through to raw stores, candidate files, direct model output, or a fifth outcome |

### Required negative paths

| Request or failure | Required default | Safe reason family |
|---|---|---|
| Unresolved `EvidenceRef` | `ABSTAIN` | `EVIDENCE_BUNDLE_UNRESOLVED` |
| Exact or inference-enabling archaeological or cultural location | `DENY` | `ARCHAEOLOGICAL_OR_CULTURAL_LOCATION_WITHHELD` |
| Substantive Apache or Pueblo story from state/federal interpretation alone | `ABSTAIN` | `NATION_AUTHORITY_OR_REVIEW_UNRESOLVED` |
| Map combination that reconstructs withheld detail | `DENY` | `INFERENCE_ENABLING_PRECISION_WITHHELD` |
| Current safety or ecological claim from the dated TMDL or historic source | `ABSTAIN` | `CURRENT_HEALTH_OR_ECOLOGICAL_STATUS_NOT_ESTABLISHED` |
| Individual legal, allocation, pumping, or compliance conclusion from LEMA context | `DENY` | `WATER_LEGAL_CONCLUSION_OUT_OF_SCOPE` |
| Private or regulated operational detail | `DENY` | `PRIVATE_OR_REGULATED_OPERATION_DETAIL_WITHHELD` |
| Parcel record used to infer access, title, or sensitive-site discovery | `DENY` | `LAND_ACCESS_OR_TITLE_AUTHORITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED` |
| Agricultural aggregate joined to a farm, user, parcel, well, or legal outcome | `DENY` | `PRIVATE_OPERATION_OR_WATER_INFERENCE` |
| Unreleased geometry or artifact requested by a public client | `DENY` | `NOT_PUBLICLY_RELEASED` |
| Public client reads `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, candidate files, or direct model output | `ERROR` | `PUBLIC_RAW_OR_DIRECT_MODEL_PATH_FORBIDDEN` |
| Correction, withdrawal, or rollback target is unresolved | `DENY` | `REVERSIBILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED` |

Reason text must stay non-sensitive. It must not reveal coordinates, proximity,
counts, identifiers, attributes, operational facts, or whether a protected
feature exists at a user-supplied location.

## 7. Evidence and readiness boundary

A Scott County public result must remain downstream of the governed KFM path:

1. identify each source once in the source-authority surface;
2. review authority, rights, time, geography, sensitivity, allowed precision,
   cultural obligations, and correction behavior;
3. admit candidate material to `RAW`, or hold it in `WORK / QUARANTINE` while
   requirements remain unresolved;
4. derive and validate `PROCESSED` material with transform, generalization,
   suppression, and provenance lineage;
5. resolve every consequential `EvidenceRef` to an `EvidenceBundle` and apply
   policy;
6. make a separate promotion and release decision with correction, withdrawal,
   and rollback targets;
7. serve only released public-safe artifacts through governed interfaces;
8. keep maps, cards, indexes, AI, and generated language subordinate to that
   evidence and decision state.
