> **One-line purpose.** Orient maintainers and reviewers to the repository-present Montgomery County planning artifacts, their proposed Elk City Lake–Verdigris River–Coffeyville environmental-history proof slice, and the evidence and governance work still required before any runtime or public claim can exist.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This lane contains planning documentation, not a released county product.** The linked build plan proposes a public-safe proof slice connecting Elk City Lake, the Verdigris River corridor, Coffeyville industrial and regulatory history, southeast Kansas geology and groundwater, recreation and wildlife context, county agriculture, and explicit public-safety restraint. Current operational questions belong with qualified official authorities.

> [!WARNING]
> **Industrial and infrastructure detail stays bounded.** A public educational narrative may explain dated official environmental or regulatory history, but it must not become a vulnerability map, facility-operations view, response-staging guide, property-impact locator, or public inference surface for sensitive infrastructure.

## Proposed proof slice

**PROPOSED thesis:** Montgomery County can become a strong proof slice by connecting river, reservoir, environmental-regulatory history, geology, agriculture, and public-land context while making time, source role, uncertainty, sensitivity, and refusal boundaries visible.

The first slice should remain deliberately narrow:

- orient users to Elk City Lake and the Verdigris River as county-scale geographic anchors;
- explain a dated Coffeyville environmental/regulatory-history episode only at the scope supported by released evidence;
- visibly distinguish historic events, later enforcement records, current monitoring sources, and current official operational guidance;
- expose source identity, source role, date basis, limitations, release state, and correction state through the Evidence Drawer;
- generalize industrial/facility geometry and exclude vulnerability-sensitive operational detail;
- abstain on current flood, contamination, health, compliance, water-safety, reservoir, road, emergency, or property-impact questions unless separately governed current evidence exists;
- keep county agriculture context aggregate and non-identifying;
- defer substantive cultural interpretation until appropriate authority and review are established;
- preserve correction and rollback from the first future public release.

### Non-goals

This slice is not a flood-warning service, contamination dashboard, refinery-monitoring system, facility-vulnerability map, current drinking-water or air-quality advisor, emergency-routing product, parcel exposure locator, title/access service, live reservoir-safety application, legal-compliance evaluator, or unrestricted AI question-answering surface.

<a id="source-roles-and-temporal-fitness"></a>

## Source roles and temporal fitness

Montgomery County is useful precisely because different official-source families can support different claim classes. They must not be flattened into one generic authority layer.

| Source-role class | Safe use in a future governed slice | Unsafe collapse |
|---|---|---|
| Reservoir stewardship/source page | General lake identity, historical/contextual description, public-source routing | Current lake level, boating safety, access, water-quality, or emergency guidance without separately released current evidence |
| River observation/monitoring source | Time-bounded observation or station identity | Flood warning, drinking-water safety, hazard conclusion, or emergency advice |
| Environmental response record | Dated description of a documented past event | Present contamination, household exposure, property damage, or health inference |
| Regulatory/enforcement record | Dated regulatory-history context | Current compliance, present facility condition, legal liability, or safety conclusion |
| KGS scientific/history source | Broad geology, groundwater, and resource-history context | Present pollution, facility operation, legal status, or water-safety conclusion |
| County GIS/parcel source | Administrative orientation or public-source routing after admission | Ownership/title truth, exposure map, access permission, household impact, or vulnerability inference |
| Agriculture aggregate | County-scale contextual metric after admission | Producer, parcel, irrigation, water-use, or causation inference |
| Map/tile/AI surface | Downstream carrier or bounded interpretation of released evidence | Source authority, policy decision, release authority, or evidence by itself |

Every future visible claim should distinguish at least the real-world validity period, source publication/update time, KFM retrieval time, and KFM release/correction state where those differ materially.

<a id="public-safety-rights-and-sensitivity"></a>

## Public safety, rights, and sensitivity

The linked plan surfaces high-consequence boundaries that must remain fail closed.

> [!WARNING]
> **Historic event ≠ present condition.** A documented past flood, spill, response, or enforcement action cannot be used to imply current contamination, current household exposure, present health risk, present compliance, or current hazard.

> [!WARNING]
> **Industrial/infrastructure sensitivity.** Public mapping should use only the precision needed for public understanding. Facility operations, vulnerabilities, response staging, sensitive infrastructure relationships, and unnecessary exact geometry remain withheld or generalized.

> [!WARNING]
> **Operational freshness.** River observations, reservoir pages, county notices, and agency status material require explicit freshness and authority before current-condition answers. Missing or stale support yields `ABSTAIN`, not extrapolation.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Property and land inference.** Parcel/GIS availability must not become ownership, title, private access, household exposure, environmental liability, or property-impact truth.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Cultural authority.** Any substantive Osage historical or cultural narrative requires appropriate authoritative evidence and review; generic planning lineage is not enough.

Public accessibility does not automatically establish redistribution rights, derivative-display permission, KFM source admission, precision suitability, or policy approval.
