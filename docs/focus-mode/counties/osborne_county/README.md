## Proposed proof slice

**PROPOSED thesis:** Osborne County can become a strong county proof slice by showing how reservoir, river, agriculture, recreation, wildlife, county-services, and cultural-history narratives remain useful while refusing stale operational reuse, unsafe infrastructure detail, sensitive ecological precision, private-property inference, and unreviewed cultural authority.

The first slice should be deliberately narrow:

- orient users to Osborne County and the western Waconda-Solomon corridor;
- distinguish county geometry from reservoir, state-park, wildlife-area, refuge, dam, river, and cultural-narrative geography;
- show source identity, source role, date basis, limitations, and release state;
- demonstrate a visible Evidence Drawer path for every consequential claim;
- preserve the difference between dated reservoir observations and current safety or operations;
- keep agriculture aggregate, suppression-aware, and non-identifying;
- generalize or deny sensitive wildlife/refuge precision;
- abstain on current boating, fishing, hunting, camping, flood, weather, water-level, water-release, road, or emergency questions unless separately governed current evidence exists;
- defer culturally consequential Waconda-related interpretation pending qualified authority;
- expose correction and rollback state when any future public artifact is released.

### Non-goals

This slice is not a live reservoir dashboard, boating/fishing/hunting advisor, flood or weather alert system, dam-operations viewer, vulnerability analysis tool, parcel/owner interface, private-well or water-right advisor, genealogy service, cultural-authority substitute, or unrestricted AI question-answering surface.

## Source roles and temporal fitness

Source roles must remain explicit because county, Census, agriculture, reservoir, recreation, wildlife, weather, historical, cultural, and generated sources support different claim classes.

| Source-role class | Safe use in a future governed slice | Unsafe collapse |
|---|---|---|
| County government | County identity, services, public-source routing, dated notices | Property/title/owner, emergency, health, genealogy, or individualized profile authority |
| Census demographic source | Vintage-specific county aggregates | Treating one vintage as timeless population truth |
| NASS agriculture source | County-scale aggregates with suppression preserved | Farm/operator/parcel inference or reconstruction of suppressed values |
| Reservoir/reclamation source | Time-stamped observations and high-level public-purpose context | Current boating/flood/release/safety conclusion or individualized water-right determination |
| Recreation/park source | Generalized facilities and enduring public-use context | Stale campground, ramp, fire, access, boating, fishing, or swimming advice |
| Wildlife-management source | Generalized habitat/management context | Precise refuge, sensitive species-use, nesting, roosting, or targeted disturbance intelligence |
| Weather source | Source routing and time-labeled official context | KFM becoming an alert or emergency authority |
| Cultural/history source | Dated historical/contextual evidence after rights and authority review | Nation/Tribal cultural authority, sacred-site disclosure, or unreviewed submerged-heritage interpretation |
| Map/tile | Downstream carrier of released public-safe geometry | Source authority or evidence by itself |
| AI-generated explanation | Bounded interpretation over released evidence | Root truth, source admission, policy decision, cultural authority, or release authority |

Time semantics matter. Future claims should distinguish observation time, valid time, source publication/update time, retrieval time, KFM release time, and correction time whenever those differences affect meaning.

## Public safety, rights, and sensitivity

The linked plan surfaces several high-consequence boundaries that must remain fail closed.

> [!WARNING]
> **Reservoir operations and safety:** reservoir level, storage, inflow, outflow, flood-control pool, release, boat-ramp, boating, swimming, fishing, and emergency conditions have different authorities and clocks. A dated observation cannot silently become present guidance.

> [!WARNING]
> **Infrastructure security:** dam, gate, release, dependency, emergency, and operational details must not become a public vulnerability analysis merely because source material is public.

> [!WARNING]
> **Sensitive ecology:** exact refuge, nesting, roosting, migration, rare-species, or management-sensitive locations must not become a public discovery tool.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Private-property and water inference:** county records, appraisal systems, agriculture data, wells, and water-allocation information must not become owner, title, household, individualized farm, private-well, water-right, potability, access, or compliance conclusions.

> [!WARNING]
> **Cultural authority:** Waconda-related sacred, Tribal, Nation, and submerged-heritage narratives remain bounded until appropriate authoritative evidence, rights, and review exist.

Public availability does not itself establish KFM redistribution rights, geometry authority, transform permission, public-safe precision, cultural authority, or policy approval.
