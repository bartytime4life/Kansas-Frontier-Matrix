# Cheyenne County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Cheyenne County planning lane, make its Arikaree Breaks, South Fork Republican River, interstate-water, rural-access, scientific-locality, property, weather, hazard, and currentness boundaries visible, and route future work through KFM evidence, policy, review, release, correction, and rollback controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation or release evidence.** This directory contains this README and one Cheyenne County build plan. Their presence prevents accidental duplicate planning; it does not establish source admission, factual accuracy, semantic correctness, validator success, runtime behavior, qualified review, release, deployment, or publication.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains unresolved.** This same-path update replaces a one-byte placeholder under `docs/focus-mode/counties/cheyenne_county/`. Accepted Directory Rules v2 treats Focus Mode as a composition scope; proposed ADR-0027 describes a different plural, kebab-case control plane but remains unaccepted. No file is moved, renamed, copied into a parallel tree, or declared the final canonical county write target here.

> [!WARNING]
> **Scenic geology and interstate-water administration are not present-use authority.** A public geological source cannot determine whether a rural road is currently passable or safe, whether access is permitted, or where a fragile locality should be targeted. Compact, river, streamflow, weather, or administrative material cannot determine an individual, parcel, farm, municipality, or well entitlement; legal compliance; current supply or irrigation availability; flood or drought status; or emergency action. Missing evidence, rights, safe resolution, policy, qualified review, or release closure must produce a bounded `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR` rather than a fluent inference.

## 5. Source-role and cross-domain boundaries

The plan names source families that answer materially different questions. They must not be merged into one undifferentiated Cheyenne County truth layer.

| Planned source or object class | Legitimate bounded role | Must not be treated as |
|---|---|---|
| Geological survey or public-geology source | Source-scoped landform, material, geomorphic, and stable scientific context with explicit limitations | Present road condition, access permission, collecting permission, current hazard, or water/legal authority |
| State water-administration or compact source | Agency responsibility, compact text, basin administration, meeting, accounting, or public legal-context material within its stated scope and date | Individual entitlement, lawful-use opinion, compliance verdict, present supply, irrigation availability, or emergency instruction |
| Hydrography or streamflow source | Identified reach, station, observation, unit, timestamp, quality, and source-role context | Water-right allocation, drinking-water judgment, flood warning, drought declaration, supply guarantee, or causation |
| County or municipal website | Current official-service routing and limited administrative context after field and cadence review | Title truth, road/passability status unless explicitly authoritative, access permission, emergency truth, or unrestricted property data |
| Weather, road, or emergency authority | Time-limited official-current redirect for its exact jurisdiction and operation | Stable county history, geological truth, water entitlement, or permission to copy operational detail into a static product |
| Parcel, assessor, ownership, or access source | Purpose-limited administrative context after privacy, rights, and field review | Title, public access, permission, liability, named-person profiling, or environmental inference |
| Ecology, archaeology, burial, cultural, or scientific-locality source | Generalized public context after rights, sensitivity, community, and specialist review | Exact protected occurrence, collecting target, sacred or burial location, or unrestricted research locality |
| Cross-state Republican River material | Source-specific Kansas, Colorado, Nebraska, federal, or compact-administration evidence with explicit jurisdiction, identity, and time | A silently harmonized legal or hydrologic truth layer outside the source's authority |
| Generated narrative, map styling, score, or derived indicator | Plain-language explanation or visualization of already admissible evidence | Evidence, source authority, policy decision, expert review, legal conclusion, release state, or causal proof |

### Cross-source combination rule

Juxtaposition is not proof. The system must not infer access, passability, permission, collecting opportunity, entitlement, compliance, current supply, irrigation availability, flood, drought, emergency state, private-property status, causation, or safe use merely because geology, river, compact, county, weather, parcel, or generated cards appear together. Every combined claim needs an explicit relation, evidence support, source roles, spatial and temporal scope, policy decision, review state, and release posture appropriate to its significance.

## 6. Public safety and governed outcomes

### Fail-closed matrix

| Request or condition | Required public outcome | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Exact or reconstructive fragile geology, collecting, archaeological, burial, sacred, ecological, or other protected locality | `DENY` | Do not confirm existence or expose precision through map, text, metadata, search, accessibility output, cache, telemetry, export, or generated explanation |
| Present road passability, route safety, trip suitability, or weather-dependent access from static geology or planning material | `ABSTAIN` | Require the appropriate current authority; do not convert an old caution into a current verdict |
| Private-property entry, crossing, access permission, or collecting permission inferred from a map or county page | `DENY` | Administrative or visual context does not grant access or permission |
| Compact or water-administration context used to answer a person, parcel, farm, well, or municipality entitlement question | `DENY` | Public compact context is not an individual legal determination |
| Compact, river, gage, or map context used as compliance, current supply, irrigation-availability, flood, drought, or emergency advice | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` | Require the exact current authority and supported operation; KFM is not an adjudication or alert surface |
| County appraiser, property, emergency, or public-works material used beyond an admitted purpose | `DENY` | Minimize fields and keep title, personal, access, and operational detail outside ordinary public Focus context |
| Cross-state sources conflict or lack stable identity, jurisdiction, time, units, or relation | `ABSTAIN` | Do not silently harmonize or choose the more convenient source |
| Material claim lacks a resolving `EvidenceRef` to `EvidenceBundle` chain | `ABSTAIN` | Cite-or-abstain |
| Policy, evidence resolver, validator, citation service, or governed runtime fails | `ERROR` | Never fallback to an answer or a less restrictive display |
| Evidence, source role, time, policy, qualified review, and release closure support the exact bounded public operation | `ANSWER` | Include citations, spatial and temporal scope, source roles, limitations, sensitivity posture, release state, and correction path |

### Public client boundary

A future Cheyenne County client may consume only governed finite envelopes and released public-safe artifacts. It must not read:

- RAW, WORK, QUARANTINE, or internal candidate stores;
- unrestricted source payloads or direct source-system responses;
- protected locality, access, property, person, water-right, legal, ecological, cultural, or infrastructure records;
- direct model output;
- policy internals or sensitive denial reasons;
- unreleased receipts, proofs, review notes, or release candidates.

A refusal is part of the trust surface. Public explanations should state that information is unavailable or withheld under the applicable public-safety boundary without revealing whether a protected record exists, how precise it is, or how a protection transform works.

## 8. Smallest safe follow-up

The next dependency-closed implementation should remain narrower than a live county feature.

1. **Recheck path and control-plane authority.** Keep the current lane stable unless ADR-0027 or a successor is accepted with migration and consumer evidence.
2. **Recheck source identity, terms, rights, and currentness.** Build a dated assessment packet for the plan's candidate geology, water-administration, county, hydrography, weather, and road-authority source families; do not fetch, ingest, or publish payloads.
3. **Identify qualified decision roles.** Record the geology, scientific-locality, road/access, water-governance, legal-scope, privacy/property, cultural, sensitivity, security, and release responsibilities required for each proposed operation without inventing people or approval.
4. **Define one no-network assessment profile.** Use synthetic, non-sensitive references to prove source-role separation, exact-locality denial, present-road abstention, private-access denial, water-entitlement denial, current-supply and hazard abstention, and fixed non-effects.
5. **Keep public runtime work on HOLD.** No Cheyenne route, map layer, governed AI answer, release, deployment, or publication should follow until contracts, machine shape, policy, evidence resolution, qualified review, correction, and rollback close.

A useful first executable slice would prove that a static geology record cannot answer a present road question, a compact record cannot determine entitlement or compliance, exact locality or private-access requests are denied, and every mutation/release effect flag remains false. It would not admit or retrieve a live source.
