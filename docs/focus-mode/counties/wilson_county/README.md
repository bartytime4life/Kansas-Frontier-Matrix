> public-health, property, access, infrastructure, burial, cultural, hazard,

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This directory contains documentation, not a released county product.** The
> sibling build plan and this README do not admit a source, resolve an

> approve a map or AI answer, or create promotion, release, deployment, or

## 5. Source-role and temporal boundary

The sibling plan records three official public source families checked on
2026-05-24. This README does not recheck them. They remain **plan-derived source
candidates** until source intake, rights review, evidence closure, and policy
review are completed.

| Plan-named source family | Proposed role | May support only after admission | Must not be silently upgraded to |
|---|---|---|---|
| National Park Service Norman No. 1 historic record | Federal historic-designation/documentary record | Bounded site identity, historical narrative, significance, and historical time basis within source scope | Current environmental condition, access, safety, title, operations, flood, or visitor status |

| KDOT Wilson County general highway map | Dated administrative/cartographic context | Dated county-scale orientation and named transport/hydrographic context within source fitness | Live route, active asset, current well/facility, access, hazard, population, environmental, or emergency truth |

### Time-basis requirements

| Time-sensitive surface | Required posture |
|---|---|
| Historical milestones or claims | Show the supported historical time basis and source role |
| KDOT-derived orientation | Display the map date/fitness statement carried by the admitted descriptor; never present it as live |
| Present environmental or health question | Require distinct current competent evidence; otherwise `ABSTAIN` |
| Current visitor access, hours, accessibility, or site condition | Require a current official source with freshness and expiry controls; otherwise `ABSTAIN` |
| Current flood, road, river, or emergency question | Redirect to the competent current authority only through a validated route; never answer from static history |
| Current infrastructure or asset question | Withhold unsafe precision and require currentness, sensitivity, and policy review |
| Cultural or Indigenous interpretation | Require appropriate authoritative sources and review; historical public prose alone is insufficient |

### Source-admission minimum

Before a Wilson source may support a public claim, the governed intake must
record at least:

- stable source identity and authority role;
- public URL or access route, retrieval time, version/date, and checksum or receipt;
- terms, rights, derivative-display, and geometry-use posture;
- temporal scope, update cadence, expiration, and stale behavior;
- allowed claim scope and prohibited inference categories;
- sensitivity posture for infrastructure, property, burial/cemetery, cultural, environmental, health, and hazard content;
- `EvidenceRef` resolution to an admissible `EvidenceBundle`;
- policy obligations, citation validation, accountable review, correction path, and rollback reference.

Public availability alone does not prove admissibility, redistribution rights,
currentness, or fitness for a particular claim.

## 6. Present-condition, property, infrastructure, and sensitivity boundary

### 6.1 Industrial history and present environmental condition

| Requested inference | Safe current posture | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| “The historic oil site means this place is contaminated today.” | `ABSTAIN` | Historical significance is not current environmental evidence |
| “The nearby river is affected, safe, or unsafe because of the historic site.” | `ABSTAIN` | Spatial adjacency does not prove cause, exposure, water quality, ecology, or safety |
| “The site is safe for children or visitors today.” | `ABSTAIN` | No current health or site-safety authority was established |
| “Cleanup or regulatory compliance is complete/incomplete.” | `ABSTAIN` | Requires distinct current regulatory evidence and bounded interpretation |
| “Drinking water is safe/unsafe.” | `ABSTAIN` | Historic and cartographic sources are not potability authority |

No generated narrative may bridge these evidence gaps.

### 6.2 Property, title, access, and visitor-currentness

Historic forms or public-history materials may contain location, legal, ownership,
or visitor language. That does not authorize current conclusions.

| Requested inference | Safe current posture |
|---|---|
| Current owner, title, parcel boundary, liability, easement, or right of entry | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` |
| Present museum/site hours, accessibility, condition, or public access | `ABSTAIN` until current authoritative intake |
| Permission to enter, explore, collect, drill, inspect, or cross property | `DENY` / official authority route |
| Property value, environmental liability, or legal advice | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN`; outside county Focus authority |

### 6.3 Infrastructure and operational precision

The plan treats exact active wells, storage, pipelines, facilities, operating
status, and vulnerability as excluded or deferred. A dated official map may
contain symbols, but that does not make those symbols current or safe for public
operational use.

Public Wilson surfaces must not:

- expose exact current infrastructure or active-well detail by default;
- infer operating status from dated cartography;
- identify vulnerabilities, tactical access, or emergency-response weaknesses;
- turn historic industrial location into current asset intelligence;
- use map completeness or absence as proof that an asset exists or does not exist.

### 6.4 Burial, cemetery, cultural, and sovereign authority

The plan notes that public maps may contain cemetery labels and that local
historical language may touch culturally sensitive subjects. First-slice public
content must not expose named burial/person linkage, fine burial precision, or
culturally authoritative interpretation merely because a public document exists.

Required posture:

- `DENY` detailed burial/person linkage and sensitive precision by default;
- `DEFER` cultural interpretation until appropriate Nation, Tribal, archival,
  cultural, or other competent authority and review are identified;
- preserve source wording as attributable historical evidence rather than KFM's
  own cultural truth;
- record any generalization, omission, redaction, or access-tier transform and
  its reason.

### 6.5 Flood, road, river, and emergency currentness

Static history and dated maps may support contextual geography only. They may
not answer whether flooding is occurring, roads are closed, water is safe,
conditions are hazardous, or emergency action is required.

The safe path is `ABSTAIN` plus a validated current-authority redirect where one
is configured. A redirect is not an `ANSWER` about the underlying condition.

## 7. Finite outcomes and evidence closure

The repository-present semantic contract names four finite outcomes:
`ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, and `ERROR`. Its own status remains proposed, so
this README uses the vocabulary as an existing planning boundary rather than
claiming active Wilson runtime enforcement.

| Outcome | Wilson use | Minimum evidence/state |
|---|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | A narrow historical or contextual statement supported by admitted evidence | Resolved `EvidenceRef` → `EvidenceBundle`, source role, time basis, policy decision, citations, limitations, and released public-safe carrier |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence is absent, stale, role-inappropriate, or insufficient for the requested current conclusion | Explicit reason and, where appropriate, a validated official-authority redirect |
| `DENY` | The request would expose prohibited precision, property/access determination, unsafe infrastructure detail, burial/person linkage, or another policy-prohibited inference | Policy reason and no sensitive payload leakage |
| `ERROR` | Resolver, schema, policy, runtime, or system operation failed | Error is not converted into a truth or safety conclusion |

### Evidence closure sequence

A future Wilson public claim must follow the governed order:

1. define the exact claim, place, time basis, audience, and requested precision;
2. identify an admitted `SourceDescriptor` with authority, rights, date, and limitations;
3. retrieve or derive through the governed lifecycle;
4. resolve every `EvidenceRef` to an admissible `EvidenceBundle`;
5. apply environmental-currentness, property/access, infrastructure, burial/cultural, hazard, and release policy;
6. validate citations and prohibited-inference boundaries;
7. obtain accountable review and a promotion decision;
8. bind a release manifest, correction route, supersession target, and rollback target;
9. serve only the released public-safe carrier through governed interfaces;
10. return a finite outcome with citations, limitations, and traceable reason codes.

A fluent story, map adjacency, document presence, passing check, branch, PR, or
merge cannot substitute for any missing gate.

### Plan-derived reason-code candidates

The sibling plan proposes reason-code families for tests and future runtime
behavior. They are **not** active policy or contract authority.

| Risk family | Candidate plan vocabulary |
|---|---|
| Unresolved evidence | `EVIDENCE_BUNDLE_UNRESOLVED`, `CITE_OR_ABSTAIN_REQUIRED` |
| History-to-current-environment overclaim | `INDUSTRIAL_HISTORY_NOT_CURRENT_ENVIRONMENTAL_STATUS`, `NO_CURRENT_ENVIRONMENTAL_EVIDENCE` |
| Health or site-safety authority absent | `NO_CURRENT_HEALTH_AUTHORITY`, `CURRENT_SITE_SAFETY_NOT_ESTABLISHED` |

- a promotion, release, correction, withdrawal, rollback, deployment, or public endpoint is proposed;
- repository evidence contradicts a `CONFIRMED` statement in this file.

### Documentation correction path

A correction to this README should:

1. identify the incorrect or stale statement;
2. cite the repository or governed evidence that requires the correction;
3. preserve the distinction between repository fact, plan-derived proposal,
   external fact, and unresolved verification;
4. update affected links, status tables, and maintenance notes together;
5. preserve review, supersession, and rollback lineage;
6. avoid changing source, policy, release, or publication state through prose.

### Rollback

Before merge, close or abandon the draft pull request and branch. Branch deletion
is a separate action. After merge, use a transparent revert or bounded forward-fix
against the actual merged commit; do not force-rewrite shared history.

A repository revert would restore documentation bytes only. It would not
automatically correct or withdraw any future map, API payload, model answer,
cache, release alias, evidence bundle, public artifact, or external consumer.
Any future public Wilson artifact requires its own correction, withdrawal,
supersession, cache-invalidation, and rollback controls.

### Current non-effects

- recheck or admit the plan-named external sources;
- confirm Norman No. 1, Neodesha, Verdigris River, Fall River, or other external
  factual claims beyond reporting what the sibling plan contains;
- determine current contamination, exposure, health, drinking-water, ecological,
  cleanup, compliance, site-safety, visitor, property, access, infrastructure,
  flood, road, river, or emergency conditions;
- create or amend a contract, schema, policy, source registry, evidence bundle,
  fixture, validator, API, map, model adapter, release, correction, or rollback object;
- migrate the county lane or create a parallel plural/kebab-case tree;
- promote, release, deploy, publish, or authorize public use.
