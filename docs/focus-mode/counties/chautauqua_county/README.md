## 4. Proposed county proof slice

The build plan proposes a county proof slice centered on four connected but non-interchangeable concerns:

1. **Generalized landscape interpretation** — Chautauqua Hills and Cross Timbers context.
2. **Draft-state literacy** — explanation of a floodplain-review process without treating draft mapping as final.
3. **Property and legal restraint** — exclusion of owner-linked, parcel-specific, title, insurance, zoning, value, access, liability, or compensation conclusions.
4. **Current-authority routing** — official road, bridge, and emergency channels without turning static KFM content into live safety guidance.

| Proposed question | Bounded planning answer | Required hold |
|---|---|---|
| What landscape story could the county lane explain? | Generalized geology and ecology with visible evidence and limitations. | No source activation or scientific claim until evidence is reverified. |
| What can be said about floodplain change? | The existence and meaning of a draft review process. | No final parcel, legal, insurance, or regulatory determination. |
| What can be shown about property? | A public explanation of why person-linked and parcel-sensitive material is withheld. | No owner list, title inference, valuation, or access conclusion. |
| What can be said about roads or emergencies? | The role of official county channels and the need for current, expiring information. | No cached present-tense road, bridge, evacuation, shelter, or alert answer. |
| What could the map carry? | Released, generalized, public-safe contextual layers only. | No direct internal-store, draft-personal, sensitive-location, or operational-detail path. |

This thesis is **PROPOSED**. It is not evidence that the named cards, layers, panels, payloads, routes, fixtures, policies, or release objects exist.

## 5. Source-role anti-collapse

The linked plan records source checks dated 2026-06-08. Those checks were not repeated in this update. Each source family must be reverified and admitted for its actual role before use.

| Source family named by the plan | Candidate role | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific geology material | Regional geologic and geomorphic interpretation. | Property, access, current hazard, road-safety, or legal authority. |
| Broad ecology material | Generalized ecosystem context. | Exact rare-species, nest, roost, habitat, or stewardship-location disclosure. |
| County homepage and departments | Administrative routing and dated public context. | Durable truth about current incidents, road conditions, ownership, or legal effect. |
| Draft floodplain project material | Process, draft-state, and public-comment context. | Final effective-map, parcel, insurance, title, zoning, value, or liability determination. |
| Emergency-management material | Official-current channel and system-role routing. | An active alert, evacuation instruction, shelter order, or substitute emergency authority. |
| Road and bridge material | Department role and official-current routing. | A structural, passability, opening, closure, route, or safety guarantee. |
| Future final flood sources | Effective-map context after admission and legal-scope review. | Legal advice, insurance advice, or parcel-specific certainty beyond source fitness. |
| Generated language | Bounded explanation over resolved evidence. | Evidence, policy, approval, current operational status, or publication authority. |

### Minimum source-admission questions

Before any source participates in a public claim, verify:

- stable source identity and issuing authority;
- source role and fitness for the exact claim;
- retrieval and publication date, valid time, and expiry behavior;
- draft, final, superseded, withdrawn, or corrected state;
- rights, attribution, redistribution, and derivative-use terms;
- property, privacy, living-person, ecology, infrastructure, and harmful-precision implications;
- correction and withdrawal behavior;
- whether every consequential claim resolves through `EvidenceRef` to `EvidenceBundle`.

<a id="6-time-draft-state-and-currentness"></a>

## 6. Time, draft state, and currentness

Chautauqua County's proposed proof slice is time-sensitive in more than one way. These dimensions must remain distinct:

| Time or state | Meaning | Failure posture |
|---|---|---|
| Source publication time | When an official page, map, notice, or dataset was issued. | Do not substitute retrieval time. |
| Retrieval/check time | When KFM last verified the source. | Mark stale or abstain after its fitness window. |
| Valid/effective time | When a map, rule, condition, or decision applies. | A draft or proposed item cannot silently acquire effective status. |
| Observation time | When a measured or observed condition occurred. | Do not present it as a present condition without support. |
| Expiry time | When road, emergency, or operational status stops being safely reusable. | Suppress or redirect if absent or expired. |
| Review time | When a qualified reviewer assessed evidence, rights, sensitivity, or release fitness. | No implied approval from document date. |
| Release time | When a governed release decision became effective. | A commit or merge is not release time. |
| Correction time | When a prior claim was corrected, superseded, withdrawn, or rolled back. | Preserve lineage and prevent stale reappearance. |

### Draft-to-final rule

A draft floodplain or process artifact may support a narrowly worded statement about the existence of a draft process. It must not support a claim that:

- a parcel is definitively inside or outside an effective floodplain;
- insurance, title, zoning, valuation, compensation, or liability changed;
- a person owns, occupies, or is affected by a specific property;
- the draft remains current after later adoption, revision, withdrawal, or supersession.

### Current-authority rule

Road, bridge, evacuation, shelter, infrastructure-emergency, missing-person, and similar operational information requires an official-current source and explicit expiry. Without that closure, the runtime must **ABSTAIN** and route the user to the responsible authority.

<a id="7-public-safe-and-sensitive-boundaries"></a>

## 7. Public-safe and sensitive boundaries

| Requested or implied content | Default outcome | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Generalized Chautauqua Hills or Cross Timbers interpretation | `ANSWER` only after evidence and release closure | Public-safe contextual interpretation may be possible. |
| Draft floodplain-process explanation | `ANSWER` only with an explicit draft-state limitation | Process literacy is distinct from final legal effect. |
| Parcel-specific final floodplain status | `ABSTAIN` | Requires authoritative effective evidence and bounded legal fitness. |
| Owner, occupant, contact, or person-linked affected-property detail | `DENY` | Living-person and property linkage is unnecessary for the public proof slice. |
| Title, access, insurance, zoning, value, liability, or compensation conclusion | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` | KFM is not the responsible legal or administrative authority. |
| Present road or bridge opening, passability, safety, or routing | `ABSTAIN` | Requires current official authority, time, and expiry. |
| Active emergency, evacuation, shelter, or protective-action guidance | `ABSTAIN` | Users must follow the responsible official-current channel. |
| Exact rare-species, sensitive habitat, nest, roost, cave, cultural, burial, or archaeological location | `DENY` | Harmful precision and sovereignty/sensitivity concerns fail closed. |
| Scenic or geologic context treated as land-access permission | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` | A map or explanation does not grant access. |
| Tactical infrastructure detail or vulnerability inference | `DENY` | Public explanation must not aid harmful reconstruction. |
| Unsupported cross-source inference | `ABSTAIN` | Multiple sources do not automatically create a valid joined claim. |
| Public access to RAW, WORK, QUARANTINE, internal policy, or direct model output | `ERROR` | Violates the governed trust membrane. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A denial should not confirm that restricted detail exists. Public responses should state the applicable boundary without exposing hidden values, locations, transform thresholds, or internal policy reasoning.
