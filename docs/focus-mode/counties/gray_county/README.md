# Gray County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Gray County planning lane, make its
> wind-energy authority, infrastructure precision, agriculture aggregation,
> suppression, private-property, worker and demographic privacy, municipal-water,
> groundwater, operational-currentness, rights, and public-safety boundaries
> visible, and route future work through KFM evidence, policy, review, release,
> correction, and rollback controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation or release evidence.**
> This directory contains a one-byte literal `y` predecessor README and one substantial
> Gray County build plan at the pinned base. Replacing the placeholder gives
> maintainers a boundary surface; it does not establish source admission,
> facility identity or status, safe public geometry, semantic correctness,
> validator success, runtime behavior, release, deployment, or publication.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains unresolved.** This same-path update repairs an existing
> README under `docs/focus-mode/counties/gray_county/`. Accepted Directory
> Rules v2 treats Focus Mode as a composition scope; proposed ADR-0027 describes
> a different plural, kebab-case control plane but remains unaccepted. No file is
> moved, renamed, copied into a parallel tree, or declared the final canonical
> county write target here.

> [!WARNING]
> **The build plan combines public context with sensitive and high-consequence
> subjects.** County agriculture and demographic aggregates must not become
> farms, facilities, workers, immigration status, households, or suppressed
> values. Municipal promotion and state energy statistics must not become
> current plant, ownership, regulatory, operational, or grid truth. Exact
> turbines, collector lines, substations, utilities, wells, feedlots, dairies,
> processing facilities, emergency assets, leases, owners, and vulnerabilities
> must not reach an ordinary public client. Stale water, road, weather, fire,
> shelter, utility, or emergency records must not become current safety advice.
> Missing evidence, rights, currentness, safe resolution, policy, release, or
> correction closure must produce a bounded `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR`
> result rather than a fluent inference.

## 5. Public safety and governed outcomes

| Request or output class | Bounded posture | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Released, broad, time-labeled county context with resolving citations | `ANSWER` only when evidence, policy, review, and release conditions pass | Keeps the result within admitted public-safe evidence |
| Missing, stale, conflicting, overly coarse, or unresolvable evidence | `ABSTAIN` | Fluency cannot substitute for evidence, source role, or currentness |
| Municipal wind-history or promotional material used as current plant technical, ownership, regulatory, operational, lease, safety, or grid authority | `ABSTAIN` | Local administrative or promotional context has a narrower authority role |
| State energy statistics used as proof of a specific Gray County facility's current capacity, generation, ownership, operation, or status | `ABSTAIN` | State scale and reporting period do not establish plant truth |
| Exact turbine, collector-line, substation, transmission, utility, airport, well, feedlot, dairy, processing-facility, control, maintenance, access-road, or emergency geometry | `DENY` | Prevents operational, infrastructure, property, and physical-security harm |
| Wind-facility, grid, utility, agriculture, water, road, or emergency weak-point, dependency, or vulnerability analysis | `DENY` | Public context must not become operational intelligence |
| Owner, parcel, lease, easement, royalty, tax, title, compensation, access, operator, worker, employer, immigration-status, household, or living-person profile | `DENY` | Prevents reidentification, property, employment, demographic, and personal harm |
| County agriculture, cattle, dairy, irrigation, Census, or business aggregate used to identify an operation, person, household, field, facility, or suppressed value | `DENY` | Aggregates and disclosure flags must not become reconstructive detail |
| Irrigated acreage or other aggregate evidence used as a private-well, water-right, allocation, aquifer-condition, environmental, or compliance conclusion | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN`, according to accepted policy | Prevents statistical, scientific, property, regulatory, and legal role collapse |
| Municipal report or facility evidence used for household water safety, private-well condition, exposure, contamination source, illness, diagnosis, liability, or current compliance | `DENY` | Prevents stale, individualized, causal, health, and regulatory overclaim |
| Road, weather, fire, tornado, shelter, utility, landfill, water, or emergency material used as a live alert, route, outage, or safety instruction without current authority | `ABSTAIN` and route to the responsible current authority | Operational information expires quickly; KFM is not the emergency authority |
| Unverified rights, source admission, safe geometry, suppression, currentness, EvidenceBundle closure, or independent review | `ABSTAIN` | Public visibility and source access do not prove safe derivative use or answer readiness |
| Resolver, policy, carrier, citation, or runtime failure | `ERROR` with no unsupported fallback claim | Failure must remain visible and fail closed |

Client-side hiding is not a sensitivity control. Protected detail must not be
delivered to an ordinary public client, included in public logs or analytics, or
reconstructed from tiles, filters, search, exports, caches, identifiers, or AI
responses.

### Minimum-display rule

Even publicly visible source material does not automatically belong in a KFM
public derivative. Display the minimum resolution and attribute set required for
the reviewed public purpose. Generalization, suppression, and omission require
traceable transform reasoning; a link to an official public source remains
preferable when KFM cannot prove safe derivative use.

The same rule applies to cross-layer reconstruction. Separate public cards must
not combine into a farm, facility, owner, lease, royalty, worker, immigration,
household, private-water, property, utility, infrastructure, or vulnerability
profile that no single card displays.

## 7. Evidence and readiness boundary

A Gray County public result must remain downstream of the governed KFM path:

1. identify a source once in the source-authority surface;
2. review authority, rights, access, time, geography, sensitivity, and correction
   behavior;
3. admit candidate material to `RAW`, or hold it in `WORK / QUARANTINE` when
   requirements are unresolved;
4. derive and validate `PROCESSED` material with transform and provenance
   lineage;
5. resolve every consequential `EvidenceRef` to an `EvidenceBundle` and apply
   policy;
6. make a separate promotion and release decision with correction and rollback
   targets;
7. serve only released public-safe artifacts through governed interfaces;
8. keep map rendering and AI interpretation subordinate to that evidence and
   decision state.
