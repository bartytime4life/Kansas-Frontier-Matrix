## 4. Proposed Jefferson County proof slice

The sibling plan proposes a **Jefferson Reservoir-Context Trust Membrane Proof**.
Its central question is whether KFM can explain a managed-water landscape and
related public context without becoming an operational, safety, property,
ecology-exposure, or cultural-site disclosure product.

| Theme in the sibling plan | Bounded future use | Required limit |
|---|---|---|
| Perry Lake and Delaware River context | Time-bounded, source-labeled landscape explanation | No current operations, release, level, closure, safety, or vulnerability inference |
| Ozawkie and reservoir-era place change | Cited public-history context | No property, burial, archaeology, cultural-authority, or exact sensitive-place inference |
| Floodplain and official source routing | Explain source roles and direct users to responsible current authorities | No parcel risk, permit, insurance, title, engineering, or emergency conclusion |
| Reservoir protection and watershed programs | Program-purpose context | No individual landowner eligibility, blame, compliance, water-quality, or health conclusion |

## 5. Public safety, sensitivity, and finite outcomes

### Fail-closed request matrix

| Request or display class | Expected bounded posture | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Bounded, released, time-labeled Jefferson landscape context with resolving evidence | `ANSWER` | Evidence, policy, source role, limitations, and release state are visible |
| Missing, stale, conflicting, revoked, or rights-unclear evidence | `ABSTAIN` | The system cannot support a trustworthy claim |
| Current boating, camping, crossing, reservoir, release, closure, or flood-safety advice | `DENY` and route to responsible current authority | KFM is not an operational or emergency service |
| Dam, outlet, protection-system, or vulnerability detail beyond public educational need | `DENY` / exclude from the public derivative | Operational and infrastructure risk |
| Parcel-specific flood, permit, insurance, title, engineering, or safe-property conclusion | `DENY` | Property and regulatory authority remain external |
| Exact sensitive habitat, wildlife concentration, management-significant place, archaeology, burial, cemetery, or culturally sensitive location | `DENY` | Harmful precision and misuse risk |
| Aggregate agriculture joined to a farm, family, person, owner, lease, or parcel | `DENY` | Privacy, property, and statistical misuse |
| Historical or program context presented as current water quality, health, compliance, or individual responsibility | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` | Source-role and time-basis collapse |
| Direct UI access to `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, candidate geometry, or model output | `ERROR` / `DENY` | Trust-membrane bypass |
| Runtime, schema, evidence, or policy failure | `ERROR` | A bounded fault must remain visible without leaking protected detail |

### Cross-layer reconstruction rule

A field that appears harmless alone may become sensitive when joined with
historic overlays, parcels, infrastructure, wildlife, agriculture, or current
observations. Future validation must test both individual layers and the
combined display or query context. Redaction in one carrier is not sufficient if
another carrier reconstructs the denied fact.

### Public-interface requirements

Any later public Jefferson surface must make these states visible and accessible:

- `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, and `ERROR`;
- evidence missing, stale, conflicting, corrected, superseded, restricted, or
  revoked;
- source role and time basis;
- generalized or withheld geometry;
- correction and withdrawal state;
- an official-source route where KFM is not the responsible authority.

A fluent response, polished map, passing schema check, or green workflow must not
hide a negative state.

## 6. Source role, rights, and currentness boundary

The build plan names source families that were checked during its 2026-05-22
authoring run. This README does not refresh those external checks and does not
admit any source.

| Plan-named source family | Proposed role | Must not be collapsed into |
|---|---|---|
| Jefferson County public surfaces | Administrative, floodplain-routing, and public-history context | Complete cultural authority, parcel judgment, current emergency instruction, or title evidence |
| U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Perry material | Federal project and public reservoir context | KFM operational guidance, engineering assessment, current release state, or vulnerability analysis |
| FEMA and Kansas flood-information surfaces | Official flood-hazard source routing and effective-map context | KFM parcel verdict, insurance decision, permit decision, or safety guarantee |
| Kansas Water Office program material | State water-planning and watershed-program context | Current water-quality diagnosis, individual eligibility, blame, or compliance finding |
| Kansas wildlife and park material | Public recreation and ecology context | Safe-current-access guarantee or unrestricted republication of sensitive location detail |
| Kansas agriculture statistics | County-level statistical aggregate | Farm, owner, family, parcel, productivity, responsibility, or compliance truth |
| Kansas Geological Survey material | Scientific and historical geologic context | Site-specific engineering, water-supply, contamination, or health conclusion |
| U.S. Geological Survey observation surface | Potential dated observation source | Alert, forecast, official instruction, or direct raw public feed |

### Admission questions for every source

Before a source can support a public claim, record and review:

1. stable source identity, publisher, authority role, and exact version or
   retrieval state;
2. publication, effective, observation, and retrieval times as distinct fields;
3. rights, attribution, extraction, transformation, redistribution, and
   derivative-display terms;
4. allowed claim scope and explicitly prohibited inference;
5. sensitivity, minimum necessary detail, safe spatial and temporal resolution,
   and cross-layer reconstruction risk;
6. freshness, outage, correction, revision, supersession, and withdrawal
   behavior;
7. deterministic provenance from candidate material through any public-safe
   derivative;
8. an `EvidenceRef` that resolves to an admissible `EvidenceBundle`;
9. policy, accountable review, release, correction, and rollback references.

### Known dated-plan ambiguity

The sibling plan records an apparent year-label inconsistency in a plan-named
agricultural page. That remains `NEEDS VERIFICATION`. A future implementation
must resolve the underlying authoritative period rather than repeat a convenient
number or silently choose one label.

Public access alone is not permission to extract, transform, join, tile,
redistribute, or publish. An official source role also does not make every field
necessary or safe for a KFM public derivative.

## 7. Evidence and readiness boundary

A Jefferson public result must remain downstream of the governed KFM path:

1. identify a source once in the appropriate source-authority surface;
2. review authority, rights, access, time, geography, sensitivity, correction,
   and minimum-necessary use;
3. admit candidate material to `RAW`, or hold it in `WORK / QUARANTINE`;
4. derive and validate `PROCESSED` material with transform and provenance
   lineage;
5. create catalog or triplet relationships without treating indexes as truth;
6. resolve every consequential `EvidenceRef` to an `EvidenceBundle` and apply
   policy;
7. make a separate promotion and release decision with correction, withdrawal,
   and rollback targets;
8. serve only released public-safe artifacts through governed interfaces;
9. keep map rendering and AI interpretation subordinate to that evidence and
   decision state.
