# Chase County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Chase County planning lane, make its Flint Hills, tallgrass-prairie, ranching, river, floodplain, geology, transportation, heritage, prescribed-fire, property, and sensitive-location boundaries visible, and route future work through KFM evidence, policy, review, release, correction, and rollback controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation or release evidence.** This directory contains this README and one Chase County build plan. Their presence prevents accidental duplicate planning; it does not establish source admission, semantic correctness, validator success, runtime behavior, release, deployment, or publication.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains unresolved.** This same-path update modernizes the existing README under `docs/focus-mode/counties/chase_county/`. Accepted Directory Rules v2 treats Focus Mode as a composition scope; proposed ADR-0027 describes a different plural, kebab-case control plane but remains unaccepted. No file is moved, renamed, copied into a parallel tree, or declared the final canonical county write target here.

> [!WARNING]
> **Landscape interpretation must not become harmful precision or operational advice.** Public prairie, geology, ranching, river, floodplain, transportation, and heritage context cannot support exact sensitive-species or cultural-site exposure, live burn operations, private-property or title conclusions, emergency guidance, infrastructure-vulnerability analysis, or current road-status claims without appropriately scoped evidence and authority. Missing evidence, currentness, rights, safe resolution, policy, review, or release closure must produce a bounded `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR` result rather than a fluent inference.

## 5. Source-role and cross-domain boundaries

The build plan names source families that answer materially different questions. They must not be merged into one undifferentiated Chase County truth layer.

| Planned source or object class | Legitimate bounded role | Must not be treated as |
|---|---|---|
| Public preserve and park interpretation | Publicly interpreted preserve, landscape, visitor, geology, and heritage context after current source review | Complete county ecology, sensitive monitoring, permission for exact locations, or cultural authority |
| State or federal agricultural aggregates | Dated county-level statistical context | Individual farm, ranch, producer, landowner, production, or private-operation truth |
| Hydrologic monitoring observation | Time-bound measurement context with units, status, revision, and source identity | Flood warning, emergency advice, regulatory determination, or parcel-specific risk |
| Floodplain viewer or program page | Routing to current effective or project-state authority | Permit, insurance, title, legal boundary, or property decision |
| Geological survey or interpretive material | Educational geologic and geohydrologic context within stated scope | Current operational water supply, mineral rights, engineering suitability, or parcel hazard |
| Archived transportation notice | Dated historical transport event and a stale-state negative fixture | Current road closure, route condition, or travel instruction |
| Current transportation authority | Present service state only after source, cadence, rights, and release review | Historic interpretation, emergency authority outside its source role, or unrestricted infrastructure detail |
| Public heritage interpretation | Narrative about expressly public and interpreted assets | Archaeological survey, burial or sacred-place disclosure, or inference about undisclosed locations |
| County GIS or property portal | Candidate administrative/reference source after terms, field, privacy, and title review | Automatic reuse permission, title truth, living-person exposure, or evidence closure |
| Fire-and-grazing education | Static public explanation of documented landscape processes | Live incident intelligence, prescribed-fire operations, private contacts, or responder positioning |
| Generated narrative | Evidence-bounded explanation after retrieval, policy, and citation checks | Source truth, rights decision, sensitivity authority, release approval, or proof |

Cross-domain interpretation must preserve the owning lane for each supported claim. A county composition may relate habitat, flora, fauna, agriculture, hydrology, geology, hazards, transportation, settlements/infrastructure, and archaeology/heritage only through governed references. It does not transfer ownership to the county folder.

<a id="public-safety"></a>

## 6. Public-safety and governed outcomes

### Finite outward behavior

| Outcome | Chase County use | Minimum public behavior |
|---|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Released, in-scope evidence resolves and policy allows the requested public-safe claim | Show bounded wording, citations, source role, time basis, limitations, release state, and correction route |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence is missing, stale, unresolved, unsupported for the requested scale/time, or the question asks for a determination KFM does not own | State the evidence gap or scope limit; route to the responsible current authority where appropriate |
| `DENY` | Rights, sensitivity, sovereignty, harmful precision, private-person/property, operational fire, infrastructure, or unreleased-state rules block disclosure | Withhold or use an approved generalized public explanation without exposing control-defeating details |
| `ERROR` | Contract, schema, resolver, policy, citation, release, or runtime processing fails | Fail closed; expose a safe reason code and correction route, not internal paths or protected context |

### County-specific fail-closed matrix

| Request or failure | Required posture | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Exact rare, threatened, vulnerable, nesting, roosting, lek, or other sensitive occurrence | `DENY` or separately reviewed coarse generalization | Ecological harm and geoprivacy risk |
| Inferred occurrence from habitat, terrain, observation absence, or model output | `DENY` | Derived likelihood must not reconstruct protected locations |
| Archaeological, burial, sacred, ceremonial, collection, or undisclosed historic location | `DENY` absent qualified authority and a reviewed safe transform | Sovereignty, cultural sensitivity, and looting risk |
| Live prescribed-fire plan, perimeter, tactical response detail, private contact, or responder position | `DENY` | Operational and public-safety sensitivity |
| Parcel ownership, title, living-person detail, private route, or farm-level inference | `DENY` or omit | County context is not title truth and does not need private-person exposure |
| Parcel-specific flood, permit, insurance, or construction determination | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` | KFM does not own the governing determination |
| Archived road notice represented as current status | `ERROR` and correction | Temporal authority is wrong even when the archive itself is authentic |
| Historical water or geology material represented as current operations | `ABSTAIN` or `ERROR` | Documentary evidence cannot silently become present operational truth |
| Exact infrastructure vulnerability or non-public utility detail | `DENY` or reviewed generalization | Security and resilience sensitivity |
| Missing EvidenceBundle, policy decision, review, release, or rollback closure | `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR` according to the failed gate | A fluent explanation cannot replace trust closure |
| Public client reaches RAW, WORK, QUARANTINE, unpublished candidate, canonical store, or direct model output | `DENY` and security correction | Trust-membrane violation |

Safe public reduction is a governed transform, not a style trick. Generalization, redaction, aggregation, suppression, or delay must occur before the public carrier is built and must retain a reason, transform identity, policy/review reference, and correction lineage appropriate to the risk. Client-side hiding alone is not a control.

## 8. Smallest safe follow-up

The smallest dependency-closed next slice is a **Chase build-plan reconciliation**, not a live source connector or public map.

That follow-up should:

1. read the complete current build plan and then-current repository authority surfaces;
