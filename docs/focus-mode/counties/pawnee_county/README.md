# Pawnee County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Pawnee County planning lane, make its
> cultural-authority, conflict-memory, burial/archaeology, flood/current-water,
> agriculture/privacy, property, visitor-safety, and currentness boundaries
> visible, and route future work through KFM evidence, policy, qualified review,
> correction, rollback, and release controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation or release evidence.**
> This directory contains this README and one Pawnee County build plan. Their
> presence establishes planning lineage only; it does not admit a source,
> resolve evidence, activate policy, deploy a product, or publish county
> knowledge.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Federal historic-site interpretation is not Nation-authoritative history.**
> Fort Larned and Santa Fe Trail material may support bounded, attributed public
> context, but KFM must not speak for affected Indigenous Nations, present a
> military or tourism narrative as complete history, or convert incomplete
> authority into generated certainty.

> [!WARNING]
> **Burial, archaeology, live water, and public-safety questions fail closed.**
> Exact cemetery, burial, archaeology, artifact, subsurface-target, or culturally
> sensitive locations must not be exposed. Dated river, floodplain, visitor,
> road, health, or operational material must not become present-tense safety,
> access, legal, property, or emergency advice.

## 4. Proposed county proof slice

The linked plan proposes a Pawnee County proof slice centered on Fort Larned,
the Santa Fe Trail, Pawnee River context, Buffalo Soldier history, conflict
memory, county agriculture, and floodplain/current-water restraint. Its value is
not simply a historic-place map. Its value is testing whether KFM can keep
source roles, historical authority, cultural sovereignty, sensitive locations,
currentness, and public-safety limits visible in one county composition.

| Proposed element | Intended value | Required boundary | Current status |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Fort Larned public context | Publicly interpretable federal historic-site orientation. | NPS interpretation is a source role, not a complete or Nation-authoritative history. | `PROPOSED` |
| Santa Fe Trail context | Explain travel, expansion, and conflict in bounded historical context. | No romanticized or military-only narrative presented as complete truth. | `PROPOSED` |
| Buffalo Soldier context | Preserve African American military-history context named by the plan. | Must remain source-attributed and historically scoped. | `PROPOSED` |
| Indigenous conflict-memory context | Surface authority gaps and the need for Nation-aware review. | KFM must not speak for Nations or fill cultural-authority gaps with generated prose. | `PROPOSED` |
| Cemetery/burial sensitivity | Make burial restraint visible at the product boundary. | No discovery, targeting, artifact hunting, precise cemetery feature, or subsurface layer. | `PROPOSED` |
| Pawnee River/floodplain context | Future bounded hydrology and floodplain orientation. | No current crossing, flood, insurance, permit, property-risk, or safety verdict. | `PROPOSED` |
| Agriculture | County-level public aggregates and land-use context. | No individual farm/operator or property inference. | `PROPOSED` |

### Composition rule

A future Pawnee County Focus Mode may relate these themes only through governed,
released identifiers. It must not reconstruct denied information by combining
public history, cadastral, trail, imagery, hydrology, or cultural layers.

## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The build plan records source families checked during its 2026-05-22 authoring
run. This README preserves those as **dated source seeds**, not newly admitted or
refreshed sources.

| Candidate source role | What it may support after admission | What it must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| National Park Service historic-site and trail interpretation | Attributed federal public-history context, site orientation, and source-native interpretation. | Nation-authoritative history, unrestricted cultural authority, burial-discovery evidence, or a complete account of conflict. |
| Future Nation-authoritative or community sources | Cultural and historical context within their stated authority. | A universal substitute for all Nations or all historical questions. |
| County civic/landmark sources | General county/place orientation. | Legal property, access, current safety, or cultural authority. |
| USGS/KGS/KDA or other scientific/admin sources | Hydrology, geology, floodplain, or agricultural context at stated time/scale. | Current safety, legal, insurance, property, or water-right determinations. |
| Generated KFM narrative | Explanation of already released evidence. | Root truth, missing cultural authority, or release permission. |

Future source use should preserve at least observation/publication/retrieval,
validity, release, correction, and supersession time where material.

## 6. Cultural authority, conflict memory, and burial boundary

### Indigenous/Nation authority

The build plan explicitly identifies an authority gap: no appropriate
Nation-authoritative Fort Larned-specific public source or cultural-review path
was established in its initial source pass. That gap must remain visible.
Generated prose, federal interpretation, tourism material, or repeated citations
must not be used to simulate Nation authority.

### Conflict memory

A public-safe product should distinguish military expansion, trail traffic,
conflict, coercion, Indigenous displacement, African American military service,
and later interpretation rather than collapsing them into a celebratory visitor
story. Which claims are supportable remains source- and review-dependent.

### Burial and archaeology

Potential or known burial, cemetery, archaeological, artifact, sacred-place, or
subsurface detail is not appropriate for discovery-oriented public mapping.
Requests for harmful precision, artifact-finding help, or targeting information
should return `DENY` or a generalized response according to applicable policy.

<a id="7-water-flood-property-and-current-safety-boundaries"></a>

## 7. Water, flood, property, and current-safety boundaries

### Pawnee River and floodplain

Future released layers may provide dated, general hydrologic or floodplain
context. They must not become real-time flood, crossing, insurance, engineering,
permit, parcel-risk, or emergency determinations.

### Property and access

Historic or public-place context does not establish modern land ownership,
legal access, easement, boundary, permit, or title rights. When a question needs
those authorities, KFM should abstain or redirect.

### Visitor and operational safety

A cached park page, road map, water observation, floodplain layer, or generated
summary does not establish current visitor access, weather, river condition,
road status, closure, health advisory, or emergency guidance. Current operational
questions require current authoritative channels.

### Agriculture and privacy

County agricultural aggregates may eventually support bounded explanation.
They must not be combined with parcels, imagery, addresses, or other layers to
identify or infer a specific farm, operator, household, or private activity.

<a id="8-finite-outcomes-and-candidate-reason-codes"></a>

## 8. Finite outcomes and candidate reason codes

Future Pawnee County runtime behavior should preserve four terminal outcomes:

| Outcome | Pawnee County use |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Released, in-scope evidence supports a bounded claim and required citations, policy, review, and release checks pass. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Cultural authority, evidence, currentness, legal fitness, or source role is insufficient for the requested claim. |
| `DENY` | Rights, sovereignty, burial/archaeology sensitivity, privacy, harmful precision, or public-safety policy blocks the requested detail. |
| `ERROR` | A required resolver, validator, policy evaluator, release lookup, or runtime component fails. |

The sibling plan also proposes county-specific reason-code families. Treat those
identifiers as **PROPOSED vocabulary only** until shared contracts, schemas,
policy, fixtures, validators, and reviewers agree. Candidate themes include:

- Nation-authority or cultural-review gap;
- burial/archaeology precision denied;
- conflict-memory support incomplete;
- live flood/current-water authority required;
- property/legal authority required;
- individual-farm/privacy inference denied;
- source rights/currentness unresolved; and
- runtime/integrity failure.

Restrictions should remain attached to one of the four terminal outcomes rather
than becoming an ungoverned fifth outcome.

## 9. Readiness boundary

Before any Pawnee County Focus composition can be treated as public-ready,
future work should establish at least:

1. current source identities, versions, rights, roles, and permitted uses;
2. an accountable cultural/Nation-authority review pathway appropriate to the
   claims being made;
3. safe geometry and transform rules for Fort Larned, cemetery/burial,
   archaeology, trail, and private-land relationships;
4. explicit temporal fitness for hydrology, floodplain, road, visitor, and other
   operationally changing material;
5. `EvidenceRef -> EvidenceBundle` closure for consequential claims;
6. policy and negative tests for burial/archaeology precision, cultural-authority
   gaps, privacy, property, current safety, and harmful recombination;
7. governed API/UI behavior for `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, and `ERROR`;
8. review, release, correction, withdrawal, cache-invalidation, and rollback
   records appropriate to public consequence.

A README, build plan, passing Markdown check, map preview, pull request, or merge
would not by itself establish those conditions.

## 10. Smallest safe follow-up

The smallest useful implementation candidate is a **no-network synthetic trust-
boundary packet**, not a live Fort Larned, cultural, burial, or water connector.

| Artifact class | Proposed test content | Required result |
|---|---|---|
| Synthetic source descriptors | Fictional federal-history, Nation-authority-needed, hydrology, and agriculture roles. | Source roles remain distinguishable. |
| Valid fixtures | Generalized public-history context with explicit authority limitations and no sensitive geometry. | Deterministic `ANSWER` candidate only when evidence/policy references are complete. |
| Invalid cultural-authority fixture | Federal interpretation is presented as complete Nation history. | Fail closed with `ABSTAIN` or `DENY`. |
| Invalid burial/archaeology fixture | Exact cemetery/burial/subsurface detail requested. | `DENY`. |
| Invalid current-water fixture | Stale river/flood material used for present safety guidance. | `ABSTAIN`. |
| Invalid property/privacy fixture | Public layers recombined to infer private ownership, access, or farm activity. | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` according to policy. |
| Validator tests | Stable reason mapping and evidence/currentness checks. | Deterministic no-network behavior. |

Stop if implementation requires live-source activation, protected cultural
material, private records, exact sensitive locations, policy-significant behavior
without review, or release/publication state.
