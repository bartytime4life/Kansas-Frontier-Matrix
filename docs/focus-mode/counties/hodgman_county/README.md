# Hodgeman County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to Hodgeman County's planning lane, make the
> current `hodgman_county` spelling drift visible, and preserve the plan's
> reservoir, lake, hunting, WIHA, archaeology, ecology, agriculture, water,
> property, health, emergency, infrastructure, rights, and currentness
> boundaries without turning planning material into operational truth.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation or release evidence.**
> This compatibility directory contains this README and one Hodgeman County
> build plan. Their presence establishes planning lineage only; it does not
> admit a source, resolve evidence, activate policy, validate a payload, deploy
> a product, or publish county knowledge.

> [!CAUTION]
> **County identity and path spelling are different facts.** The county is
> spelled **Hodgeman**. The tracked directory and plan filename currently use
> `hodgman`. This update preserves those paths for compatibility. It does not
> approve the misspelling, complete a rename, create a corrected parallel tree,
> or resolve the migration recorded by the county index.

> [!WARNING]
> **Do not use this lane for live safety or access decisions.** It cannot tell a
> user whether a reservoir or lake is safe today, where hunting or WIHA access
> is lawful, who owns land, whether a well is safe, what an active emergency
> requires, or where sensitive archaeological or ecological features are.

## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The build plan lists seven source families checked on 2026-06-11. That list is
planning evidence, not an admitted source ledger.

| Plan source family | Candidate role | It may support only after admission | It cannot establish by itself |
|---|---|---|---|
| County/community site | Administrative directory and public-context candidate | Office/civic redirects and generalized public context with checked date | Live reservoir safety, hunting access, title, water rights, emergency response, or archaeology release |
| Census QuickFacts | Federal statistical candidate | Properly vintaged county statistical context | Current conditions, individual people, property, access, or operations |
| USDA NASS 2022 county profile | Statistical aggregate candidate | Reporting-year county aggregates with suppression rules | Individual farm/operator facts, current operations, parcel status, or water rights |
| KDA Division of Water Resources | Regulatory-authority redirect candidate | Official water-governance routing and source-role explanation | A KFM water-right, permit, impairment, private-well, or potability decision |
| KGS WIZARD | Scientific groundwater-portal candidate | Scientific portal context with time and fitness limits | Potability, legal pumping, private-well condition, ownership, or access |
| KDOT past county maps | Historical map-publication candidate | Map-edition discovery and publication lineage | Live road conditions, closures, construction, legal route, or safe access |
| NWS Dodge City | Current official hazard redirect candidate | A redirect to current official weather/hazard authority | A cached KFM emergency instruction or present safety guarantee |

### Minimum source-admission questions

Before any plan source supports a public claim, verify:

1. What is the stable source identity and responsible authority?
2. What exact claim scope can the source support?
3. What publication, reporting, effective, retrieval, and expiry times apply?
4. What rights, terms, citation, and derivative-display constraints apply?
5. What privacy, cultural, ecological, property, security, or harmful-precision
   restrictions apply?
6. Which geometry source, generalization, and public-safe transformation apply?
7. Which stale, outage, supersession, correction, and rollback behavior applies?
8. Can the EvidenceRef resolve to a reviewed EvidenceBundle?

### Anti-collapse rule

Public visibility is not authority. A tourism page is not a live safety feed;
a directory is not title or emergency truth; an aggregate is not an individual
record; a scientific portal is not a legal or potability determination; a map
edition is not a current road condition; a museum narrative is not permission
to disclose archaeology; and AI output is not evidence.

## 6. Reservoir, hunting, ecology, and public safety

### Minimum necessary display

Any future public representation should prefer generalized county context,
office-level redirects, visible source time, and minimum necessary geometry.
It must avoid access-enabling or vulnerability-ready detail.

| Request pattern | Finite outcome | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| “What public context does the plan describe for HorseThief Reservoir?” | `ANSWER` only with admitted evidence | General context; no current rules, closures, water quality, conditions, or safety. |
| “Is the reservoir or city lake safe today?” | `ABSTAIN` | Requires current official facility, water-quality, weather, and emergency authority. |
| “Where can I hunt without asking?” | `DENY` | Never infer access, permission, current WIHA status, season, license, or ownership. |
| “Show exact wildlife or nesting locations.” | `DENY` | Sensitive ecological precision fails closed. |
| “Which roads are open or safe right now?” | `ABSTAIN` | KDOT map history is not current operational road truth. |
| “Show dam, road, utility, or emergency weak points.” | `DENY` | Tactical or vulnerability-ready infrastructure detail is prohibited. |

### Non-leaking denial

A denial must name only the policy category and safe alternative. It must not
confirm whether a sensitive feature, access route, private parcel, vulnerable
asset, or archaeological/ecological location exists at the requested place.

### Live-authority boundary

KFM may point to an official authority family after current verification. It
must not cache a redirect as current safety truth, repeat an unverified closure
or condition, or imply that a repository commit makes live information fresh.

archaeology, ecology, health, or emergency data to infer access, identity,

assert that the linked page is current, that a user qualifies for access, or
that KFM has resolved the underlying question.

## 8. Finite outcomes and reason codes

Current Focus semantics use exactly four terminal outcomes:

| Outcome | Use | Display requirement |
|---|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Admitted evidence supports a narrow claim and policy permits it. | Show time basis, source role, EvidenceBundle-backed citations, and restrictions. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, authority, rights, currentness, or fitness is insufficient. | State what is missing and provide only a verified safe redirect. |
| `DENY` | The request crosses sensitivity, access, privacy, cultural, ecological, property, security, or harmful-precision policy. | Give a non-leaking reason and no prohibited detail. |
| `ERROR` | Evidence resolution, policy evaluation, validation, or runtime failed. | Return a bounded error; never guess or coerce to `ANSWER`. |

> [!NOTE]
> The plan uses `ANSWER_WITH_LIMITS` in illustrative fixture tables. Unless an
> accepted contract explicitly changes the finite envelope, implement this as
> `ANSWER` plus visible restrictions—not as a fifth terminal outcome.

## 15. Maintenance, migration, correction, and rollback

### Documentation maintenance

- Preserve the `doc_id`, correct county identity, current compatibility path,
  explicit anchors, and planning-only authority until an accepted migration
  says otherwise.
- Update evidence-snapshot hashes when governing repository surfaces are
  materially re-inspected.
- Keep confirmed repository evidence separate from proposed plan content and
  dated external observations.
- Correct stale source, spelling, safety, access, rights, sensitivity, water,
  property, health, emergency, and currentness statements visibly.
- Never set released, published, approved, or public-safe merely because
  documentation, tests, commits, pull requests, or merges exist.

### Migration boundary

A spelling correction is structural work, not an editorial replacement. It
requires exact source and destination paths, `git`-recoverable history,
inbound-reference repair, index and generated-output reconciliation, duplicate-
authority prevention, compatibility/deprecation handling, validator coverage,
and rollback. Never create both `hodgman_county` and `hodgeman_county` as
independent writable lanes.

### Before merge

Close or abandon the unmerged draft PR and feature branch. Remote branch
deletion is a separate administrative action and is not required for rollback.

### After merge

Use a transparent revert or forward-fix PR against the actual merged commit.
Do not rewrite shared history. Revert or correct this README and its generated
receipt together. A future spelling migration must preserve one authority and
must not be reversed in a way that recreates two writable homes.

### Public correction boundary

This documentation-only change creates no source activation, release,
deployment, or publication to undo. If a future public artifact relies on
Hodgeman content, a Git revert alone may be insufficient: the governed
correction path may also require source-state update, release supersession,
withdrawal, cache invalidation, API/UI correction, and retained lineage.
