## Harvey County public-safety boundary

| Request or theme | Default posture until closure | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Released, broad, time-labeled county context with resolving citations | `ANSWER` only after evidence, policy, review, and release pass | Keeps claims bounded to admitted public-safe evidence. |
| Missing, stale, conflicting, inaccessible, over-precise, or unresolvable evidence | `ABSTAIN` | Fluency cannot substitute for evidence, authority, freshness, or safe precision. |
| Official floodplain, property-risk, insurance, permit, engineering, evacuation, or parcel determination | `ABSTAIN` and route to the responsible authority | A contextual map or summary is not an official site-specific determination. |
| Exact well-field, water-supply facility, vulnerability, private-well, or operational infrastructure detail | `DENY` | Public context does not authorize harmful operational or site-specific inference. |
| Parcel owner, title, private address, living-person, occupancy, farm, producer, or property profile | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` | County-scale and public-record context does not prove or justify a combined personal or property profile. |
| Exact archaeology, burial, cemetery plot, sacred or cultural site, or rare-species location | `DENY` or generalize after qualified review | Precision may enable disturbance, harm, exploitation, or unwanted disclosure. |
| Current flood, weather, road, rail, emergency, utility, evacuation, or public-safety instruction | `ABSTAIN` and route to the responsible live authority | Historical, cached, or generated context is not operational authority. |
| Claim with an unresolved `EvidenceRef` | `ERROR` | Missing resolution must remain visible; no unsupported fallback. |
| Public request reaching `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, restricted, or direct-model content | `DENY` | Public clients use governed APIs or released public-safe artifacts only. |

These are documentation-level boundaries, not active policy. They require agreement in the canonical policy surface, evaluator, fixtures, qualified review, runtime binding, and released carrier before any public behavior can be claimed.

## Source-role anti-collapse

A future Harvey slice must keep source roles explicit rather than combining every official or public page into one undifferentiated authority.

| Candidate source family implied by the plan | Permitted planning role | Must not be treated as |
|---|---|---|
| County and municipal GIS or administrative sources | Geography, locally maintained records, or notices within a verified remit | Universal title, boundary, flood, water, emergency, historical, or release authority |
| Floodplain regulators and effective flood-map sources | Regulatory or mapped flood-hazard evidence within an identified version and remit | Property-specific insurance, engineering, permit, evacuation, or safety determination by KFM |
| Groundwater and geoscience authorities | Observed, modeled, or published aquifer and hydrology context within a stated method and date | Operational water-supply, private-well, parcel, contamination, vulnerability, or current-safety evidence |
| Agriculture and soil authorities | Dated aggregate agriculture, soil, or land-characterization context | Producer, operation, parcel, yield, eligibility, compliance, or current-condition evidence |
| Transportation authorities | Route, project, network, or current operational information within the source's remit and time window | Property, legal-access, emergency, vulnerability, or durable universal truth |
| Historical, museum, archive, and cultural sources | Dated interpretation, collections, and public-history evidence | Sole sovereign narrative, living-person authority, or permission to expose sensitive places |
| Archaeology, cemetery, ecology, and protected-resource authorities | Sensitivity and review inputs within qualified governance | Public release authority for exact locations or a substitute for consultation |
| Generated narrative, cards, maps, tiles, and AI output | Downstream interpretation over released evidence | Evidence, sensitivity decision, policy outcome, current alert, review approval, or publication authority |

Public availability, official branding, coordinates, statistics, or map detail does not by itself authorize KFM to cache, join, transform, tile, spatially amplify, infer from, or release source material.

## Trust path and finite outcomes

A public Harvey County claim would need to follow the established KFM trust direction:

```text
source candidate
  -> RAW capture
  -> WORK / QUARANTINE review
  -> PROCESSED role-aware object
  -> EvidenceRef -> EvidenceBundle
  -> CATALOG / TRIPLETS projection
  -> sensitivity + policy + validation + review + release decision
  -> PUBLISHED public-safe artifact
  -> governed API
  -> Explorer presentation
```

The map, UI, county index, catalog, AI response, and this README are downstream or interpretive surfaces; none is sovereign truth.

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Evidence, policy, citation, time, precision, release, and consumer requirements all permit the bounded claim. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, freshness, authority, rights, geometry, currentness, safe precision, or release support is insufficient. |
| `DENY` | The request conflicts with property, groundwater, floodplain, archaeology, cemetery, ecology, infrastructure, privacy, rights, trust-membrane, or release controls. |
| `ERROR` | Required object shape, resolver, validator, policy evaluator, or governed service failed; do not degrade into an unsupported answer. |

`HOLD` remains a governance or workflow state rather than a public answer.

## Smallest safe follow-up

The next dependency-closed implementation should remain **no-network and fixture-only**, matching the plan's recommended first milestone:

1. reconcile proposed paths and object names with current accepted homes without creating a second authority;
2. define one bounded public-safe profile for county orientation, generalized hydrology, floodplain abstention, groundwater non-inference, aggregate agriculture, transport currentness, and sensitive cultural-site handling;
3. create deterministic `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, and `ERROR` fixtures without real living-person, parcel, well, facility, vulnerability, burial, sacred-site, archaeology, rare-species, or live emergency data;
4. validate no official floodplain claim, parcel conclusion, private-well inference, operational infrastructure exposure, sensitive-site disclosure, stale transport or emergency answer, or unresolved EvidenceRef fallback;
5. produce a mock governed response and Evidence Drawer specification from fixtures only;
6. rehearse correction and removal of a stale, unsupported, harmful, over-precise, or incorrectly attributed candidate;
7. keep live source activation, production ingest, public routes, map layers, release, deployment, and publication out of scope.

Stop when the work would require unverified rights, site-specific floodplain adjudication, private-property or water-supply inference, precise infrastructure or sensitive-site detail, living-person data, live operational data, or a parallel authority path.
