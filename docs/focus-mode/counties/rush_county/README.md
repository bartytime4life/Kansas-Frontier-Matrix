## Proposed proof slice

**PROPOSED thesis:** Rush County can become a strong rural proof slice by showing how county identity, heritage, agriculture, roads, recreation, water-governance, and official-current redirects can be combined without collapsing source roles or creating unsafe legal, property, operational, or exact-location inferences.

The first slice should be deliberately narrow:

- orient users to Rush County's public civic and settlement context;
- show county-scale Census and agriculture aggregates with explicit vintage and aggregate scope;
- present post-rock, museum, courthouse, trail, and historic-site narratives as public interpretation rather than complete or precise cultural-resource truth;
- present recreation candidates with clear currentness limits and official KDWP/local routing;
- treat KDOT map vintages as historical/reference mapping rather than current road status;
- use KDA-DWR and NWS as authority redirects rather than cached legal, water-right, well-safety, or live-weather answers;
- avoid parcel-owner, title, legal-access, farm/operator, private-well, infrastructure-vulnerability, or living-person inference;
- expose source identity, source role, date basis, limitations, and release state;
- demonstrate a visible Evidence Drawer path for consequential claims;
- expose correction and rollback state when any future public artifact is released.

### Non-goals

This slice is not a parcel/title service, emergency alert system, live weather service, water-right or private-well advisor, hunting/fishing regulation service, recreation-safety monitor, road-closure service, archaeological-site finder, cemetery/burial locator, living-person dossier, or unrestricted AI question-answering surface.

## Source roles and temporal fitness

Source role must remain explicit. Different source families support different claim classes and clocks.

| Source-role class | Safe use in a future governed slice | Unsafe collapse |
|---|---|---|
| County/city administrative source | Public service and civic orientation at stated date | Legal advice, emergency status, property truth, or unrestricted redistribution |
| Census/USDA aggregate | County-scale demographic/agricultural context | Individual, parcel, farm, operator, or household inference |
| KDOT map/archive source | Dated county-map and transportation context | Current road closure, bridge safety, legal access, or routing authority |
| KDA-DWR authority source | Agency-role and water-governance routing | Private-well safety, potability, water-right standing, or individualized legal conclusion |
| KDWP/local recreation source | General recreation context and official-current routing | Current season/license/open/safe/access status from stale material |
| NWS/county emergency source | Current-authority redirect when live conditions matter | Cached KFM emergency, forecast, warning, evacuation, or safety verdict |
| Historic/visitor interpretation source | Generalized public narrative and period context | Exact archaeology, sacred, burial, cemetery-sensitive, restricted, or private-access detail |
| Parcel/tax/appraisal source | Potential restricted/internal reference after policy review | Title truth, owner profile, lawful access, debt, tax liability, or living-person inference |
| Map or tile | Downstream carrier of released public-safe geometry | Source authority or evidence by itself |
| AI-generated explanation | Bounded interpretation over released evidence | Root truth, source admission, policy decision, or release authority |

Time semantics matter. Every future visible claim should distinguish observation/effective time, source publication or update time, retrieval time, and KFM release time where material.

## Public safety, rights, and sensitivity

The linked plan surfaces several high-consequence boundaries that must remain fail closed.

> [!WARNING]
> **Property and living-person boundary:** county parcel, tax, appraisal, or service links must not become title, ownership, lawful-access, debt, tax-liability, household, or living-person profiles.

> [!WARNING]
> **Current emergency and recreation boundary:** KFM must not infer live weather, emergency, fire, flood, road closure, hunting/fishing regulation, facility availability, or recreation safety from dated planning material or cached public pages.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Water boundary:** KDA-DWR or creek context does not authorize private-well safety, potability, water-right, drainage-liability, allocation, or property-level conclusions.

> [!WARNING]
> **Cultural and location boundary:** exact archaeology, burial, sacred, cemetery-sensitive, vulnerable ecological, or newly inferred sensitive locations must not become public discovery surfaces.

> [!WARNING]
> **Infrastructure boundary:** public roads, bridges, utilities, emergency services, or facilities must not become vulnerability or tactical-response analysis.

Public availability does not itself establish KFM redistribution rights, geometry authority, transform permission, public-safe precision, or policy approval.

## Finite outcomes

A future governed Rush runtime should converge on the same bounded public outcome family used across KFM:

| Outcome | Rush County example |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Released evidence supports a bounded county identity, aggregate agriculture, dated map, or generalized heritage claim and policy permits exposure. |
| `ABSTAIN` | User asks for current weather, emergency, recreation status, hunting/fishing legality, road condition, water-right, well safety, title, or legal access without suitable current authority. |
| `DENY` | Request would expose sensitive cultural/ecological precision, private/property/living-person detail, individual farm inference, infrastructure vulnerability, or prohibited internal paths. |
| `ERROR` | Evidence resolution, policy evaluation, citation validation, or another required trust component fails. |

Candidate `RUSH_*` reason codes in the build plan remain **PROPOSED vocabulary** until governing contract, policy, validator, and registry surfaces adopt them.

## Maintenance, correction, and rollback

Documentation changes are not publication changes, but they still need a reversible maintenance path.

1. Correct the README in a focused follow-up branch.
2. Preserve the evidence snapshot and explain which prior statement changed.
3. If the linked plan is also wrong, update it separately rather than silently rewriting planning lineage in this README.
4. Do not present a documentation correction as correction of any future released county product unless the corresponding release/correction objects are also changed through their governing process.
