# Graham County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Graham County planning lane, make its
> living-community, descendant-authority, cultural-landscape, private-property,
> burial, archaeology, oral-history, agriculture, groundwater-currentness, and
> public-safety boundaries visible, and route future work through KFM evidence,
> policy, review, release, correction, and rollback controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation or release evidence.**
> This directory contains a one-byte predecessor README and one substantial
> Graham County build plan at the pinned base. Replacing the placeholder gives
> maintainers a boundary surface; it does not establish source admission,
> community or descendant review, semantic correctness, validator success,
> runtime behavior, release, deployment, or publication.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains unresolved.** This same-path update repairs an existing
> README under `docs/focus-mode/counties/graham_county/`. Accepted Directory
> Rules v2 treats Focus Mode as a composition scope; proposed ADR-0027 describes
> a different plural, kebab-case control plane but remains unaccepted. No file is
> moved, renamed, copied into a parallel tree, or declared the final canonical
> county write target here.

> [!WARNING]
> **The build plan combines public context with sensitive and high-consequence
> subjects.** Nicodemus is framed by the plan as a living Black community, not
> an extractable story layer. Living-descendant identity, kinship, present
> location, family-linked property, oral-history linkage, exact cemetery,
> burial, archaeology, dugout, artifact, or sensitive cultural-landscape detail
> must not reach an ordinary public client. Historical groundwater evidence
> must not become a present well, supply, health, property, or legal conclusion.
> Missing evidence, rights, representation review, safe resolution, policy,
> release, or correction closure must produce a bounded `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or
> `ERROR` result rather than a fluent inference.

## 5. Public safety and governed outcomes

| Request or output class | Bounded posture | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Released, broad, time-labeled county context with resolving citations | `ANSWER` only when evidence, policy, review, and release conditions pass | Keeps the result within admitted public-safe evidence |
| Missing, stale, conflicting, overly coarse, or unresolvable evidence | `ABSTAIN` | Fluency cannot substitute for evidence, source role, or currentness |
| NPS, public-history, or generated narrative presented as the complete voice of the living Nicodemus community | `ABSTAIN` | Public interpretation is not resident or descendant authority |
| Living descendant, kinship, family, present-location, property, oral-history, attendance, or affiliation linkage | `DENY` | Prevents profiling, reidentification, unwanted contact, and reconstruction of living-community relationships |
| Exact cemetery, burial, archaeology, dugout, artifact, historic farmstead, sacred, or otherwise sensitive cultural-place location or route | `DENY` | Prevents cultural, privacy, property, and physical harm |
| Historic-structure context used to infer access, title, ownership, permission, condition, or route safety | `DENY` or route to the responsible current authority | A public map or history source is not access or property authority |
| Historical KGS or USGS evidence used for present groundwater supply, well condition, water quality, drinking-water safety, health, irrigation, or legal conclusions | `DENY` | Prevents temporal, scientific, health, and legal role collapse |
| GMD4 or DWR district context used to infer an individual water right, pumping status, obligation, compliance outcome, or farm viability | `DENY` | District administration does not prove an individual legal or operational state |
| County agriculture aggregate used to identify a farm, operator, family, parcel, descendant, or individual water use | `DENY` | Aggregate context must not become private-property or family reconstruction |
| Road, weather, flood, fire, site-access, or field context used as a live alert, route, emergency, or safety instruction | `ABSTAIN` and route to the responsible current authority | KFM is not the operational safety or emergency authority |
| Unverified rights, community review, safe geometry, representation, or EvidenceBundle closure | `ABSTAIN` | Public availability does not prove safe derivative use or authority to speak |
| Resolver, policy, carrier, citation, or runtime failure | `ERROR` with no unsupported fallback claim | Failure must remain visible and fail closed |

Client-side hiding is not a sensitivity control. Protected detail must not be
delivered to an ordinary public client, included in public logs or analytics, or
reconstructed from tiles, filters, search, exports, caches, identifiers, or AI
responses.

### Minimum-display rule

Even publicly visible source material does not automatically belong in a KFM
public derivative. Display the minimum resolution and attribute set required for
the reviewed public purpose. Generalization, suppression, and omission require
traceable transform reasoning; a link to an official public source remains
preferable when KFM cannot prove safe derivative use.

The same rule applies to cross-layer reconstruction. Separate public cards must
not combine into a descendant, family, property, oral-history, burial,
archaeology, or private-water-use profile that no single card displays.

## 6. Source-role and currentness boundary

The plan's source list is a research queue, not an admitted source registry.
Future work must assign each candidate one bounded role and preserve the
distinctions below.

| Candidate source family named by the plan | May support after admission | Must not be collapsed into |
|---|---|---|
| Graham County official website | Local administrative identity, office role, and current-source routing within its actual scope | Complete history, property or access authority, sensitive geometry, or permission to republish every accessible field |
| NPS statutory and Nicodemus public-interpretation materials | Bounded federal designation, site, structure, and public-history context within the source's actual scope | Complete resident or descendant voice, community endorsement, reuse rights, title, access, or present private-life facts |
| Resident, descendant, or community authority identified through a future accountable process | Representation, terminology, review, and correction input within documented consent and scope | Automatic permission to identify people, expose relationships, republish stories, or disclose property and sensitive places |
| NPS stories, visitor pages, images, and oral-history-related materials | Public source routing and reviewed contextual excerpts or metadata when rights permit | Blanket reuse, living-person profiling, family linkage, sensitive geometry, or proof that a visit or route is currently safe |
| Kansas Department of Agriculture county statistics | Dated county-level aggregate agricultural context with definitions and limitations | Farm, operator, family, parcel, descendant, property, viability, or individual water-use facts |
| KDA or DWR Groundwater Management District 4 materials | District identity, administrative scope, and official-source routing under the source's actual authority | Individual water right, pumping status, obligation, compliance result, title, or farm viability |
| Kansas Geological Survey Graham County geology and groundwater report named by the plan | Historical geoscience and groundwater context with the original publication date and non-current status visible | Present supply, well condition, water quality, safe drinking water, health, irrigation decision, legal status, or timeless trend |
| USGS water-table publication named by the plan | Historical observation or interpretation with its publication and measurement dates visible | Current groundwater state, current well safety, health conclusion, property condition, or one complete county truth |
| Future KDOT, FEMA, USGS, ecology, or emergency-source candidates | Their separate transportation, regulatory, physical, ecological, or current-routing roles after admission | Live alerts, parcel determinations, field safety, property permission, or undifferentiated county truth |

For every admitted source, record authority role, access method, rights and terms,
geographic and temporal scope, cadence, known limitations, sensitivity,
correction behavior, and the evidence references it may support. A browsed page,
source seed, or polished citation is not an `EvidenceBundle`.

## 7. Evidence and readiness boundary

A Graham County public result must remain downstream of the governed KFM path:

1. identify a source once in the source-authority surface;
2. review authority, rights, access, time, geography, sensitivity, and correction
   behavior;
3. admit candidate material to `RAW`, or hold it in `WORK / QUARANTINE` when
   requirements are unresolved;
4. derive and validate `PROCESSED` material with transform and provenance
   lineage;
5. resolve every consequential `EvidenceRef` to an `EvidenceBundle` and apply
   policy;
6. make a separate promotion and release decision with correction and rollback
   targets;
7. serve only released public-safe artifacts through governed interfaces;
8. keep map rendering and AI interpretation subordinate to that evidence and
   decision state.
