## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The plan records several public pages observed on 2026-06-08: a county
homepage, Emergency Services, Public Works, Road & Bridge, a museum or
historical-society page, and a county-appraiser page. It also records incomplete
or inaccessible NPS Santa Fe Trail and KGS geology or water source checks in
its authoring environment. Those observations are plan lineage, not current
source admission.

### Candidate source ledger requirements

A future source descriptor or source-intake record should preserve at least:

| Field | Required question |
|---|---|
| `source_role` | Is the source historical, archaeological, administrative, scientific, regulatory, current-safety, contextual, or derivative? |
| `temporal_basis` | What observation, effective, retrieval, update, and supersession times constrain use? |
| `spatial_generalization` | What precision may be retained, transformed, or publicly displayed? |
| `access_limitation` | Does the source support historical orientation only, or any lawful-access conclusion? |
| `water_legal_scope` | Can it support regional context, an administrative record, or an individual right decision? |
| `property_minimality` | Which parcel, owner, mineral, lease, royalty, value, tax, or person fields are prohibited or minimized? |
| `health_scope` | Can it support environmental context only, or a competent private-well or health conclusion? |
| `evidence_refs` | Which immutable evidence objects support each visible claim? |
| `policy_decision_ref` | Which policy version and outcome govern exposure? |
| `release_state` | Is the object candidate, reviewed, released, corrected, superseded, withdrawn, or unavailable? |

### Anti-collapse rules

- A county-office page can establish an office's stated role at a dated
  retrieval; it cannot prove a live service, incident, closure, or emergency.
- A historical or museum source can support contextual narrative; it cannot
  authorize entry or disclose sensitive site precision.
- A trail reference can support generalized history; it cannot establish exact
  archaeology, current ownership, or public access.
- A river or aquifer source can support bounded physical context; it cannot
  establish private-well safety, water-right standing, lawful use, or
  property-level impact.
- An appraiser source can support administrative-role context; it cannot
  establish title, mineral ownership, royalty value, tax advice, investment
  suitability, or access.
- An energy or leasehold reference cannot establish current operation,
  infrastructure condition, production, compliance, ownership, or value.
- A map overlap is a spatial candidate, not proof of a causal, legal,
  operational, ownership, access, or health relationship.
- AI output can summarize resolved released evidence; it cannot become evidence
  or fill an authority gap.

## 6. Historic trail, river, and access boundaries

### Public-safe historic context

A future public-safe historical view may show generalized, time-attributed
context only when its source, rights, sensitivity, and review records support
that use. It should distinguish:

- documented source statements from later interpretation;
- named or generalized corridors from exact sites;
- historical route context from current roads or lawful access;
- river context from private-property access;
- observed evidence from reconstruction or inference; and
- released geometry from withheld precision.

### Required negative states

| User request or implied claim | Required posture |
|---|---|
| "Show the exact historic or archaeological site." | `DENY`; withhold precision and avoid reconstruction clues. |
| "Does this trail or river context mean I may enter the property?" | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN`; historical context is not access permission. |
| "Is this mapped segment the exact historic route?" | `ABSTAIN` unless admitted evidence, scale, uncertainty, and release state support that precision. |
| "Does the map prove the site still exists or is publicly accessible?" | `ABSTAIN`; current condition and access require separate authority. |
| "Can I infer a protected site's location from nearby features?" | `DENY`; do not provide triangulation or confirmation. |

### Visual and export constraints

- generalized geometry must carry its transform reason and scale limits;
- hidden coordinates must not leak through popups, URLs, logs, exports,
  screenshots, source metadata, or error messages;
- a historic label must not imply current ownership, access, condition, or
  official designation;
- a reconstruction must be visibly identified as interpretive; and
- exported material must preserve evidence, time, release, correction, and
  sensitivity state appropriate to the carrier.

## 7. Groundwater, property, energy, and current-safety boundaries

### Groundwater and water rights

Regional aquifer or river context must not become a private-well, potability,
property-impact, water-right, lawful-use, contamination, health, or
future-supply conclusion. Any individual-right question requires the competent
current authority and a policy-approved route. Any private-well or health
question requires competent testing or health authority; a map cannot answer
it.

### Property, minerals, leasehold, and royalties

County appraisal or public administrative context may explain source roles and
limitations. It must not identify or infer:

- title or ownership;
- mineral ownership or severance;
- lease validity, encumbrance, or lawful access;
- royalty interest, amount, value, or payment;
- current production, regulatory standing, or operational condition;
- tax, legal, investment, or appraisal advice; or
- a living person's relationship to a property or interest.

### Energy and infrastructure

The first public slice excludes exact wells, facilities, pipelines, operational
infrastructure, vulnerability-ready detail, and present-status conclusions.
Any later generalized energy context requires current source fitness,
operational-security review, minimum necessary precision, and a non-leaking
denial design.

### Roads, emergencies, and official-current channels

A future Kearny Focus surface may identify a verified official-current channel.
It must not cache or restate road-open, road-safe, evacuation, incident,
weather, emergency, or travel guidance as authoritative. When a user needs
current action guidance, the runtime should abstain and redirect safely rather
than infer from stale pages or map state.

| Consequential question | Safe outcome without competent current evidence |
|---|---:|
| Is a private well safe or potable? | `DENY` / `ABSTAIN` |
| Does this parcel have a valid water right? | `ABSTAIN` |
| Is a particular use lawful? | `DENY` / competent-authority redirect |
| Who owns the minerals or receives royalties? | `DENY` |
| Is a gas well active, safe, compliant, or valuable? | `ABSTAIN` / `DENY` |
| Is this road open or safe now? | `ABSTAIN` / official-current-channel redirect |
| What should I do in an emergency? | `ABSTAIN` / emergency-authority redirect |

documentation and synthetic-fixture first. It should not connect live
historical, water, parcel, energy, road, or emergency services.

   fixture authorities without creating a parallel county-local source of
   truth.

   source role, time, digest, rights, limitations, precision, sensitivity,
   allowed claims, prohibited joins, correction, and rollback.
4. Obtain qualified historical, archaeological, cultural-resource, hydrologic,
   water-governance, property/mineral/access, energy-operational-security,
   emergency, road, rights, and release review.

   evidence/policy/runtime `ERROR` paths.

   EvidenceRef-to-EvidenceBundle resolution, deterministic identity, replay,

   deployment, and publication as separate governed transitions.

homes are verified, precision and prohibited joins fail closed, every visible
claim resolves to evidence, finite outcomes are deterministic, and rollback is
credible. A successful fixture test does not admit a source or authorize a
public product.

## 15. Maintenance, correction, and rollback

### Documentation maintenance

- Preserve the `doc_id`, current path, explicit anchors, and planning-only
  authority unless an accepted migration says otherwise.
- Update evidence-snapshot hashes when governing repository surfaces are
  materially re-inspected.
- Keep confirmed repository evidence separate from the plan's proposed content
  and dated external observations.
- Correct stale historic, access, water, property, mineral, royalty, energy,
  road, emergency, rights, sensitivity, and currentness statements visibly.
- Never set released, published, approved, or public-safe merely because
  documentation, tests, commits, or pull requests exist.

### Before merge

Close or abandon the unmerged draft PR and feature branch. Remote branch
deletion is a separate administrative action and is not required for rollback.

### After merge

Use a transparent revert or forward-fix PR against the actual merged commit.
Do not rewrite shared history. Revert or correct this README and its generated
receipt together. Preserve correction and supersession links rather than
creating two writable authorities.

deployment, or publication to undo. If a future public artifact relies on
Kearny content, a Git revert alone may be insufficient: the governed correction
path may also require source-state update, release supersession, withdrawal,
cache invalidation, API/UI correction, and retained lineage.
