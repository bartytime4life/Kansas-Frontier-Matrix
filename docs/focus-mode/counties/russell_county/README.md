## 4. Proposed Russell County proof slice

The sibling plan proposes a **Russell County Reservoir Currentness and
Sensitive-Location Boundary Proof**. Its central question is whether KFM can
explain a reservoir-centered county landscape while refusing to become a live
lake, park, fishing, boating, fire, weather, access, collecting, water-right,
infrastructure-security, fossil-locality, archaeology, or wildlife-intelligence
service.

### Plan-defined public-safe themes

| Theme in the sibling plan | Bounded future use | Required limit |
|---|---|---|
| Wilson Lake and Saline River | Generalized, source-attributed reservoir/river relationship and durable project-purpose context | No live level, release, water-safety, boating, swimming, fishing, flood, or operations conclusion |
| USACE and KDWP roles | Distinct federal project-management and state park/wildlife-management cards | One agency's page or notice must not be projected onto the other's lands, facilities, or authority |
| Smoky Hills and Rocktown | Reviewed county-scale geology, landform, prairie, and post-rock interpretation | No exact fossil or cultural locality, access route, collecting advice, engineering assurance, or implied permission |
| Public recreation | Durable identity and carefully scoped management context | Current facilities, closures, reservations, fire, fishing, trail, and access status must redirect |
| Wildlife and habitat | Generalized public-management and ecological context | No exact refuge-use, nesting, denning, roosting, migration, or vulnerable habitat detail |
| Cultural and archaeological context | Reviewed, generalized, already-public interpretation | No petroglyph, burial, sacred-place, artifact, archaeological, or discovery-enabling precision; Nation-authoritative review where relevant |
| Agriculture and population | County aggregates with explicit reporting vintages | No farm, producer, household, living-person, lease, or suppressed-value inference |
| Energy and transportation history | Later generalized historical context | No active well, pipeline, tank, lease, operator, ownership, routing, bridge-condition, emergency, or vulnerability intelligence |

### Durable versus operational information

| Information class | Example | Required treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Durable project context | Reviewed Wilson Lake purposes and general management role | May support a cited, released card after evidence and policy closure |
| Scientific interpretation | Reviewed Smoky Hills or Rocktown explanation | Must show source, scale, limits, and no-access/no-collecting notice |
| Statistical aggregate | NASS 2022 or a stated Census vintage | Keep reporting period and suppression visible |
| Historical interpretation | Reviewed public energy, settlement, or transportation history | Keep source role and cultural limitations visible |
| Operational notice | Park alert, campground status, facility hours, fire notice, reservation state, fishing report, lake value, weather warning | Expiring envelope or official redirect; never durable prose |
| Sensitive detail | Dam/outlet, active energy, exact cultural/fossil/ecology site | Deny, exclude, or generalize under reviewed policy |
| Permission or legal question | Access, collecting, hunting/fishing compliance, water right, property/title | Deny or route to responsible authority |

### Product and authority boundary

A future Russell County Focus Mode may become map-rich and evidence-visible only
after the relevant source, contract, schema, policy, fixture, validator, review,
release, correction, and rollback objects exist in their proper responsibility
roots. This README does not create those objects by describing them.

## 5. Public safety, sensitivity, and finite outcomes

### Expected finite outcomes

| Request class | Expected outcome | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| General Wilson Lake project-purpose question | `ANSWER` only after evidence, policy, review, citation, and release closure | State that it is general context, not current operations or safety advice |
| USACE versus KDWP role question | `ANSWER` only from role-separated released evidence | Do not imply one agency controls all lands, notices, facilities, or conditions |
| NASS 2022 agriculture question | `ANSWER` with reporting period and suppression limits | No individual farm or producer inference |
| Census population-vintage question | `ANSWER` with distinct vintages | No living-person or household inference |
| Current swimming, boating, fishing, fire, weather, facility, campground, trail, closure, or road question | `ABSTAIN` with current-authority redirect | No cached or guessed present-tense status |
| Missing, stale, rights-unclear, unsupported-precision, or unresolved evidence | `ABSTAIN` | Explain the gap without pretending a positive result |
| Exact dam, outlet, maintenance, utility, road, emergency, active-well, pipeline, or other vulnerability detail | `DENY` | Do not echo or reconstruct protected detail |
| Exact petroglyph, burial, sacred, archaeology, artifact, fossil, refuge-use, nesting, denning, roosting, or migration location | `DENY` | Offer a reviewed generalized alternative where safe |
| Access, trespass, property, title, route, hunting/fishing permission, collecting, excavation, private-well, water-right, or potability conclusion | `DENY` or official route | A map, public page, or geologic description grants no permission or legal/health assurance |
| Individual farm, household, producer, lease, operator, or suppressed-value reconstruction | `DENY` | Prevent direct and cross-layer profiling |
| Evidence resolver, schema, citation, policy, integrity, service, or release-closure failure | `ERROR` | Never fall back to uncited generation |

The plan proposes `RS-*` reason codes. Those strings are useful fixture seeds,
but they are not declared canonical here. Reuse or extension must be checked
against the current reason-code and runtime-envelope authorities.

### Cross-layer reconstruction guardrail

A public-safe layer can become unsafe when joined with another public layer. A
future implementation must test not only each layer in isolation but also whether
combinations can reveal:

- precise cultural, burial, sacred, petroglyph, archaeological, fossil, or
  sensitive ecological locations;
- private access routes or collecting opportunities;
- dam, utility, road, emergency, or energy-infrastructure vulnerabilities;
- private wells, water rights, property interests, lease interests, farms,
  households, or living people; or
- suppressed agricultural values.

When reconstruction risk cannot be bounded, the result is `DENY`, stronger
generalization, staged access, or no release.

## 6. Source role, rights, and currentness boundary

The build plan names or proposes several source families. A checked or public
source is not automatically admitted, licensed for derivative display, current,
or safe at every scale.

| U.S. Census Bureau | Population, geography, housing, economy, and other stated statistical vintages | Living-person or household truth |
| USDA NASS | 2022 county agriculture profile and suppression-aware aggregates | Current farm condition, individual operation, or evidence for reconstructing suppressed values |
| Kansas Geological Survey | Candidate geology, paleontology, and generalized energy-history authority | Access or collecting permission, site engineering assurance, water-potability advice, or active-infrastructure intelligence |
| Kansas Corporation Commission | Candidate regulatory context for oil and gas | Scientific geology authority, ownership/title proof, or permission to expose security-sensitive operations |
| Kansas Department of Transportation | Candidate public route and map context | Live closure, road-safety, bridge-condition, evacuation, hazardous-routing, or vulnerability authority |
| U.S. Geological Survey / Kansas Water Office / KDHE | Candidate hydrology, observation, planning, or water-quality context | Current swimming/boating safety, private-well conclusion, water-right decision, or individualized health advice |
| Russell County and municipalities | Candidate administration and civic context | Title, access, appraisal, tax, person, household, or legal truth |
| KSHS, Nation-authoritative, and other cultural sources | Candidate historical and cultural context within their stated authority | Permission to expose exact sensitive sites or let generated prose speak for a Nation or community |
| Ecology and occurrence sources | Candidate generalized habitat or wildlife context | Exact vulnerable occurrence, refuge-use, nesting, denning, roosting, migration, or collection intelligence |

No source is admitted or released by being mentioned in this README or the
