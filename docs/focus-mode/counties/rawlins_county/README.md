## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The build plan names five official source families checked on 2026-06-08. Their
role separation is more important than the mere fact that the pages were public.

| Source family in the plan | Proposed role | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| Rawlins County official website | County service and department routing | Title, private access, road condition, service eligibility, emergency, or personal conclusion |
| City of Atwood official website | Municipal public information and dated operational notices | Permanent burn-ban, active alert, legal permission, or current safety authority in KFM |
| Lake Atwood / Campground page | General recreation and environmental-learning context | Current availability, dock safety, fishing/boating legality, water quality, accessibility, or site-condition authority |
| Rawlins County Emergency Management / CodeRED | Official local emergency-notification routing | Active incident, warning, road closure, protective action, or emergency truth |
| Rawlins County Road & Bridge | Departmental role and contact route | Passability, closure, bridge condition, safety, or liability guarantee |
| Future KDWP, KDHE, NWS, KDOT/KanDrive, reservation, or parcel source | Candidate qualified authority for a narrowly defined role after admission | Automatic public truth, currentness, legal advice, health advice, title, or permission by source availability alone |
| AI-generated narrative | Bounded explanation after evidence and policy checks | Evidence, source authority, policy decision, human review, or release authority |

### Temporal rules

1. Preserve each source's observation, publication, effective, retrieval,
   supersession, rescission, expiry, and release times where material.
2. A dated planning observation does not answer a current-condition question.
3. Operational notices require explicit validity and expiry behavior; missing or
   ambiguous expiry suppresses the candidate answer.
4. A current source page does not automatically make every statement on it a
   current operational status.
5. Static amenity descriptions do not imply present availability, maintenance,
   accessibility, safety, or legality.
6. Corrections and supersessions must remain visible when a source or released
   KFM object changes.
7. Missing or unclear temporal fitness for consequential claims produces
   `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR`, not a guessed current answer.

### Source-role anti-collapse examples

| Invalid collapse | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Municipal lake page → campsite available tonight | Stable description is not a live booking or operations feed. |
| Dated burn resolution → burning is prohibited now | Current legal applicability and supersession were not established. |
| CodeRED enrollment page → emergency is active | Notification-system existence is not incident evidence. |
| Road & Bridge page → road is open and safe | Departmental identity is not a condition inspection. |
| County Appraiser or Register of Deeds route → public title/access answer | Administrative records require qualified interpretation and privacy/legal controls. |
| Nature-trail description → exact wildlife location | Educational context does not authorize occurrence precision. |
| AI summary → evidence | Generated language remains downstream of admitted evidence and policy. |

## 6. Lake Atwood recreation and wildlife learning

### Stable context versus present condition

The sibling plan records that the checked municipal page described recreation
and environmental-learning features. That can support a future evidence-bound
context card, but it does not establish any present operational fact.

| Question | Safe posture |
|---|---|
| “What kind of place is Lake Atwood?” | A narrow `ANSWER` may describe admitted, cited, time-bounded general context. |
| “Is a campsite or hookup available tonight?” | `ABSTAIN`; require current qualified reservation or municipal authority. |
| “Is the dock safe?” | `ABSTAIN`; a description is not an inspection or safety certification. |
| “Is the water safe to swim in or drink?” | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY`; require fit water-quality and health authority. |
| “Can I fish or boat there today?” | `ABSTAIN`; current rules, licenses, closures, access, and conditions require qualified authority. |
| “Is the park accessible for my needs?” | `ABSTAIN` unless current accessibility information is admitted for that scope. |
| “Can I burn at the campground?” | `ABSTAIN`; current local restrictions and site rules control. |

### Wildlife-learning boundary

A nature-trail or observation feature may support generalized environmental
education. It does not authorize:

- exact species-occurrence points;
- nesting, roosting, breeding, den, or rare-habitat locations;
- habitat-management or survey detail that increases discovery risk;
- predictive overlays that reconstruct sensitive occurrence;
- personalized wildlife-finding assistance.

### Cross-layer reconstruction

Generalization must be evaluated across the complete public composition.
Multiple individually safe layers must not reconstruct private parcels, exact
wildlife locations, vulnerable facilities, closed or unsafe areas, or current
operations through overlay, proximity, labels, filtering, search, downloads, or
exports.

### Accessibility, maintenance, and service limits

A future public card should distinguish:

- amenity existence from current operation;
- historical or published description from present maintenance;
- physical feature presence from accessibility compliance;
- map proximity from lawful or safe access;
- general recreation context from permit, fee, reservation, or license
  eligibility.

## 7. Burn bans, CodeRED, roads, and emergency currentness

### Burn restrictions

Burn restrictions are legally and operationally time-sensitive. A future KFM
surface may explain the source role and show an approved official-current route,
but it must not infer that a dated resolution remains effective.

Minimum obligations for any operational candidate include:

- issuing authority and jurisdiction;
- exact effective time;
- rescission or supersession handling;
- retrieval and validation time;
- expiry or suppression behavior;
- source availability and conflict state;
- visible “official authority controls” limitation;
- correction and cache-invalidation path.

Without those obligations, the response remains `ABSTAIN`.

### CodeRED and Emergency Management

CodeRED enrollment and Emergency Management pages may identify an official
notification route. They do not prove:

- that an emergency is active;
- that a user is enrolled or will receive a message;
- the content, severity, or geographic reach of an alert;
- a road closure, water interruption, missing-person event, or weather warning;
- a protective action or evacuation recommendation;
- completeness or delivery reliability.

A future public surface should prefer an official-current redirect and a
plain-language boundary rather than copying alert content into a durable KFM
card.

### Roads and bridges

Road & Bridge can support department-role and contact-routing context. It
cannot, by page presence alone, establish:

- whether a road or bridge is open;
- whether a route is passable or safe;
- construction, maintenance, snow, flood, fire, or emergency condition;
- legal travel permission or weight restriction;
- detour suitability;
- infrastructure vulnerability.

### Weather and emergency advice

KFM is not a warning, dispatch, emergency-management, road-condition, fire
command, or protective-action authority. Current questions should route to
qualified official channels. Cached or generated information must not be
presented as live advice.

## 8. Property, water, recreation legality, and infrastructure

### Property, title, valuation, and people

Do not turn county or public GIS/record sources into:

- title or ownership determinations;
- permission-to-enter advice;
- owner or living-person profiles;
- private contact disclosure;
- parcel-value or tax conclusions;
- easement, boundary, dispute, or liability opinions;
- recreation-access assumptions based on proximity or map appearance.

Administrative source routing may be useful, but KFM must not become a title
company, appraiser, surveyor, court, landowner, or access authority.

### Fishing and boating

General recreation context is not personalized legal advice. A future answer
must not determine whether a person may fish or boat at a specific time or
place, whether a license or permit is sufficient, whether a closure applies, or
whether conditions are safe. Qualified current rules and site authority control.

### Water quality and health

A lake, campground, or municipal recreation page is not a water-quality
assessment. Potability, harmful conditions, public-health status, swimming
safety, and exposure risk require fit-for-purpose, current, reviewed evidence and
qualified authority. In its absence, KFM abstains or denies consequential health
interpretation.

### Infrastructure minimization

Exact or operationally sensitive details about roads, bridges, emergency
communications, utilities, public-health operations, or other facilities should
not be exposed merely because they are visible in a public source or can be
joined spatially. Public benefit, minimum necessary detail, current policy, and
review govern exposure.

### Agriculture and rural context

County-scale rural context may be useful in later work, but this lane does not
authorize joins that infer individual operator behavior, household conditions,
private access, land value, compliance, or service vulnerability.

## 16. Maintenance, migration, correction, and rollback

### Maintenance triggers

Revisit this README when:

- the sibling plan changes materially;
- Focus Mode placement or naming authority changes;
- source admission, rights, temporal-fitness, recreation, emergency, property,
  ecology, infrastructure, or sensitivity review establishes new evidence;
- a Rawlins County contract, schema, fixture, validator, API, UI, policy, or
  release object is implemented and verified;
- the county index is fully recomputed;
- a correction, withdrawal, or supersession affects a cited county claim.

### Structural migration posture

The current singular compatibility path remains the single writable county lane
for this update. Proposed ADR-0027 cannot authorize a move by itself. A future
move or rename must:

1. verify the accepted target authority;
2. inventory inbound links and consumers;
3. preserve history;
4. prevent two writable copies;
5. update indexes, aliases, validators, and references coherently;
6. record correction and rollback behavior;
7. validate the complete migrated state before retiring the old path.

Creating a second `rawlins-county` tree while this path remains writable is not
an acceptable shortcut.

### Correction posture

A documentation correction should preserve the prior claim's lineage where
material and identify the evidence that changed. A public correction is a
separate governed action and may require source, evidence, API, map, cache,
export, search, AI, notification, or release propagation beyond a Markdown edit.

For operational-currentness defects, correction may require immediate
suppression rather than waiting for a normal documentation cycle.

### Rollback

Before merge, rollback is to close or abandon the draft pull request and feature
branch. After merge, use a transparent revert or forward fix against the actual
merged commits; do not rewrite shared history.

If a future released Rawlins County artifact is implicated, a Git revert alone
may be insufficient. Correction, withdrawal, cache invalidation, supersession,
notification, and release rollback must follow the applicable KFM controls.
