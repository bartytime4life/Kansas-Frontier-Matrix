<a id="4-proposed-lane-county-proof-slice"></a>

## 4. Proposed Lane County proof slice

The sibling plan proposes a county-scale trust demonstration centered on the
High Plains/Ogallala groundwater system, public groundwater-management context,
aggregate agriculture, Dighton civic orientation, and authoritative flood and
weather redirection. Its central test is whether KFM can explain linked public
context without exposing or inferring private, legal, operational, or harmful
detail.

### Plan-defined public-safe themes

| Theme in the sibling plan | Bounded future use | Required limit |
|---|---|---|
| Lane County frame and Dighton civic anchor | Public orientation, county/municipal context, and navigation | No current service, utility, permit, emergency, or legal guarantee |
| High Plains/Ogallala aquifer context | Time-bounded scientific explanation of regional groundwater context | No well-specific condition, yield, potability, pumping legality, or current depletion claim without governed evidence |
| GMD1 Four-County LEMA | Explain a public groundwater-management program and its geographic/temporal context | No individual water-right, operator, parcel, compliance, enforcement, or impairment determination |
| Aggregate agriculture | County-level, source-labeled statistics and trends | No individual farm, operator, field, financial, yield, or reidentifying cross-layer inference |
| FEMA flood-map authority | Redirect flood-hazard and map questions to current authoritative products | KFM is not an insurance, engineering, evacuation, or emergency authority |
| NWS Dodge City authority | Redirect current warning, watch, forecast, or emergency-weather questions | Dated plan text must never substitute for live official guidance |
| Transportation and historical orientation | Public narrative and context after source, rights, and time review | KDOT and KSHS-related ideas remain deferred or unverified in the plan |

### Proposed minimum card set

The plan describes a small future card set. These remain planning objects, not
registered or released artifacts:

1. **County frame card** — identity and map extent, with no parcel or person join.
2. **Dighton civic card** — public civic orientation with currentness and service
   disclaimers.
3. **GMD1 / Four-County LEMA card** — public governance context, never an
   individual compliance record.
4. **High Plains/Ogallala science card** — KGS-style scientific context with
   time, method, scale, and uncertainty visible.
5. **Aggregate agriculture card** — county-level NASS-style statistics with
   disclosure and small-cell review.
6. **FEMA authority redirect card** — map-context handoff without safety,
   insurance, or engineering conclusions.
7. **NWS authority redirect card** — current operational-weather handoff without
   KFM alert substitution.

Transportation and historical cards remain deferred until their source,
rights, temporal, and review conditions are resolved.

### Public-safe composition rule

A future Lane County composition may relate these cards only through governed,
released identifiers. It must not reconstruct denied detail by combining
otherwise public layers. Examples of prohibited reconstruction include:

- inferring a specific private well or water-right holder from management areas,
  parcels, fields, and water context;
- identifying a farm or operator from aggregate agriculture plus imagery,
  infrastructure, or parcel data;
- converting a general aquifer or LEMA context into an individual legal or
  compliance conclusion;
- turning a generalized cultural, ecological, or infrastructure layer into an
  exact protected location;
- using a map click, graph edge, vector result, or model response as evidence.

## 5. Water, agriculture, property, and public-safety boundary

### Groundwater, LEMA, and water-right questions

| Request class | Default bounded response | Why |
|---|---|---|
| General aquifer or management-area explanation with current released evidence | `ANSWER` with citations, time, scale, source role, and limitations | Public scientific or governance context may be explainable |
| Current individual water-right status, ownership, priority, impairment, allocation, or legal effect | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY`; route to current qualified authority | Legal and person/property-specific decision |
| Private-well location, construction, yield, condition, ownership, or use | `DENY` or generalize | Privacy, security, property, and harmful-precision risk |
| Drinking-water safety or potability | `ABSTAIN`; route to current health/environmental authority | KFM does not perform sampling, diagnosis, or current health clearance |
| Individual LEMA compliance, enforcement, pumping, penalty, or exemption | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY`; route to current program authority | Program context is not a compliance decision |
| Aquifer trend or depletion claim without time-appropriate released evidence | `ABSTAIN` | Dated or mismatched support cannot answer a current claim |

The system must preserve the difference between a scientific observation, a
management-program description, a legal or regulatory record, and an
individual compliance determination. One source role cannot silently stand in
for another.

### Agriculture and reidentification

County-level agricultural statistics may support public aggregate explanation
only when source role, release period, uncertainty, suppression, and fitness for
use remain visible. Future implementations must fail closed on:

- individual farm, operator, landowner, tenant, or financial identification;
- field-level yield or practice claims inferred from imagery or other layers;
- cross-layer joins that make aggregate statistics reidentifying;
- small-cell or sparse-category disclosure;
- claims that an aggregate estimate proves a particular operation's behavior.

### Property, access, and title

This README and the sibling plan are not parcel, title, easement, boundary,
access, valuation, ownership, tax, or legal-opinion authorities. Property
questions require current source identity, allowed use, qualified review, and
a public-safe projection. When that support is absent, the finite result is
`ABSTAIN` or `DENY`, not a plausible-looking answer.

### Flood, weather, civic, and emergency currentness

- FEMA-related planning context does not make KFM a current flood, insurance,
  evacuation, or engineering authority.
- NWS-related planning context does not make KFM an alert, warning, forecast, or
  emergency authority.
- A Dighton or county civic page does not prove a current service, office,
  utility, road, closure, permit, or emergency condition.
- A stale, missing, inaccessible, or conflicted source cannot be replaced by
  model knowledge or cached prose.
- Every operational question must route to current authoritative channels or
  return `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR`.

### Cultural, ecological, and infrastructure sensitivity

Exact archaeological, burial, sacred, rare-species, habitat, private-land,
critical-infrastructure, and vulnerability detail remains fail closed. Public
use requires reviewed generalization or redaction, a transform record, policy
support, evidence closure, release state, correction support, and rollback.

### Admission questions for every future source

Before use, verify and record:

1. stable source identity and authority role;
2. current endpoint or retrieval method;
3. terms, rights, attribution, reuse, and redistribution;
4. spatial and temporal coverage;
5. observation, publication, retrieval, validity, and correction time;
6. sensitivity, personal-data, property, exact-location, and aggregation risks;
7. source-native identifiers and deterministic snapshot hashes;
8. stale, outage, conflict, supersession, and correction behavior;
9. allowed public resolution and minimum-necessary fields;
10. evidence, policy, review, release, and rollback dependencies.

A URL, downloadable file, map, dashboard, statistic, or official publisher name
is not enough to promote a source into KFM authority.

### Time and version rules

A future answer must distinguish at least:

- when the source observed or measured something;
- when the source published or revised it;
- when KFM retrieved it;
- the interval for which it is valid;
- the release used by the public surface;
- any correction, withdrawal, or supersession time.

When those times are absent, incompatible, stale, or outside the user's scope,
KFM must narrow or abstain.

## 7. Finite outcomes and candidate reason codes

The current Focus boundary preserves exactly four terminal runtime outcomes.
This README documents their intended meaning; it does not implement them.

| Outcome | Lane County meaning |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Released evidence is in scope, source roles remain explicit, policy and review permit use, citation validation passes, and the answer carries time, geography, limitations, correction state, and release identity. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence is missing, stale, conflicted, too coarse, too precise, legally insufficient, outside scope, or unable to support the requested claim. |
| `DENY` | Rights, privacy, property, sensitive location, infrastructure, legal, safety, consent, or public-exposure policy blocks the request or required detail. |
| `ERROR` | A required resolver, validator, policy evaluator, adapter, schema, release lookup, or runtime component failed. The system does not fall back to unsupported prose. |

| `LC-AGG-AG-ONLY` | Restrict agriculture responses to reviewed aggregate support |

## 8. Proposed cards and interface boundary

### Card contract

Every future Lane County card should declare, at minimum:

- stable card and scope identity;
- the exact claim or orientation function;
- spatial and temporal scope;
- source roles and source-version references;
- `EvidenceRef` entries resolvable to `EvidenceBundle`;
- rights, sensitivity, public-resolution, and redaction state;
- policy and review references;
- release, correction, withdrawal, and rollback references;
- visible limitations and authoritative redirect behavior.

No card may use map appearance, geographic proximity, a graph edge, model
confidence, or repeated narrative as proof.

### Map and Evidence Drawer

A future map shell may show only released, policy-filtered, public-safe
derivatives. The Evidence Drawer should make source role, temporal scope,
evidence support, rights, sensitivity, stale state, review, release, correction,
and limitation information visible. The renderer must not query canonical,
`RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, private, or direct-model stores.

### Focus interaction

A future Focus request must pass through governed interfaces in this order:

```text
bounded request and map context
  -> EvidenceRef resolution
  -> EvidenceBundle support
  -> rights / sensitivity / consent / policy / review
  -> finite ANSWER / ABSTAIN / DENY / ERROR envelope
  -> released public-safe projection
```

AI may summarize supported, released evidence. It may not decide source
authority, water law, property, privacy, sensitivity, public safety, release, or
publication.

### Accessibility and trust-visible states

Future UI acceptance should include keyboard operation, logical focus order,
clear headings and labels, non-color-only status cues, readable tables, and
visible loading, empty, stale, restricted, denied, corrected, superseded,
degraded, and error states. Accessibility does not replace evidence or policy;
it ensures that trust state is perceivable.

## 10. Smallest safe follow-up

After this README is reviewed, the smallest useful implementation candidate is
a **no-network, synthetic, non-release proof packet**. It should remain one
review boundary and should not activate a live source.

### Proposed packet

| Artifact class | Proposed content | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Semantic review note | Confirm the minimum Lane claim set and source-role anti-collapse rules | Does not accept shared contracts |
| Synthetic source descriptors | DWR/GMD1 governance, KGS science, NASS aggregate, FEMA redirect, and NWS redirect profiles using fictionalized endpoints and values | No live network or production data |
| Valid fixtures | Aggregate county context with complete source/evidence/time/policy references | Proves fixture grammar only |
| Invalid fixtures | Private-well detail, individual water-right inference, farm/operator reidentification, stale weather, unsupported property claim, exact sensitive location | Must fail closed for the expected reason |
| Validator tests | Deterministic positive/negative assertions and stable reason-code mapping | No policy or release authority |
| Documentation update | Record what the proof establishes and what remains held | Must not claim source activation or public readiness |

### Stop conditions

Stop or narrow if the work would:

- require live source access, credentials, private data, or production records;
- expose exact wells, water rights, parcels, operators, farms, protected sites,
  or infrastructure vulnerabilities;
- create a parallel Focus path or authority;
- rely on proposed ADR-0027 as accepted;
- introduce policy-significant behavior without accountable review;
- make a current legal, health, emergency, property, or compliance conclusion;
- create release, deployment, promotion, publication, or administrative state.
