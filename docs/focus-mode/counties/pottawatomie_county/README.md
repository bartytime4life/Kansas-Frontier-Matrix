The linked plan proposes a public-safe county evidence dashboard spanning
county GIS, parcel context, agriculture, floodplain mapping, hydrology,
Tuttle Creek, U.S. 24 corridor planning, geology, and historic resources.
That composition is useful only when source roles and policy boundaries stay
visible.

| Proposed element | Intended value | Required boundary | Current status |
|---|---|---|---:|
| County and municipal orientation | Public geographic context. | Boundary geometry is not parcel title, survey, service, or jurisdictional advice. | `PROPOSED` |
| Parcel context | Public planning orientation. | Parcel maps are not title, ownership, easement, legal boundary, or survey truth. | `PROPOSED` |
| Agriculture | County-scale statistics and land-cover summaries. | No individual farm/operator or private-owner inference. | `PROPOSED` |
| Floodplain status | Draft/effective floodplain context. | Not insurance, regulatory, engineering, evacuation, or parcel-specific legal advice. | `PROPOSED` |
| Hydrology | Big Blue/Vermillion/Kansas River context and source metadata. | No emergency, crossing, water-right, or live safety conclusion without current evidence. | `PROPOSED` |
| Tuttle Creek | General flood-control and reservoir context. | No operational vulnerability, dam-security, or live control-state detail. | `PROPOSED` |
| Transportation | U.S. 24 and county road/planning context. | No traffic-engineering, closure, or current-condition guarantee. | `PROPOSED` |
| Geology | County-scale scientific context. | Scale, map vintage, method, and uncertainty must remain visible. | `PROPOSED` |

### Composition rule

A future county Focus Mode may connect these themes only through governed,
released identifiers. Public layers must not be recombined to reconstruct
withheld detail—for example, deriving a burial or archaeological target from a
historic layer plus parcels, or inferring a private landowner from parcel,
agriculture, and address data.

## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The build plan names candidate source families. They remain **source seeds**,
not admitted KFM sources, until current identity, terms, rights, coverage,
cadence, sensitivity, and allowed-use posture are verified.

| Candidate source role | What it may support after admission | What it must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| County GIS and civic sources | Public orientation and source-native administrative context. | Title/survey truth, unrestricted redistribution, emergency status, or universal county truth. |
| Parcel/assessor mapping | Public planning context with explicit limitations. | Ownership, title, access, legal boundary, valuation, or private-person dossier. |
| KDA/FEMA floodplain sources | General flood-map and status context at the source's stated date. | Insurance, engineering, evacuation, or parcel-specific legal determination. |
| USGS/USACE hydrology and reservoir sources | Observation metadata and general public context. | Emergency alert, current safe-use verdict, water-right allocation, or infrastructure vulnerability analysis. |
| KDA/USDA agriculture sources | Aggregate statistics and reviewed land-cover derivatives. | Individual farm/operator truth or economic forecast. |
| KDOT/public transportation sources | Corridor and project context at stated date/scale. | Real-time closure, engineering approval, or safety guarantee. |
| KGS | Geology and scientific context. | Parcel-level property, mineral-title, or engineering determination. |
| Historical institutions and public collections | Historical interpretation, route, place, and documentary context. | Exact restricted archaeology, burial precision, access authorization, or unqualified present-day claim. |

A source page, official publisher name, or map service is not enough by itself.
Future use should preserve observation/publication/retrieval/release and
correction time separately where material.

<a id="6-parcel-floodplain-infrastructure-heritage-and-current-safety-boundaries"></a>

## 6. Parcel, floodplain, infrastructure, heritage, and current-safety boundaries

### Parcel and property questions

County parcel or assessor geometry does not automatically establish title,
ownership, easement, lawful access, surveyed boundary, valuation, or legal
rights. Those questions require source-role and legal fitness appropriate to the
claim. Otherwise the result is `ABSTAIN` or `DENY`.

### Floodplain and emergency questions

KFM may eventually provide released, dated floodplain reference context. It
must not act as an emergency-warning, evacuation, insurance, engineering, or
parcel-specific regulatory authority. Draft and effective states must remain
explicitly distinct.

### Infrastructure and Tuttle Creek

General public context may be appropriate after review, but operational
vulnerability, control-state, dam-security, utility dependency, response-route,
or disruption-analysis detail remains fail closed.

### Heritage, cemeteries, and archaeology

Historic trails and mission history can support public orientation. Exact
cemetery, grave, burial, archaeology, sacred, culturally sensitive, private-land,
or newly inferred resource locations require qualified review and may need
suppression or generalization.

### Roads and current operational state

A planning document, GIS layer, or project page does not prove a road is
currently open, safe, restricted, under construction, or passable. Current
operational questions require current authoritative evidence.

## 7. Finite outcomes

Future Pottawatomie County runtime behavior should preserve KFM's four
terminal outcomes:

| Outcome | Pottawatomie County use |
|---|---|
