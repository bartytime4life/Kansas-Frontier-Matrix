## 5. Geographic-center method, marker, and access boundaries

The plan's most important design contribution is not a point on a map. It is a
contract for explaining **what kind of center is being discussed** and how its
representations differ.

### Required center-claim fields

| Field | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| `target_geography` | Distinguishes the contiguous 48 states from 50-state, population, gravity, or other center concepts. |
| `source_role` | Separates county/local tradition, historical geodetic documentation, archival evidence, and generated interpretation. |
| `method` | Identifies the calculation or historical determination rather than presenting a point without provenance. |
| `boundary_model` | Records which boundary representation and inclusions/exclusions influenced the result. |
| `method_applied_at` | Separates historical computation time from current display and release time. |
| `coordinate_reference_system` | Prevents silent coordinate or datum ambiguity. |
| `precision_statement` | Quantifies uncertainty when supported or states that precision is unresolved. |
| `point_type` | Distinguishes computed point, commemorative marker, visitor site, and generalized uncertainty area. |
| `relationship_type` | States whether objects coincide, approximate, commemorate, or remain unresolved. |
| `access_status` | Records public-confirmed, restricted, not-established, or unknown access. |
| `property_non_claim` | Makes clear that a point or marker does not prove ownership, easement, or permission. |
| `correction_lineage` | Allows a revised method or relationship to supersede a prior representation without erasing history. |

### Representation rule

A future map should use visually and semantically distinct objects:

1. **Historical computed point** — only when authoritative method and coordinates
   are admitted.
2. **Commemorative marker** — a physical or historical marker with its own source,
   date, and geometry.
3. **Visitor site** — a currently verified public destination, if one is admitted.
4. **Uncertainty area** — a generalized area when point precision is unsupported.
5. **Adjacent property** — excluded from the public proof slice unless a separate
   fit-for-purpose claim and policy decision exists.

> [!WARNING]
> The system must never convert a tourism phrase, marker symbol, search result, or
> local narrative into a claim that one coordinate is universally exact.

### Access rule

A public answer may state that a currently verified public visitor site exists
only when evidence supports current access. It must not:

- route users across private land;
- infer an easement from a road or marker;
- identify an owner for the purpose of gaining access;
- treat historical coordinates as a destination; or
- imply that map visibility authorizes entry.

## 6. Home on the Range heritage and rights boundaries

The build plan proposes a heritage card connecting Smith County to the history
of *Home on the Range*. This README preserves that idea without reproducing or
endorsing the plan's external historical assertions as current fact.

### Heritage-source separation

| Source role | What it may eventually support | What it must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| County/local historical publisher | Local association, interpretation, visitor context, and source routing. | Sole archival or statewide authority. |
| Historical society or archival institution | Document identity, chronology, provenance, collection context, and variants. | Automatic rights clearance for every asset. |
| National Register or other formal documentation | Bounded site/history evidence at its stated date and scope. | Current property access, unrestricted media reuse, or cultural consent. |
| Published work, sheet music, recording, photograph, scan, or map | Asset-specific evidence when identity and rights are resolved. | A freely reusable bundle merely because it is visible online. |
| AI-generated explanation | Bounded interpretation over released evidence. | Historical authority, canonical variant, rights decision, or source replacement. |

### Asset-level rights rule

Text, lyrics, music, recordings, photographs, scans, maps, and archival images
must each have a reviewable rights record before reproduction or derivative use.
A narrative card may summarize a rights-cleared claim while omitting the asset
itself.

Required states include:

- `allowed`;
- `restricted`;
- `unclear`;
- `prohibited`; and
- `not_reviewed`.

`unclear` and `not_reviewed` default to omission or `ABSTAIN`, not optimistic
publication.

### Variant and attribution rule

A future heritage object should preserve:

- work identity;
- variant/version identity;
- creator and contributor attribution where supported;
- publication, performance, recording, or collection dates;
- source and archival identifiers;
- changes or transformations;
- rights status by asset; and
- correction or supersession lineage.

> [!CAUTION]
> This README does not reproduce lyrics, music, recordings, photographs, or
> archival images, and it does not make a public-domain or copyright
> determination.

## 7. Agriculture, public-record, property, and rural-privacy boundaries

### Agriculture

The plan proposes a 2022 county agriculture snapshot from a federal statistical
source. This README does not revalidate the recorded values. Any future card
must preserve:

- reporting year and publication vintage;
- source definitions and suppression flags;
- county aggregate scope;
- observed versus derived fields;
- uncertainty and comparability limits; and
- the rule that aggregate values do not identify an operation, producer, field,
  parcel, lease, facility, or financial condition.

Agriculture layers must not be combined with parcels, irrigation, addresses,
roads, inspections, deeds, or public records to infer an individual farm.

### Public-record anti-profile rule

Public availability does not equal permission to aggregate every record into a
person, family, property, or risk profile.

| Candidate record family | Potential safe use after review | Prohibited collapse |
|---|---|---|
| Tax and appraisal | Source routing and generalized administrative context. | Title, ownership, wealth, occupancy, or access truth. |
| Deeds and recorded instruments | Fit-for-purpose document lookup through the authoritative office. | Simplified title conclusion or owner dossier. |
| Genealogy and cemetery records | Historical research context under rights and living-person controls. | Living-person family graph or household inference. |
| Election and official records | Public civic context at appropriate scale. | Political profiling or household linkage. |
| Property-inspection notices | Generalized current-authority redirect. | Owner/parcel targeting, route reconstruction, or inspection schedule map. |
| Addresses and contacts | Official routing when necessary. | Persistent person or household dataset. |
| Farm statistics | County aggregate evidence. | Producer, field, facility, or operation identification. |

### Property and access

A marker, map, road, visitor page, parcel outline, tax record, deed index, or
inspection notice does not automatically establish:

- current title;
- surveyed boundary;
- easement;
- lawful access;
- owner identity fit for the requested purpose;
- public right-of-way;
- permission to enter; or
- current site condition.

Property-specific requests must `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` unless a separate governed
contract, evidence package, policy decision, and qualified authority support the
exact claim.

### Small-community privacy

Smith County's rural scale increases reidentification risk. A future query
review should consider:

- small cells and rare combinations;
- joins across county and city systems;
- temporal linkage;
- household, family, occupation, and farm inference;
- indirect location reconstruction;
- query repetition and differencing; and
- whether a generalized answer still reveals the subject.

## 8. Municipal water, weather, operations, and infrastructure boundaries

### Operational source rule

County and city pages may carry notices about inspections, burn bans, snow
routes, utilities, water, alerts, airport services, meetings, permits, roads, and
emergency services. These are volatile operational records, not timeless county
facts.

A future `OperationalNoticeEnvelope` should carry:

| Field | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| `authority` | Identifies the responsible county, city, state, or federal publisher. |
| `notice_id` | Stable source-native identity when available. |
| `published_at` | Source publication time. |
| `retrieved_at` | KFM retrieval time. |
| `effective_from` / `effective_to` | Operational validity when supplied. |
| `checked_at` | Last authority/currentness verification. |
| `expires_at` | Explicit expiry or conservative KFM staleness boundary. |
| `status` | Active, expired, superseded, withdrawn, unknown, or unavailable. |
| `official_redirect` | Current authority destination. |
| `correction_ref` | Prior-state correction or supersession. |

An expired, unavailable, or unverified notice must not silently remain active.

### Water and health

A municipal water-quality page or report may eventually support a bounded,
system-level, dated explanation after source and rights review. It does not
support an individualized answer such as whether a household's water is safe,
what caused a symptom, or whether a private connection is compliant.

Household potability, health, pressure, outage, contamination, treatment,
plumbing, and medical questions default to `DENY` or official-current-source
redirect according to the governing contract.

### Weather, burn bans, roads, and emergency information

- Current weather and warning questions route to the responsible official
  weather authority.
- A city weather widget is not automatically the authoritative forecast source.
- Burn-ban status requires a current, admitted operational source and expiry.
- Road or snow-route context does not prove current passability, closure, safety,
  or legal access.
- KFM does not issue evacuation, emergency, fire, law-enforcement, dispatch, or
  public-safety orders.

### Infrastructure-security boundary

Public context about airports, utilities, water systems, roads, communications,
emergency services, and public facilities must remain generalized. The public
slice must not expose:

- weak points or dependencies;
- control, monitoring, or response systems;
- exact operational topology;
- outage or disruption scenarios;
- security posture;
- restricted facility geometry;
- sensitive staff or contact relationships; or
- combined cross-layer vulnerability analysis.

## 9. Source role and temporal fitness

The build plan names candidate source families. They remain **source seeds** or
dated planning observations, not admitted KFM sources, until current identity,
terms, rights, coverage, cadence, sensitivity, and allowed-use posture are
verified.

| Candidate source role | What it may support after admission | What it must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| County official site | County identity, local historical statements, public-office routing, and dated notices. | Geodetic authority, title truth, rights clearance, or durable operational state. |
| City official site | Municipal identity, service categories, and current-authority redirects. | Household water advice, infrastructure map, or permanent status feed. |
| Geodetic/cartographic authority candidate | Historical method, target geography, coordinates, datum, precision, and revisions. | Property access, visitor-site identity, or universal agreement beyond its scope. |
| Historical society / archive / formal registration | Chronology, provenance, document/site identity, and bounded historical interpretation. | Automatic asset rights, living-person profile, or access permission. |
| Federal statistical source | Time-bounded county aggregates and source definitions. | Current farm, household, person, or property truth. |
| Operational weather authority | Current official products and dated archives. | Timeless county fact or KFM-issued alert. |

## 10. Finite outcomes and reason codes

A future governed Smith County runtime should terminate in one of four outcomes:

| Outcome | Smith County example | Minimum condition |
|---|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | A bounded explanation of the historical contiguous-48 center association, with target geography, method, uncertainty, point type, citations, non-access notice, policy allow, and released evidence. | Evidence, method, rights, policy, review, citation, and release closure pass. |
| `ABSTAIN` | A user asks for a universally exact center coordinate, current burn-ban status, unreviewed heritage media, or current weather without suitable released evidence. | Missing, stale, conflicted, rights-unclear, method-unclear, or out-of-scope evidence. |
| `DENY` | A user asks for a private route, owner identity, living-person genealogy, individual farm, household-water verdict, or infrastructure weak points. | Privacy, property, rights, safety, infrastructure, or prohibited-use rule blocks the request. |
| `ERROR` | Evidence resolution, integrity, contract validation, policy service, or release dependency fails. | Required trust component fails; no fallback to uncited prose. |

| Owner profiling | `SC-OWNER-PROFILE` | `DENY` |

| Individual farm | `SC-INDIVIDUAL-FARM` | `DENY` |
| Household water | `SC-HOUSEHOLD-WATER` | `DENY` |

### Required negative-state behavior

- `ABSTAIN` states what is unresolved and identifies a safe authority or evidence
  requirement.
- `DENY` does not echo owner identities, private routes, inspection details,
  family links, restricted assets, or infrastructure detail.
- `ERROR` never falls back to an uncited generative answer.
- The UI must not disguise a non-answer as a successful answer.
- Every future runtime result should carry an auditable response or decision
  reference.
