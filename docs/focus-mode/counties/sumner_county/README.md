## 5. Public safety, privacy, and sensitivity boundaries

### 5.1 Historic corridor and access

Historic route evidence can be useful, but the following relations must not
collapse:

```text
historic trail ≠ current public road
historic rail grade ≠ active railroad
mapped line ≠ easement
public webpage ≠ permission
parcel boundary ≠ ownership proof
historic continuity ≠ lawful present access
```

A future public product should display an explicit access status such as
`not_established`, `public_confirmed`, `restricted`, or `unknown` only when the
underlying contract and evidence support that vocabulary. The README does not
adopt that machine contract.

### 5.2 Property and living-person protection

Parcel, tax, appraisal, deed, zoning, address, and map systems are high-risk
join surfaces. The first county slice should not:

- identify or rank owners, occupants, households, farms, operators, creditors,
  taxpayers, or businesses;
- combine historic corridor geometry with owner or parcel records;
- infer lawful access, title, easement, debt, tax liability, appraisal accuracy,
  zoning entitlement, or property condition;
- reconstruct suppressed agricultural values through joins;
- expose living-person or household profiles through map interaction, search,
  export, AI answers, or indirect cross-layer reconstruction.

### 5.3 Infrastructure and operational minimization

Airport, electric, water, wastewater, roads, GIS, fire/EMS, police, shelter,
rail, and emergency pages may establish that an authority or service exists.
They do not authorize KFM to publish:

- exact network topology or interdependency;
- access controls, weak points, failure paths, bottlenecks, capacity, or outage
  consequences;
- hazardous cargo, active train, dispatch, maintenance, crossing, or schedule
  intelligence;
- tactical emergency routing, response gaps, shelter-capacity ranking, or
  evacuation optimization;
- current road, airport, utility, railroad, fire, EMS, or police operational
  status from stale or unreviewed material.

### 5.4 Planning-status discipline

A plan document may have several separate states:

- drafted;
- published;
- adopted;
- amended;
- implemented;
- funded;
- superseded;
- withdrawn;
- status unknown.

KFM must not infer one state from another. A PDF, web page, agenda, map, or
chapter index is not enough to establish current legal or implementation effect.

### 5.5 Water, flood, and weather

Generalized hydrologic, floodplain, geology, soils, groundwater, and weather
history may be appropriate after source admission and review. The first slice
must not provide:

- live flood, tornado, fire-weather, road, or emergency guidance;
- property-specific flood, drainage, insurance, legal, or safety conclusions;
- private-well safety, potability, contamination, yield, or construction advice;
- water-right, pumping, diversion, allocation, or jurisdictional conclusions;
- stale warning or operational status presented as current.

Current questions should route to the responsible official authority or return
`ABSTAIN`.

### 5.6 Cultural, archaeological, and burial sensitivity

Public history does not make all related locations or interpretations fit for
unrestricted display. Exact archaeological, artifact, burial, sacred, culturally
restricted, or discovery-enabling detail should be withheld or generalized
unless appropriate authority, rights, sensitivity review, and release decisions
support a safer representation.

### 5.7 Agriculture and statistical disclosure

County statistics remain aggregates for a reporting period. A future product
must preserve suppression and uncertainty markers and prevent:

- operation-level or field-level inference;
- producer, farm, lease, or facility identification;
- reconstruction of suppressed values;
- presentation of a historical reporting year as current conditions;
- causal claims about a particular person, parcel, farm, or business.

### 5.8 Cross-layer reconstruction

Individually public layers can become unsafe when combined. Review must consider
whether corridor, parcel, owner, address, airport, utility, rail, road, shelter,
emergency, agriculture, and cultural layers together reveal information that no
single layer discloses directly.

| Future USGS/FEMA/KGS/NRCS/KDHE sources | Hydrology, flood, geology, soils, groundwater, or environmental context | Private-water, property-level, legal, insurance, health, or live emergency determination |
| Map or tile | Downstream carrier of released public-safe geometry | Source truth, legal access, or evidence by itself |
| AI-generated language | Bounded interpretation over released evidence | Source admission, policy approval, legal advice, or release authority |

### Required source-intake questions

Before a source leaves candidate status, reviewers should establish:

1. canonical publisher and stable source identity;
2. source role and what the source cannot prove;
3. publication, event, adoption, effective, retrieval, and expiry times;
4. terms, attribution, redistribution, screenshot, map, and derivative-display
   rights;
5. geometry authority, CRS, scale, vintage, uncertainty, and reconstruction
   method;
6. access, property, privacy, infrastructure, emergency, cultural, archaeology,
   and small-cell sensitivity;
7. deterministic acquisition identity and integrity receipt;
8. correction, supersession, withdrawal, and authoritative-currentness route;
9. public transform and its redaction/generalization receipt;
10. release, correction, and rollback closure.

### Time dimensions that must remain separate

| Time field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `event_time` or `observed_at` | When an event or observation occurred |
| `reporting_period` | Statistical period represented |
| `published_at` | When the source was published |
| `adopted_at` | When an accountable authority adopted a plan or decision, if proven |
| `effective_from` / `effective_to` | Legal or administrative applicability |
| `retrieved_at` | When KFM captured or checked the source |
| `expires_at` | When operational content may no longer answer |
| `released_at` | When KFM released a reviewed artifact |
| `corrected_at` | When a correction or supersession took effect |

A retrieved page does not prove adoption, current operation, legal access, or
ongoing validity.

### What a passing documentation check would not prove

A Markdown check cannot prove:

- source admission or rights;
- current trail, rail, road, airport, utility, emergency, flood, or weather
  status;
- legal access, title, ownership, easement, or plan effect;
- safe geometry or absence of cross-layer reconstruction risk;
- policy enforcement or runtime behavior;
- current external source fitness;
- release, deployment, promotion, or publication.

## 9. Smallest safe follow-up

The next bounded implementation should remain no-network and synthetic.

### Proposed proof objective

Demonstrate that a deterministic county composition can:

- answer one aggregate agriculture question with a reporting-year boundary;
- answer one Census-vintage question without collapsing vintages;
- answer one historical railroad question while leaving current operation
  unresolved;
- present one generalized Chisholm Trail candidate with access explicitly not
  established;
- display a planning-document index without claiming adoption or implementation;
- abstain from current rail, road, airport, utility, hazard, and plan-status
  questions when evidence is insufficient;
- deny owner-profile, parcel/access, infrastructure, vulnerability,
  private-water, exact archaeology, and individual-farm requests;
- return `ERROR` on deterministic integrity or evidence-resolution failure;
- demonstrate correction and rollback metadata without publishing anything.

### Candidate synthetic matrix

| Synthetic case | Expected result | Boundary demonstrated |
|---|---|---|
| Released county aggregate with reporting period | `ANSWER` | Statistical scope and citation closure |
| Historical rail card with current status unknown | `ANSWER` or narrowed answer | Historic/current role separation |
| Generalized historic trail with no access evidence | `ANSWER` with `access not established` or `ABSTAIN` | No permission inference |
| Plan page without adoption record | `ABSTAIN` | Planning-status discipline |
| Owner-plus-corridor join | `DENY` | Privacy/property anti-collapse |
| Active train, cargo, airport-access, utility-topology, or shelter-capacity request | `DENY` | Infrastructure and tactical minimization |
| Private-well or property-level flood request | `DENY` or official redirect | Private-water/property boundary |
| Exact archaeology, burial, sacred-place, or artifact request | `DENY` | Sensitive-place protection |
| Missing evidence, digest mismatch, or release closure | `ERROR` | Fail-safe integrity behavior |

### Non-goals for that follow-up

- live source connectors;
- production, private, restricted, or rights-unclear data;
- direct parcel, deed, tax, owner, active-rail, airport, utility, shelter,
  emergency, weather, flood, or private-well queries;
- source admission by fixture existence;
- public route, tile, deployment, promotion, or publication;
- accepting ADR-0027 or renaming the county lane through implementation drift.

## 12. Maintenance, correction, and rollback

### Maintenance triggers

Review this README when:

- the sibling plan is renamed, moved, superseded, or materially revised;
- ADR-0027 changes effective status;
- Directory Rules or the Focus documentation placement decision changes;
- a Sumner source is admitted, corrected, withdrawn, or has rights/currentness
  changes;
- a contract, schema, policy, reason-code registry, fixture family, validator,
  API, UI, correction, or rollback object becomes authoritative for the lane;
- a public-safe Sumner artifact is actually released or withdrawn;
- cross-layer reconstruction review changes the allowed precision or fields.

### Documentation correction

A correction should:

1. identify the affected claim or boundary;
2. preserve the prior revision in Git history;
3. cite the repository or accepted-decision evidence supporting the correction;
4. update direct references and the county index only when their own evidence
   snapshots are intentionally refreshed;
5. avoid converting a documentation correction into source, policy, release, or
   publication state.

### Rollback

Before merge, rollback is closure or abandonment of the unmerged branch and pull
request. After merge, use a transparent revert or forward-fix PR against the
actual merged commit.

A README rollback does not reverse a separate future source admission, policy
decision, release manifest, deployment, public correction, cache invalidation,
or publication transition. Each of those requires its own governed record and
rollback path.

### Current rollback boundary

This slice is limited to:

- `docs/focus-mode/counties/sumner_county/README.md`; and
- its generated authoring receipt.

The sibling plan, county index, contracts, schemas, policy, fixtures, runtime,
workflows, releases, and published artifacts are not modified by this slice.
