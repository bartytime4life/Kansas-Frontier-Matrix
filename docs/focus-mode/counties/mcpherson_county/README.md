## Proposed proof slice

**PROPOSED thesis:** McPherson County can become a strong county proof slice by showing how a public-facing wetland narrative stays useful while refusing unsafe precision, stale operational reuse, private-land inference, and uncited interpretation.

The first slice should be deliberately narrow:

- orient users to the McPherson Valley Wetlands as a county-scale ecological feature;
- show source identity, source role, date basis, limitations, and release state;
- demonstrate a visible Evidence Drawer path for every consequential claim;
- preserve a clear boundary between enduring context and dated operational material;
- generalize or deny sensitive species-location detail;
- abstain on current water, hunting, access, road, safety, or regulatory questions unless separately governed current evidence exists;
- keep agriculture context aggregate and non-identifying;
- expose correction and rollback state when any future public artifact is released.

### Non-goals

This slice is not a wetland-condition dashboard, species finder, hunting advisor, access planner, route-safety service, parcel/owner interface, live agency mirror, or unrestricted AI question-answering surface.

## Source roles and temporal fitness

Source role must remain explicit. A single public page may contain fields suitable for one claim class and unsuitable for another.

| Source-role class | Safe use in a future governed slice | Unsafe collapse |
|---|---|---|
| Official enduring wetland description | Generalized orientation and evidence-backed historical/contextual claims | Treating it as proof of present water or operational conditions |
| Dated wildlife/recreation update | Evidence that the source carried operational content at a stated date | Reusing it as current hunting, regulation, access, or trip-planning advice |
| County-government news/project page | Public-source routing and dated civic context | Present road-status or route-safety claim without current governed authority |
| Agriculture aggregate | County-scale contextual metric after admission | Producer, parcel, water-use, or property inference |
| Map or tile | Downstream carrier of released public-safe geometry | Source authority or evidence by itself |
| AI-generated explanation | Bounded interpretation over released evidence | Root truth, source admission, policy decision, or release authority |

Time semantics matter. Every future visible claim should distinguish the source's publication/update time from the real-world validity period and KFM release time when those differ.

## Public safety, rights, and sensitivity

The linked plan surfaces several high-consequence boundaries that must remain fail closed.

> [!WARNING]
> **Sensitive ecology:** exact nest, roost, concentration, rare-species, or management-sensitive locations must not become a public discovery tool merely because a source is publicly accessible.

> [!WARNING]
> **Operational freshness:** dated water, hunting, access, check-in, regulation, or trip-planning content cannot be presented as current without a separately admitted and current authority path.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Private-land inference:** wetland and agriculture context must not infer owners, operators, parcel practices, irrigation, access rights, or property impacts.

> [!WARNING]
> **Road and safety status:** county news or project pages do not authorize current road closure, safe-route, emergency, or infrastructure-condition answers.

Public availability does not itself establish KFM redistribution rights, geometry authority, transform permission, public-safe precision, or policy approval.
