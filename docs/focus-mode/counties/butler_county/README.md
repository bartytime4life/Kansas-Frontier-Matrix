# Butler County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Butler County planning lane, make its environmental-remediation, historic-petroleum, reservoir, water, habitat, property, infrastructure, and currentness boundaries visible, and route future work through KFM evidence, policy, review, release, correction, and rollback controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation or release evidence.** This directory contains this README and one Butler County build plan. Their presence prevents accidental duplicate planning; it does not establish source admission, semantic correctness, validator success, runtime behavior, release, deployment, or publication.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains unresolved.** This same-path update repairs the one-byte placeholder under `docs/focus-mode/counties/butler_county/`. Accepted Directory Rules v2 treats Focus Mode as a composition scope; proposed ADR-0027 describes a different plural, kebab-case control plane but remains unaccepted. No file is moved, renamed, copied into a parallel tree, or declared the final canonical county write target here.

> [!WARNING]
> **Historic petroleum and official remediation sources must not become present personal judgments.** They cannot support exposure, health, compliance, liability, property-value, title, or safety conclusions without appropriately scoped evidence and authority. Operational infrastructure detail, exact sensitive wildlife or cultural locations, private property detail, and stale recreation, flood, or water information must not reach an ordinary public client. Missing evidence, rights, safe resolution, policy, review, or release closure must produce a bounded `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR` result rather than a fluent inference.

## 5. Source-role and cross-domain boundaries

The build plan names source families that answer materially different questions. They must not be merged into one undifferentiated Butler County truth layer.

| Planned source or object class | Legitimate bounded role | Must not be treated as |
|---|---|---|
| County site, GIS, or geoportal | Source discovery, administrative context, and candidate geometry inventory after terms and rights review | Automatic reuse permission, title authority, evidence closure, or public release |
| Federal reservoir or project material | Broad project, river, lake, and public-purpose context | Live operations, vulnerability analysis, current access, or safety advice |
| Historic geological or petroleum publication | Time-bounded scientific and historical interpretation | Current production, active-well status, contamination, hazard, compliance, or property evidence |
| Environmental regulatory or remediation record | Source-specific official record and bounded regulatory context | Personal exposure, health, liability, present compliance, parcel safety, or property-value judgment |
| Wildlife-area or habitat material | Generalized public habitat and stewardship context after sensitivity review | Exact species, facility, movement, management, or operational precision |
| Municipal or state water-planning narrative | Administrative, program, storage, sediment, or planning context | Water right, allocation, quality, present availability, engineering, or legal determination |
| Hydrologic monitoring source | Time-stamped observation routing and later candidate observations through governed intake | Safety advice, causal interpretation, current-condition assurance, or emergency instruction |
| Flood-hazard product source | Source-specific regulatory product routing, effective-date context, and supersession warning | Property-specific safety, insurance, legal, evacuation, or emergency advice |
| Public-history source | Source-specific civic interpretation with visible authority and time scope | Scientific proof, archaeology authority, current condition, or unreviewed cultural conclusion |
| Generated explanation | Bounded interpretation of resolved, allowed, released evidence | Evidence, source authority, policy decision, review, or release approval |

Cross-domain composition must keep at least these distinctions visible:

- historical petroleum interpretation versus current environmental or operational evidence;
- remediation-record existence versus present exposure, health, liability, or compliance;
- reservoir project context versus live operations or safety;
- water observation versus regulatory, forecast, modeled, or advisory meaning;
- habitat context versus exact wildlife or facility precision;
- administrative geometry versus legal title, ownership, access, or parcel truth;
- official-source routing versus KFM endorsement or republication authority;
- generated language versus EvidenceBundle-supported claims.

## 6. Public safety and governed outcomes

| Request or output class | Bounded posture | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Released, broad, time-labeled county context with resolving citations | `ANSWER` only when evidence, policy, review, and release conditions pass | Keeps the result within admitted public-safe evidence |
| Missing, stale, conflicting, overly coarse, or unresolvable evidence | `ABSTAIN` | Fluency cannot substitute for evidence, source role, or currentness |
| Present exposure, health, contamination, compliance, liability, parcel-safety, or property-value inference from a remediation or historic source | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` | The source role and claim scope do not support individualized or present judgments |
| Current production, active-well, or present-condition inference from dated petroleum history | `ABSTAIN` | Historic interpretation is not current operational evidence |
| Dam, reservoir, refinery, water-system, energy, transportation, or facility vulnerability and operational detail | `DENY` | Public context does not authorize harmful operational inference |
| Exact or reconstructive rare-species, habitat, facility, cultural, burial, archaeological, private-property, or living-person detail | `DENY` | Prevents ecological, cultural, privacy, site-protection, and security harm |
| Live lake, water, flood, closure, access, boating, or emergency instruction | `ABSTAIN` with responsible official-source routing when available | KFM is not an alert, emergency, navigation, water-safety, or legal authority |
| Parcel, appraisal, GIS, or map content treated as title, survey, ownership, access, or legal boundary truth | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` | Administrative mapping does not establish legal title or rights |
| Cross-source inference of causation, engineering fitness, legal status, or personal risk | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN`, as accepted policy requires | Distinct contextual sources do not jointly prove the inferred claim |
| Resolver, policy, carrier, citation, or runtime failure | `ERROR` with no unsupported fallback claim | Failure must remain visible and fail closed |

Client-side hiding is not a sensitivity control. Protected detail must not be delivered to an ordinary public client, included in public logs or analytics, or reconstructable from tiles, filters, search, exports, caches, identifiers, or AI responses.

### Minimum negative proof for a future Butler slice

A future no-network proof should demonstrate deterministic failure for at least:

- remediation record rendered as a present exposure or health judgment;
- historic petroleum material rendered as current production or condition;
- reservoir, refinery, water-system, or facility operational detail exposed publicly;
- exact wildlife, habitat, facility, cultural, burial, or archaeological precision;
- parcel or administrative GIS rendered as title or ownership truth;
- live water, flood, recreation, closure, or safety advice without current governed support;
- an unresolved `EvidenceRef` treated as evidence;
- a public payload referencing `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, restricted, or direct model state;
- a generated narrative treated as source authority;
- a release candidate lacking correction and rollback support.

## 7. Evidence and readiness boundary

A Butler County public result must remain downstream of the governed KFM path:

1. identify a source once in the source-authority surface;
2. review authority, rights, access, time, geography, source role, sensitivity, and correction behavior;
3. admit candidate material to `RAW`, or hold it in `WORK / QUARANTINE` when requirements are unresolved;
4. derive and validate `PROCESSED` material with transform and provenance lineage;
5. resolve every consequential `EvidenceRef` to an `EvidenceBundle` and apply policy;
6. obtain accountable review appropriate to environmental, ecological, property, cultural, and operational consequences;
7. make a separate promotion and release decision with correction and rollback targets;
8. serve only released public-safe artifacts through governed interfaces;
9. keep map rendering and AI interpretation subordinate to evidence, policy, review, and release state.
