identifiers. It must not reconstruct denied or private detail by joining
apparently public layers. Examples include identifying a student or family from
historical school records plus address data, profiling political views from
civic participation plus household geography, or deriving public-building
vulnerabilities from roads, floor plans, access points, utilities, and response
districts.

<a id="5-source-role-and-temporal-fitness"></a>

## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The plan names candidate source families. They remain **source seeds**, not
admitted KFM sources, until current identity, terms, rights, scope, sensitivity,
cadence, correction behavior, and permitted-use posture are verified.

| Candidate source role | What it may support after admission | What it must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| County/city administrative source | Source-native civic, planning, institution, and county-service context. | Legal advice, emergency state, unrestricted redistribution, or universal county truth. |
| State-government or public-law source | Official role, decision, institution, or historical record at stated scope. | Generated legal conclusion, current building-access authority, or complete public-memory account. |
| NPS, historical society, archive, or marker source | Attributed public-history, archival, site, and interpretation context. | Private-person truth, complete civil-rights authority, culturally authoritative voice for every affected community, or unqualified present-day claim. |
| Court or case record | Source-bound legal record and decision history. | Legal advice, current case analysis, or generated statement beyond the record's scope. |
| School or education source | Public institutional and historical context after privacy review. | Student, family, disciplinary, health, security, or household-level disclosure. |
| Hydrology, floodplain, or weather authority | Dated river, flood-map, observation, model, or official-current routing context. | Current emergency, engineering, insurance, evacuation, or parcel-specific legal determination. |
| Transportation or planning source | Road, rail, bridge, corridor, project, or planning context at stated date and scale. | Live closure, safety guarantee, routing order, access permission, or vulnerability analysis. |
| Parcel, appraisal, or property source | Potential internal reference under strict policy and temporal controls. | Title truth, owner profile, lawful access, political profiling, or household inference. |
| Map, tile, graph, or index | Downstream carrier of reviewed public-safe information. | Source authority, evidence closure, policy permission, or release proof. |
| AI-generated explanation | Bounded interpretation over released evidence. | Root truth, source admission, legal interpretation, policy decision, or release authority. |

Future claims should distinguish at least:

- event, decision, observation, or valid time;
- source publication, update, or effective time;
- retrieval time;
- KFM review and release time; and
- correction, withdrawal, or supersession time.

A historical date does not establish a current civic function, and a current
institutional page does not by itself establish historical meaning.

<a id="6-civic-civil-rights-education-property-and-safety-boundaries"></a>

## 6. Civic, civil-rights, education, property, and safety boundaries

### Civil-rights, legal, and public-memory questions

KFM may eventually present released, source-framed civil-rights and legal-history
context. It must preserve the distinction between a legal decision, an official
record, site interpretation, public memory, scholarship, community testimony,
and generated explanation. An evidence gap produces `ABSTAIN`; a request for
private records or unsafe profiling produces `DENY`.

### Students, families, schools, and living persons

Public-history value does not authorize private student, family, disciplinary,
health, address, household, or security records. Small-cell combinations,
historical records that identify living people, and joins across schools,
addresses, parcels, demographics, and participation data require privacy review
and may remain restricted even when individual inputs are publicly accessible.

### Statehouse, courthouse, government, and public institutions

Public-building locations and public institutional roles may support general
orientation. Exact access controls, security procedures, restricted interiors,
operational dependencies, protective measures, response gaps, and exploitable
infrastructure detail remain fail closed.

### Property, parcels, and civic profiling

Parcel, appraisal, zoning, planning, election, meeting, or public-comment data
must not become title truth, lawful-access advice, household dossiers, political
opinion profiles, or targeted analysis of individuals. A public record retains
its source-native role and legal limitations.

### Kansas River, floodplain, roads, bridges, and current safety

Dated river, floodplain, transportation, and infrastructure material can support
historical or general planning context. It does not establish current flood
conditions, bridge or road safety, closures, evacuation needs, engineering
fitness, insurance status, or emergency guidance. Current questions should route
to the appropriate authoritative channel or abstain.

### Indigenous, pre-territorial, and cultural context

The plan's early time buckets include Indigenous and pre-territorial context.
Future implementation requires appropriate source and cultural-authority review.
Public institutional archives or generated narratives must not speak for Nations
or communities, expose sensitive sites, or flatten contested history into a
single civic story.

## 7. Finite outcomes and candidate reason codes

Future Shawnee County runtime behavior should preserve KFM's four terminal
outcomes:

| Outcome | Shawnee County use |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Released, in-scope evidence supports the bounded civic, historical, hydrologic, transportation, or institutional claim and required citation/policy checks pass. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence is missing, stale, conflicted, legally unfit, historically incomplete, too coarse, too precise, or unsupported for the requested current/time/space scope. |
| `DENY` | Privacy, student/family data, political profiling, public-institution security, infrastructure vulnerability, property misuse, restricted detail, or an internal/public-path violation blocks the request. |
| `ERROR` | Evidence resolution, policy evaluation, citation validation, contract validation, integrity checking, or a required dependency fails. |

The build plan does not register a county reason-code vocabulary. The following
codes are **PROPOSED examples only** and must not be treated as current contract
or policy enums:

|---|---:|---|
