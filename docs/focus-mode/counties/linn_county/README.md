## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The build plan names several source families. Their role separation is more
important than the mere fact that they are public.

| Source family in the plan | Proposed role | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service | Refuge mission and public context | Sensitive-species locator, operational access intelligence, current closure authority unless explicitly current and in scope |
| USGS monitoring location | Monitoring-source reference and, after admission, time-bounded observations | Flood warning, safe-crossing, boating, habitat-health, or emergency authority |
| Kansas floodplain source / FEMA-linked context | Floodplain/source-status literacy | Parcel-specific insurance, permitting, legal, current-flood, or evacuation determination |
| KDA / USDA aggregate agriculture | County-scale statistical context | Farm, operator, household, parcel, causation, or compliance profile |
| Linn County official site | Civic/source-routing and county orientation | Title, ownership, private access, emergency, or legal authority beyond source scope |
| AI-generated narrative | Bounded explanation after evidence and policy checks | Evidence, source authority, policy decision, review approval, or release authority |

### Temporal rules

1. Preserve the source's own observation, publication, update, retrieval, and
   validity times when material.
2. A dated planning observation does not answer a current-condition question.
3. Missing or unclear freshness for consequential claims produces `ABSTAIN`,
   `DENY`, or `ERROR`, not a guessed current answer.
4. A current source page does not automatically make every statement on it a
   current operational status.
5. Corrections and supersessions must remain visible when a source or released
   KFM object changes.

## 6. Ecology, refuge access, floodplain, and public safety

### Ecology and refuge boundary

The build plan explicitly treats refuge ecology and closed-area information as a
high-sensitivity public-design problem. A future product should prefer
refuge-scale or otherwise reviewed generalized context and avoid discovery
assistance for sensitive species, nesting/roosting, rare habitat, management
operations, or restricted-area access.

### Floodplain and current-condition boundary

Floodplain maps, river monitoring, and historical flood context can explain
landscape relationships, but they do not make KFM a current flood-warning or
safety service. Current flood, safe-crossing, road, evacuation, recreation, or
emergency questions require current responsible authority.

### Recreation and access boundary

A public refuge page, map, or recreational context does not establish that a
particular area is open, safe, lawful to enter, hunt, fish, boat, park, or cross
at a given time. Personalized or current access conclusions should abstain,
deny, or redirect as appropriate.

### Cross-layer reconstruction

Generalized public layers must be reviewed in composition, not only one at a
time. Multiple individually safe layers must not reconstruct restricted habitat,
closed-area, private-property, or operational detail through overlay,
proximity, labeling, search, or export.

<a id="7-property-agriculture-monitoring-and-infrastructure"></a>

## 7. Property, agriculture, monitoring, and infrastructure

### Property and people

Do not turn county or public GIS sources into:

- title or ownership determinations;
- permission-to-enter advice;
- living-person profiles;
- private-land conflict narratives;
- household, operator, or assessed-value inference.

### Agriculture

The build plan's agriculture material is aggregate planning context. Any future
KFM product should preserve aggregation and source limitations rather than
joining it to parcel/person/operator records to infer individual behavior or
conflict with refuge management.

### Monitoring

A monitoring station or gauge is evidence-source context. Its presence does not
establish current safety, future conditions, ecological health, legal status, or
warning authority. Observation interpretation must remain tied to source role,
time, method, evidence, and policy.

### Infrastructure

Exact vulnerability-sensitive infrastructure, emergency routes, or operational
facilities should not be exposed merely because they can be spatially joined or
are visible in another public source. Minimum necessary detail and current
policy govern exposure.

## 8. Finite outcomes and candidate reason codes

The public Focus posture remains exactly four terminal outcomes:

| Outcome | Linn County use |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Narrow, released, evidence-supported, policy-safe context with citations and limitations. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, currentness, source fitness, scope, or authority is insufficient. |
| `DENY` | Request seeks harmful precision, restricted access intelligence, private-person/property inference, or otherwise prohibited content. |
| `ERROR` | Contract, evidence resolution, policy, citation validation, or runtime failure prevents a trustworthy response. |

Candidate reason codes from the build-plan design remain **PROPOSED**. Useful
families include:

- `EVIDENCE_NOT_RESOLVED`
- `TEMPORAL_FITNESS_UNRESOLVED`
- `SENSITIVE_ECOLOGY_DETAIL_WITHHELD`
- `RESTRICTED_ACCESS_DETAIL_WITHHELD`
- `CURRENT_FLOOD_OR_SAFETY_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED`
- `PROPERTY_OR_ACCESS_DETERMINATION_DENIED`
- `AGGREGATE_TO_PRIVATE_INFERENCE_DENIED`
- `PUBLIC_INTERNAL_STORE_ACCESS_DENIED`

A reason-code name appearing in this README is not acceptance of a contract,
schema, or policy enum.

## 15. Maintenance, correction, and rollback

### Maintenance triggers

Revisit this README when:

- the sibling plan changes materially;
- Focus Mode placement or naming authority changes;
- source admission, rights, or sensitivity review establishes new facts;
- a Linn County contract, schema, fixture, validator, API, UI, or release object
  is implemented and verified;
- public-safe detail or generalization policy changes;
- a correction, withdrawal, or supersession affects a cited county claim.

### Correction posture

A documentation correction should preserve the old claim's lineage where
material and identify the evidence that changed. A public correction is a
separate governed action and may require API, map, cache, export, search, or AI
propagation beyond a Markdown edit.

### Rollback

Before merge, rollback is to close or abandon the draft pull request and feature
branch. After merge, use a transparent revert or forward-fix against the actual
merged commit; do not rewrite shared history. If a future released Linn County
artifact is implicated, a Git revert alone may be insufficient: correction,
withdrawal, cache invalidation, supersession, and release rollback must follow
the applicable KFM controls.
