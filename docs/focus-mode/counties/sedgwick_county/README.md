## 4. Proposed county proof slice

**PROPOSED thesis:** Sedgwick County can prove that a major metropolitan,
aviation, industrial, river, severe-weather, transportation, public-health, and
historical interface remains useful while refusing operational intelligence,
private data, current-warning substitution, legal/property overreach, and
uncited model interpretation.

### Proposed public-safe composition

| Proposed element | Public value | Required boundary | Status |
|---|---|---|---:|
| County and Wichita metro orientation | Establish county and settlement context. | Administrative geometry is not parcel, service, legal, or jurisdictional advice. | `PROPOSED` |
| Aviation-manufacturing history | Explain public historical and economic context. | Generalize facilities and supply chains; exclude operational, defense, security, and vulnerability detail. | `PROPOSED` |
| Airport and military-base public context | Show broad public geography and historical role. | No operations, access-control, readiness, response, logistics, vulnerability, or sensitive infrastructure detail. | `PROPOSED` |
| Arkansas and Little Arkansas river context | Explain urban hydrology and place relationships. | Not flood warning, crossing safety, water-quality, water-right, engineering, or utility guidance. | `PROPOSED` |
| Chisholm Trail and cattle-era context | Explain movement, commerce, and historical transition. | Preserve route uncertainty, source role, cultural context, and property boundaries. | `PROPOSED` |
| Rail and highway corridors | Explain metro development and movement. | Not live traffic, closure, construction, routing, security, or safety authority. | `PROPOSED` |
| Severe-weather historical context | Distinguish events, observations, models, forecasts, and warnings. | Historical or modeled context must never masquerade as a current official warning. | `PROPOSED` |
| Public-health and service context | Explain aggregate institutional/service geography. | No patient, household, cause-of-death, medical, forensic, or person-level exposure. | `PROPOSED` |
| Public historical and civic context | Support public learning and timeline navigation. | Exact restricted cultural resources, burials, sacred places, or private-person detail remain withheld. | `PROPOSED` |

### Composition and anti-inference rule

A future Focus Mode may connect released themes only through governed identifiers
and public-safe transforms. Public layers must not be recombined to reconstruct
withheld detail. Prohibited examples include:

- inferring a defense or aviation supplier's vulnerability from industry, parcel,
  road, utility, and airport layers;
- identifying a household health condition from service areas, addresses,
  demographics, and public records;
- turning airport or base context plus transportation data into an operational
  access or response map;
- deriving a sensitive cultural or archaeological location from historic
  narratives, parcels, terrain, and imagery;
- treating floodplain, assessor, or utility layers as a property-specific legal,
  insurance, title, engineering, or safety determination.

### Non-goals

The first slice is not:

- a flight, airport, base, industrial, utility, or supply-chain operations tool;
- a severe-weather warning, forecast, alert, evacuation, or emergency service;
- a household-health, forensic, medical, demographic-profiling, or address tool;
- a parcel-title, ownership, access, valuation, zoning, insurance, or legal tool;
- a flood, water-quality, crossing-safety, engineering, or potability service;
- an exact archaeology, burial, sacred-site, rare-species, or habitat-discovery
  surface;
- a direct browser-to-model route or an unrestricted AI encyclopedia.

## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The build plan names candidate source families. They remain **source seeds**, not
admitted KFM sources, until current identity, terms, rights, coverage, cadence,
sensitivity, allowed-use posture, and source-native limitations are reviewed.

| Candidate source role | What it may support after admission | What it must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| County and city civic/GIS sources | Public orientation and source-native administrative context. | Title, survey, emergency, unrestricted redistribution, or universal county truth. |
| Aviation and public-history sources | Dated, evidence-backed historical and economic context. | Facility operations, defense posture, supply-chain intelligence, or security authority. |
| Airport and military public-information sources | Broad public history and generalized geography. | Access, readiness, procedures, logistics, vulnerability, or response intelligence. |
| USGS/FEMA/Kansas water and flood sources | Observation or regulatory context at named times and scales. | Current warning, legal determination, insurance advice, engineering result, or water-safety verdict. |
| NOAA/NWS hazard sources | Historical events, observations, forecasts, or warnings according to source role. | KFM-issued alert, timeless severe-weather truth, or model-as-observation. |
| Public-health and service sources | Aggregate statistics, institutional roles, and public program context. | Patient, household, diagnosis, forensic, cause-of-death, or individual risk claims. |
| Parcel/assessor/planning sources | Public planning and valuation context with explicit limitations. | Ownership, title, easement, lawful access, surveyed boundary, or private-person dossier. |
| Historical institutions and collections | Public historical interpretation and documentary context. | Unreviewed Indigenous/cultural authority, exact restricted sites, unrestricted media rights, or present-day inference. |
| AI-generated explanation | Bounded interpretation over released evidence. | Source truth, policy decision, reviewer approval, release authority, or proof. |

### Time dimensions to preserve

Where material, a future claim should distinguish:

- the real-world valid or event time;
- observation time;
- source publication or update time;
- KFM retrieval time;
- KFM review time;
- release time; and
- correction, supersession, withdrawal, or rollback time.

A source page marked “current,” a recent timestamp, or an official publisher name
does not by itself establish fitness for a particular claim.

## 6. Aviation, industry, defense, and infrastructure boundaries

### Public aviation and industrial context

A future public layer may explain reviewed historical, economic, or geographic
context at an appropriate level of generalization. It must preserve source date,
role, rights, limitations, uncertainty, and release state.

### Operational and vulnerability-sensitive detail

The following remain fail closed unless a separately governed, qualified process
explicitly supports a narrower use:

- airport or airbase access-control, operational, security, readiness, logistics,
  response, or restricted facility information;
- aircraft-production or industrial supply-chain dependencies that expose
  vulnerability, chokepoints, critical vendors, sensitive routing, or disruption
  analysis;
- detailed utility, water, communications, hospital, school, bridge, rail,
  emergency-service, or fuel-system dependencies;
- response staging, continuity plans, restricted floor plans, access paths,
  security zones, surveillance, or protection measures;
- compound analysis that reconstructs sensitive detail from individually public
  layers.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Public availability is not the same as KFM admissibility or public-safe
> recombination. The significance of a disclosure can arise from aggregation.

### Appropriate negative outcomes

- broad historical aviation question with released evidence: `ANSWER`;
- current facility operations or readiness question: `ABSTAIN` or `DENY`;
- exact vulnerability or access request: `DENY`;
- evidence, policy, or citation service failure: `ERROR`.

## 7. Severe-weather, floodplain, water, and emergency boundaries

### Source roles do not collapse

| Source/output class | Meaning | Unsafe substitution |
|---|---|---|
| Historical event record | Documents a past event under a named source/method. | Current warning or future prediction. |
| Observation or gage value | Measurement at a stated place and time. | Flood safety, crossing safety, evacuation, or forecast. |
| Forecast/model output | Estimated future or modeled condition with method and uncertainty. | Observation or official warning. |
| Official warning/advisory | Time-bounded operational message from a responsible authority. | Timeless KFM fact or durable copied alert. |
| Regulatory floodplain product | Official mapping under a named program/effective state. | Parcel-specific insurance, legal, engineering, or evacuation determination. |
| Derived KFM context layer | Released downstream interpretation or visualization. | Root evidence or independent emergency authority. |

### Required public posture

- show source role and timestamp;
- preserve valid/effective and release time;
- label stale or historical content;
- link users to responsible current authorities for operational action;
- abstain when current support is missing;
- deny attempts to expose restricted operational or vulnerability detail;
- never silently convert a model, historical event, screenshot, map color, or AI
  summary into an official warning.

### Water and floodplain limits

KFM must not infer:

- that a parcel is safe, insurable, outside regulation, or suitable for
  construction;
- that a road, bridge, crossing, trail, or neighborhood is currently passable or
  safe;
- that water is potable, uncontaminated, or fit for a specific use;
- that a utility or flood-control system is secure, available, or operating in a
  particular state;
- that historical flood impact proves present household or property exposure.

## 8. Public-health, property, demographic, cultural, and ecological boundaries

### Public-health and forensic context

Only aggregate, reviewed, purpose-limited context belongs in a future public
slice. Person-level health, forensic, medical, cause-of-death, household,
address, or case information remains outside the public path unless an
exceptionally specific lawful and governed basis exists.

### Parcel, title, and property

County parcel, tax, planning, zoning, or assessor data may support bounded public
orientation after admission. It does not automatically establish:

- ownership or title;
- easement, access, occupancy, or lawful use;
- surveyed boundary or legal description;
- current valuation or transaction status;
- household identity, demographics, health, or economic condition;
- liability, insurance, flood, zoning, or development entitlement.

### Demographic and urban analysis

Aggregate statistics and released analytical products must retain geography,
vintage, methodology, uncertainty, suppression, and non-individual scope.
Joining small-area demographics with property, health, education, policing, or
service records must not create re-identification or profiling risk.

### Cultural resources and historical interpretation

Public historical narratives may support learning. Exact archaeology, burial,
grave, sacred, traditional cultural property, restricted collection, or newly
inferred locations require qualified authority and may need denial,
generalization, staged access, or quarantine.

### Ecology and rare species

Public habitat context must not become a discovery or disturbance tool. Exact
rare-species, nest, roost, concentration, migration, or management-sensitive
locations remain fail closed unless qualified authority supports a public-safe
transform.

## 14. Maintenance, correction, and rollback

### Re-review triggers

Re-review this README when any of these change materially:

- the linked build plan or county-lane placement;
- accepted Directory Rules, Focus Mode ADRs, or parent lane contracts;
- admitted Sedgwick source inventory or source rights;

  policy;

- governed Explorer/API/runtime behavior;
- release, correction, withdrawal, cache invalidation, or rollback machinery.

### Documentation correction

A factual or repository-state error should be corrected through a reviewable
follow-up that cites the new evidence and preserves prior Git history. Do not
silently convert a dated plan statement into current truth.

### Before merge

Rollback is to close or abandon the draft pull request and branch. Remote branch
deletion is a separate action and is not required to stop the proposed change.

### After merge

Use a transparent revert or forward-fix pull request against the actual merged
commit. Do not rewrite shared history. Because this packet creates no public
county artifact, no public correction is triggered by this documentation change.

### Future public reliance

A future released Sedgwick County surface would require more than a Git revert:
correction notice, withdrawal or supersession state, cache and export
invalidation, map/search/story/AI propagation, and a recorded rollback target.
