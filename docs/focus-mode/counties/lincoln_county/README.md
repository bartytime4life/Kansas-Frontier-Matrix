# Lincoln County Focus Mode Documentation Boundary

> **Purpose.** Explain the repository-present Lincoln County planning lane and
> the evidence, geology, access, currentness, privacy, safety, review, correction,
> and rollback conditions that must remain visible before any Lincoln County
> Focus Mode can become more than a proposal.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This directory contains documentation, not a released county product.**
> The tracked build plan and this README do not admit a source, create an
> `EvidenceBundle`, activate Focus policy, implement a governed API route, approve
> a map or AI answer, or create release or publication state.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains a compatibility HOLD.** This README stays at the existing
> singular, snake_case path. Accepted Directory Rules v2 permits same-path human
> documentation under `docs/`, while proposed ADR-0027 cannot authorize migration
> to a plural, kebab-case control plane. Do not create a second writable Lincoln
> lane or rename this one through inference.

> [!WARNING]
> **Geology, collecting, property access, and live conditions can create unsafe
> confidence.** The sibling plan proposes Post Rock, Wilson Lake, agriculture,
> and historical-map context, but KFM must not turn dated planning sources,
> generalized geology, maps, statistics, or generated language into collecting
> permission, property access, an exact fossil or quarry guide, current reservoir
> or road safety, private-well advice, infrastructure detail, or an individual
> person, farm, business, owner, or parcel profile.

## 4. Proposed Lincoln County proof slice

The sibling plan proposes a **Lincoln County Post Rock Access, Suppression, and
Historical-Currentness Boundary Proof**. Its central question is whether KFM can
explain geologic heritage, county-scale statistics, dated transportation records,
and managed-water context without becoming an access guide, a sensitive-locality
map, a live operational service, or a profile system.

### Plan-defined public-safe themes

| Theme in the sibling plan | Bounded future use | Required limit |
|---|---|---|
| Lincoln County administrative frame | Released, vintage-labeled county context | No parcel, ownership, title, address, household, or individual inference |
| Population and business aggregates | Period-labeled public statistical explanation | No person, household, establishment, employer, worker, veteran, or business profile |
| County agriculture aggregates and disclosure flags | Explain reported totals and visible suppression states | No individual farm, producer, field, facility, worker, well, owner, parcel, or reconstructed hidden value |
| Post Rock and Fence-post limestone context | Generalized educational geologic heritage | No collecting, quarrying, fossil-locality, sampling, land-access, ownership, or legal permission |
| Historical transportation maps | Dated map lineage and change-over-time context | No current route, closure, access, property, or emergency conclusion |
| Current road and weather source routing | Direct users to responsible current authorities | No cached KFM operational answer or stale safety representation |
| Wilson Lake and Saline River context | Potential dated landscape context after separate governance | No current lake, dam, ramp, release, boating, fishing, swimming, fire, weather, or safety answer |
| Future scientific or operational observations | Potential timestamped context after source admission and release | No direct raw-to-public path, inferred authority, or alert role |

### Product and authority boundary

A future Lincoln Focus Mode may become map-rich and evidence-visible only after
the relevant objects are admitted, validated, policy-reviewed, released, and
served through governed interfaces. The renderer, Evidence Drawer, timeline,
search, or generated explanation remains downstream presentation.

The first observable proof should be synthetic and no-network. It should test
negative outcomes and cross-layer reconstruction before any live source or public
carrier is considered.

## 5. Public safety, sensitivity, and finite outcomes

### Fail-closed request matrix

| Request or display class | Expected bounded posture | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Bounded, released, time-labeled Lincoln context with resolving evidence and explicit limitations | `ANSWER` | Evidence, policy, source role, limitations, and release state are visible |
| Missing, stale, conflicting, revoked, inaccessible, or rights-unclear evidence | `ABSTAIN` | The system cannot support a trustworthy claim |
| Generalized Post Rock educational context | `ANSWER` only after evidence, scale, rights, and release closure | Geology may be explained without creating access permission |
| Fossil, stone, quarry, sampling, roadcut, or private-property collecting guidance | `DENY` | Scientific context is not legal, landowner, or land-manager permission |
| Exact fossil, quarry, archaeological, burial, habitat, or vulnerable geologic locality | `DENY` and withhold sensitive detail | Harmful precision, resource damage, trespass, and cultural sensitivity risk |
| Current Wilson Lake, dam, ramp, boating, fishing, swimming, road, fire, weather, or emergency advice | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` and route to responsible current authority | KFM is not an operational, alert, or emergency service |
| Dam, utility, communications, emergency, transportation, quarry, or facility detail beyond minimum public need | `DENY` / exclude from the public derivative | Operational and infrastructure risk |
| Aggregate statistics joined to a person, household, business, worker, farm, producer, owner, lease, well, or parcel | `DENY` | Privacy, property, statistical-disclosure, and misuse risk |
| Suppressed or hidden value reconstructed from other sources | `DENY` | Suppression must remain effective after joins and derived analysis |
| Private-well, water-right, potability, contamination, aquifer, remaining-life, or health conclusion | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` | KFM lacks property, legal, laboratory, medical, and regulatory authority |
| Historical map presented as current route, access, closure, or safe-travel truth | `ABSTAIN` | Historical and current authority roles must remain distinct |
| Direct UI access to `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, candidate geometry, or model output | `ERROR` / `DENY` | Trust-membrane bypass |
| Runtime, schema, evidence, citation, digest, or policy failure | `ERROR` | A bounded fault must remain visible without leaking protected detail |

The plan's `LC-*` reason codes are useful design lineage only. They remain
`PROPOSED` until an accepted contract and policy vocabulary establishes their
meaning and compatibility.

### Cross-layer reconstruction rule

A field that appears harmless alone may become sensitive when joined with
historic maps, parcels, farms, wells, geology, transportation, reservoir
features, infrastructure, or current observations. Future validation must test
both individual layers and the combined display or query context. Redaction in
one carrier is not sufficient if another carrier reconstructs the denied fact.

### Public-interface requirements

Any later public Lincoln surface must make these states visible and accessible:

- `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, and `ERROR`;
- evidence missing, stale, conflicting, corrected, superseded, restricted, or
  revoked;
- source role and time basis;
- generalized or withheld geometry and a plain-language reason;
- suppression and anti-reconstruction state;
- correction and withdrawal state;
- an official-source route where KFM is not the responsible authority.

A fluent response, polished map, passing schema check, or green workflow must
not hide a negative state or imply collecting, access, safety, property, or
individual-level authority.

## 6. Source role, rights, and currentness boundary

The build plan names source families that were checked during its 2026-06-10
authoring run. This README does not refresh those external checks and does not
admit any source.

| Plan-named source family | Proposed role | Must not be collapsed into |
|---|---|---|
| U.S. Census Bureau county statistics | Population, business, and disclosure-aware aggregate context | Person, household, establishment, employer, worker, veteran, or current local-government authority |
| USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service county profile | County-level agricultural statistics and suppression signals | Individual farm, producer, field, facility, owner, worker, well, water-right, or hidden-value truth |
| Kansas Geological Survey / GeoKansas Post Rock material | State scientific and educational geologic context | Collecting, quarrying, sampling, property access, exact fossil locality, title, engineering, or legal permission |
| Kansas Department of Transportation historical county maps | Dated transportation-map lineage | Current road, closure, route, access, property, or emergency truth |
| Kansas Department of Transportation current travel surface | Current road-condition source routing | Durable KFM safety answer, historical-map authority, or property access |
| National Weather Service Wichita material | Candidate current weather and hazard routing | KFM alert authority, guaranteed full-county coverage, or a durable cached answer |
| Future county and municipal public surfaces | Administrative and public-service context | Complete local authority, title evidence, person profile, or emergency instruction |
| Future USACE, KDWP, USGS, KDA, KDHE, FEMA, and water-source families | Potential managed-water, recreation, observation, regulation, and hazard context after review | Guessed source role, direct raw public feed, private-well conclusion, current safety, or infrastructure vulnerability |

### Admission questions for every source

Before a source can support a public claim, record and review:

1. stable source identity, publisher, authority role, and exact version or
   retrieval state;
2. publication, effective, observation, valid, retrieval, release, correction,
   and supersession times as distinct fields where material;
3. rights, attribution, extraction, transformation, redistribution, and
   derivative-display terms;
4. allowed claim scope and explicitly prohibited inference;
5. sensitivity, minimum necessary detail, safe spatial and temporal resolution,
   and cross-layer reconstruction risk;
6. freshness, outage, correction, revision, supersession, and withdrawal
   behavior;
7. deterministic provenance from candidate material through any public-safe
   derivative;
8. an `EvidenceRef` that resolves to an admissible `EvidenceBundle`;
9. policy, accountable review, release, correction, and rollback references.

### Role and currentness anti-collapse

- KGS or GeoKansas educational geology is not collecting, quarrying, access,
  title, or land-management authority.
- A historical KDOT map is not a current road, closure, routing, access, or
  emergency source.
- NASS county statistics are not farm, producer, well, water-right, owner, or
  parcel records.
- Census business aggregates are not establishment, employer, worker, veteran,
  or household profiles.
- NWS may support current weather routing only within verified product and
  coverage scope; it does not make KFM an alert service.
- USACE, KDWP, USGS, state water, environmental, emergency, and local-government
  roles must be verified rather than inferred from topic or visibility.
- Public access alone is not permission to extract, transform, join, tile,
  redistribute, or publish.

## 7. Evidence and readiness boundary

A Lincoln public result must remain downstream of the governed KFM path:

1. identify a source once in the appropriate source-authority surface;
2. review authority, rights, access, time, geography, sensitivity, correction,
   and minimum-necessary use;
3. admit candidate material to `RAW`, or hold it in `WORK / QUARANTINE`;
4. derive and validate `PROCESSED` material with transform and provenance
   lineage;
5. create catalog or triplet relationships without treating indexes as truth;
6. resolve every consequential `EvidenceRef` to an `EvidenceBundle` and apply
   policy;
7. make a separate promotion and release decision with correction, withdrawal,
   and rollback targets;
8. serve only released public-safe artifacts through governed interfaces;
9. keep map rendering and AI interpretation subordinate to that evidence and
   decision state.

current accepted object families and validators where they exist. New contracts,
schemas, policy families, reason-code authority, or paths require separate
