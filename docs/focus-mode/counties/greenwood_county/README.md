# Greenwood County Focus Mode

> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Greenwood County planning lane, make
> its fire, smoke, air-health, reservoir, flood, emergency-currentness,
> infrastructure, private-land, evidence, rights, and public-safety boundaries
> visible, and route future work through KFM source, evidence, policy, review,
> release, correction, and rollback controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation, authorization, safety
> guidance, approval, or release evidence.** This directory contains this
> README and one Greenwood County build plan. Their presence prevents accidental
> duplicate planning; it does not establish source admission, semantic
> correctness, validator success, runtime behavior, fire or smoke decision
> authority, release, deployment, or publication.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Placement remains a compatibility question.** This same-path update repairs
> an existing README under `docs/focus-mode/counties/greenwood_county/`.
> Directory Rules v2 is accepted through ADR-0029; proposed ADR-0027 describes a
> plural, kebab-case control plane but remains unaccepted. No file is moved,
> renamed, copied into a parallel tree, or declared canonical here.

> [!WARNING]
> **No burn decision and no live smoke or health guidance.** The linked plan
> proposes public-safe education about Flint Hills, prescribed-fire, smoke-
> management, and Fall River Lake source roles. KFM must not authorize or
> recommend a burn, interpret current smoke or air conditions for a person,
> replay alerts as its own guidance, or turn lake, flood, water, and emergency
> material into current operational advice.

> [!WARNING]
> **Sensitive and unsafe inference must fail closed.** Missing authority,
> evidence, rights, safe geometry, currentness, policy, or review closure
> requires `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR`. Exact or reconstructive private-land,
> living-person, infrastructure-vulnerability, wildlife-sensitive,
> archaeological, sacred, or other harmful-precision detail must not reach an
> ordinary public client.

## 4. Proposed Greenwood County proof slice

The build plan proposes a public-safe learning slice organized around three
related but non-interchangeable concerns:

1. **Flint Hills and prescribed-fire source literacy** — explain why public
   authorities provide fire and smoke-management context without issuing a
   burn decision or reproducing dynamic model output.
2. **Fall River Lake and watershed context** — provide bounded public setting
   and source-role orientation without operational, vulnerability, current
   safety, water-right, or legal inference.
3. **Generalized prairie and public-landscape context** — support broad public
   learning without private-land, sensitive ecology, cultural, archaeological,
   facility, or other harmful precision.

### Proposed first-slice surfaces

| Proposed surface | Intended value | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Greenwood County orientation frame | Locate the county and route users to responsible public authorities | No parcel, owner, ranch-operation, title, tax, residence, or emergency inference |
| Prescribed-fire and smoke-management context card | Explain candidate authority roles and why current information may matter | No burn authorization, recommendation, active-burn depiction, live smoke prediction, or personalized health conclusion |
| Current smoke, air-health, and emergency redirect card | Identify the responsible current official route without answering the live question | Link-out and checked-at state only; no cached KFM alert, health clearance, or operational verdict |
| Fall River Lake context card | Explain proposed broad lake, river, watershed, and public-purpose context | No engineering, control, vulnerability, current access, boating, flood-response, or lake-safety guidance |
| Water-program and flood-source card | Distinguish program, regulatory, currentness, and official-source roles | No water-right, allocation, insurance, compliance, evacuation, property, or legal determination |
| Generalized prairie and public-landscape card | Show reviewed county-scale context at public-safe precision | No exact wildlife-sensitive, private-ranch, archaeological, sacred, or unnecessary facility detail |
| Evidence Drawer and boundary panels | Expose evidence state, source role, time basis, limitations, review, and reason codes | Unresolved evidence, policy, or release state cannot be polished into an answer |

These are **PROPOSED planning surfaces**. No card, layer, payload, source
descriptor, evidence bundle, policy decision, route, or released artifact is
confirmed by this README.

### Deferred planning surfaces

- direct consumption of dynamic smoke forecasts, current air quality, burn-ban
  status, active-fire data, or emergency alerts;
- live lake levels, reservoir operations, flood observations, closures, or
  recreation-access status;
- detailed public-land, facility, wildlife, private-ranch, parcel, burn-plan,
  water-right, or agricultural-operation joins;
- current regulatory or compliance interpretation;
- structured history, archaeology, or cultural-place geometry without the
  appropriate authority, rights, sensitivity, and review support;
- public tiles, API behavior, AI answers, or release beyond deterministic
  fixtures and separately governed promotion.

### Excluded or denied by default

- whether, when, where, or how a burn should occur;
- whether current smoke or air conditions are safe for a person, household,
  school, worker, animal, property, or activity;
- cached emergency, warning, restriction, closure, evacuation, road, lake, or
  flood-safety advice;
- dam, reservoir, water-system, fire-response, or other operational and
  vulnerability-relevant detail;
- exact sensitive wildlife, private-ranch, archaeological, burial, sacred,
  culturally restricted, living-person, or unnecessary facility precision;
- property, ownership, title, insurance, access, water-right, or individual
  legal and compliance conclusions;
- direct public access to `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, restricted material,
  source-system actions, or model output.

## 5. Source-role and currentness boundary

### Source seeds are research leads, not admissions

The build plan records candidate source roles from a 2026-05-23 planning run.
This README confirms only that the plan names those candidates. It does not
repeat that run, establish that a page is still current, validate a source's
authority for every proposed claim, or clear rights, caching, transformation,
geometry, sensitivity, or release.

| Candidate source role named by the plan | May support after current verification, admission, and review | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| Greenwood County administrative/public material | County identity, civic orientation, and responsible official routing | Private-land, parcel, emergency, regulatory, title, access, or living-person truth |
| U.S. Army Corps of Engineers public lake material | Bounded public setting and broad project-purpose context | Reservoir or dam operations, engineering, vulnerability, current access, boating, flood-response, or safety guidance |
| Kansas Flint Hills Smoke Management public material | Explanation of the source route and decision-support purpose | KFM burn approval, recommendation, active-fire layer, model replay, or freshness guarantee |
| Kansas Department of Health and Environment material | Dated policy context or responsible public-health/current-source routing within an admitted scope | Personalized medical judgment, exposure clearance, present local condition, or unrestricted derivative product |
| Kansas Water Office public material | Program and current-source role orientation | Individual water right, allocation, supply guarantee, compliance, quality, current safety, or operations determination |
| FEMA flood-hazard public route | Official product discovery and supersession/currentness explanation | Live flooding, emergency action, insurance, legal, evacuation, or property-specific verdict |
| Candidate ecological, geological, transportation, history, or monitoring sources | Later bounded context after authority, rights, precision, and currentness review | Automatic admission, public precision, current operational truth, or cross-domain authority collapse |
| Generated narrative | Plain-language rendering after evidence and policy closure | Evidence, source authority, policy decision, health or safety advice, or release proof |

### Minimum admission record

Before a Greenwood source may support a public artifact, record at least:

- stable source identity, accountable authority, and exact allowed claim role;
- access and check time, version or effective date, temporal coverage, cadence,
  stale behavior, supersession, outage, and correction route;
- rights, terms, attribution, caching, redistribution, and derivative-use
  posture;
- spatial precision, ecological, cultural, archaeological, infrastructure,
  private-land, living-person, and reconstruction risk;
- allowed and prohibited fields, transformations, generalization, redaction,
  quarantine, denial, and retention behavior;
- EvidenceRef and EvidenceBundle relationships plus citation, policy, review,
  release, correction, and rollback prerequisites.

If any required element is unresolved, the applicable candidate stays in
research, `WORK`, or `QUARANTINE`, or is limited to a responsible-authority
redirect. It does not become a public answer because its source is public.

### Currentness anti-collapse

| Source character | Safe public posture | Unsafe inference |
|---|---|---|
| Dated plan or policy context | Show date, role, limitations, and supersession risk | Treat as a current rule, condition, restriction, recommendation, or forecast |
| Dynamic smoke, air, fire, alert, lake, or flood route | Redirect to responsible current authority unless a separately governed snapshot is admitted and released | Cache, paraphrase, or predict current safety as KFM truth |
| Program or regulatory material | Explain scope and source role | Infer a person's legal status, entitlement, compliance, insurance, evacuation, or property outcome |
| General public information | Bound claims to the admitted evidence and checked temporal scope | Assume public visibility clears rights, sensitivity, precision, or release |

## 6. Public safety and governed outcomes

### Finite outcome examples

| Request pattern | Required posture | Public explanation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| “According to reviewed sources, what is the proposed public context for Greenwood County or Fall River Lake?” | `ANSWER` only after EvidenceBundle, policy, citation, review, and release closure | Bounded setting and source-role context with time basis and limitations |
| “Why do public authorities provide prescribed-fire and smoke-management information?” | `ANSWER` only for admitted, reviewed context | Explains purpose; never recommends or authorizes a burn |
| “Should I burn today, and where will the smoke go?” | `DENY` or `ABSTAIN` with responsible-authority routing | KFM is not a burn-decision or live smoke-forecast service |
| “Is the smoke safe for my child, workers, animals, or property right now?” | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` with responsible public-health/current-source routing | No individualized or live health and safety judgment |
| “What is the current burn ban, warning, closure, lake, or flood status?” | `ABSTAIN` with responsible-current-authority routing | No cached KFM alert or operational advice |
| “Show dam controls, emergency staging, or vulnerable infrastructure.” | `DENY` | No operational or vulnerability-relevant disclosure |
| “Does this flood or water source decide my evacuation, insurance, water right, allocation, or legal status?” | `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` | Source literacy is not an individual emergency, property, or legal determination |
| “Show exact wildlife-sensitive, private-ranch, archaeological, sacred, or facility locations.” | `DENY` or generalized reviewed output only | Harmful precision remains withheld |
| “Answer from a raw capture, internal store, live model, or generated text directly.” | `DENY` or `ERROR` | Public trust membrane and cite-or-abstain remain enforced |

These examples are documentation expectations, not proof that a policy engine,
reason-code registry, governed API, or UI currently implements them.

### Public trust path

A future Greenwood public answer must follow the inherited KFM trust path:

1. define the county, spatial, temporal, and question scope;
2. identify an admitted source and allowed source role;
3. resolve `EvidenceRef` to an `EvidenceBundle`;
4. apply currentness, rights, sensitivity, precision, privacy, infrastructure,
   and policy checks;
5. require accountable review and released public-safe artifacts;
6. return a finite `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR` envelope with
   citations, limitations, time basis, and reason codes;
7. preserve correction, withdrawal, supersession, and rollback lineage.

An ordinary public client must remain downstream of the governed API and
released public-safe artifacts. It must not read canonical/internal stores,
invoke a live source or model directly, or treat a map, badge, screenshot,
fixture, plan, or generated explanation as root truth.

### Persistent Greenwood safety cues

Any future map or AI surface proposed by the plan must keep these cues visible:

- context is not a burn authorization or recommendation;
- KFM does not provide live smoke, air-health, warning, lake, flood, emergency,
  or access guidance;
- official redirects leave KFM and do not mean KFM answered the live question;
- source role, checked time, effective time, stale/supersession state,
  limitations, and review state are inspectable;
- generalized or withheld geometry is explicit;
- unresolved evidence or policy produces a finite negative state, not polished
  uncertainty;
- corrected, withdrawn, stale, denied, loading, empty, degraded, and error
  states remain distinguishable.

Until those gates are evidenced, the lane remains planning-only and must not be

  or sensitive-location negative case does not fail closed;
- a public client would reach `RAW`, `WORK`, `QUARANTINE`, canonical stores,
  source-system actions, or a model directly;
