> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This directory contains planning documentation, not a released county
> product.** The README and sibling build plan do not admit a source, resolve an
> `EvidenceBundle`, activate policy, implement an authenticated Focus route,
> approve a map or AI answer, or create promotion, release, deployment, or
> publication state.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Indigenous history, living tribal communities, burial grounds, sacred
> places, and archaeological context require accountable cultural-authority and
> sensitivity review.** A public marker, tourism page, archive, map, or already
> published coordinate does not automatically authorize KFM to reproduce,

## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

Plan-named sources remain candidates until identity, terms, rights, scope,
sensitivity, cadence, correction behavior, and permitted use are verified.

| Candidate source role | May support after governed admission | Must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| Tribal or culturally accountable authority | Preferred terminology, living-community context, stewardship, sensitivity limits, correction or withdrawal requests | Generic tourist narrative, exhaustive history, legal judgment, or permission to expose sensitive detail |
| Official local government | Current administrative structure, public services, boundaries, plans, and official notices within scope and date | Complete historical authority, tribal authority, property title, legal advice, or emergency substitution |
| Historical record, archive, museum, library, or map collection | Dated records, maps, photographs, catalogs, provenance, and public-history context | Automatic rights to reproduce, georeference, enrich, publish, or infer current conditions |
| Journalism or public-history narrative | Interviews, context, contested-memory framing, and leads for corroboration | Sovereign evidence, complete history, legal finding, or current operational authority |
| Hydrologic or flood source | Observations, modeled surfaces, regulatory boundaries, or official forecasts according to role | Interchangeable river truth, parcel safety, insurance, engineering, recreation, or emergency advice |
| Environmental screening or modeled indicator | Comparative context with method, time, uncertainty, and limitations | Measured exposure, contamination, diagnosis, causation, culpability, or individual risk |
| Regulated-facility or enforcement record | Source-specific record existence and status at a stated time | Complete facility condition, guilt, innocence, compliance verdict, cleanup success, or health finding |
| Public-health aggregate | Population-level measure for stated geography, period, method, and suppression rules | Household or individual diagnosis, causation, prognosis, or reidentification |
| Transportation, rail, bridge, or utility source | Public network orientation and source-specific historical or administrative context | Tactical details, vulnerabilities, live conditions, maintenance status, access, or emergency routing |
| Parcel, assessor, tax, or planning source | Source-specific administrative record at a stated date | Title, ownership certainty, lawful access, residency, valuation truth, or legal conclusion |
| Generated narrative or model output | Bounded explanation after evidence, policy, review, and citation gates | Evidence, cultural authority, policy decision, review, or release proof |

### Temporal fitness rules

- Current governance, services, boundaries, facilities, or responsibilities
  require official-current sources, retrieval time, effective date, and
  supersession handling.
- Flood, weather, water level, road, bridge, transit, utility, incident, or
  emergency questions route to the responsible current authority.
- Facility, permit, inspection, complaint, enforcement, cleanup, or
  redevelopment status binds to the exact source system, date, vocabulary, and
  correction path; absence is not proof.
- Environmental and health indicators disclose represented period, release
  date, geography, method, uncertainty, missingness, and suppression.
- Historical maps and events disclose represented date, creation/publication
  date, source role, later interpretation, and known limits.
- Property and planning records disclose jurisdiction, record type, effective
  date, and the non-title boundary.
- Generated interpretation binds to the evidence, policy, and review snapshot
  that permitted it and is regenerated or withdrawn after material correction.

A source can be authoritative for one field and unfit for another.

## 6. Indigenous, tribal, burial, sacred, and archaeological boundary

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Public availability is not cultural clearance.** Searchability, a visitor
> page, historic marker, map, coordinate, or prior publication does not by itself
> authorize KFM to reproduce exact detail, join it to other layers, or generate
> a culturally authoritative narrative.

The plan uses both “Wyandot National Burying Ground” and “Huron Cemetery.”
This README does not silently choose a canonical or preferred name. Identity,
terminology, jurisdiction, living-community context, stewardship, and correction
expectations remain **NEEDS VERIFICATION** with appropriate authorities.

| Question or data class | Default posture | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| High-level explanation of cultural or historical significance | `ABSTAIN` until evidence and accountable review support the wording; then narrowly `ANSWER` | Fluent summary cannot substitute for authority or review |
| Exact grave, burial, remains, sacred, archaeological, ceremonial, access, or subsurface detail | `DENY` or generalize | Protect dignity, sovereignty, safety, and misuse risk |
| Already-public coordinate or visitor marker | Possible orientation point only | Public discovery is not permission to enrich or republish |
| Historic maps, photographs, transcriptions, or burial records | Quarantine or restricted review | Rights, context, sensitivity, and permitted use remain separate |
| Interpretation of removal, allotment, treaty, sovereignty, identity, or community history | `ABSTAIN` unless admitted evidence and qualified review support the exact claim | Legal, cultural, and historical meanings are authority-sensitive |
| Descendant, relative, living-community-member, or current-address identification | `DENY` absent explicit lawful and policy-approved purpose | Living-person privacy and reidentification risk |
| Inference of unrecorded locations from terrain, imagery, parcels, maps, or narrative clues | `DENY` | Cross-layer reconstruction can recreate withheld precision |
| Accountable correction, restriction, or withdrawal request | Hold affected projection and route through governed review | Cultural harm should not wait for routine cadence |

A future public card or answer must state whose terminology and interpretation
are represented, avoid presenting living communities only as historical
subjects, disclose omissions and contested framing, preserve correction and
restriction paths, and omit exact or reconstructable detail unless explicitly
authorized through governed release review.

|---|---|---|
| Neighborhood, immigration, labor, or community history | Source-supported, time-bounded, aggregate historical context | No living-person legal status, nationality, ethnicity, religion, politics, health, address, family, employer, or household profiling |
| Demographic or socioeconomic data | Reviewed aggregate statistics with geography, period, denominator, uncertainty, and suppression | No small-cell reconstruction, protected-class targeting, or claim that an aggregate describes an individual |
| School, youth, or family context | Public institutional and historical orientation at safe precision | No student, family, attendance, disciplinary, special-needs, address, or security detail |
| Parcel, assessor, tax, zoning, or planning record | Source-specific administrative context with date and jurisdiction | No title, ownership certainty, tenancy, lawful access, residency, value truth, compliance, or person-property linkage |
| Environmental screening or modeled indicator | Method-bounded aggregate comparison | No measured-exposure, contamination, disease, causation, or facility-culpability conclusion |
| Public-health aggregate | Population-level context with method and limitations | No diagnosis, prognosis, treatment, individual risk, household inference, or reidentification |
| Facility, permit, complaint, incident, inspection, or enforcement record | Source-specific record context when lawfully public and policy-approved | No allegation-as-fact, completeness claim, guilt, innocence, compliance verdict, or retaliation aid |
| Road, rail, bridge, transit, utility, riverfront, industrial, or public-institution context | Generalized public orientation | No vulnerabilities, access controls, tactical routes, nonpublic dependencies, live conditions, or emergency operations |
| Historic address, directory, map, or photograph | Reviewed historical context at fit precision | No automatic join to current parcels, occupants, descendants, contacts, or sensitive facilities |

### Environmental-justice source-role rule

Every field must be labeled as observed, modeled, screened/indexed, derived,
regulatory/administrative, community-reported, historical, or generated. KFM
must abstain or deny rather than:

- equate screening with measured exposure;
- equate facility listing with contamination or culpability;
- equate a permit with safety, danger, compliance, or noncompliance;
- infer health outcomes from proximity;
- rank communities with unsupported “healthy,” “unsafe,” “clean,” “polluted,”
  “desirable,” “vulnerable,” or similar labels;
- reconstruct household conditions from aggregate indicators;
- hide uncertainty, source disagreement, missingness, suppression, or staleness.

### River, infrastructure, and jurisdiction rules

- Geographic river context is not hydrologic measurement, flood status,
  navigability, recreation safety, or access guidance.
- Observations, modeled flood surfaces, regulatory boundaries, historic
  accounts, and water-quality records retain separate roles.
- No public composition may expose control systems, weak points, floor plans,
  sensors, maintenance gaps, hazardous-material movement, responder locations,
  outages, customer identity, or tactical dependencies.
- County, city, Kansas, Missouri, tribal, federal, private, and regional records
  retain explicit jurisdiction and correction paths; they are not
  interchangeable.
- Active flood, severe-weather, closure, transit, outage, fire, spill,
  evacuation, shelter, access, or emergency questions route to the responsible
  current authority.

Property data is not title truth. Environmental indicators are not medical
conclusions. Static planning documents are not current-safety guidance.

## 8. Finite outcomes and candidate reason codes

A future governed Focus interaction must normalize to exactly one outcome.

| Outcome | Minimum condition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Bounded scope; resolved evidence; fit source role and time; rights, sensitivity, privacy, policy, review, citation, and release state allow the exact projection | Reviewed public-safe county geography or historical context at authorized precision |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence is missing, stale, conflicted, outside scope, culturally unreviewed, or unable to support the requested inference | Unverified date, unsupported health inference, unclear jurisdiction, unresolved terminology |
| `DENY` | Request seeks prohibited precision, personal data, cultural/sacred detail, infrastructure vulnerability, or reconstruction of withheld information | Exact sensitive burial detail, household profile, living-person immigration status, tactical facility information |
| `ERROR` | Request/response is malformed or a required governed dependency fails | Invalid envelope, missing policy/evidence carrier, incompatible schema, resolver failure |

Candidate codes below are **PROPOSED documentation vocabulary only**.

| Candidate code | Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `WYANDOTTE_EVIDENCE_UNRESOLVED` | `ABSTAIN` | Adequate evidence does not resolve |
| `WYANDOTTE_SOURCE_OR_TIME_UNFIT` | `ABSTAIN` | Source role or represented time cannot support the inference |
| `WYANDOTTE_CULTURAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` | `ABSTAIN` | Cultural authority, terminology, or interpretation review is incomplete |
| `WYANDOTTE_SACRED_BURIAL_PRECISION_DENIED` | `DENY` | Request seeks sensitive or reconstructable burial, sacred, or archaeological detail |
| `WYANDOTTE_LIVING_PERSON_PRIVACY_DENIED` | `DENY` | Request seeks or would infer protected person or household information |
| `WYANDOTTE_PROPERTY_TITLE_UNSUPPORTED` | `ABSTAIN` | Administrative property data cannot support a legal or title conclusion |
| `WYANDOTTE_ENV_HEALTH_INFERENCE_UNSUPPORTED` | `ABSTAIN` | Evidence cannot support exposure, diagnosis, disease, causation, or culpability |
| `WYANDOTTE_INFRASTRUCTURE_DETAIL_DENIED` | `DENY` | Request seeks vulnerability, tactical, nonpublic, or live operational detail |
| `WYANDOTTE_JURISDICTION_OR_CURRENTNESS_UNRESOLVED` | `ABSTAIN` | Authority, jurisdiction, or current-safety state is unclear |
| `WYANDOTTE_GOVERNED_DEPENDENCY_ERROR` | `ERROR` | Required schema, evidence, policy, resolver, or runtime dependency failed |

These identifiers require contract, schema, policy, fixture, validator, and
reviewer work before operational use.

## 9. Proposed layers, interfaces, and anti-reconstruction

### Plan-defined layer candidates

| Candidate | Intended role | Required public boundary |
|---|---|---|

| Wyandot history | Indigenous and public-history framing | Accountable review, preferred terminology, evidence, limitations, correction |
| Burial-ground context | Explain sensitivity and public-history role | Generalized/restricted precision; no graves, remains, access, or reconstructed detail |
| Rail and industrial corridors | Economic, labor, and urban-form history | Generalized public context; no live condition, hazardous movement, or vulnerability |
| Environmental context | Screening, regulatory, modeled, observed, or derived context | Field-level role labels, uncertainty, privacy, no health or culpability inference |
| Immigration and labor context | Historical community and workforce development | Aggregate, historical, privacy-reviewed, no living-person status inference |
| City-county governance | Civic and administrative orientation | Official-current verification, jurisdiction, date, non-legal-advice boundary |
| Timeline and atlas claims | Time-aware, clickable explanations | Resolved evidence, policy/release support, limitations, correction |

### Plan-defined interface candidates

| Interface | Bounded role | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| Map canvas | Render authorized public-safe layers and selection state | Truth store, policy engine, unrestricted data browser, or emergency console |
| Layer registry | Show source role, time, evidence, policy, review, sensitivity, limits, and release state | UI-only list bypassing governed registry or release |
| Timeline | Filter authorized claims by represented time | Proof of completeness or causality |
| Evidence Drawer | Resolve claims to evidence, source roles, rights, review, limitations, correction, and supersession | Generated summary without evidence closure |
| Atlas Card | Carry bounded, correctable explanation | Sovereign historical, cultural, legal, environmental, or health truth |
| Governed AI panel | Return `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, or `ERROR` over authorized context | Browser-to-model path, direct model output, policy authority, or uncited answer |
| Shareable state | Reproduce public-safe map/time context | Token revealing restricted data or outliving correction/release state |

### Anti-reconstruction rule

A public release is reviewed as a composition, not only layer by layer. KFM
must prevent joins that recreate denied detail, including:

- burial orientation plus maps, parcels, imagery, terrain, addresses, or clues
  that reveal unapproved precision;
- environmental screening plus household, school, health, parcel, complaint, or
  demographic data that profiles people or implies diagnosis;
- facility context plus utilities, hazardous routes, response districts, access
  points, outages, or security data that reveals vulnerabilities;
- labor or immigration history plus current addresses, employers, family links,
  nationality, legal status, or identity resolution;
- flood layers plus occupants, property values, insurance, or access routes that
  create parcel-specific safety or financial conclusions.

An individually public layer can still create an unsafe composition.

## 14. Maintenance, correction, withdrawal, and rollback

Update this README when the sibling plan changes, an accepted decision changes
placement, the county index no longer describes the direct pair, or material
source, rights, terminology, cultural-review, privacy, policy, contract, schema,
validator, runtime, release, correction, rollback, or public evidence changes.

A correction should identify:

- the affected claim, layer, card, source, time, geometry, interface, and
  audience;
- the evidence or authority that changed;
- whether the issue is factual, cultural, terminological, rights, privacy,
  sensitivity, temporal, spatial, policy, release, or presentation related;
- whether immediate hold, redaction, generalization, withdrawal, or denial is
  required;
- every derived artifact, cache, search index, share link, generated answer, and
  public client carrying the issue;
- the superseding evidence, review, release, withdrawal, and rollback records.

Before merge, rollback is to close or abandon the feature branch and draft pull
request. After merge, use a transparent revert or bounded forward-fix pull
request against the actual merged commit. Do not rewrite shared history.

Reverting this README restores documentation bytes only. It does not revert an
external source, evidence bundle, policy decision, public map, runtime, release,
cache, deployment, or publication without separate governed rollback actions.
