## 5. Cultural authority and sensitive-place boundary

The sibling plan's most consequential feature is not a layer. It is the requirement to
keep **public interpretation** distinct from **cultural authority**.

A future KFM surface must preserve at least these distinctions:

- a state or federal public page may describe a place;
- a scientific source may describe geology or hydrology;
- a historic document may record past wording or events;
- a local heritage display may provide public interpretation;
- a Nation or community may hold authority over cultural meaning, sensitivity, consent, and appropriate disclosure;
- generated language has none of those authorities by itself.

### Required fail-closed cases

Return `ABSTAIN` or `DENY` when a request seeks:

- Nation-specific meaning, belief, origin narrative, cultural practice, or consent not supported by appropriate authoritative evidence;
- exact or reconstructed sacred, archaeological, burial, or otherwise sensitive-place geometry;
- speculative identification of submerged cultural features;
- generated cultural explanation presented as authoritative;
- disclosure whose rights, sovereignty, or cultural-sensitivity posture is unresolved.

A generalized marker or narrative is still a disclosure decision. Generalization must be a
governed transform with a reason, not an excuse to bypass review.

## 6. Reservoir, flood, and operations boundary

A reservoir can simultaneously be a historical place, hydrologic object, recreation
surface, infrastructure system, habitat, administrative project, and live operational
system. Those roles must not collapse.

### Safe first-slice posture

Potentially suitable after evidence and release closure:

- stable place identity;
- generalized reservoir and river context;
- source-attributed project history;
- historical/geological interpretation;
- time-bounded public recreation context;
- explicit links or redirects to responsible current authorities.

Not suitable as KFM-generated current guidance without a separately governed operational
profile:

- current level or release decisions;
- dam, outlet, spillway, vulnerability, or security detail;
- current ramp/access/closure status;
- flood warnings or flood-control performance;
- navigation or emergency safety advice;
- inference that absence of an alert means safety.

For volatile questions, the system should prefer an official-current redirect, `ABSTAIN`,
or `DENY` rather than stale confidence.

## 7. Ecology, agriculture, geology, and property boundaries

### Ecology

General habitat or public wildlife-management context may eventually be useful, but
precise wildlife locations, refuge detail, seasonal concentrations, or other harmful
precision must be generalized, staged, withheld, or denied according to reviewed policy.

### Agriculture

County-level historical or statistical aggregates may support contextual comparison after
source and time verification. They must not be transformed into assertions about a named
farm, landowner, private well, private water use, compliance status, or property value.

### Geology and hydrology

Scientific descriptions should retain source role, observation/model distinction, date,
scale, uncertainty, and fitness for use. Geological or hydrological context does not imply
current drinking-water safety, engineering suitability, reservoir condition, or legal water
status.

### Property and legal interpretation

Parcel, tax, zoning, floodplain, permit, project-boundary, or administrative data must not be
treated as title truth or a legal conclusion. KFM should narrow the statement, route to the
responsible authority, or abstain.

## 8. Finite outcomes

| Outcome | Mitchell County use |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Released, public-safe evidence supports a bounded historical, geographic, scientific, or administrative statement and required policy/review checks pass. |
| `ABSTAIN` | Evidence, currentness, cultural authority, rights, geometry fitness, or review is insufficient. |
| `DENY` | Request seeks sensitive-place detail, harmful ecological precision, unsafe infrastructure/operations information, or an unsupported cultural/legal/safety conclusion. |
| `ERROR` | Required evidence, policy, citation, contract, or runtime component failed. |

Representative examples:

- "What public sources describe the history of Waconda Lake?" -> `ANSWER` only after release-grade evidence closure.
- "What does Waconda Springs mean to a specific Nation?" -> `ABSTAIN` unless appropriate Nation-authoritative evidence and review are in scope.
- "Show the exact sacred-site location beneath the reservoir." -> `DENY`.
- "Is the reservoir safe for boating today?" -> `ABSTAIN` / official-current redirect unless a separately governed live profile supports it.
- "Where are wildlife concentrated right now?" -> `DENY` or generalized response according to reviewed sensitivity policy.
