## 4. Proposed county proof slice

The linked plan frames Saline County as a manageable central-Kansas hub where
transportation, river and floodplain context, agriculture, civic GIS, geology,
public history, and weather preparedness can be composed under one governed
interface.

| Proposed element | Intended public value | Required boundary | Current status |
|---|---|---|---:|
| County and municipal orientation | Place Salina and county communities in context. | Administrative geometry is not parcel title, surveyed boundary, service guarantee, or endorsement. | `PROPOSED` |
| Transportation hub | Explain the public role of major interstate and highway corridors. | Not live routing, closure, construction, freight-security, bridge, airport, rail, or vulnerability guidance. | `PROPOSED` |
| Rivers and floodplain | Show Smoky Hill / Saline River and flood-map context. | Not emergency, evacuation, insurance, engineering, or parcel-specific regulatory advice. | `PROPOSED` |
| Agriculture and land cover | Present county aggregates and classified land-cover context. | No individual farm/operator inference; classifications remain derived and year-specific. | `PROPOSED` |
| Civic GIS | Route users to public county/city map context. | Parcel and infrastructure layers retain source limits and cannot become legal or operational truth. | `PROPOSED` |
| Geology and water resources | Provide county-scale scientific and educational context. | No parcel-level engineering, groundwater-potability, mineral-right, or suitability conclusion. | `PROPOSED` |
| Historic and cultural context | Support reviewed public-history narratives. | Exact archaeology, burial, sacred, culturally sensitive, private-land, or newly inferred locations fail closed. | `PROPOSED` |
| Weather and hazards | Explain preparedness and source roles. | KFM is not a warning, forecast, dispatch, or emergency authority. | `PROPOSED` |
| Evidence Drawer and Focus Q&A | Make support, limitations, and refusals inspectable. | Every consequential response must use released evidence and finite outcomes. | `PROPOSED` |

### Composition rule

A future Saline County Focus Mode may connect these themes only through
governed, released identifiers and public-safe transforms. Cross-layer analysis
must not reconstruct withheld detail—for example, inferring a sensitive site
from history plus parcels, an infrastructure vulnerability from transport plus
utilities, or a private operator from agriculture plus addresses.

### First-slice non-goals

The first slice is not a parcel/title service, flood or weather alert system,
route-safety tool, construction-status tracker, infrastructure assessment,
groundwater-safety service, archaeological locator, rare-species finder,
individual-farm profile, or unrestricted AI county encyclopedia.

## 5. Source role and temporal fitness

The build plan lists candidate source families. They remain **source seeds**,
not admitted KFM sources, until current identity, terms, rights, coverage,
cadence, geometry authority, sensitivity, and permitted-use posture are
verified.

| Candidate role | May support after governed admission | Must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| County and city GIS | Public administrative and civic-map orientation. | Title, surveyed boundary, unrestricted reuse, emergency status, or universal local truth. |
| Parcel/appraiser systems | Limited public-service and valuation context with explicit caveats. | Ownership, title, easement, access, legal boundary, or personal dossier. |
| KDOT and public transport sources | Corridor and project context at stated date and scale. | Live closure, route safety, engineering approval, freight-security, or infrastructure-vulnerability claim. |
| KDA/FEMA floodplain sources | Draft/effective flood-map context with source-native status. | Insurance, evacuation, engineering, or parcel-specific legal determination. |
| USGS/NOAA/NWS observations | Time-bounded river, weather, and preparedness context. | Warning replacement, safe-use verdict, forecast reinterpretation, or timeless condition. |
| KDA/USDA agriculture | Aggregate county statistics and reviewed derivatives. | Individual farm/operator truth, parcel crop certainty, or economic forecast. |
| Land-cover products | Classified, method- and year-specific context. | Ground observation or current parcel truth. |
| KGS | Geology and water-resource context at published scale. | Engineering, potability, drilling, ownership, or site-suitability determination. |
| Historical institutions and public collections | Reviewed documentary and public-history context. | Exact restricted archaeology, burial precision, sacred-site authority, or unrestricted media reuse. |
| AI-generated language | Bounded explanation over released evidence. | Source authority, evidence, policy decision, review, or release permission. |

### Time fields that must stay distinct

A future public claim may need separate:

- observation or event time;
- source publication/update time;
- retrieval time;
- validity/effective period;
- model or classification vintage;
- KFM processing time;
- KFM release time; and
- correction, supersession, or withdrawal time.

A source page that appears current today does not make every field current, and
a historical record does not establish present conditions.

<a id="6-floodplain-property-infrastructure-heritage-and-current-safety-boundaries"></a>

## 6. Floodplain, property, infrastructure, heritage, and current-safety boundaries

### Floodplain, rivers, and weather

KFM may eventually show released, dated reference context. It must not issue
warnings, determine evacuation, insurance, engineering, or regulatory outcomes,
or reinterpret a stale observation as current. Missing current authority yields
`ABSTAIN`; a required trust-service failure yields `ERROR`.

### Parcel and property

Parcel or assessor geometry does not establish ownership, title, easement,
lawful access, surveyed boundary, valuation correctness, or private rights. The
public product should avoid names and addresses unless a separately governed
claim genuinely requires them and qualified review permits exposure.

### Transportation and infrastructure

Public corridor and project information can support general orientation.
Detailed bridge, airport, rail, utility, response, dependency, control, or
vulnerability analysis is not needed for the first public slice and remains
`DENY` or generalized.

### Geology and groundwater

Published geologic context can be educational. It cannot support parcel-level
foundation, contamination, potability, well-yield, mineral-right, excavation,
or engineering conclusions without claim-appropriate evidence and review.

### History, archaeology, and cultural resources

Public museum, library, and historic-inventory material can support reviewed
context. Exact archaeological, burial, sacred, culturally sensitive,
private-land, or newly inferred locations require qualified authority,
sensitivity review, rights review, and usually suppression or generalization.

### Agriculture and ecology

County aggregates and generalized classifications must not become individual
farm, producer, financial, owner, crop, water-use, or management conclusions.
Rare species and sensitive habitat precision fail closed.

<a id="7-finite-outcomes"></a>

## 7. Finite outcomes

Future Saline County runtime behavior should converge on four terminal outcomes:

| Outcome | Saline County example |
|---|---|
| `ANSWER` | Released evidence supports a bounded statement about county-scale transportation, agriculture, geology, floodplain reference context, or public history, and policy permits exposure. |
| `ABSTAIN` | A user asks for current flood, weather, road, construction, property, insurance, engineering, groundwater, or safety status without released fit-for-purpose evidence. |
| `DENY` | A request seeks exact sensitive cultural/ecological locations, private-person/property detail, infrastructure vulnerability, unreleased material, or a prohibited internal path. |
| `ERROR` | Evidence resolution, citation validation, policy evaluation, contract validation, or another required trust component fails. |

`ANSWER` is never the fallback. Uncertainty must terminate visibly rather than
being hidden behind fluent prose or a map.

- the linked build plan or county-lane placement;
- accepted Directory Rules, ADRs, or Focus Mode conventions;
- source-admission, evidence, policy, reason-code, runtime, or release contracts;

### Correction posture

dated source observations as current facts. A later external-source refresh
should record source identity, retrieval time, changed facts, rights/sensitivity
review, and the claims affected.

branch. After merge, use a transparent revert or forward-fix pull request
against the actual merged commit; do not rewrite shared history.

also require correction, withdrawal, supersession, cache invalidation, and
rollback lineage appropriate to its public reliance.
