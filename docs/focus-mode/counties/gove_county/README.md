> **Purpose.** Orient maintainers to the Gove County planning lane, make its
> legacy-source, groundwater, well/privacy, scientific-locality, collecting,
> water-right, agriculture, flood/property, and currentness boundaries visible,
> and route future work through KFM evidence, policy, qualified review,
> correction, rollback, and release controls.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tracked planning artifacts are not implementation or release evidence.**
> This directory contains this README and one Gove County build plan. Their
> presence establishes planning lineage only; it does not admit a source,
> resolve evidence, activate policy, validate a payload, deploy a product, or
> publish county knowledge.

> [!CAUTION]
> **The existing path remains a compatibility lane.** Accepted ADR-0029 adopts
> Directory Rules v2, while ADR-0027 still proposes a plural, kebab-case county
> control plane. This update modifies the tracked README in place and keeps any
> move, rename, alias retirement, or parallel-tree creation on **HOLD**.

> The build plan describes an explicitly unupdated 1960 geology and groundwater
> publication that contains well and fossil-rich formation context. KFM must not
> turn that planning evidence into present groundwater, well-location,
> water-right, fossil-target, collecting-access, property, flood, insurance, or

<a id="4-proposed-county-proof-slice"></a>

## 4. Proposed county proof slice

The build plan proposes a trust proof in which useful public scientific context
remains bounded by source fitness, minimum necessary display, and finite denial:

1. **Legacy geohydrology** — broad, dated explanation must remain historical
   and must never become a current groundwater or well conclusion.
2. **Chalk and paleontology** — generalized scientific context may be useful,
   while fossil localities, collecting targets, and access routes stay withheld.
3. **Private-well restraint** — well/test-hole material is not a public point
   layer and may not be joined to parcels, people, or water-right conclusions.
4. **Working-landscape context** — dated county aggregates remain statistical
   context, not attribution to farms, wells, irrigators, or responsibility.
5. **Flood/property restraint** — official routing may be offered without a
   parcel, insurance, permit, evacuation, or safety determination.
6. **Honest abstention** — named natural-landmark presentation remains deferred
   when authority, rights, access, geometry, or sensitivity evidence is missing.

| Proposed question | Bounded planning answer | Required hold |
|---|---|---|
| What can KFM explain about Gove geology? | Generalized, evidence-visible formation and valley context with original-source dates. | No detailed map authority, access, collecting, engineering, or property conclusion. |
| What can a legacy groundwater report support? | Historical scientific context and a source-fitness explanation. | No current aquifer, well, water-quality, irrigation, or water-right answer. |
| Why are well records not mapped? | Minimum necessary display does not require targetable well or test-hole detail. | No coordinates, property joins, owner links, or present-use inference. |
| Why is fossil detail generalized? | Broad paleontological significance may be educational. | No precise localities, collection targets, access routes, or removal guidance. |
| What can agriculture totals establish? | A dated county-scale statistical context after source admission. | No identification of farms, wells, irrigators, environmental impact, or compliance. |
| What can a flood card do? | Route a user to a verified responsible authority. | No parcel, insurance, permit, evacuation, or safety outcome. |
| Can a named landmark be displayed? | Only after authority, rights, access, geometry, and sensitivity review. | `ABSTAIN` or defer while those gates remain open. |

This proof slice is **PROPOSED**. It is not evidence that the named cards,
layers, panels, payloads, routes, fixtures, policies, redirects, release objects,
or correction paths exist.

## 5. Source-role and legacy-currentness boundary

The linked plan records external checks dated 2026-05-22. Those pages were not
rechecked for this README. Each source family must be verified and admitted for
its actual role before use.

| Source family named by the plan | Candidate role | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| Kansas Geological Survey county-map material | Scientific/cartographic source-fitness context. | Detailed local mapping, access, collecting, engineering, property, or current groundwater authority. |
| KGS legacy county bulletin | Historical geology and groundwater interpretation with explicit age/fitness limits. | Present aquifer, well, water-quality, irrigation, legal, or safety truth. |
| KGS formation and fossil context | Broad geologic and paleontological explanation. | Fossil-locality target, collecting guide, access permission, or specimen-source map. |
| Kansas agricultural statistics | Dated county aggregate context. | Farm, irrigator, well, landowner, impact, violation, or compliance attribution. |
| Kansas floodplain routing surface | Responsible-authority routing and checked-date context. | Parcel, insurance, permit, evacuation, or property-safety determination. |
| Candidate natural-landmark authority | Potential named-feature evidence after admission. | A plausible landmark claim filled from familiarity, generated language, or insufficient retrieval. |
| Generated language | Bounded explanation over resolved, released evidence. | Evidence, currentness, permission, source authority, policy approval, or publication authority. |

### Minimum source-admission questions

Before any source supports a public claim, verify:

- stable source identity, publisher, issuing authority, and exact source role;
- original publication, web update, retrieval, observation, effective, and
  expiry dates as applicable;
- legacy, current, draft, final, superseded, withdrawn, or corrected state;
- fitness for the exact geology, groundwater-history, agriculture, flood-route,
  or landmark claim;
- rights, attribution, redistribution, caching, transformation, imagery, map,
  GIS, and derivative-display terms;
- well/privacy, scientific-locality, collecting/access, water-governance,
  property, flood, and harmful-precision implications;
- minimum necessary geometry and whether detail must be generalized, withheld,
  quarantined, or denied;
- correction, supersession, withdrawal, and source-currentness behavior;
- whether every consequential claim resolves from `EvidenceRef` to an eligible
  `EvidenceBundle` before display.

### Anti-collapse rule

Source roles must remain separate. Legacy science does not become current water
authority; public records do not become a targeting layer; geology does not
grant access or collecting rights; aggregates do not identify private actors;
an official route does not decide a parcel question; and generated language
does not cure missing evidence.

## 6. Wells, scientific localities, and property

### Minimum necessary display

Public availability does not establish a public need to reproduce every source
field. The proposed first slice should carry only the minimum detail necessary
for broad education and safe routing.

| Detail class | Public posture | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| County-scale generalized geology | Candidate after evidence, rights, geometry, policy, and review closure. | Broad educational context may be useful. |
| Historical groundwater interpretation | Candidate only with conspicuous legacy/currentness limits. | Historical context must not support operative use. |
| Well or test-hole coordinates | `DENY` / withhold by default. | Targetability, property association, and present-use misuse. |
| Parcel, owner, address, or water-right join | `DENY`. | Privacy, title, legal, and reidentification risk. |
| Broad paleontology context | Candidate after scientific-sensitivity review. | General learning may not require location disclosure. |
| Fossil locality, specimen-source, or collecting target | `DENY` / withhold. | Scientific-resource, preservation, access, and property risk. |
| Access, entry, excavation, collection, or removal permission | `DENY` / route to responsible authority. | A map or science source cannot grant permission. |
| County agricultural aggregate | Candidate after admission and anti-attribution review. | Aggregate context must not be reversed into private facts. |
| Parcel-specific flood or insurance result | `DENY` / official process only. | High-stakes property reliance. |

### Non-leaking denial

A denial must not reveal the protected well, locality, parcel, person, route, or
attribute it is refusing. Public reason codes should identify the rule category,
not confirm that a sensitive target exists.

## 7. Time, currentness, and official authority

The build plan combines source materials with different temporal roles. Future
implementation must keep them explicit rather than collapsing them into one
“current” county story.

| Time field | Meaning | Gove requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Original publication time | When the scientific or statistical work was issued. | Visible on every dependent claim. |
| Web page update time | When a host page changed. | Never silently treated as the scientific observation date. |
| Observation or data period | When an underlying measurement or aggregate applies. | Required for groundwater, agriculture, flood, or map claims. |
| Retrieval/check time | When KFM or a reviewer inspected a source. | Recorded, but not proof of continuing validity. |
| Effective/operative time | When a regulatory, flood, or public-safety source governs. | Requires competent current authority. |
| Expiry/stale time | When a source may no longer support its intended use. | Must cause `ABSTAIN`, withdrawal, or re-review. |
| Correction/supersession time | When a later source changes prior meaning. | Must preserve lineage without making the legacy source current. |

The build plan's 2026-05-22 checks are planning evidence only. They do not prove
that the external pages, terms, numbers, current effective status, or authority
remain unchanged on 2026-08-21.

For operative groundwater, water-right, access, collecting, flood, property,
insurance, evacuation, or safety questions, KFM must use a verified responsible
current authority or return a bounded non-answer. It must not interpolate a
current conclusion from historic text or a cached route.

## 12. Smallest safe follow-up

The next coherent slice is **not** a live connector, well map, fossil-locality
layer, property query, current groundwater card, public route, or publication.
It is a reviewable source-and-boundary packet.

1. Re-pin current `main`, recheck Gove path and PR overlap, and reconcile the
   shared county index in a separately scoped inventory change.
2. Reverify the plan's named official sources without copying source material
   into public or canonical lanes.
3. Draft source descriptors that keep legacy science, current water authority,
   agriculture aggregates, flood routing, landmark authority, and generated
   language in distinct roles.
4. Record rights, time, well/privacy, scientific-locality, access/property,
   water-governance, flood, and spatial-precision questions as admission gates.
5. Reconcile candidate outcomes and reason codes with the current semantic
   contract, machine schema family, and inactive policy lane.
6. Add synthetic valid and invalid fixtures for legacy-as-current, public well
   points, parcel/person joins, water-right decisions, fossil targets,
   collecting access, aggregate attribution, parcel flood results, unresolved
   evidence, and internal-store exposure.
7. Run repository-native validators in an isolated no-network environment;
   preserve failures rather than weakening gates.
8. Stop at a reviewed candidate. Source activation, runtime exposure, release,
   deployment, and publication remain separate decisions.

### Follow-up acceptance boundary

The follow-up is complete only when synthetic evidence demonstrates that:

- broad historic/scientific context is evidence-bound, dated, and limited;
- source roles and observation times cannot collapse;
- well, property/person, water-right, locality, collection, flood, and private
  attribution requests fail closed without leaking protected detail;
- every outcome is finite and deterministic;
- no public client can reach internal lifecycle or candidate source stores;
- correction and rollback implications are recorded;
- no source is activated and no public artifact is released.

## 15. Maintenance, correction, and rollback

### Documentation maintenance

- Preserve the `doc_id`, current path, explicit anchors, and planning-only
  authority unless an accepted migration says otherwise.
- Update evidence-snapshot hashes when governing repository surfaces are
  materially re-inspected.
- Keep confirmed repository evidence separate from the plan's proposed content
  and dated external observations.
- Correct stale source, currentness, rights, well/privacy, locality, access,
  water-governance, property, and flood statements visibly.
- Never set released, published, approved, or public-safe merely because
  documentation, tests, commits, or pull requests exist.

### Before merge

Close or abandon the unmerged draft PR and feature branch. Remote branch
deletion is a separate administrative action and is not required for rollback.

### After merge

Use a transparent revert or forward-fix PR against the actual merged commit.
Do not rewrite shared history. Revert or correct this README and its generated
receipt together. Preserve correction and supersession links rather than
creating two writable authorities.

### Public correction boundary

This documentation-only change creates no source activation, release,
deployment, or publication to undo. If a future public artifact relies on Gove
content, a Git revert alone may be insufficient: the governed correction path
may also require source-state update, release supersession, withdrawal, cache
invalidation, API/UI correction, and retained lineage.
