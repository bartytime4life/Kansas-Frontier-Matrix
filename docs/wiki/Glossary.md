# Glossary

KFM uses precise vocabulary because similar-looking artifacts can carry very different authority. This glossary is an orientation aid; the current doctrine, contracts, schemas, and accepted decisions control exact meaning.

### ABSTAIN

Finite outcome used when evidence is missing, stale, conflicting, unsafe to narrow, or outside scope.

### Canonical

The currently governing or source-of-record surface for a specific responsibility; canonical does not mean infallible or public.

### CATALOG

Governed discovery/index stage or lane. A catalog record does not by itself prove or release its subject.

### Cite-or-abstain

A consequential claim must resolve admissible evidence; otherwise the system does not assert it.

### CorrectionNotice

Record explaining a correction, affected identity, time, prior state, and forward lineage.

### DENY

Finite outcome where policy, rights, sensitivity, role, release state, or exposure risk blocks an operation or response.

### Derived artifact

A tile, index, model, graph projection, summary, scene, or other rebuildable carrier produced from upstream truth.

### EvidenceBundle

Resolved evidence package describing sources, roles, scope, provenance, limitations, and citation support.

### Evidence Drawer

UI surface that presents evidence-backed detail and trust state for a selected claim or feature.

### EvidenceRef

Stable pointer from a claim or artifact to evidence that should resolve through governed services.

### Focus Mode

Bounded, map-context-aware interpretive interface that returns finite evidence-subordinate outcomes.

### Governed API

Executable trust membrane used by ordinary public and semi-public clients.

### HOLD

Review or transition outcome indicating that a prerequisite remains unresolved; not a permissive default.

### Inspectable claim

Claim whose evidence, source role, spatial/time scope, policy, review, release, correction, and rollback can be examined.

### Lifecycle

Governed movement from RAW through WORK/QUARANTINE, PROCESSED, CATALOG/TRIPLETS, and PUBLISHED.

### MapLibre

The primary web map renderer and interaction runtime; a downstream carrier rather than truth or policy authority.

### PolicyDecision

Reviewed or machine-evaluated allow, deny, restrict, hold, redact, or obligation result.

### Promotion

Governed state transition backed by evidence, validation, policy, review, proof, release, correction, and rollback—not a file move.

### Proof

Verifiable support for a declared condition. A proof remains distinct from a receipt, catalog, or release.

### PUBLISHED

Governed lifecycle state for reviewed, released public or semi-public artifacts; placement alone is insufficient.

### QUARANTINE

Fail-closed lane for unresolved, unsafe, malformed, conflicting, or restricted material.

### Receipt

Process memory describing what ran, inputs, tools, outputs, and outcomes; not factual or release proof by itself.

### ReleaseManifest

Release-governance record binding a released set, versions, digests, dependencies, and rollback/correction context.

### SourceDescriptor

Governed source identity, role, steward, rights, access, cadence, scope, citation, and sensitivity record.

### TRIPLETS

Derived relationship/graph projection stage or lane; edges do not become sovereign truth without evidence.

### Trust membrane

Boundary preventing ordinary clients from reaching canonical/internal or unreleased state directly.

### Watcher

Automation that detects source or repository change and emits candidates/receipts; never a publisher.
