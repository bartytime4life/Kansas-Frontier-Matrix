Why KFM

Most maps answer where. KFM is being built to answer where, when, what supports the claim, what changed, and what can responsibly be shown.

  • Connected systems

    Move from a place to its water, ground, sky, history and infrastructure — on one map, on one clock.

  • Every layer keeps its clock

    Observation, publication, retrieval and release times stay separate, so a reading never masquerades as a map edition.

  • Click anything, see its receipts

    The Evidence Drawer shows source, time, role, uncertainty and limits for what you selected.

  • Gaps stay visible

    Missing, stale or unsupported data becomes an honest gap, hold or abstain — never a confident guess or an implied all-clear.

  • Sensitive places stay protected

    Archaeology, burials, rare species, living people and critical infrastructure are generalized, withheld or denied by default.

Where the data comes from

KFM draws on public data from federal, state, university and community sources. It reaches KFM in two ways:

  • Live context in the Explorer. A fixed allowlist of official providers rendered as attributed external context: visible and inspectable, never silently promoted to evidence.
  • Connector lanes in the repository. Source-specific capture, parsing and admission code. A lane existing does not mean the source is active, admitted or published.

Browse every theme and lane in the project README or the connectors directory. Every source keeps its own terms, attribution and access limits.

How KFM protects meaning

Public clients never reach into internal stores. Every source moves through the same lifecycle — RAW → WORK / QUARANTINE → PROCESSED → CATALOG / TRIPLETS → PUBLISHED — and promotion is a governed decision, not a file move.

When KFM is asked something it answers with a finite outcome: ANSWER when evidence supports it, ABSTAIN when support is missing or stale, DENY when policy forbids it, and ERROR when something failed.
