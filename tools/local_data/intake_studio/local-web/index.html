A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

Careful next steps.

Upload photos or PDFs to this development PC. Originals and review records are saved on disk and remain available after restarting the local server.

What analysis means here

Local tools extract document text and, where available, read image text with OCR. Keyword matches suggest possible categories. This is not a cloud AI service, factual verification, or a guarantee that extraction is complete. Review the original.

What a destination means

A domain and a surface describe where this evidence could belong. They are provisional proposals, not verified KFM integrations. Describe your photograph when text extraction cannot explain it. Locations are entered by you; this studio does not infer exact locations or identify people.

What happens after review

Save a complete review as “Ready for integration review”, then choose “Send to KFM”. The original, extracted text and saved review are captured privately and indexed in Working Data. You can open Working Data from the success message. Sending retains public-use holds; it does not publish a website page or map layer. Export review packages remains available for separate review. Editing a sent review creates a new revision that must be reviewed before sending again.

Use “Take a photo”, then “Start camera” to request camera permission. No microphone is requested. Capture one image or choose a photo file instead. Closing the camera stops it. No files leave this computer through this studio.
