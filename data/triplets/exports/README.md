# `data/triplets/exports/` — Internal Relationship Export Boundary

This lane is reserved for rebuildable relationship-export candidates derived at
the CATALOG/TRIPLET stage. It is **not** a public download directory, release
record, publication decision, or source of sovereign truth.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> “Export” describes a derivative representation. It does not mean “released” or
> “published.” Public-safe carriers belong under [`data/published/`](../../published/README.md)
> only after a separate governed release decision.

## Authority and lifecycle boundary

[`data/triplets/`](../README.md) owns optional, rebuildable relationship and
graph projections. This child lane may hold candidate serializations of those
projections when an implemented writer and validation contract are established.

It does not own:

- canonical domain records or source payloads;
- contracts, schemas, policy decisions, receipts, proofs, or release manifests;
- public-serving routes or delivery authorization;
- factual truth merely because a graph edge or export file exists.

The governing lifecycle remains:

```text
RAW -> WORK / QUARANTINE -> PROCESSED -> CATALOG / TRIPLET -> PUBLISHED
```

Moving or copying bytes does not perform the transition to `PUBLISHED`.

## Preconditions for an export payload

Do not treat this lane as an operational destination until the proposed payload
has an evidence-backed contract covering the applicable items below.

| Required boundary | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|
| Identity | Stable export identity, version, media type, and content digest |
| Derivation | Resolvable input identities, transformation version, and run receipt |
| Semantics | Contract/schema binding and defined relationship vocabulary |
| Evidence | Evidence or source references supporting each consequential relation |
| Scope | Spatial and temporal extent, CRS where applicable, and known limitations |
| Rights and sensitivity | Rights, attribution, privacy, sovereignty, harmful-precision, and access disposition |
| Validation | Deterministic positive and negative checks with declared coverage |
| Correction | Supersession, withdrawal, and stale-state handling |
| Release | Separate review, proof, manifest, public-safe transform, and rollback target |

Missing or conflicted evidence must narrow the export, route it to a governed
hold, or prevent creation. It must not be replaced by generated prose or a
plausible default.

## Validation and review

No lane-specific executable validator was verified. Until one exists, a review
of a proposed payload should at minimum confirm:

1. the payload is derived and reproducible rather than a parallel canonical
   record;
2. identifiers and digests agree with the referenced inputs and receipts;
3. unsupported relations are absent or explicitly withheld;
4. sensitive joins and precise locations fail closed;
5. no public client or deployment reads this internal lane directly;
6. release, correction, withdrawal, and rollback references are distinct and
   resolvable.

A Markdown, schema, fixture, test, workflow, pull request, merge, or passing
check is bounded evidence only. None independently establishes review approval,
release, deployment, promotion, or publication.

## Maintenance guidance

- Preserve the three placeholder directories until a reviewed topology change
  explicitly adds, moves, or retires them.
- Add a domain payload only with its direct contract, validation, provenance,
  sensitivity, and rollback dependencies.
- Update this inventory when the committed tree, writer, consumer, or authority
  boundary changes.
- Keep public-safe released carriers and their delivery sidecars in
  [`data/published/`](../../published/README.md), not here.
