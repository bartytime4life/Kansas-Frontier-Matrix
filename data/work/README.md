## Purpose

Own mutable, reviewable, domain-scoped intermediate products and candidate assertions after RAW capture or governed quarantine exit and before PROCESSED promotion.

Artifacts here may carry or support inspectable claims, but directory placement cannot make a claim true, rights-cleared, policy-admitted, reviewed, released, or public.

## Authority level

**Canonical; WORK responsibility.**

This path does not own object meaning, machine shape, policy, release decisions, public serving, or factual truth. Those remain under `contracts/`, `schemas/`, `policy/`, `release/`, and governed delivery interfaces.

## What belongs here

- normalization, extraction, alignment, crosswalk, conflation, deduplication, unit conversion, and geometry-repair outputs;
- candidate assertions, candidate joins, QA packets, model/classification candidates, and redaction/generalization trials;
- run-local indexes, manifests, digests, disposition notes, and references to source, receipt, policy, evidence, and review state;

Local README, inventory, digest, migration, and disposition sidecars may explain the boundary without creating parallel authority.

## What does NOT belong here

| Do not place here | Correct home or action |
|---|---|
| immutable source captures | `data/raw/` |
| unsafe or unresolved held material | `data/quarantine/` |
| validated normalized products ready for downstream projection | `data/processed/` |
| proofs, receipts, registries, release records, and public artifacts | their dedicated authority lanes |
| Credentials, private keys, private endpoints, or unsafe logs | Approved secret and restricted operational systems |
| Maps, reports, graphs, indexes, or AI output presented as sovereign truth | Resolve governed evidence/release state or abstain |

## Inputs

RAW captures, governed quarantine exits, accepted pipeline specifications, and bounded manual stewardship with inspectable lineage.

As applicable, inputs must resolve stable identity, source role, space/time scope, rights, sensitivity, contract/schema, code/spec/run, validation/receipt, evidence/proof, policy/review, release, correction, and rollback references.

## Outputs

Candidates that either return to QUARANTINE, advance to PROCESSED after validation, or are denied/abstained/superseded with recorded disposition.

Public clients must not use an internal or compatibility lane as a direct data service.

## Validation

Validate placement; identity/digest/version; source role and lineage; rights/sensitivity; spatial/temporal support; contract/schema; execution and receipts; evidence/citations/proofs; policy/review; catalog/triplet parity; release/correction/rollback dependencies; links/anchors/metadata; and sensitive-content exposure.

No complete lane-wide validator was verified. A pass proves only the check’s declared scope.

## Review burden

Accountable ownership remains **NEEDS VERIFICATION**. Changes should include data, pipeline, and domain stewards as applicable. Payload, source activation, rights/sensitivity, migration, public-serving, correction, or rollback changes require corresponding specialist and independent review. CODEOWNERS routing is not approval evidence.
