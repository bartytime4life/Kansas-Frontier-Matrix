## Purpose

`data/proofs/fauna/` is the Fauna domain proof-support lane. It may hold or index claim-scoped proof artifacts under an accepted profile so animal-related claims can be reconstructed to evidence, source role, geography, time, rights, sensitivity, safe representation, validation, review, release dependency, correction lineage, and rollback support.

The lane exists to make these questions inspectable before a Fauna claim advances:

1. What exact claim scope is supported?
2. Which governed `EvidenceRef` values and reconstructable source records support it?
3. Which taxon concept, object family, source role, geography, time, method, uncertainty, and limitations qualify the support?
4. Which citations, rights, sensitivity labels, transforms, checksums, and governing specification identity close the evidence packet?
5. Which exact/internal representation and public generalized, withheld, staged, aggregated, or denied representation apply?
6. Which policy, stewardship, review, release, correction, withdrawal, invalidation, and rollback dependencies remain outside the proof artifact?
7. Which finite downstream outcome is justified?

This lane supports evidence resolution. It does not admit sources, determine animal truth, define taxonomy, execute geoprivacy, decide policy, approve stewardship, authorize release, publish maps, provide operational wildlife guidance, or create public claims.

## Authority level

**Implementation-bearing domain proof-support lane under the canonical `data/proofs/` responsibility.**

Authority remains split by responsibility:

- [`data/proofs/fauna/`](./README.md) owns the Fauna-domain proof-support index and any accepted domain proof packet.
- [`data/proofs/evidence_bundle/`](../evidence_bundle/README.md) owns the EvidenceBundle proof-family index.
- [`data/proofs/citation_validation/`](../citation_validation/README.md) owns citation-resolution proof support.

- [`schemas/contracts/v1/evidence/evidence_bundle.schema.json`](../../../schemas/contracts/v1/evidence/evidence_bundle.schema.json) defines its current machine shape.
- `policy/`, review records, and `release/` retain admissibility, stewardship, release, correction, withdrawal, and rollback authority.
- Governed APIs and already released public-safe artifacts remain the normal public path.

The expected Fauna child READMEs under `data/proofs/evidence_bundle/fauna/` and `data/proofs/citation_validation/fauna/` are **not established at the inspected base commit**. This README does not create those paths, claim that they exist, or use their absence as permission to duplicate their future responsibilities here.

When one artifact could fit more than one proof axis, an accepted contract and profile must select one authoritative home. Other lanes may hold immutable references, indexes, or validation summaries; they must not duplicate mutable proof authority, restricted source material, receipts, policy decisions, release records, or public state.

## What belongs here

After a Fauna proof profile, producer, validator path, policy posture, access controls, and review burden are accepted, this lane may hold or index:

- claim-scoped EvidenceBundle records, immutable references, or indexes for Fauna claims;
- `EvidenceRef` resolution maps and claim-to-evidence manifests;
- digest-closure manifests tying admitted sources, processed artifacts, catalog rows, triplets, receipts, candidate records, correction records, rollback targets, and proof artifacts to evidence;
- proof indexes for taxon identity, taxon crosswalk, conservation status, occurrence, range, seasonal range, migration, monitoring, sensitive site, mortality, disease, invasive species, and public-safe derivative claims;
- source-role closure summaries that preserve observed, regulatory, modeled, aggregate, administrative, candidate, and synthetic distinctions;
- geoprivacy and sensitivity proof summaries that reference governed transforms and review records without exposing restricted originals or control parameters;
- public-safe representation support showing that a released derivative is generalized, aggregated, suppressed, staged, withheld, or denied as policy requires;
- correction, supersession, withdrawal, and invalidation pointers that identify affected proof identities and downstream release dependencies;
- lane-local README, index, inventory, digest, migration, or disposition sidecars that explain the boundary without becoming a second authority surface.

No non-README proof payload should be added while the current `build-proof-fauna` workflow hold remains in place.

## Inputs

Inputs are references to governed records, not permission to copy their protected contents into this lane.

A candidate proof packet should receive, as applicable:

- stable claim and artifact identity;
- admitted `SourceDescriptor` and source-role references;
- processed record and catalog/triplet identities;
- `EvidenceRef` members and reconstructable source-record handles;
- citations and stated limitations;
- spatial, temporal, taxonomic, method, units, uncertainty, and confidence context;
- rights and sensitivity posture;
- public-safe transform references and checksums;
- validation, receipt, policy, review, candidate, release, correction, withdrawal, supersession, and rollback references.

Missing or incompatible inputs narrow the claim or yield a finite negative outcome. They do not invite plausible completion.

## Outputs

This lane may support:

- evidence and citation review;
- release-candidate preflight;
- Evidence Drawer and governed API resolution;
- public-safe map, export, and Focus Mode readiness checks;
- correction, withdrawal, supersession, and rollback impact analysis;
- auditable `ANSWER`, `ABSTAIN`, `HOLD`, `RESTRICT`, `DENY`, or `ERROR` decisions.

A proof artifact is not a public response body, public route, map layer, tile source, release manifest, policy decision, or publication record. Public clients must not read this directory directly.

## Validation

Validation must be profile-specific and fail closed. At minimum, a future accepted Fauna proof validator should check:

1. stable proof identity and declared claim scope;
2. global EvidenceBundle schema conformance where that shape is used;
3. cross-record `EvidenceRef` resolution and source-record reconstructability;
4. taxon concept, object family, source role, geography, time, method, units, uncertainty, and limitation compatibility;
5. rights, attribution, redistribution, sensitivity, stewardship, and geoprivacy dependencies;
6. transform references and digest/spec-hash closure without exposing control parameters;
7. receipt, policy, review, candidate, release, correction, withdrawal, invalidation, and rollback linkage;
8. absence of exact or reverse-engineerable restricted details;
9. source-role anti-collapse and public-derivative lineage;
10. finite negative outcomes for missing, stale, conflicting, restricted, superseded, withdrawn, invalidated, or unreleased support.

The current global wrapper at [`tools/validators/validate_evidence_bundle.py`](../../../tools/validators/validate_evidence_bundle.py) validates the shared JSON Schema fixture family. It was **not run in this Markdown task**, and schema validation alone does not prove Fauna taxonomic correctness, source admission, rights clearance, sensitivity safety, geoprivacy execution, review, release, or public use.

The current Fauna workflow performs two additional boundary checks:

- it requires the literal compatibility line `**Status:** draft / PROPOSED`; and
- it holds proof production if any non-README proof artifact, accepted proof target, or potential producer appears before governed wiring is established.

Changing that compatibility token or adding proof payloads requires a coordinated workflow, contract, validator, fixture, policy, access-control, review, and rollback change—not a README-only edit.

## Fauna proof packet

The fielded shared EvidenceBundle schema currently requires these top-level fields:

| Required field | Shared meaning | Fauna closure expectation |
|---|---|---|
| `bundle_id` | Stable bundle identity | Deterministic, scoped identity; no sensitive location encoded in the identifier |
| `claim_scope` | Exact claims the bundle may support | Names the Fauna object family, taxon concept, space/time scope, method, and limitations without leaking protected precision |
| `evidence_refs` | Governed evidence pointers | Resolve to compatible evidence; a pointer alone is not closure |
| `source_records` | Reconstructable source handles | Preserve original authority and source role |
| `citations` | Publication-ready citations | Support the exact claim scope and remain rights/sensitivity compatible |
| `rights` | Effective rights summary | Includes `license`; does not imply release permission |
| `sensitivity` | Exposure constraints | Must reflect sensitive-taxon, sensitive-site, stewardship, private-land, and re-identification posture |
| `transforms` | Ordered derivation references | Preserve redaction/generalization/aggregation reviewability without exposing control parameters |
| `checksums` | Critical input/output hashes | Detect drift or tampering across evidence and derived artifacts |
| `spec_hash` | Governing contract/schema identity | Pins the bundle to the evaluated profile |

The current schema forbids undeclared top-level fields. Fauna-specific qualifiers therefore must be represented through accepted referenced records and profile semantics—not invented ad hoc as extra fields that bypass schema/version review.

A complete Fauna proof packet also needs separate references to policy, review, candidate/release, correction, withdrawal, invalidation, and rollback records where applicable. EvidenceBundle does not absorb those authorities.

## Fauna claim and source-role closure

| Claim or object family | What proof may support | What it must not silently become |
|---|---|---|
| `Taxon` / `TaxonCrosswalk` | Taxon concept, authority, version, synonym or crosswalk relationship | A permanent identity detached from source authority and time |
| `ConservationStatus` | Named status under a stated authority, geography, and effective period | Observed occurrence or current legal advice |
| `OccurrenceEvidence` | Evidence that an observation or source record supports a bounded occurrence claim | Public-safe geometry or released occurrence by default |
| `OccurrenceRestricted` | Restricted exact/internal occurrence support under approved controls | Public occurrence, map feature, download, or API response |
| `OccurrencePublic` | Governed public-safe derivative with release and transform support | The restricted original or proof that exact precision is safe |
| `RangePolygon` / `SeasonalRange` | Distribution or seasonal-use interpretation under stated source roles and uncertainty | A point occurrence, nest, den, roost, hibernaculum, or spawning site |
| `MigrationRoute` | Bounded route/use evidence or model support under sensitivity review | Exact vulnerable movement location or access guide |
| `SensitiveSite` | Restricted evidence that a sensitive site exists and requires protected handling | Public site discovery, exact geometry, access directions, or collection aid |
| `MonitoringEvent` | Monitoring method, time, scope, and result support | General animal truth beyond the monitored scope |
| `MortalityObservation` | Bounded mortality evidence with method and time | Disease diagnosis, regulatory conclusion, emergency warning, or cause beyond support |
| `DiseaseObservation` | Bounded surveillance or observation evidence with caveats | Medical, veterinary, public-health, or emergency authority |
| `InvasiveSpeciesRecord` | Bounded occurrence/status evidence with private-land and source-role safeguards | Parcel targeting, enforcement action, or operational guidance |
| Habitat/model/aggregate/synthetic support | Context, suitability, trend, summary, or modeled distribution under explicit role | Observed occurrence unless the EvidenceBundle independently closes that claim |

Source access path is not source role. Aggregators, models, catalogs, and public derivatives must preserve the authority and limitations of the underlying evidence.

## Sensitivity, geoprivacy, and safe representation

Fauna proof support must fail closed for:

- sensitive taxa and exact occurrences;
- nests, dens, roosts, hibernacula, spawning sites, breeding or aggregation sites;
- telemetry or movement detail;
- steward-, rights-holder-, agency-, cultural-, or private-land-controlled records;
- joins that re-identify a withheld location;
- source terms that restrict redistribution or public display;
- transform details that would defeat generalization or suppression.

A public-safe derivative may be supported only when the proof packet can point to:

1. the restricted input identity in an approved system;
2. the applicable sensitivity and rights decision;
3. a reviewed public-safe transformation or denial;
4. the transformed artifact identity and checksums;
5. validation and review records;
6. release, correction, withdrawal, and rollback dependencies.

The ordinary repository proof lane should contain only safe summaries and references. It must not contain enough detail to reverse the transform.

## Fauna proof guardrails

- EvidenceBundle outranks generated summaries, map pixels, screenshots, and UI properties.
- An `EvidenceRef` is a pointer; it does not guarantee closure.
- Proof records support evidence closure; they are not source data, receipts, catalog records, release manifests, or public products.
- Schema validity does not prove taxonomy, evidence quality, rights, sensitivity, geoprivacy, policy approval, review, release, or public safety.
- Exact sensitive locations must not leak through identifiers, citations, free text, joins, checksums-as-lookups, filenames, examples, or generated indexes.
- A public derivative must not be reverse-engineerable to the restricted original.
- Habitat suitability, modeled range, seasonal support, migration context, aggregate density, and synthetic summaries are not observed occurrence evidence unless separately supported.
- Cross-lane evidence must retain the owning domain, source role, rights, sensitivity, and correction lineage.
- AI may summarize only governed released evidence within a validated finite envelope; AI text is not proof.
- KFM is not a wildlife emergency, disease alert, hunting, fishing, collection, land-access, enforcement, legal, medical, veterinary, or life-safety authority.
- Public clients and Focus Mode use governed APIs, released artifacts, and EvidenceBundle-backed envelopes—not this directory directly.

> [!CAUTION]
> Do not expose `data/proofs/fauna/` as a public map, API, UI, download, Focus Mode answer, AI source, exact-species-location service, nest/den/roost/spawning discovery surface, access guide, landowner/parcel targeting aid, enforcement aid, or operational guidance product.
