## Purpose

`data/proofs/geology/` is the Geology and Natural Resources domain proof-support lane under the canonical `data/proofs/` responsibility.

It supports reviewable evidence closure for geologic units, surficial units, lithology, stratigraphy, age, structures, boreholes, well logs, cores, samples, geophysics, geochemistry, mineral occurrences, resource deposits and estimates, extraction and production context, reclamation, cross-sections, hydrostratigraphic context, and public-safe derivatives.

This README explains the lane. It does not create a proof inventory, source admission, semantic meaning, machine shape, policy permission, review approval, release decision, public map, legal conclusion, engineering certification, or publication state.

## Inputs

A proof packet must identify the bounded claim or artifact it supports and, where applicable, resolve:

- stable claim, object, artifact, release-candidate, and proof identity;
- object family and explicit claim class;
- source identity, source role, authority role, rights, license, access class, and citation requirements;
- geographic support, map scale, geometry role, exact/internal versus public-safe precision, and coordinate-reference system;
- depth, elevation, vertical datum, interval, and subsurface reference where material;
- valid time, observation time, interpretation time, source vintage, retrieval time, review time, release time, expiry, and correction time;
- `EvidenceRef` members and expected `EvidenceBundle` identity and digest;
- citations, caveats, limitations, confidence, uncertainty, and disputed interpretation state;
- ordered transforms from source evidence to processed and public-safe artifacts;
- checksums, `spec_hash`, code/spec/run identity, and validation profile;
- sensitivity classification, rights posture, policy decision, review state, and obligations;
- catalog, triplet, receipt, release, correction, withdrawal, and rollback references.

Missing required support is a named blocker, not an invitation to infer closure.

## Outputs

Permitted outputs are proof support for:

- processed-to-catalog and catalog-to-release review;
- Evidence Drawer and governed API resolution;
- citation validation and bounded Focus Mode review;
- sensitivity, public-safe representation, and correction review;
- release-candidate assembly, independent review, correction, withdrawal, and rollback.

This lane emits no public truth by itself. Public clients, maps, exports, search, graphs, Evidence Drawer, and AI surfaces must consume released, policy-safe, evidence-resolved interfaces—not repository proof files.

## Validation

A passing check proves only its declared profile. Validation should cover, where applicable:

1. **Placement and identity:** correct path, stable IDs, versions, digests, `spec_hash`, predecessor/successor, and correction lineage.
2. **Claim scope:** the proof supports only the named object, field, relation, geography, time, depth, scale, audience, and use.
3. **Source closure:** source records resolve; source role, authority role, rights, license, access class, and caveats remain explicit.
4. **Evidence closure:** required `EvidenceRef` members resolve to the expected claim-scoped `EvidenceBundle`.
5. **Shared schema shape:** the fielded EvidenceBundle schema checks required fields and rejects undeclared top-level properties.
6. **Claim-class anti-collapse:** occurrence, deposit, estimate, permit, production, reserve, observation, interpretation, model, aggregate, and synthetic roles remain distinct.
7. **Spatial and temporal support:** geometry role, CRS, precision, scale, depth/vertical reference, time scopes, vintage, stale state, and expiry match the claim.
8. **Rights and sensitivity:** exact subsurface points, private wells, restricted logs, extraction-targetable detail, private-parcel/operator joins, and rights-limited content fail closed.
9. **Representation:** generalized, redacted, aggregated, masked, withheld, staged, modeled, cross-section, and 3D products retain named transforms, uncertainty, and reality-boundary support.
10. **Receipts and integrity:** relevant run, transform, validation, policy, review, redaction, representation, and AI receipts resolve without being duplicated here.
11. **Catalog and triplet parity:** identities, source roles, claim classes, evidence refs, sensitivity, and correction state do not diverge.
12. **Release dependency:** any public-bound use resolves review, policy, immutable release identity, correction/withdrawal state, and rollback target.
13. **Negative cases:** missing evidence, role collapse, rights ambiguity, harmful precision, stale evidence, unsupported scope, invalid checksums, withdrawn release, and failed resolution yield finite negative outcomes.
14. **Sensitive-content scan:** no exact protected location, transform secret, credential, private endpoint, or restricted source payload enters ordinary repository paths.

**Current validation posture:** the shared EvidenceBundle schema, validator wrapper, and minimal fixture family are present. Geology-specific proof schemas, resolver checks, synthetic fixtures, accepted validator commands, CI graduation, policy execution, and runtime consumers remain **NEEDS VERIFICATION**. The current Geology workflow explicitly holds validation, proof production, and release readiness.

## Subsurface sensitivity and safe representation

- Exact boreholes, private wells, cores, well logs, geochemistry samples, and similar subsurface points default to denied or generalized public exposure.
- Extraction-targetable mineral/resource detail requires sensitivity and public-interest review even when source data is technically public.
- Rights-restricted logs, proprietary records, terms-limited feeds, and private-parcel/operator joins fail closed.
- A high-quality source does not override sensitivity, rights, sovereignty, trespass, or harmful-precision concerns.
- Generalization, aggregation, masking, withholding, redaction, and staged access must reference named/versioned transform profiles and applicable receipts.
- Public proof pointers must resolve to the public-safe derivative and its transform lineage, never leak the restricted original or parameters that defeat the transform.
- Cross-sections, 3D scenes, interpolations, reconstructions, and modeled surfaces must preserve interpretation/model labels, uncertainty, input evidence, and reality-boundary notes.
- Geology proof support must not become mineral-rights evidence, property-rights evidence, legal advice, engineering certification, investment advice, extraction recommendation, hazard warning, emergency alert, or life-safety guidance.

```mermaid
    POLICY["Policy + sensitivity review"] -. "decision and obligations" .-> PROOF
    PROOF --> REVIEW["Candidate and independent review"]
    REVIEW --> RELEASE["ReleaseManifest + correction + rollback"]
    RELEASE --> PUBLIC["Governed API / released public-safe carrier"]
```

The arrows are governed dependencies, not automatic promotion. A proof packet may support a held, denied, restricted, withdrawn, or unreleased result.

## Correction, withdrawal, and rollback

A proof dependency changes when upstream evidence, source role, rights, sensitivity, interpretation, model, validation, policy, review, catalog, or release state changes.

Required behavior:

1. identify affected claim, proof, catalog, triplet, candidate, release, and public-carrier identities;
2. mark stale, invalidated, superseded, restricted, denied, withdrawn, or held state explicitly;
3. prevent unresolved or withdrawn proof from supporting a new `ANSWER` or public release;
4. preserve prior evidence and decision lineage rather than silently rewriting history;
5. rebuild or withdraw dependent catalogs, indexes, graph projections, tiles, exports, Evidence Drawer payloads, and AI caches through their owning processes;
6. publish correction or withdrawal records when public reliance requires them;
7. preserve an immutable rollback target and verify rollback without exposing restricted originals.

Documentation rollback for this update is a transparent revert to prior blob `e5b4e1d7a14e138607e718cf28f2b8db39e20e24`. Reverting this README does not roll back proof payloads, evidence, policy, release, APIs, maps, caches, or access state.
