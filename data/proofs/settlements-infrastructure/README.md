## Purpose

`data/proofs/settlements-infrastructure/` is the parent domain proof-support lane for two related but distinct evidence regimes:

1. **Settlement-side place and community identity** — `Settlement`, `Municipality`, `CensusPlace`, `Townsite`, `GhostTown`, `Fort`, `Mission`, and `ReservationCommunity`.
2. **Infrastructure-side asset and network evidence** — `InfrastructureAsset`, `NetworkNode`, `NetworkSegment`, `Facility`, `ServiceArea`, `Operator`, `ConditionObservation`, and `Dependency`.

A proof packet or index in this lane should make these questions answerable:

1. Which `EvidenceBundle` supports the bounded claim, relation, candidate, or public-safe derivative?
2. Which source role was assigned at admission, and was it preserved rather than upgraded by normalization, mapping, graph projection, promotion, or generated language?
3. Are settlement identities, legal entities, statistical geographies, historic places, assets, facilities, networks, operators, observations, and dependencies kept distinct?
4. Are source, observed, valid, retrieval, census-vintage, legal-status, operation, condition-observation, dependency-validity, release, and correction times separated where material?
5. Which rights, critical-infrastructure, cultural, sovereignty, archaeology, living-person, private-property, or harmful-precision controls apply?
6. Which validation reports, receipts, policy decisions, review records, catalog records, release dependencies, corrections, withdrawals, invalidations, and rollback targets remain outside the proof packet?
7. Which finite outcome is justified when support is missing, stale, conflicted, restricted, or unsafe?

This directory is not a raw source lane, canonical domain store, catalog, release-decision lane, public API, emergency or planning authority, or infrastructure-disclosure surface.

<a id="3-what-belongs-here"></a>

## What belongs here

Only repository-safe proof support useful for review, release evaluation, correction, rollback, or audit belongs here.

| Proof family | Supported question | Required posture |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence closure | Does each consequential claim resolve to an EvidenceBundle? | Preserve citations, rights, sensitivity, transforms, checksums, and uncertainty. |
| Identity resolution | Were place, legal, statistical, historic, asset, facility, operator, service-area, and dependency identities reconciled or deliberately kept separate? | Never merge by name or geometry alone. |
| Source-role closure | Did administrative, regulatory, observed, aggregate, modeled, candidate, synthetic, historic, and contextual roles remain distinct? | Promotion does not upgrade source authority. |
| Temporal closure | Are legal status, census vintage, operation, condition, dependency, release, and correction times explicit? | Time is identity-bearing, not decorative. |
| Boundary and geometry support | What evidence supports a boundary, footprint, service area, network segment, or generalized public geometry? | Record vintage, uncertainty, sensitivity, and public-geometry posture. |
| Condition-observation support | What supports a bounded condition, inspection, status, or service observation? | Exact condition or vulnerability detail defaults to restricted or denied. |
| Dependency support | What supports a directed asset, facility, network, operator, or service dependency? | Restricted by default; public derivatives require aggregation or redaction evidence. |
| Cultural and sovereignty review | Were ReservationCommunity, mission, fort, sacred, cultural, archaeology-adjacent, or community-sensitive claims reviewed? | Preserve cultural ownership and public-safe geometry posture. |
| Critical-asset review | Were facility, bridge, dam, levee, utility, water or wastewater, operator, condition, or dependency details assessed? | Do not declare a tier here; fail closed until accepted policy and review support a bounded derivative. |
| Cross-lane closure | Are Roads/Rail, Hydrology, Hazards, Archaeology, People/Land, and Frontier Matrix ownership boundaries preserved? | Cite the owning lane; do not absorb its truth. |
| Release-support closure | Are validation, policy, review, catalog, release, correction, withdrawal, invalidation, and rollback dependencies explicit? | Release authority stays under `release/`. |

Proof support may reference stable object IDs, source descriptors, EvidenceRefs, EvidenceBundles, validation reports, receipts, review records, policy decisions, catalog records, release candidates, correction notices, withdrawal notices, and rollback targets. Those referenced objects retain their own authority and lifecycle.

## Inputs

A reviewable proof packet may reference, but must not absorb:

- the bounded claim, relation, object, graph snapshot, derivative, or release-candidate identity;
- `SourceDescriptor` identifiers, source roles, retrieval records, rights, citation, cadence, and freshness state;
- `EvidenceRef` values resolved to `EvidenceBundle` support;
- domain contract and schema versions or immutable digests;
- validation reports and transform, generalization, redaction, aggregation, citation, and review receipts;
- policy decisions, access class, sensitivity posture, reviewer conditions, expiry, and obligations;
- catalog or triplet closure references where applicable;
- release-candidate, correction, withdrawal, invalidation, and rollback references; and
- public-geometry or public-field allowlists when a bounded derivative is under review.

Unresolved evidence, rights, sensitivity, ownership, precision, identity, time, or release state is an input to a negative outcome, not permission to omit the field.

## Outputs

Permitted outputs are repository-safe proof-support records or indexes under an accepted profile, including:

- evidence-closure and identity-resolution support;
- source-role, temporal, geometry, and sensitivity closure summaries;
- validation and review support that references independently addressable records;
- release-candidate evaluation support;
- correction, withdrawal, invalidation, and rollback support; and
- explicit finite outcomes and reason codes.

An output must not claim more than its evidence. Depending on the applicable contract, incomplete or unsafe support should yield `ABSTAIN`, `DENY`, `HOLD`, `ERROR`, quarantine, restriction, or another defined negative state. Do not invent a universal outcome enum when the relevant contract has not been accepted.

No output in this directory is public merely because the repository is public or a file is reviewable.

- the [domain workflow](../../../.github/workflows/domain-settlements-infrastructure.yml) performs bounded static readiness checks and records explicit validation, proof-production, and release-dry-run holds.

Before a future proof packet supports release review, verify at least:

- [ ] The proof identifies proof family, object group, object or release scope, source family, spatial scope, temporal scope, and intended public surface.
- [ ] Every consequential claim resolves to an EvidenceBundle or records a contract-defined negative outcome.
- [ ] SourceDescriptor references carry source role, rights, sensitivity, citation, cadence or vintage, retrieval time, and digest where applicable.
- [ ] Settlement-side and infrastructure-side identities are not silently merged.
- [ ] Municipality, CensusPlace, Townsite, GhostTown, Fort, Mission, and ReservationCommunity identities remain distinct.
- [ ] Asset, facility, network node or segment, service area, operator, condition observation, and dependency identities remain distinct.
- [ ] Source, observed, valid, retrieval, release, correction, census-vintage, legal-status, operation, condition, and dependency times remain distinct where material.
- [ ] Boundary, footprint, service-area, and network geometry has source vintage, uncertainty, valid time, sensitivity, public-geometry posture, and release state.
- [ ] Critical-infrastructure, condition, vulnerability, operator-sensitive, service-area, and dependency details are denied, restricted, aggregated, or generalized under accepted policy.
- [ ] Cultural, sacred, sovereignty, Indigenous/community, and archaeology-adjacent contexts have appropriate review and public-safe representation.
- [ ] Living-person, DNA, residence, ownership, parcel, and person-place joins are denied, aggregated, or routed to the owning lane.
- [ ] Cross-lane joins preserve Roads/Rail, Hydrology, Hazards, Archaeology, People/Land, and Frontier Matrix ownership.
- [ ] Validation, receipt, policy, review, catalog, release, correction, withdrawal, invalidation, and rollback closure is traceable.
- [ ] Public clients receive only released, public-safe carriers through governed interfaces.
- [ ] Tests use deterministic, synthetic, no-network, public-safe fixtures and exercise positive and fail-closed cases.

Passing shape or readiness checks cannot prove a place claim, legal status, infrastructure condition, dependency, public safety, policy approval, evidence closure, release, or publication.

## Review burden

The current [CODEOWNERS file](../../../.github/CODEOWNERS) routes `data/proofs/` changes to `@bartytime4life`. Routing is not an accountable `ReviewRecord`, policy decision, release approval, or proof that independent review occurred.

Review should cover the responsibilities implicated by the change:

- Settlements / Infrastructure domain and the affected settlement-side or infrastructure-side object family;
- evidence, proof, source role, identity, time, validation, and catalog closure;
- rights, security, critical infrastructure, cultural, sovereignty, archaeology, privacy, and harmful precision;
- policy, public-safe transformation, governed API, and governed AI behavior;
- release, correction, withdrawal, invalidation, and rollback; and
- documentation accuracy and stable-link preservation.

High-risk, public-surface, behavioral, policy-significant, or authority-changing changes require independent review appropriate to their significance. Unverified role labels are requirements to assign, not executable GitHub identities.

## Identity, source-role, and temporal gates

| Gate | Required support | Fail-closed response |
|---|---|---|
| Settlement versus infrastructure | Evidence that place or community identity and asset, facility, or network identity are not silently merged | Deny the merge, abstain, or require an explicit relation |
| Legal versus census versus historic | Evidence separating Municipality, CensusPlace, Townsite, GhostTown, Fort, Mission, and ReservationCommunity | Deny, abstain, or hold |
| Asset versus facility versus network | Evidence separating assets, facilities, nodes, segments, service areas, operators, observations, and dependencies | Deny identity collapse or hold |
| Administrative, regulatory, observed, or modeled role | Source role assigned from source scope and preserved through transforms | Deny source-role upcast |
| Boundary, footprint, or service-area vintage | Source, vintage, valid time, uncertainty, and public-geometry posture | Abstain, mark stale, restrict, or hold |
| Deterministic identity | Source ID, object role, temporal scope, and normalized digest | Error or hold under the applicable contract |
| Condition observation | Observed-at time, measurement or inspection context, uncertainty, and source authority | Deny, restrict, or hold |
| Dependency validity | Directed relation, evidence basis, temporal scope, sensitivity, and rollback support | Deny public graph release or restrict |
| Cross-lane relation | Owning-lane support and explicit relation identity | Abstain or deny if ownership collapses |

Promotion, map rendering, graph projection, generated summaries, or a newer retrieval time must not silently change identity, source role, valid time, legal status, condition, dependency, or authority.

## Sensitivity and public-safe representation

| Risk surface | Required support | Default when unresolved |

| Critical asset, facility, utility, bridge, dam, levee, water or wastewater, or dependency detail | Accepted sensitivity decision, need-to-publish basis, public-safe transform, review, release dependency, and rollback | Deny, restrict, or generalized summary only |

## Lifecycle and governed handoff

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  RAW["RAW<br/>source capture"] --> WQ["WORK or QUARANTINE<br/>normalize or hold"]
  WQ --> PROC["PROCESSED<br/>validated candidate"]
  PROC --> CAT["CATALOG or TRIPLET<br/>identity and evidence closure"]
  CAT --> PROOF["PROOF SUPPORT<br/>this domain lane"]
  PROOF --> REVIEW["POLICY and REVIEW<br/>allow, restrict, abstain, deny, or hold"]
  REVIEW --> REL["RELEASE<br/>decision, manifest, correction, rollback"]
  REL --> PUB["PUBLISHED<br/>public-safe carrier"]
  PROOF -. "never publishes by itself" .-> PUB
```

Proof support may cite receipts, contracts, schemas, policy decisions, review records, catalogs, release candidates, and rollback targets. It must not absorb their authority. Public clients consume governed APIs or released artifacts, never this internal proof-support lane as a normal data source.

AI may help retrieve, compare, or summarize an already authorized proof packet. It does not create evidence, decide sensitivity, approve release, or make generated language authoritative.

## Correction, withdrawal, invalidation, and rollback

When support changes:

1. Preserve the prior proof identity and bytes; do not rewrite historical meaning.
2. Record why evidence, identity, source role, time, rights, sensitivity, policy, review, or release support changed.
3. Emit or reference the applicable correction, withdrawal, supersession, invalidation, and rollback records in their owning roots.
4. Propagate invalidation to dependent catalogs, triplets, maps, reports, stories, API payloads, caches, indexes, and AI retrieval material.
5. Re-evaluate any public-safe transformation; a corrected source can invalidate an aggregation, geometry, field allowlist, or derivative.
6. Require a new governed release decision before republishing corrected material.

Deleting or editing a proof-shaped file is not a complete correction. A Git revert does not by itself invalidate downstream released carriers or reconstructive derivatives.

<a id="12-failure-modes"></a>

## Failure modes

| Failure mode | Why it matters | Required response |
|---|---|---|
| Settlement and infrastructure identities merge for convenience | Place and asset regimes have different authority and sensitivity | Split identities or add an evidence-backed relation |
| Municipality and CensusPlace merge because names match | Legal and statistical identities are different | Deny, split, or require explicit crosswalk evidence |
| Administrative compilation is cited as observation | Source-role collapse overstates what the source proves | Deny or relabel to its actual role |
| Proof support appears without EvidenceBundle closure | A proof-shaped file becomes unsupported authority | Abstain, deny, hold, or quarantine |
| Exact critical-asset detail enters the public repository | The proof lane becomes an exposure channel | Remove from public scope; quarantine, restrict, aggregate, generalize, or deny |
| Condition, vulnerability, or dependency graph is public by default | It may expose operational or cascading-risk detail | Deny until accepted policy, review, and public-safe transformation close |
| ReservationCommunity naming or geometry is released without review | Sovereignty and community harms are possible | Deny, restrict, stage, or require steward review |
| Archaeology-adjacent site geometry is exposed | It can reveal protected cultural locations | Quarantine, generalize, or deny |
| Living-person, parcel, ownership, residence, or DNA join appears | It crosses the People / DNA / Land trust boundary | Deny, aggregate, or route to the owning lane |
| Proof support acts as a ReleaseManifest | Proof and release authority collapse | Keep release decisions under `release/` and reference them |
| A receipt is treated as proof closure | Process execution is mistaken for evidentiary support | Require resolved evidence and the applicable proof profile |
| A green held workflow is described as validation or release readiness | Readiness scaffolding is overstated | Preserve the explicit hold and bounded claim |
| AI-generated settlement or infrastructure language replaces evidence | Fluency becomes root truth | Deny; resolve EvidenceBundle support and cite or abstain |
| Correction updates only this file | Downstream carriers remain stale or unsafe | Propagate invalidation and use governed correction and rollback |
