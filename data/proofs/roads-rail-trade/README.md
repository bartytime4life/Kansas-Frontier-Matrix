## Purpose

`data/proofs/roads-rail-trade/` is the domain proof-support lane for roads, rail corridors, routes and route memberships, historic and trade corridors, crossings, bridges, ferries, depots, sidings, yards, transport facilities, restrictions, operator and status intervals, Movement Story Nodes, and derived network projections.

The lane exists to make bounded questions inspectable before a claim or artifact advances:

1. Which admitted sources and EvidenceBundles support the exact object, relation, event, graph projection, or release-candidate scope?
2. Which source role was assigned at admission, and was it preserved rather than upgraded by processing or promotion?
3. Which source, observed, valid, retrieval, vintage, event, release, correction, and stale-state times govern interpretation?
4. Which geometry uncertainty, rights, cultural, archaeological, private-land, critical-infrastructure, or operational-sensitivity controls apply?
5. Which validation reports, receipts, policy decisions, reviews, catalog records, release dependencies, corrections, withdrawals, and rollback targets remain outside the proof packet?
6. Which finite outcome is justified under the applicable contract when support is missing, stale, conflicted, restricted, or unsafe?

This directory supports evidence and release review. It does not admit sources, own canonical processed transport objects, decide policy, approve release, publish a layer, or operate a transport service.

## What belongs here

Only repository-safe proof support that is useful for review, release evaluation, correction, rollback, or audit belongs here.

| Proof family | Supported question | Required posture |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence closure | Does a segment, route, crossing, facility, restriction, historic claim, or graph edge resolve to admitted EvidenceBundle support? | Preserve scope, source role, time, uncertainty, and release dependency. |
| Source-role closure | Were regulatory, observed, administrative, historic, contextual, modeled, candidate, synthetic, and derived roles kept distinct? | A source role is fixed at admission and cannot be upgraded by promotion. |
| Temporal closure | Are source, observed, valid, retrieval, vintage, event, release, correction, and stale-state times distinguishable? | Required when the claim depends on current status, historical interpretation, or an event interval. |
| Network-projection closure | Can a derived node or edge be traced to source-bound objects, memberships, catalog records, and EvidenceBundles? | Graph projections remain downstream and non-canonical. |
| Corridor-generalization closure | Is uncertain, historic, Indigenous, treaty, cultural, or trade-corridor geometry appropriately bounded? | Harmful or unsupported precision fails closed. |
| Restriction-event closure | Is a closure, detour, weight limit, hazard relation, or access-restriction claim time- and source-bounded? | Never becomes KFM routing or life-safety guidance. |
| Facility-sensitivity closure | Are crossing, bridge, depot, yard, freight, intermodal, capacity, and vulnerability details reviewed? | Restrict, redact, generalize, or deny where required. |
| Cross-lane closure | Are Hydrology, Hazards, Settlements/Infrastructure, Archaeology, People/Land, and Frontier Matrix ownership boundaries preserved? | Cite the owning lane; do not absorb its truth. |
| Release-support closure | Are validation, policy, review, catalog, release, correction, withdrawal, and rollback dependencies explicit? | Release authority stays under `release/`. |

## Inputs

Proof packets may reference, but must not absorb:

- stable object, relation, event, graph-projection, or release-candidate identity;
- admitted SourceDescriptors with fixed role, provider, rights, terms, citation, cadence, freshness, authority limits, sensitivity, retrieval time, and digest;
- EvidenceRefs that resolve to EvidenceBundles;
- validation, transform, catalog-build, generalization, redaction, citation, review, and correction receipts;
- contract- and schema-version references;
- policy decisions and independent review records where applicable;
- catalog, triplet, release, correction, withdrawal, and rollback references;
- distinct spatial and temporal scope, uncertainty, stale state, and public-representation posture.

Missing, stale, conflicted, rights-unclear, sensitivity-unclear, or evidence-incomplete inputs must narrow or block the downstream transition according to the applicable contract. Do not invent one universal outcome enum where surfaces differ.

## Source-role, temporal, and operational boundaries

| Boundary | Required support | Default when unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| Modern graph versus historic route | Historic evidence must support a historic claim; modern geometry may remain context only | Deny or relabel the claim |
| Historic claim versus current access | Current authority evidence must support operational or legal-access assertions | Deny the operational assertion |
| Live/event feed versus routing | Official-source context, validity, freshness, expiry, and explicit non-routing posture | Deny KFM runtime guidance |
| Source vintage | Vintage and retrieval time for static or periodic products | Hold, quarantine, or abstain under the governing contract |
| Event validity | Issue, valid, expiry, event, and correction intervals for restrictions and status claims | Mark stale, hold, abstain, or deny as defined by the surface |
| Route, segment, and membership identity | Separate stable identities and time-bounded relations | Deny collapsed identity |
| Graph projection | Reversibility to source-bound objects, memberships, evidence, and catalog state | Deny graph-as-truth use |
| Cross-lane context | Evidence and ownership retained by the contributing domain | Split the claim or abstain |

Source role is fixed at admission. Promotion, cataloging, mapping, graph projection, review, or release cannot upgrade an observation, candidate, administrative name, contextual source, model, or historical interpretation into regulatory or operational authority.

## Sensitivity and public-safe representation

| Risk surface | Required support | Default when unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| Critical transport facilities, capacity, vulnerability, or structural detail | Sensitivity review, public-safe transform or redaction evidence, policy decision, and review record | Restrict or deny |
| Indigenous, treaty, cultural, oral-history, trade, or mobility corridor context | Sovereignty/cultural review, source-role label, uncertainty, generalized representation, and evidence | Deny exact exposure or publish only an approved generalized form |
| Historic-route overprecision | Uncertainty representation and a reviewed generalization decision | Abstain or deny |
| Archaeology-adjacent route claims | Archaeology ownership and protected-location handling | Deny exact exposure |
| Private land or living-person adjacency | People/Land ownership and non-identifying aggregation | Deny or aggregate |
| Live restriction or work-zone context | Official-source limits, validity/expiry, stale-state handling, and no operational guidance | Deny runtime guidance |
| Hazard closure or detour context | Hazard-event ownership and a bounded transport relation | Abstain or deny if role or time is unclear |
| Public graph or network carrier | Reversibility, digest closure, public-safe geometry, policy/review, and release dependency | Hold or deny |

Public-safe representation must be independently reviewable and reversible to the protected source-bound object and transform evidence without exposing the protected value.

## Lifecycle and governed handoff

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  A["RAW<br/>source capture"] --> B["WORK / QUARANTINE<br/>normalize or hold"]
  B --> C["PROCESSED<br/>validated candidate"]
  C --> D["CATALOG / TRIPLET<br/>discovery and projections"]
  D --> E["PROOF SUPPORT<br/>evidence and release review"]
  E --> F["RELEASE<br/>decision and rollback authority"]
  F --> G["PUBLISHED<br/>public-safe carrier"]
  H["Receipts, contracts, schemas,<br/>policy, and independent review"] -. "support and gate" .-> E
```

The lifecycle is a governed state transition, not a directory move. Proof support may reference receipts, contracts, schemas, policy decisions, reviews, catalogs, triplets, and release objects, but it does not replace them. A graph, map, story, API payload, badge, or AI response remains a downstream carrier.

## Workflow and automation posture

The current [`domain-roads-rail-trade` workflow](../../../.github/workflows/domain-roads-rail-trade.yml) has read-only `contents` permission, runs on pull requests and pushes to `main`, and contains three bounded jobs:

| Job | Current behavior | What a green result does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| `validate-roads-rail-trade` | Confirms responsibility boundaries, parses current schema and fixture structure, recognizes the known smoke placeholder, and detects validator or test work that requires deliberate graduation | Semantic validity, source truth, policy enforcement, test coverage, operational safety, release readiness |
| `build-proof-roads-rail-trade` | Confirms this README and related boundaries, preserves the maturity token, expects no non-README proof payload, and records that no accepted proof producer or command is established | Evidence closure, ProofPack production, proof integrity, route or graph truth |
| `publish-dry-run-roads-rail-trade` | Confirms release boundaries and records that no accepted candidate manifest contract or release dry-run command is established | Candidate approval, release, deployment, publication, routing, or safe-passage authority |

Graduation requires an accepted bounded profile, deterministic no-network fixtures and commands, substantive positive and negative tests, source-role and temporal controls, receipts, policy decisions, independent review, release dependency, correction and withdrawal behavior, and a rollback target.

A workflow status badge is intentionally omitted. The present green state can include successful holds and would be easy to misread as semantic validation, proof closure, release readiness, or operational authority.

## Correction, withdrawal, invalidation, and rollback

Proof support must preserve:

1. stable identity and the exact superseded scope;
2. corrected evidence, source role, time, uncertainty, rights, sensitivity, and policy/review state;
3. affected catalogs, graphs, maps, APIs, stories, caches, and AI indexes;
4. correction, supersession, or withdrawal notices as required;
5. the prior release and explicit rollback target;
6. verification that stale public and derived carriers no longer present the retracted meaning.

Operational rollback authority remains under `release/` and the reviewed [Roads/Rail/Trade rollback runbook](../../../docs/runbooks/roads-rail-trade/ROLLBACK_RUNBOOK.md). A Git revert may restore documentation bytes; it does not by itself correct a relied-on claim, withdraw a release, invalidate caches, or restore an operational state.
