## Purpose

`data/catalog/domain/settlement/` preserves discoverability for catalog
references that use the short `settlement` segment. Its bounded role is to
redirect, crosswalk, or explain those references without evolving separately
from the broader Settlements/Infrastructure context.

A compatibility index can help a steward find the working record family. It
does not:

- make a place, municipality, census place, townsite, historic community, or
  infrastructure assertion true;
- replace an EvidenceBundle, SourceDescriptor, contract, schema, policy
  decision, review record, receipt, proof, or release manifest;
- establish current legal status, occupancy, facility operation, service
  availability, safety, ownership, jurisdiction, or public readiness; or
- authorize lifecycle promotion, release, publication, or direct client access.

## Inputs and outputs

### Permitted inputs

This lane may consume only enough metadata to resolve a compatibility pointer:

- prior short-name identifiers and paths;
- a verified target identifier in the working compound-domain catalog lane;
- source, evidence, time, rights, sensitivity, policy, review, receipt, release,
  correction, and rollback references required to keep the pointer honest; and
- an accepted path decision, migration record, or supersession record when the
  alias state changes.

It must not copy the referenced payload into this directory.

### Permitted outputs

The normal output is navigation: a deterministic pointer, crosswalk entry, or
migration note that leads a reviewer to the working catalog record.

This lane emits no source admission, domain truth, proof, policy decision,
release decision, public artifact, or runtime response.

## Compatibility contract

The short lane and working compound-domain lane must remain one-way and
non-competing:

1. A short-name entry resolves to one declared target in the working lane.
2. It does not mint a second domain identity, version history, source binding,
   evidence chain, policy posture, or release state.
3. It does not shadow or override a compound-domain record.
4. It preserves unresolved status instead of guessing when the target is
   missing, ambiguous, stale, restricted, corrected, withdrawn, or superseded.
5. It preserves stricter rights, sensitivity, temporal, review, and access
   posture from the target.
6. It fails closed when resolution would expose restricted or
   reverse-engineerable information.
7. Any consolidation, redirect, migration, or retirement requires accepted
   authority, an explicit path map, reference closure, validation, correction
   handling, and a reversible rollback plan.

The singular form is legitimate in some explicitly bounded settlement
sublanes, including documented proof and receipt surfaces. That does not make
this catalog path an independent domain authority.

## Source, evidence, rights, and sensitivity

Catalog metadata is not domain truth. A future alias must preserve the source
role and authority scope of its target rather than flattening a gazetteer,
census, legal, historic, operational, modeled, administrative, or derived
source into a generic “settlement source.”

Examples of boundaries that must remain visible:

- a `Settlement` is not automatically an incorporated `Municipality`;
- a `CensusPlace` is not municipal legal status;
- a `Townsite` or `GhostTown` assertion is time-scoped and may overlap
  archaeology or cultural sensitivity;
- a place-name match is not deterministic identity;
- a facility point is not proof of current operation, condition, service,
  safety, ownership, or public disclosure permission; and
- a service area or dependency relation may be modeled, sensitive, stale, or
  unsafe to expose.

EvidenceRef should resolve to an EvidenceBundle before a consequential claim is
presented as supported. Unknown rights, attribution, source role, time,
sensitivity, sovereignty, private-property or living-person joins, and exact
critical-infrastructure detail fail closed.

## Guardrails

- Keep this path subordinate to the working compound-domain catalog lane.
- Do not use the alias to create duplicate catalog records, source bindings,
  schema versions, policy decisions, evidence chains, receipts, releases, or
  public routes.
- Keep `Settlement`, `Municipality`, `CensusPlace`, `Townsite`, `GhostTown`,

  review, correction, withdrawal, supersession, and rollback state.

  logs, examples, screenshots, maps, graphs, search, Focus Mode, or generated
  summaries.
- Prefer an explicit unresolved, held, abstained, quarantined, denied, stale,
  corrected, withdrawn, or superseded result over a plausible but unsupported
  resolution.
