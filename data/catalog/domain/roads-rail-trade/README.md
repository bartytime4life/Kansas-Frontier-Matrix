## Purpose

`data/catalog/domain/roads-rail-trade/` is the canonical domain-scoped catalog lane for discoverable Roads / Rail / Trade records inside the broader [`data/catalog/`](../../README.md) responsibility root.

It may describe released or release-candidate road and rail segments, routes and corridors, route memberships, crossings and transport facilities, time-bound restriction or status context, operators, freight context, historic route claims, derived network views, and Movement Story Nodes. It owns the **catalog boundary and indexing posture**, not the underlying source records, processed domain truth, proof objects, release decisions, public artifacts, or client behavior.

A catalog record supports discovery, steward review, cross-format catalog closure, and release preparation. It must preserve the identity, source role, spatial and temporal scope, uncertainty, evidence, policy, review, correction, and rollback posture of the object it describes.

## Inputs

Catalog records may reference, but must not absorb, the following governed inputs:

- validated processed Roads/Rail/Trade objects with stable identity and declared object family;
- admitted SourceDescriptors with fixed source role, rights, terms, cadence, authority limits, freshness, and sensitivity posture;
- resolvable EvidenceRefs and EvidenceBundles;
- validation, transform, catalog-build, generalization, citation, review, and correction receipts;
- PolicyDecisions and ReviewRecords where rights, sensitivity, uncertainty, current status, public representation, or cultural context are material;
- release-candidate and immutable release references;
- prior catalog identity, supersession, correction, withdrawal, and rollback lineage.

Missing, stale, conflicted, rights-unclear, sensitivity-unclear, or evidence-incomplete inputs must keep the record held, narrowed, withdrawn, superseded, or absent from public use according to its governing contract.

<a id="roads-rail-trade-guardrails"></a>

## Roads / Rail / Trade guardrails

- **Catalog is not truth.** A record describes and links; it cannot make a segment, route, event, graph, or narrative claim true.
- **Source roles do not collapse.** Observed, regulatory, administrative, modeled, aggregate, candidate, context, synthetic, and restricted sources retain their admitted role downstream.
- **Object families stay separate.** Segments, routes, memberships, crossings, facilities, operators, events, restrictions, graph edges, historic claims, and story nodes are not interchangeable.
- **Time stays explicit.** Historical validity, source time, current operational status, retrieval time, release time, and correction time must not be flattened.
- **Geometry is not legal access.** A mapped line or crossing does not prove ownership, public access, current passability, safety, legality, or operating status.
- **Historic and cultural claims stay bounded.** Interpretation, oral history, Indigenous and cultural corridors, archaeological relations, and uncertain alignments retain uncertainty, authority, sensitivity, and steward review.
- **Graph stays derived.** Connectivity and route projections remain reversible to source-bound segments, memberships, catalog records, and EvidenceBundles.
- **Cross-domain joins preserve ownership.** Hydrology, Settlements/Infrastructure, Archaeology, Hazards, Agriculture, People/Land, and other lanes retain their identity, evidence, policy, sensitivity, correction, and rollback authority.
- **AI stays subordinate.** Generated language cannot upgrade evidence, source role, uncertainty, policy, review, release, or sensitivity state.
- **Public Git is public.** “Unreleased,” “review-only,” or “restricted” wording does not make tracked bytes confidential.
- **Publication remains separate.** A successful build, badge, check, commit, PR, merge, catalog write, or rendered map is not a release.

## Correction, supersession, and rollback

### Catalog records

If a source, object identity, geometry, time, EvidenceBundle, policy decision, review, sensitivity state, release, or limitation changes:

1. stop serving the dependent catalog projection through governed consumers;
2. mark the record held, corrected, withdrawn, superseded, or otherwise disposed according to its governing contract;
3. preserve the prior identity, digest, source role, and lineage rather than rewriting history;
4. invalidate or rebuild dependent STAC, DCAT, PROV, triplet, graph, map, search, export, StoryNode, API, cache, and generated-summary surfaces;
5. issue any required correction or withdrawal through the release authority; and
6. restore only an approved public-safe prior target with a valid rollback reference.

### This README

Before merge, rollback means leaving the review branch unmerged. After merge, use a transparent revert commit or focused revert PR against the actual merged commit; do not rewrite shared history.
