## Purpose

`data/catalog/domain/people-dna-land/` is the canonical responsibility placement for governed People, genealogy, DNA, and land catalog records and indexes within the `CATALOG / TRIPLET` lifecycle stage.

Candidate catalog families include person and relationship assertions, genealogy relationship hypotheses, family-group context, DNA evidence references and public-safe derivative summaries, consent and revocation pointers, land-ownership assertions, ownership intervals, land instruments, parcel versions, legal-description references, assessor and tax administrative context, public-safe derivative pointers, review references, and release-linked indexes. Listing a family here does not prove that its profile, records, sources, evidence, schema, validator, policy, consent control, release, or public representation exists.

This lane organizes governed references. It does not own source truth, canonical person truth, biological or genealogical truth, raw DNA, consent authority, legal or title determinations, cadastral or survey authority, semantic contracts, machine schemas, policy, evidence, release authority, or public delivery.

## Authority level

**Canonical responsibility placement for People/DNA/Land domain catalog records / repository-grounded draft / concrete catalog profile and realization PROPOSED / not source, evidence, identity, consent, policy, legal, title, release, or publication authority.**

This lane may carry catalog descriptors and governed references. It cannot replace semantic meaning under `contracts/`, machine shape under `schemas/`, admissibility under `policy/`, source identity and activation under `data/registry/`, process memory under `data/receipts/`, EvidenceBundles and proof support under `data/proofs/`, release decisions under `release/`, public-safe carriers under `data/published/`, or competent legal, title, survey, court, recorder, tribal, medical, genetic-counseling, or other authoritative processes.

## Inputs

Catalog inputs should be governed references and validated derivatives, not raw truth by placement:

- processed object identities, versions, digests, object families, temporal roles, jurisdictions, and bounded spatial support;
- source-registry references with admitted source role, activation decision, rights, current terms, sensitivity, retrieval context, and issuing authority identity;
- accepted contracts, schemas, catalog profiles, policy decisions, applicable consent decisions, and review records;
- EvidenceRef-to-EvidenceBundle resolution and applicable proof support;
- `CatalogBuildReceipt`, `RunReceipt`, `ValidationReport`, `RedactionReceipt`, applicable `ConsentGrant` or `RevocationReceipt`, PolicyDecision, correction, and release-support receipts;
- STAC, DCAT, PROV, graph, and triplet references when those projections exist;
- immutable release, correction, withdrawal, supersession, and rollback references.

Unknown or conflicted identity, source role, rights, sensitivity, living status, consent applicability, policy, evidence, review, release, correction, or rollback state holds a record from public-bound or current-state use.

## Outputs

This lane may emit or support:

- internal or reviewable People/DNA/Land catalog records;
- deterministic domain indexes and bounded catalog subsets;
- catalog-side identity, lineage, source-role, temporal, sensitivity, consent-reference, review, correction, and release metadata;
- domain references used by separately governed STAC, DCAT, PROV, graph, and triplet projections;
- release-linked descriptors for public-safe derivatives after all applicable gates close;
- correction, withdrawal, supersession, revocation-impact, and rollback references.

This lane must not emit source-of-truth identity, raw DNA, legal or title conclusions, policy decisions, consent grants, evidence bundles, release decisions, public routes, or mutable `current` state without the separately governed authority that owns each object.

## Lifecycle and catalog boundary

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  RAW["RAW source capture"] --> HELD["WORK / QUARANTINE"]
  HELD --> PROCESSED["PROCESSED candidates"]
  PROCESSED --> CATALOG["CATALOG / TRIPLET — this lane"]
  CATALOG --> GATES["Evidence, policy, review, and release gates"]
  GATES --> PUBLISHED["PUBLISHED public-safe carriers"]
  CATALOG -. "separate governed projections" .-> PROJECTIONS["STAC / DCAT / PROV / graph"]
```

This folder owns catalog-stage domain responsibility only. It is not a RAW, WORK, QUARANTINE, PROCESSED, or PUBLISHED store. It is also not a proof store, receipt store, source registry, schema or contract home, policy or consent engine, implementation root, release authority, public API, legal/title authority, or canonical identity store.

Transitions are governed, not implied by file movement:

1. upstream source and processed candidates retain identity, evidence, rights, temporal, uncertainty, and sensitivity context;
2. catalog assembly creates deterministic descriptors and references without collapsing those objects;
3. separately governed projections agree on shared identity and release state where required;
4. validation, policy, consent where applicable, accountable review, release, correction, and rollback gates close;
5. only approved public-safe carriers become eligible for governed public delivery.

A file, schema pass, workflow pass, pull request, merge, generated receipt, or catalog entry cannot skip this sequence.

<a id="privacy-consent-and-anti-collapse-guardrails"></a>

## Privacy, consent, and anti-collapse guardrails

- **Catalog is not truth.** A record is a governed carrier, not canonical person, relationship, genealogy, DNA, ownership, title, parcel, boundary, consent, policy, or release truth.
- **Person assertions stay assertions.** Conflicts, source attribution, uncertainty, evidence, time, and reviewer state must remain visible; identity resolution cannot silently fuse people.
- **Genealogy hypotheses stay hypotheses.** A suggested parent, spouse, household, migration, or family relationship must not become fact through indexing, search rank, graph projection, or AI narration.
- **Raw DNA stays out.** Raw sequence, segment, kit, vendor identifier, triangulation output, living-person association, and disallowed inference must not become public catalog material.
- **DNA derivatives remain sensitive.** Aggregation or deidentification does not by itself prove non-reidentification, consent compatibility, policy admission, or release safety.
- **Living persons fail closed.** Unknown living status is not permission. Protected addresses, contact details, private relationships, and person-parcel joins require explicit controls and review.
- **Consent is scoped and revocable.** A consent pointer is not authority by presence. Purpose, scope, affected objects, expiry, revocation, cleanup, tombstone, embargo, and downstream invalidation must remain enforceable.
- **Land evidence is not title.** Assessor and tax records are administrative evidence, parcel geometry is not an adjudicated or surveyed boundary, and KFM does not determine legal ownership, title, heirship, encumbrances, or instrument effect.
- **Source roles do not collapse.** Observed, regulatory, modeled, aggregate, administrative, candidate, synthetic, primary, corroborating, context, and other tracked terms must not be normalized until their conflict is resolved.
- **Time roles do not collapse.** Event, effective, observed, recorded, retrieved, corrected, released, withdrawn, and superseded times are independently meaningful.
- **Neighbor context cannot weaken controls.** Cross-domain joins inherit the strictest applicable sensitivity, rights, consent, precision, evidence, and release posture.
- **AI remains subordinate.** AI may assist search, comparison, explanation, or review, but cannot invent evidence, resolve identity, infer consent, determine title, waive policy, approve release, or override abstention.
- **Existence is not publication.** A catalog file, record, generated receipt, workflow result, PR, merge, or repository URL does not create public authority.

When required support is absent, stale, conflicted, revoked, or unverifiable, the safe outcomes are `DENY`, `ABSTAIN`, hold, quarantine, withdraw, or correct—not silent completion.

## Projection and catalog closure

The inspected repository provides a proposed shared catalog-matrix design, but bounded path inspection did not surface People/DNA/Land-specific STAC, DCAT, PROV, graph, or triplet children. Because the inspection was not a recursive clone, exhaustive absence remains `NEEDS VERIFICATION`.

Projection rules:

- one projection may not invent identity, source, evidence, sensitivity, consent, rights, temporal, correction, or release state absent from the governed record;
- required projections must agree on stable identity, version, lineage, spatial and temporal scope, sensitivity, release state, withdrawal, and supersession;
- a missing optional projection does not make an internal catalog record false, but missing required closure blocks claims of catalog-matrix completion and public release readiness;
- projection disagreement is a hold, not a majority vote;
- new projection paths must follow Directory Rules and an accepted profile; this README does not create them by naming them.

## Release, correction, and rollback closure

Catalog readiness, release readiness, and public availability are distinct:

1. **Catalog readiness** requires an accepted profile, deterministic records, resolvable source/evidence/policy/consent/review references, meaningful fixtures, executable validation, and required projection agreement.
2. **Release readiness** additionally requires an accountable review packet, approved PromotionDecision, ReleaseManifest, immutable artifact identity, rights and sensitivity closure, correction and withdrawal paths, rollback target, and no unresolved hold.
3. **Public availability** additionally requires approved public-safe carriers and a governed API or static-delivery route that does not expose internal stores.

Current bounded evidence proves none of those completion states for this lane. A generated documentation receipt, schema-shape pass, green unrelated workflow, pull request, merge, or file under `data/published/` cannot substitute for them.

Correction, revocation, or new adverse evidence must be able to:

- identify every affected record, projection, derivative, cache, index, public carrier, and explanation;
- preserve the correction reason, actor, time, evidence, prior state, and replacement or withdrawal state;
- invalidate downstream products whose permission, consent, evidence, or source support no longer holds;
- prevent stale `current` resolution and direct public reads;
- retain enough lineage for audit without republishing restricted content.
