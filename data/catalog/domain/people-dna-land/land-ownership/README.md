## Purpose

`data/catalog/domain/people-dna-land/land-ownership/` is the canonical responsibility placement for governed land-ownership catalog records and indexes within the People/DNA/Land domain at the `CATALOG / TRIPLET` lifecycle stage. Candidate families include Land Ownership Assertions, ownership intervals, LandInstrument descriptors, Parcel Version and LegalDescription references, assessor and tax administrative context, chain candidates, conflict notices, and public-safe derivative pointers.

This lane organizes governed references. It does not own source truth, legal or title determinations, cadastral or survey authority, contracts, schemas, policies, evidence, consent, release authority, or public delivery. Listing a record family does not prove that its records, source descriptors, contract, schema, validator, evidence, rights decision, consent decision, release state, or public route exist.

## Authority level

**Canonical responsibility placement for People/DNA/Land land-ownership catalog records / repository-grounded draft / concrete catalog profile and realization PROPOSED / not source, evidence, identity, policy, consent, legal, title, release, or publication authority.**

This lane may carry catalog descriptors and governed references. It cannot replace land-ownership meaning under `contracts/`, machine shape under `schemas/`, admissibility under `policy/`, source identity and activation under `data/registry/`, process memory under `data/receipts/`, EvidenceBundles and proof support under `data/proofs/`, release decisions under `release/`, public-safe carriers under `data/published/`, or official county, recorder, court, survey, title, tribal, federal, state, or other competent authority records.

## Inputs

Catalog inputs should be governed references and validated derivatives, not raw truth by placement:

- processed object identities, versions, digests, object families, temporal roles, jurisdiction, and bounded spatial support;
- source registry references with admitted source role, activation decision, rights, current terms, sensitivity, retrieval context, and issuing or recording authority identity;
- accepted contracts, schemas, catalog profiles, policy decisions, consent decisions where applicable, and review records;
- EvidenceRef-to-EvidenceBundle resolution and applicable proof support;
- `CatalogBuildReceipt`, `RunReceipt`, `ValidationReport`, `RedactionReceipt`, applicable `ConsentGrant` or `RevocationReceipt`, policy, correction, and release-support receipts;
- STAC, DCAT, PROV, and triplet references when those projections exist;
- immutable release, correction, withdrawal, supersession, and rollback references.

Unknown or conflicted identity, jurisdiction, source role, rights, sensitivity, legal-description support, temporal role, evidence, consent applicability, policy, review, release, correction, or rollback state holds a record from public-bound or current-state use.

## Outputs

This lane may emit or support:

- internal or reviewable land-ownership catalog records;
- deterministic land-ownership indexes and bounded catalog subsets;
- release-linked catalog descriptors that reference, but do not replace, release authority;
- inspectable agreement inputs for domain, STAC, DCAT, PROV, triplet, evidence, source, policy, consent, and release closure;
- correction-, dispute-, withdrawal-, supersession-, and rollback-aware discovery metadata for governed APIs and review tools.

Catalog placement is not publication, legal notice, title certification, or boundary authority. Public clients receive only policy-safe, review-approved, release-approved representations through governed interfaces and public-safe carriers.

- contradictory identity, digest, role, time, rights, sensitivity, access, correction, or release state across catalog, evidence, source, policy, consent, and release surfaces;
- candidate, disputed, withdrawn, superseded, restricted, or unreleased records reaching a current-state or public surface;
- AI extraction, OCR, geocoding, entity resolution, chain inference, generated prose, or map presentation treated as evidence, legal interpretation, title authority, or release authority.

## Review burden

Land ownership combines contested evidence, legal and temporal nuance, jurisdictional variation, person and parcel identity, rights, privacy, consent, and join-induced sensitivity. Material catalog changes therefore need review proportional to risk across:

- People/DNA/Land and land-record semantics;
- recording, assessor, tax, cadastral, survey, and jurisdictional source roles;
- catalog, evidence, identity, temporal modeling, provenance, and schema;
- source activation, citation, license, rights, current terms, and retention;
- privacy, sensitivity, consent where applicable, deidentification, and harmful joins;
- validation, release, correction, withdrawal, supersession, rollback, and public delivery.

[CODEOWNERS](../../../../../.github/CODEOWNERS) currently routes repository review to `@bartytime4life` by default. That routing is not proof of accepted stewardship, specialist review, separation of duties, quorum, legal review, title review, privacy approval, or release authorization. Accountable assignments and required specialist reviewers remain `NEEDS VERIFICATION`.

## Lifecycle and catalog boundary

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    R["RAW<br/>source captures"] --> W["WORK / QUARANTINE<br/>transform or hold"]
    W --> P["PROCESSED<br/>validated candidates"]
    P --> C["CATALOG / TRIPLET<br/>governed discovery"]
    C --> U["PUBLISHED<br/>release-approved carriers"]
    L["This land-ownership lane"] -. "catalog responsibility" .-> C
```

This lane is the catalog-stage responsibility, not a storage shortcut across the lifecycle. RAW captures, working data, quarantined material, processed payloads, source descriptors, evidence, receipts, policies, release decisions, and published carriers remain in their own governed roots.

Promotion into a catalog record should be deterministic, receipt-backed, schema-validated, evidence-linked, policy-admitted, rights-aware, sensitivity-aware, correction-aware, and reviewable. Promotion toward public use additionally requires applicable consent handling, release closure, public-safe transformation, governed routes, and tested rollback. A file move, README, catalog index, rendered map, or successful check is not promotion.

<a id="land-ownership-guardrails"></a>

## Land Ownership guardrails

| Guardrail | Required posture |
|---|---|
| Evidence is not title | KFM may organize evidence and assertions; it does not render a title opinion, determine marketability, or adjudicate ownership |
| One instrument is not a complete chain | Preserve predecessor and successor references, gaps, overlaps, conflicts, exceptions, adverse evidence, and uncertainty |
| Assessor and tax records are administrative | Do not promote assessment, billing, mailing, situs, or taxpayer fields into title, possession, residency, or beneficial-ownership conclusions |
| Geometry is not legal boundary | Keep parcel geometry, survey evidence, legal descriptions, and adjudicated boundaries distinct |
| Name is not identity | Preserve source spelling, aliases, confidence, and resolution evidence; do not silently merge same-name parties or split one party |
| Interests cannot collapse | Keep fee, life estate, leasehold, easement, mineral, water, lien, encumbrance, beneficial, possessory, and other interests typed and scoped |
| Temporal roles cannot collapse | Keep execution, acknowledgment, recording, effective, assessment, tax, retrieval, review, release, correction, and supersession times distinct |
| Jurisdiction matters | Preserve county, recorder, court, tribal, federal, state, survey, and other authority context and its limits |
| Chain gaps remain visible | Do not infer continuity, exclusivity, priority, completeness, or current ownership across missing or contradictory evidence |
| Source role is identity-bearing | Preserve the admitted role through catalog, joins, derivatives, evidence, release, and correction; unresolved role fails closed |
| Living-person joins are deny-default | Private person-parcel, protected-address, genealogy, DNA, and cross-domain joins require applicable policy, consent, deidentification, review, and receipts |
| Public-safe does not mean harmless by default | Evaluate re-identification, stalking, targeting, discrimination, fraud, trespass, and aggregation risk before release |
| Catalog carriers are not source truth | Resolve claims to evidence and source records; do not infer truth from path, metadata completeness, or presentation quality |
| AI remains interpretive | OCR, extraction, matching, geocoding, chain inference, summaries, and classifications are candidates, never root evidence, legal interpretation, title authority, or release authority |

The inspected source registry defines seven current roles: `observed`, `regulatory`, `modeled`, `aggregate`, `administrative`, `candidate`, and `synthetic`. Older land-ownership documentation also uses `observation` and `authority`. Until an accepted decision resolves the vocabulary, preserve the original source-role value and provenance, do not silently normalize it, and hold records that cannot satisfy the accepted contract and policy.

The current catalog path uses the `people-dna-land` segment required by the inspected Directory Rules. Older documentation maps some contract, schema, policy, sensitivity, and consent surfaces to `people`. This README does not migrate or alias those roots. The conflict remains visible until an accepted placement decision and migration receipt resolve it.

## Migration, correction, and rollback

Use reversible, receipt-backed change:

1. identify the affected catalog identity, digest, object and interest type, jurisdiction, source role, temporal roles, sensitivity, projection set, release reference, and public carriers;
2. quarantine or hold contradictory, restricted, rights-unclear, identity-unclear, role-unclear, evidence-unclear, consent-unclear, or invalid candidates;
3. issue dispute, correction, withdrawal, tombstone, revocation, or supersession state without rewriting prior history or hiding chain gaps;
4. regenerate and validate affected domain, projection, evidence, source, policy, consent, receipt, and release references;
5. obtain required domain, land-record, source, rights, privacy, sensitivity, consent, schema, release, correction, and rollback review;
6. publish only through an approved release decision and preserve the prior rollback target.

Rollback is required if this lane becomes a raw-data root, work area, quarantine store, processed-data store, source registry, proof store, policy root, schema root, validator root, implementation root, release-decision root, published-output root, direct public read, legal or title authority, private-person lookup, or public-exposure shortcut.
