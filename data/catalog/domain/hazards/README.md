## Purpose

`data/catalog/domain/hazards/` is the canonical responsibility placement for governed Hazards-domain catalog records and indexes at the `CATALOG / TRIPLET` lifecycle stage. Candidate record families include historical hazard events, regulatory context, operational warning/advisory/watch context, disaster declarations, scientific observations, remote-sensing detections, modeled derivatives, exposure summaries, resilience summaries, timelines, and public-safe Hazards products.

This lane organizes governed references. It does not own source truth, legal determinations, emergency instructions, schemas, policies, evidence, release authority, or public delivery. Listing a record family does not prove that its records, contract, schema, validator, evidence, source activation, freshness enforcement, release state, or public route exist.

## Authority level

**Canonical responsibility placement for Hazards domain catalog records / repository-grounded draft / concrete catalog profile and realization PROPOSED / not truth, regulatory, life-safety, evidence, policy, release, or publication authority.**

This lane may carry catalog descriptors and governed references. It cannot replace Hazards meaning under `contracts/`, machine shape under `schemas/`, admissibility under `policy/`, source identity and activation under `data/registry/`, process memory under `data/receipts/`, EvidenceBundles and proof support under `data/proofs/`, release decisions under `release/`, public-safe carriers under `data/published/`, or the issuing authority for any current hazard.

## What belongs here

| Accepted family | Required posture |
|---|---|
| Hazards domain catalog records | Stable identity, object family, knowledge character, source role, spatial and temporal scope, lifecycle state, and release state |
| Hazards domain catalog indexes | Steward-facing or release-linked discovery; never an ungoverned public or operational shortcut |
| Historical-event descriptors | Event/source identity, observed or valid time, geography, evidence, uncertainty, revision, and correction state |
| Regulatory-context descriptors | Issuing authority, effective/version time, regulatory knowledge character, evidence, and explicit non-observation posture |
| Operational-context descriptors | Warning, advisory, or watch as context only, with issuer, issue time, expiry time, freshness state, official-source link, and not-for-life-safety posture |
| Scientific-observation descriptors | Observation source, measurement time, method, uncertainty, evidence, and owning-lane reference where applicable |
| Remote-sensing detection descriptors | Detection source, acquisition time, confidence or uncertainty, candidate status, and no silent conversion to confirmed event |
| Modeled, exposure, resilience, and timeline descriptors | Model/run identity, inputs, assumptions, bounds, source roles, sensitivity review, evidence, and release context |

## Inputs

Catalog inputs should be governed references and validated derivatives, not raw truth by placement:

- processed Hazards object identities, versions, digests, knowledge character, and temporal scope;
- source registry references with canonical source role, activation decision, rights, sensitivity, retrieval context, and official-source identity;
- contracts, schemas, catalog profiles, policy decisions, and review records;
- EvidenceRef-to-EvidenceBundle resolution and applicable proof support;
- `CatalogBuildReceipt`, `RunReceipt`, `ValidationReport`, freshness, correction, and release-support receipts;
- STAC, DCAT, PROV, and triplet references when those projections exist;
- immutable release, correction, withdrawal, supersession, and rollback references.

Unknown or conflicted source role, knowledge character, identity, rights, sensitivity, evidence, freshness, expiry, policy, review, release, correction, or rollback state holds a record from public-bound or current-state use.

## Outputs

This lane may emit or support:

- internal or reviewable Hazards catalog records;
- deterministic Hazards domain indexes and catalog subsets;
- release-linked catalog descriptors that reference, but do not replace, release authority;
- inspectable agreement inputs for domain, STAC, DCAT, PROV, triplet, evidence, source, and release closure;
- correction-, withdrawal-, supersession-, stale-state-, and rollback-aware discovery metadata for governed APIs and review tools.

Catalog placement is not publication, real-time readiness, or life-safety authority. Public clients receive only policy-safe, release-approved representations through governed interfaces and public-safe carriers.

| Spatial and temporal support | Geography/version, precision, event/observed/valid/issue/expiry/retrieval/release/correction times, and uncertainty remain distinct where material |
