# Soil promotion materiality fixtures

Synthetic, no-network inputs and expected shared `MaterialChangeAssessment` outputs.

- `unchanged` and `timestamp_only` are `NON_EVENT`.
- changes to content/source/schema/validator/policy hashes are `PROMOTION_CANDIDATE` only.
- missing baseline or incomplete evidence is `HOLD`.
- malformed or temporally inconsistent inputs are `ERROR`.

No fixture activates a source or authorizes promotion, release, publication, or public use.
