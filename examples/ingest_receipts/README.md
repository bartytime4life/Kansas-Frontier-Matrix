## Purpose

Teach source-head capture, source-role preservation, ingest outcomes, quarantine routing, and the receipt ≠ proof ≠ catalog ≠ release boundary.

This lane exists to make review and learning faster. It must not become a parallel contract, schema, policy, fixture, test, proof, receipt, source registry, runtime, release, or publication authority.

## Authority level

**Non-authoritative receipt example lane; emitted process memory belongs under `data/receipts/`.**

Operational meaning remains owned by the relevant `docs/`, `contracts/`, `schemas/`, `policy/`, implementation, `tests/`, `fixtures/`, `data/`, and `release/` surfaces.

## What belongs here

- synthetic source-intake receipt sketches
- fake ETag, Last-Modified, generation, cursor, and digest examples
- `PASS`, `WARN`, `FAIL`, `HOLD`, `DENY`, `QUARANTINE`, `ABSTAIN`, and `ERROR` examples
- redacted review-safe summaries
- walkthroughs showing receipt references without treating them as proof

Every new file must be visibly synthetic or safely transformed, name its learning objective, identify the operational home it does not replace, and declare its expected finite outcome.

## What does NOT belong here

| Do not place here | Correct home or action |
|---|---|
| emitted receipts, signatures, receipt indexes, or checksums | `data/receipts/ingest/` or an accepted receipt lane |
| real source payloads or SourceDescriptor records | `data/raw/`, registry, or restricted storage |
| proof, catalog, policy, or release objects | their canonical roots |
| connector, pipeline, validator, fixture, test, or workflow code | their implementation roots |
| credentials, consent/revocation tokens, private review notes, or sensitive coordinates | never commit; synthesize or deny |

## Inputs

Synthetic source-head metadata, example SourceDescriptor refs, fake digests, illustrative policy states, and current receipt doctrine.

Input provenance, real-versus-synthetic status, rights, sensitivity, and the operational object being illustrated must be explicit.

## Outputs

Static receipt-shape demonstrations marked non-authoritative and never emitted process memory.

Outputs may be reviewed, corrected, or proposed for separate fixture/test graduation. They do not become operational merely by being copied.

## Validation

- Confirm all hashes, signatures, IDs, source refs, and source-head values are synthetic.
- Confirm source role, approval state, rights, sensitivity, and handoff outcome remain explicit.
- Confirm the receipt does not claim proof, catalog closure, release approval, or source truth.
- Confirm negative conditions route to finite fail-closed outcomes.
- Validate snippets, links, and sensitive-content posture.

Every consequential example must declare:

```yaml
example: true
authority: non_authoritative_example

maturity: STATIC_WALKTHROUGH
real_vs_synthetic: explicit
expected_outcome: ANSWER | ABSTAIN | DENY | ERROR | HOLD | QUARANTINE | NOT_APPLICABLE

validation_boundary: "<exact checks performed>"
correction_trigger: "<contract, policy, source, runtime, or release change>"
```

`ANSWER` requires support appropriate to the scenario. Missing, stale, conflicting, or citation-invalid evidence yields `ABSTAIN`; prohibited rights/sensitivity/role exposure yields `DENY` or `HOLD`; tool/schema/runtime failure yields `ERROR`.

## Guardrails

| Risk | Guardrail |
|---|---|
| Receipt becomes source truth | A receipt records process observations, not factual correctness. |
| Receipt becomes proof or catalog | Keep proof/catalog families separate. |
| Receipt becomes release approval | Release remains a governed decision under `release/`. |
| Sensitive process detail leaks | Use redacted/synthetic summaries and no secrets. |
