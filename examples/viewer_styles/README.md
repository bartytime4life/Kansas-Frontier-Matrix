## Purpose

Teach MapLibre style fragments, legends, color ramps, feature states, trust badges, negative states, accessibility, and the rule that styling is not governance.

This lane exists to make review and learning faster. It must not become a parallel contract, schema, policy, fixture, test, proof, receipt, source registry, runtime, release, or publication authority.

## Authority level

**Non-authoritative style example lane; operational styles, manifests, runtime helpers, UI components, published layers, and release decisions remain elsewhere.**

Operational meaning remains owned by the relevant `docs/`, `contracts/`, `schemas/`, `policy/`, implementation, `tests/`, `fixtures/`, `data/`, and `release/` surfaces.

## What belongs here

- synthetic paint/layout fragments
- legend and color-ramp examples with units and caveats
- released/stale/denied/restricted/error trust-badge examples
- hover/selection examples that identify candidates rather than proof
- accessibility and Reality Boundary style notes

Every new file must be visibly synthetic or safely transformed, name its learning objective, identify the operational home it does not replace, and declare its expected finite outcome.

## What does NOT belong here

| Do not place here | Correct home or action |
|---|---|
| production style JSON, manifests, tile URLs, sprites, glyphs, or published style payloads | released layer/artifact homes and `release/` |
| MapLibre helpers, UI components, app routes, or runtime config | `packages/maplibre/`, `packages/ui/`, and `apps/explorer-web/` |
| proofs, receipts, policy decisions, release decisions, schemas, tests, or fixtures | their canonical roots |
| sensitive geometry hidden only by filters, opacity, zoom, or labels | transform before rendering or deny |
| design tokens or compatibility roots promoted to authority | canonical package/app homes and accepted migration |

## Inputs

Layering/map-shell doctrine, synthetic layer/style refs, finite policy states, accessibility requirements, and example evidence/release refs.

Input provenance, real-versus-synthetic status, rights, sensitivity, and the operational object being illustrated must be explicit.

## Outputs

Static style and trust-state demonstrations marked `not_a_style_manifest` and `do_not_publish`.

Outputs may be reviewed, corrected, or proposed for separate fixture/test graduation. They do not become operational merely by being copied.

## Validation

- Confirm style never supplies truth, policy, geoprivacy, access control, or release authority.
- Confirm sensitive detail is transformed before rendering.
- Confirm trust state is not represented by color alone.
- Confirm stale/unreleased/unsigned/denied/citation-failed states render safe negative states.
- Validate snippets, links, labels, accessibility notes, and synthetic refs.

No examples-specific runner or complete validator was verified. A Markdown/source check proves only the declared static scope.
