## What belongs here

- synthetic HabitatPatch and context examples
- land-cover and ecoregion examples with source-role and crosswalk caveats
- suitability-not-occurrence and connectivity-not-movement demonstrations
- restoration-opportunity-not-prescription examples
- negative examples for rare species, rare plants, archaeology, private land, and infrastructure joins

Every new file must be visibly synthetic or safely transformed, name its learning objective, identify the operational home it does not replace, and declare its expected finite outcome.

## What does NOT belong here

| Do not place here | Correct home or action |
|---|---|
| real Habitat source or lifecycle payloads | `data/<phase>/habitat/` |
| operational catalog, proof, receipt, published layer, or release objects | `data/catalog/`, `data/proofs/`, `data/receipts/`, `data/published/`, and `release/` |
| schemas, contracts, policy, validators, tests, fixtures, or implementation code | their canonical responsibility roots |
| exact sensitive occurrence or cross-lane joins | generalize, redact, restrict, delay, quarantine, or deny |
| management prescriptions or regulatory determinations | owning agencies and governed evidence/release paths |

## Inputs

Habitat doctrine, verified operational references, synthetic geometry, source-role labels, and example evidence/policy states.

Input provenance, real-versus-synthetic status, rights, sensitivity, and the operational object being illustrated must be explicit.

## Outputs

Static synthetic Habitat demonstrations with explicit model/context limitations and finite outcomes.

Outputs may be reviewed, corrected, or proposed for separate fixture/test graduation. They do not become operational merely by being copied.

## Validation

- Confirm suitability is not occurrence and HabitatPatch is not critical-habitat designation.
- Confirm land cover is context, not Habitat, Fauna, Flora, Soil, Hydrology, or regulatory truth by itself.
- Confirm sensitive joins are synthetic/generalized/denied.
- Confirm cross-domain ownership remains visible.
- Validate links, examples, guardrails, and finite outcomes.

No examples-specific runner or complete validator was verified. A Markdown/source check proves only the declared static scope.

## Review burden

Examples/docs, Habitat, evidence, policy/sensitivity, release, and any referenced domain steward.

CODEOWNERS routing is not stewardship, approval evidence, policy permission, or release authorization.
