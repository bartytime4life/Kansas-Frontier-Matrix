# eBird redistribution review

Draft review guidance, 2026-10-08. Source terms, individual decisions and enforcement must be established for the exact candidate.

## Scope and source role

This guide prepares review of an explicitly identified eBird product and intended use. The Fauna file-system plan records the eBird Basic Dataset as requiring rights review before activation. That planning statement is not a current license determination and does not establish permission for another eBird product.

Keep the provider product, edition/download identifier, query or selection, observer-related fields and native record identifiers. A community-science aggregate is not a regulatory-status authority, and missing records do not establish species absence.

## Review packet

| Record | Reason |
|---|---|
| Exact product and terms URL, captured review date | Different products and access routes can have different conditions |
| Requested operations and audience | Local analysis, derived display, redistribution and publication are separate uses |
| Selected fields and geometry precision | Observation and observer information can require distinct controls |
| Proposed derivative and attribution | Review what a consumer can actually recover |
| Reviewer disposition and unresolved obligations | Prevent a download from becoming implied approval |

Before any redistribution, verify the applicable current terms and decision for that exact product. Keep unresolved permissions at review; do not copy raw records into public fixtures, tiles, logs or examples. Review joined/exported representations as well as the map.

## Completion and correction

A completed review identifies the candidate, terms evidence, allowed scope, obligations and responsible decision. It must remain linked to the source descriptor and derivative lineage. Changed terms, fields, audience, product edition or geometry precision trigger reconsideration. This Markdown guide does not implement an evaluator or authorize redistribution.
