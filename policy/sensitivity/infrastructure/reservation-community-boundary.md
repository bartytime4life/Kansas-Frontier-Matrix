# Reservation and community boundary review

Draft review guidance, 2026-10-08. Source terms, individual decisions and enforcement must be established for the exact candidate.

## Representation boundary

A mapped reservation, community, place or administrative boundary has a source-specific meaning and edition. It must not silently become a legal-title, sovereignty, service-area or identity determination. This guide prepares a review of the intended representation and preserves the relevant community/authority relationship.

## Review inputs

| Input | Question |
|---|---|
| Provider/product and native feature ID | Which authority and definition produced this geometry? |
| Edition and valid period | Is this a historical or current representation? |
| Geometry and precision | What limitations or disputed boundaries are documented? |
| Labels and joins | Could naming or combined detail imply a claim beyond the source? |
| Audience and intended use | Is a public/generalized representation permitted? |
| Review route | Which qualified authority must assess the specific question? |

Describe uncertainty, omissions and conflicting sources explicitly. Preserve source attribution and avoid filling gaps with inferred boundaries. Review facility or infrastructure joins for harmful precision and unintended claims about communities.

## Handoff and maintenance

Provide the exact candidate, representation and source evidence to the relevant review route. Do not substitute repository ownership for cultural, community, source or release authority. No decision or current legal boundary is established by this Markdown guide. Revisit the assessment when the source edition, label, geometry, audience or join changes, and retain correction lineage.
