# NatureServe Explorer Pro product review

Draft review guidance, 2026-10-08. Source terms, individual decisions and enforcement must be established for the exact candidate.

## Product boundary

Review the exact NatureServe product, edition and access route requested. A reference to Explorer Pro does not establish an entitlement or permission to download, cache or redistribute its content. Do not transfer assumptions between product tiers or between a public web view and an export.

## Required packet

Record product/record identifiers, taxonomic and status fields, edition and valid dates, access conditions, current terms evidence, intended operation and audience. Preserve the distinction between conservation/status context and an observed occurrence. Identify the custodian and qualified review route without inventing an assigned steward.

## Derivative review

Explain how the candidate transforms or joins the material. Check whether a map, table, download, report or AI explanation could reveal restricted precision or substitute a generalized status for local observation. Retain required attribution and original field meaning. If a field or use condition cannot be verified, keep it explicitly unresolved.

## Completion criteria

The review is usable when the exact product and candidate are identifiable, the requested use is bounded, terms/access evidence is recorded, sensitivity has been considered separately, and an authorized disposition can be linked. This documentation change supplies none of those decisions by itself. Future product, edition, audience or access changes require renewed review.
