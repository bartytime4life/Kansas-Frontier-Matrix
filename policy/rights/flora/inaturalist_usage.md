# iNaturalist usage review

Draft review guidance, 2026-10-08. Source terms, individual decisions and enforcement must be established for the exact candidate.

## Distinguish the material

Review observation data, images or other media, identity/attribution fields and location precision as separate parts of the intended use. Record the actual rights declarations provided for each selected resource instead of assigning one assumed permission to the entire provider.

## Review checklist

| Input | Required explanation |
|---|---|
| Observation and media identifiers | Which exact resources the candidate uses |
| Source time and retrieval time | What the observation represents and when it was obtained |
| Rights declarations and current terms | Evidence for the selected operation and audience |
| Location privacy representation | Whether coordinates are obscured, restricted, generalized or unresolved |
| Attribution and derivative lineage | How source identity survives transformations |
| Join/export behavior | Whether a downstream product reveals more than the original representation |

Do not attempt to recover obscured locations through joins, auxiliary text or media metadata. Preserve uncertainty and privacy indicators. A quality label or taxonomic determination does not itself establish suitability for a KFM claim or grant publication rights.

## Handoff

Pass the exact candidate, selected resources, intended use and unresolved questions to the source-rights and sensitivity review routes. Unknown conditions remain pending. Changed media, audience, coordinates or reuse purpose requires a new assessment. This guide contains no active policy implementation and makes no blanket license determination.
