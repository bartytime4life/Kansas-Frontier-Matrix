# GBIF product rights review

Draft review guidance, 2026-10-08. Source terms, individual decisions and enforcement must be established for the exact candidate.

## Review unit

Review the specific GBIF-mediated dataset or download and its contributing records. Do not replace record/dataset attribution and rights information with a generic provider label. Preserve native occurrence and dataset identifiers, download identity where available, source role, edition and retrieved rights metadata.

## Procedure

1. Identify the exact dataset/download and requested operation.
2. Record the current terms and license evidence attached to that material; distinguish provider service conditions from contributing content declarations.
3. Preserve attribution and citation information through normalization and derivative creation.
4. Review sensitive coordinates and joins separately from reuse rights.
5. Record the reviewer disposition for the candidate and intended audience; keep unknown conditions unresolved.

## Acceptance examples

A useful packet can trace a displayed record back to its dataset and rights declaration, explains the derivative, and identifies the decision permitting its use. A packet is incomplete when it says only “from GBIF,” discards contributor attribution, or treats public access as permission for every export.

This page supplies a review method, not a verified current license interpretation. Current terms must be read for the actual product during review. Do not infer a machine-enforced rule from this document or the presence of a Flora rights test filename.
