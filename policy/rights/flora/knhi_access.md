# Kansas Natural Heritage Inventory access review

Draft review guidance, 2026-10-08. Source terms, individual decisions and enforcement must be established for the exact candidate.

## Purpose

Prepare a source-specific access and sensitivity assessment for material identified in the Flora source guide as KNHI-related. A planning reference to this source does not prove that an account, agreement, downloadable product or reusable dataset is available.

## Before obtaining or using records

Identify the actual custodian, product, access route and proposed research purpose. Record any agreement or written permission by reference without placing restricted terms, credentials or sensitive records in this public repository. Confirm which fields, location precision, audience and retention conditions apply to the requested material.

Keep heritage status, taxonomic identity and occurrence evidence distinct. A record used for conservation context does not automatically authorize publication of a precise occurrence or justify inferring absence elsewhere.

## Review deliverable

A useful packet identifies the source/product, permitted purpose, approved audience if any, constraints on location detail and redistribution, retention/correction contact, and unresolved questions. It also describes the actual derivative and how its exports avoid revealing restricted details.

No fixed download URL, entitlement, current terms, approval or source activation is established by this page. Missing access evidence remains a concrete review blocker. Use synthetic examples for tests and documentation rather than real restricted locations.
